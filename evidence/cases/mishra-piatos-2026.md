## Held

- Whether a de facto relationship existed is an evaluative finding reviewed for **correctness**.
- No separate 'temporary breakdown' gateway: a **holistic** s 4AA(2) assessment, with periods aggregated under s 90SB(a).
- No procedural unfairness (counsel agreed the trial plan; the late affidavit left time to respond). The AI-drafted grounds 'obscure the task of identification of error'. Appeal dismissed; no costs.
