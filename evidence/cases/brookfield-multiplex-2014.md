The High Court held that the builder owed **no duty of care** in negligence to a subsequent owners
corporation for pure economic loss arising from latent defects, largely because the parties were
sophisticated and had allocated risk by contract.

## Use it for

The **failure** that starts the reform chain. On its own it is a common law case about negligence; in
an answer it is the evidence that the common law could not protect owners, which is what makes s 37
of the *Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020* (NSW) a response rather than an initiative.
