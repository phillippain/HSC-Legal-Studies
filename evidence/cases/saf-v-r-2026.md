SAF was convicted by Judge Smith SC in a judge-alone District Court trial of six counts of indecent assault on a child and acquitted on others. About a year late he sought leave to appeal, arguing the complaint finding was wrong and the verdicts inconsistent.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| The complaint finding | No merit |
| Inconsistent verdicts | No merit |
| The delay | Unexplained |
| Result | Extension of time refused; the release application was not considered |

## Use it for

- **crime.4.5** — time limits and a demanding test protect finality.
- **Balance** — a complainant's long-delayed account against fair process for the accused.

## Caution

Procedural (extension of time); whether leave would have been granted was not decided.
