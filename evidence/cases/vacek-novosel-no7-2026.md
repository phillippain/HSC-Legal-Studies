The seventh judgment in one family's litigation. Earlier orders required the husband to pay the
mortgages; he did not. After a five-day final hearing in 2025 and an enforcement application when
sale proceeds were not distributed as ordered, the final judgment (March 2026) split the property
55/45 in the wife's favour and found his conduct showed "forensic opportunism … designed to
subordinate the mother's autonomy, impose cost and inconvenience, and secure bargaining leverage".
She then sought her costs: **$634,031**.

## Held — costs fixed at $113,000; indemnity costs refused

- **s 114UB(1)** — the general rule is that each party pays their own costs; **s 114UB(2)–(3)**
  lets the court depart from it, weighing means, conduct, disclosure, compliance with orders,
  success and offers to settle.
- He gave "late, incomplete and selective disclosure" [42]. Five costs orders had already been made
  against him ($106,621.98, of which $71,938.80 was unpaid).
- Not "wholly unsuccessful", and her 2023 offer was unproven; indemnity costs are for exceptional
  cases and she produced no itemised bills.
- A fixed sum rather than an assessment, because assessment "would most likely lead to further
  litigation … the parties need this matter to come to an end" [85]: **$100,000** for the final
  hearing (her costs "at least 20 per cent higher" because of him) plus **$13,000** for enforcement,
  taken from his property entitlement — and if that falls short, a property is sold.

## Use it for

- **Compliance** — the fullest example in the collection of non-compliance and the law's
  graduated response: disclosure duties, enforcement, repeated costs orders.
- **fam.2.4** — family violence continuing *through* the court process after separation, recognised
  in a finding about litigation itself.
- **Effectiveness** — a precise measure of the law's limits: the successful party is still left
  with **$414,409.02** of costs no order covers.
- **fam.2.5** — the overarching purpose (ss 190–191 of the *Federal Circuit and Family Court Act
  2021*): justly, quickly, inexpensively.
