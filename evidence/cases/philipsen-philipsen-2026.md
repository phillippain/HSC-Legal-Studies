A wife sought property orders after a 2014 overseas marriage. The husband sought nullity and dismissal.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Does a void marriage remove property jurisdiction? | No |
| Validity or nullity | Evidence too thin for either declaration |
| Result | Dismissal refused; the property claim goes on |
