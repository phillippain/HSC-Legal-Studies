## The steps

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 2020 | **Brereton Report**: credible information of **39 unlawful killings** by or involving 25 special forces personnel |
| 2021 | **Office of the Special Investigator** (OSI) established to investigate with the AFP |
| March 2023 | **Oliver Schulz** charged with the war crime of murder; pleaded not guilty; trial expected **February 2027**; his defence has signalled it will argue he was following orders |
| 7 April 2026 | **Ben Roberts-Smith** charged with **five counts** of the war crime of murder (s 268.70) over killings in 2009 and 2012; bail granted 17 April |

At April 2026 the OSI had examined **53 allegations**: 39 finalised with no charge, 10 ongoing.
**All charges are unproven** and both men are presumed innocent.

## Why so slow

Investigators **cannot enter Afghanistan** to examine scenes or bodies; witnesses are overseas; and
classified material is managed under national security information rules, which slows every
pre-trial step.

## Use it for

- **Complementarity** (Rome Statute art 17): Australia investigating its own keeps the ICC out.
- **Domestic measures**: Div 268 and the Attorney-General's consent (s 268.121).
- **Limitations**: evidence abroad, delay and cost.
- Non-legal: investigative journalism and whistleblowers brought the allegations to light.
