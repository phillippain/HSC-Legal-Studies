Lynette Dawson was last heard from in January 1982. Two inquests found she was probably murdered;
charges came only after the case was reopened and *The Teacher's Pet* podcast (2018) drew attention.
Dawson sought a **permanent stay** for prejudicial publicity: refused, though a temporary stay was granted.
In May 2022 a **judge-alone** order was made with the prosecution's agreement.

- **31 August 2022**: Harrison J found him guilty of murder. No single circumstance proved guilt, but
  together they left murder as the only rational inference.
- **2 December 2022**: 24 years, non-parole 18.
- **Dawson v R [2024] NSWCCA 98** (13 June 2024): conviction appeal dismissed. High Court special leave refused (2025).
- **Law reform**: NSW "no body, no parole" laws (October 2022).

## Use it for

- **Judge-alone trials** (Criminal Procedure Act s 132) — contrast Kristian White, whose application was refused in 2024.
- **Circumstantial evidence and standard of proof** after 40 years' delay.
- **Media and the presumption of innocence**: publicity reopened the case and endangered the trial.
