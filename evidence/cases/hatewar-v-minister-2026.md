Outside Australia since 2022, the applicant challenged a delegate's May 2026 refusal of a return visa under **s 501** (character),
arguing the delegate misread **Direction 110**, acted illogically and ignored medical evidence.

| Ground | Held |
|---|---|
| Misreading Direction 110 | Rejected — a delegate must apply a binding direction, and did |
| Illogicality | Rejected |
| Ignoring medical evidence | Rejected |
| Fourth ground | Rejected |

Application **dismissed with costs**, decided on the papers.
