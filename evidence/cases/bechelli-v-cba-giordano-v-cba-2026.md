Ms Giordano, who has a disability, and her carer-cousin Ms Bechelli visited a Commonwealth Bank branch on 13 December 2024. The
manager asked to speak to Ms Giordano alone and her account was locked. After the Anti-Discrimination Board's President declined
their complaints, they needed NCAT's **leave** to proceed.

| Complaint | Leave |
|---|---|
| Giordano: disability discrimination | **Refused** |
| Bechelli: disability discrimination (as associate) | **Refused** |
| Bechelli: sexual harassment by the manager and bank | **Granted** — listed for a case conference |

The Tribunal read protective law widely (a non-customer in a branch), but s 49M leaves the *manner* of service uncovered — a gap.

## Use it for

- **hr.2.9 / hr.2.11** — a state anti-discrimination law enforced through a complaints body and a tribunal.
- **Effectiveness** — a leave filter protects respondents but can end a complaint at the first hurdle.

## Caution

A **leave decision only**: the harassment allegation is untested and denied. The file has paragraph 55 twice.
