Nikita Kirby, a 33-year-old Aboriginal woman, fatally stabbed her partner once during a drunken argument on 28 May 2024. A murder charge ended with a guilty plea to manslaughter (unlawful and dangerous act).

## What the court decided

| Factor | Finding |
|---|---|
| Objective seriousness | Below the middle of the range |
| Moral culpability | Significantly reduced by cognitive impairment and deprivation |
| Discount | 25% for the plea |
| Special circumstances | Found |
| Sentence | 6 years from 28 May 2024 (non-parole 3 years); first parole date 27 May 2027 |

Sentence only; the pre-discount term and a s 23A question are not decided or not in the file. A death in a domestic setting — teach with care.
