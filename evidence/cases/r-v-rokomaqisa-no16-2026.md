During the Crown case a police witness described the accused's associates and 'police holdings'. The defence applied to discharge the jury for incurable prejudice.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Three passages examined | Any prejudice minor |
| Curable? | Yes, by directions |
| Result | Discharge refused — not necessary to prevent a miscarriage of justice |
