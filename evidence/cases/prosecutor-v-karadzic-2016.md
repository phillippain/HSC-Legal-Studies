## Facts and verdict

Radovan Karadzic was tried on **11 counts** for 1991-95 crimes in Bosnia. The Trial Chamber found him **not guilty of Count 1** (genocide in the municipalities) and **guilty of Counts 2 to 11**: genocide (Srebrenica), persecution, extermination, murder, deportation, forcible transfer, terror (the siege of Sarajevo), unlawful attacks on civilians and hostage-taking, mostly through four joint criminal enterprises. **Single sentence of 40 years**, with credit from 21 July 2008. Judges Morrison and Baird each dissented on one incident only.

## Caution

This is the **trial judgement only**; the appeal is not in the file. Outside the judgment: the IRMCT Appeals Chamber on 20 March 2019 increased the sentence to **life imprisonment** (public record: confirm before teaching). Only key passages of the ~2,500-page judgement were read. Mass-atrocity subject matter: keep factual.
