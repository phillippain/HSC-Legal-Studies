Maria Radaich ran a milk bar in a Mosman lock-up shop under a five-year deed of 29 May 1954 that called her a **licensee**. She asked the Fair Rents Board to fix a fair rent; the owners said a licence was outside its jurisdiction.

## Held

The High Court (Dixon CJ, McTiernan, Taylor, Menzies and Windeyer JJ) **unanimously allowed the appeal**. The question is whether the deed gave a legal right to **exclusive possession** for a term; if it did, there is a lease whatever the parties called it. Windeyer J found only a licence over the toilet and passage.

## Use it for

- **Leasing**: the test that separates a tenant from a licensee (`shel.2.2`).
- **Protection and boarders/lodgers**: why an arrangement without exclusive possession gets less protection (`shel.2.3`).
- **Courts**: a characterisation question decided by the High Court on special leave (`shel.2.6`).

## Caution

This is a **commercial shop** under 1950s fair rents law, not a residential tenancy, so use it for the principle only. The file gives no hearing date or counsel and no section numbers of the Landlord and Tenant (Amendment) Act, and the NSW label for that Act is inferred.
