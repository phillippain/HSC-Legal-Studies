Ms Moltter, 71, an on-hire project manager, said she was told to retire when her engagement ended in February 2025. Anti-Discrimination NSW declined her complaint as lacking in substance, so she needed NCAT's leave.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Her evidence at its highest, including a remark not denied | Budget was the likely real reason |
| Reasonably arguable? | No |
| Result | Leave to proceed refused |
| Not decided | Whether the remarks were made |

## Use it for

- **hr.2.9 / hr.2.11** — a state anti-discrimination law, a complaints body and a tribunal; compare *Bechelli v CBA*.
- **Law reform** — is 'reasonably arguable' too strict when a discriminatory remark is not denied?

## Caution

Leave only — no findings of fact.
