Molly Ticehurst, 28, ended her relationship with Daniel Billings in 2023. On **5 April 2024** she
reported him at Forbes Police Station; he was charged with sexual assault, property damage and
killing her puppy, and a provisional AVO was made. The next morning **Dubbo Local Court granted
conditional bail**. On **21 April 2024** he drove an hour to Forbes, smashed her bedroom window and
stabbed her fifteen times in 59 seconds. He pleaded guilty to murder in the Local Court, declining to
rely on substantial impairment.

## Held — N Adams CJ at CL, 11 September 2026

- **Objective seriousness** — planned, retributive, in her home, with a hunting knife, and a "very
  serious 'public justice' offence" because she was the witness against him: the high end of the
  range. The Crown did not seek life.
- **Bail** — being on conditional liberty is aggravating under *Crimes (Sentencing Procedure) Act*
  **s 21A(2)(j)**, but for the purposes of sentencing, not objective seriousness. The judgment records
  the grant of bail and her fear, **without criticising** the magistrate.
- **The man** — depression and an abusive childhood (*Bugmy*) reduced moral culpability, but "a
  reduction in moral culpability does not automatically translate to a reduced sentence".
- **The plea** — entitled to 25% for an early plea; under **s 25F(2)** reduced to **20%** for a revenge
  killing of a complainant. Starting point 35 years → **28 years, non-parole 20** (expiring 2044).
- Five family victim impact statements were received, and the harm to her family accepted as harm to
  the community (s 30E(3)).

## Caution

The sexual assault charges were never decided — do not describe them as proved. The judgment does
not cite the *Bail Act* or criticise the bail decision; the reform link is the judge's own one-line
remark plus the legislative record. An appeal was lodged in October 2026 (ABC record).
