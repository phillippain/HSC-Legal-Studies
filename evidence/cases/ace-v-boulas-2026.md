In a dispute between siblings over their 103-year-old mother's 2023 will, the son tried to tender a translated transcript of part of a
phone call he had secretly recorded through CCTV.

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Was the recording made in breach of the law? | Yes — s 7(1)(a) of the *Surveillance Devices Act 2007* (NSW) |
| Should it be admitted under s 138? | No — weighing the s 138 factors, the desirability of admitting it did not outweigh the undesirability of how it was obtained |

Section 138 is not limited to evidence obtained by police (*Kadir v The Queen; Grech v The Queen*).
