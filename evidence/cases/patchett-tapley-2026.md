Four Aboriginal children, aged 18 to 8, of parents who never married. The mother's partner, Mr B,
was alleged to have assaulted and indecently assaulted a nine-year-old. A criminal court acquitted
him; one child protection investigation substantiated harm and another did not; and the family court
found the **father had coached the allegations** — he wrote the script and put it on the fridge.
Four institutions, four answers on one set of facts. The judge also found the father "is a racist".

## Held — all four children live with their father, under injunctions

- Both parents keep parental responsibility, but engineered: written notice, a **14-day response
  period**, and a tie-break to the father for three children and to the resident parent for the
  fourth.
- **s 68B injunctions**: the father restrained from denigrating the children's **Indigenous race,
  culture and heritage**; both parents restrained from denigration, exposing the children to
  violence, verbal abuse and physical discipline.
- Time with the mother follows the two eldest children's wishes; the youngest escalates to alternate
  long weekends and week-about holidays. Mr B is restrained from contact with X for three months —
  the father had proposed eighteen.
- **s 68Q**: the state domestic violence order protecting X is invalid to the extent of any
  inconsistency; copies to police, the department and the Local Court.
- The Independent Children's Lawyer's proposals became the orders; a contact centre, an Indigenous
  liaison officer and a parenting app do the work the parents cannot.

## Use it for

- **fam.3.2 and law reform** — a worked example of the post-2023 Act: the rewritten s 60CC list, no
  presumption of equal shared parental responsibility, and the **standalone cultural right in
  s 60CC(3)**, protected by injunction rather than by any discrimination statute.
- **fam.2.4** — family violence in four directions at once, including through the legal system, as
  coached allegations (systems abuse).
- **Changing community values** — a judicial finding of racism, and orders built on it.
- **Effectiveness** — three systems, three standards of proof, three different answers.

## Caution

Findings on the balance of probabilities in a parenting case are not findings of guilt: Mr B was
**acquitted** in the criminal court. Summary prepared for class from the judgment; quotes checked
against the judgment text.
