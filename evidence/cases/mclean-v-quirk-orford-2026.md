Mr McLean ran his own renovation alongside Mr Quirk-Orford under an oral cost-plus deal; the builder held no home warranty insurance.

## What the court decided

| Claim | Held |
|---|---|
| Consumer-law and overpayment claims | Rejected |
| Incomplete work | Many items treated as work the owners took over |
| Defects (e.g. wet-area tiling) | $29,281.70 within 28 days |
| Compliance certificates | For certifiable work the builder actually completed |
| Builder's set-off | Barred by an earlier NCAT decision |
