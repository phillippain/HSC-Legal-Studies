Four children of a sibling group of five, serious untested risks in **both** households, and a
mother who withdrew from the case and never returned. Allegations included coercive control by the
stepfather — a man who entered the family when they were most vulnerable, because the mother was
weighing **homelessness against family violence**. The father had an apprehended violence order,
breach charges and a criminal sanction against him. The children had not been to school; their views
were found to be "the product of indoctrination by the mother". Nothing could be proved or disproved.

## Held — undefended, and openly provisional

The three younger children live with their mother, who has sole decision-making for them; the eldest
lives with the father, who has sole decision-making for him — "there is no possibility of shared
parental responsibility" where the parties cannot communicate at all. All four spend 1.5 hours a
month together at a named park. Quarterly "recognition time" of up to four hours with the father and
grandmother, professionally supervised at his cost; at 15 each child may decide for themselves. The
mother **must enrol the children in school** by the first day of term 4, 2026.

The file, reports, reasons and orders go to **child protection and the Department of Education**,
with a note that these orders are no impediment to action in the Children's Court.

## Use it for

- **Effectiveness** — the sharpest statement available of the limits of legal responses: the judge
  says he has no confidence the children are safe where he has ordered them to live, and hands the
  problem to another agency.
- **fam.3.4** — care and protection as the backstop, and the referral out as the only bridge between
  a federal parenting court and a state child protection system.
- **shel.3.3** — homelessness as the risk weighed against family violence; out-of-home care as the
  alternative the judge contemplates.
- **Rights of the child** — the right to be heard, and its limits where views are found to be
  indoctrinated; the right to education used as a protective measure, because children at school are
  children independent adults can see.

## Caution

Oral reasons in an **undefended** hearing with no finding of unacceptable risk against anyone. Its
value is the judge's candour about what a court cannot do, not any factual finding about this
family. Summary prepared for class from the judgment; quotes checked against the judgment text.
