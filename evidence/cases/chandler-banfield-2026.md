## Held

- The Court proceeded in his absence (r 10.27); he may apply to set aside.
- Unacceptable risk from methamphetamine use, serious mental illness and family violence; children aged 11 and 7: views not given determinative weight.
- Sole decision-making to the mother, children live with her, **no contact**, s 68B injunctions (200 metres), passport and s 65Y travel orders.
