Mr Mackell was charged with using a carriage service to menace, harass or offend after about 192 calls and 8 emails to a federal MP's office. After the charge he emailed the MP and her chief of staff.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Was the email witness intimidation? | Yes — a bail concern existed |
| No-contact and no-entry conditions | Kept |
| Result | Application to vary bail dismissed |

## Use it for

- **crime.2.5** — bail concerns and conditions protecting witnesses.
- **Balance** — the accused's liberty and political speech versus witness protection.

## Caution

A bail variation ruling, not a finding of guilt.
