A trial before Altobelli J (30 March–2 April and 6 May 2026) over a child born in **2024**. The relationship began in
March 2022; the parties separated in **July 2024**; the father filed in December 2024. Interim orders in February
2025 gave the mother sole decision-making and supervised time; consent orders in June 2025 moved to unsupervised
time. Judgment and final orders: **25 September 2026**. (Pseudonyms.)

## What the court found

- **Evidence ruling**: a counsellor's affidavit was excluded — she went beyond her role, gave opinions and showed
  partisanship.
- **Family violence (s 4AB)**: the father pathologised the mother, sent petty and threatening messages and drew
  **daycare** into the dispute; this was **coercive and controlling** behaviour. Conscious intent is not required.
- **Test**: the 2024 amendments "require a more nuanced, proactive and future-focused approach" to safety (at
  [34]); conflict is not itself violence, and the court needed an actual persuasion (*Briginshaw*).

## The orders

| | Order |
|---|---|
| Decision-making | **Sole** to the mother, after asking the father's views in writing; **no parenting co-ordinator** |
| Father's time | Staged: day time each Tuesday and Sunday to Feb 2027; adds a weekend overnight to Nov 2027; builds to four nights a fortnight; holidays shared, alternate 7-night blocks in Term 4 to Year 7 |
| Communication | Co-parenting app |
| Restraints | Neither parent may denigrate the other or discuss the case with the child |
