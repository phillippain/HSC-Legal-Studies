After a jury trial on demanding property with menaces and DV-related offences ($163,331 paid under threat), Taouk received six years (non-parole four).

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Threatened violence double counted? | No — not an element of s 99 |
| Who proves mitigation? | The offender |
| New arguments on appeal | Rarely allowed |
| Result | Leave granted; appeal dismissed |
