Deemah Stone pleaded guilty to failing its s 19(1) duty between February 2021 and February 2022: workers cut and ground stone without a safe system for silica dust, despite earlier SafeWork notices.

## What the court decided

| Factor | Finding |
|---|---|
| Objective seriousness | Mid range |
| Aggravating factors | None |
| Mitigation | Real |
| Fine | $140,000, reduced 25% for the early plea to **$105,000**; half paid to the prosecutor; costs |
| Capacity to pay | No reduction — no submission made |

## Use it for

- **crime.4.6** — fines as a penalty for a corporate offender; general and specific deterrence.
- **Compliance** — improvement notices and warnings that did not secure compliance.

## Caution

A regulatory offence; no worker injury is described in the summary.
