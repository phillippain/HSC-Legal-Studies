A man from Lebanon who arrived without a travel document sought a Safe Haven Enterprise visa, claiming threats after refusing work linked to Hezbollah. The Tribunal affirmed the refusal.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Did the Tribunal ignore his 'party of Satan' evidence? | No — it need not mention every item |
| Was the complaint really about weight? | Yes, which is not jurisdictional error |
| Result | Application dismissed |

## Caution

Judicial review only; the Court did not decide whether he is a refugee.
