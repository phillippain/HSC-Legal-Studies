The applicant's visa was refused partly over a document the delegate treated as a bogus degree. He missed two Tribunal hearings
and his review was dismissed; a judge then summarily dismissed his judicial review application. He asked the Federal Court for more
time to seek leave to appeal.

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Was the delay explained? | Yes |
| Do the proposed grounds have merit? | No |
| Extension of time | **Refused** |
