A jury found CL (initials only), then 16, guilty of **murder** and **armed robbery causing wounding** in Nowra on 4 January 2022.
The Crown could not prove who stabbed the victim, so he was sentenced as a **non-stabber** (parties to a crime).

## How the sentence was reached

| Factor | Finding |
|---|---|
| Youth | Child sentencing principles; rehabilitation carries more weight |
| Background | Deprived background and mental impairments reduce culpability |
| Parity | Matched co-offender CS |
| SNPP (20 years for murder) | Not applied — CL was a juvenile |
| Special circumstances | Found |

**Sentence**: murder 14 years 6 months (24 January 2023 to 23 July 2037), non-parole 9 years 6 months (to 23 July 2032); armed
robbery 2 years 6 months concurrent.

- **crime.5.4** — penalties for children convicted of the most serious crime.
- **crime.1.7** — joint criminal liability where the stabber cannot be identified.

CL is a young person identified by initials only. The header and orders give different end dates.
