**Adrian Gatt** pleaded guilty to **20** domestic violence and public justice offences, including a day of assaults on his
wife and children (31 May 2023) and, from custody, having family press his wife to withdraw her statement. In September
2024 he received an aggregate **12 years** (non-parole 8).

## Held — appeal allowed (one of four grounds)

- Grounds 1–2 failed: the finding that a "perverted" patriarchal motive raised his moral culpability was open.
- **Ground 3 upheld:** the judge rejected **unchallenged expert evidence** of harsh childhood discipline **without warning**
  the defence — a **practical injustice** (procedural fairness).
- Ground 4 (weight given to his minor record) failed.
- **Resentenced: 11 years, non-parole 7.** Dhanji J would have set 10 years (non-parole 6 years 6 months).

## Use it for

- **crime.4.5 (appeals)** — error, resentencing and a split court on the number.
- **Procedural fairness** — a judge must give a party a chance to answer before rejecting uncontested evidence.

## Caution

A **class case summary**; family violence offending against a wife and children. The judges split on the final sentence.
