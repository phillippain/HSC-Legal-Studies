Receivers for Gratory Pty Ltd sued Robert Pastrello, licensee-manager of the Eaglehawk Hotel & Bistro and a director from 2019.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| ATM payments and beer rebates diverted? | Yes — $793,970.40 and $382,937.72 |
| Personal goods charged to the company | $3,855.40 |
| Cash-shortfall claims | Failed or not quantified |
| Result | Judgment for $1,180,763.52 plus interest |
