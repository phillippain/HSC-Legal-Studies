A Malaysian man who had lived in Australia for nearly 13 years applied for a protection visa in 2019 and was refused in 2023.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Openly gay and active in LGBTQI+ events? | Accepted |
| Real chance of serious harm? | Yes — from authorities and community, anywhere in Malaysia |
| Could he hide his sexuality? | Not a reasonable answer (s 5J(3)) |
| Result | Refusal set aside; remitted |
