An interim decision. A mother moved her two-year-old to a nearby city; the father wanted the child returned, and his parents (joined in the property case) sought $75,000 security for costs from her.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Return of the child | Refused — interim arrangements still worked |
| Relocation | Parents may live where they choose unless the child's welfare is affected |
| Security for costs | Refused — it would stifle a genuine property claim |
