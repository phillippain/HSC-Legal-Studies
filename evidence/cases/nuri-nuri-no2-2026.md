In a parenting dispute the Independent Children's Lawyer subpoenaed clinical notes of the psychologist treating two young children. The psychologist objected that production would harm therapy.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Probative value? | Likely |
| Tangible harm shown? | No — outweighed by the need for contemporaneous notes |
| Protection | Parents restrained from discussing the notes with the children |

A subpoena ruling; no parenting orders made.
