In the Wulli Wulli native title claim in Central Queensland, Mr Collins, a respondent, said his people held the area. The applicant asked the Court to remove him.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Prima facie case of competing interests? | No |
| Support from other Gooreng Gooreng people? | None |
| Sites shown | Outside the claim area |
| Result | Removed as a party; costs open |
