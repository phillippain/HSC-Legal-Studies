Ms Chimmalee pleaded guilty to one rolled-up count of fraud and received 6 years 3 months. Acting for herself, she appealed against conviction and sentence.

## What the court decided

| Argument | Held |
|---|---|
| Agreed Facts altered; lawyers incompetent; life sentence threatened | No miscarriage of justice and no real question about guilt |
| Withdrawing the plea | Not ordered |
| Manifest excess | Not shown |
| Result | Leave granted; appeal dismissed |

## Use it for

- **crime.3.4** — pleas, a rolled-up count and withdrawing a guilty plea.
- **crime.4.5** — appeal as a safeguard, balanced with finality.

## Caution

A self-represented appellant; the reasons address her claims one by one.
