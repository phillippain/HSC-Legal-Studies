Peter Catanuto pleaded guilty to taking part in manufacturing large quantities of MDMA and methylamphetamine, and to supplying a precursor. He received 18 years.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Went beyond the Agreed Facts | Error |
| Tied remorse to refusing to name others | Error |
| Assumed a drug value; did not engage with a psychiatrist's opinion | Errors |
| Resentence | 14 years, non-parole 10 years 6 months |
