A jury found Trevor Bobak guilty of murder for a stabbing at Woy Woy on 17 December 2022. He was also sentenced for a choking offence and an aggravated robbery.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Intent | Intent to cause very serious harm, not to kill |
| Objective seriousness | Just below midrange |
| Prior record | A 2002 conviction denied leniency |
| Sentence | 26 years, non-parole 19, for murder; partly accumulated with the robbery |

- **crime.4.1** — standard non-parole periods and totality.

Para 92 says 27/20 in total; the orders give 26/19 for murder (followed). Non-graphic; deceased and witnesses not named.
