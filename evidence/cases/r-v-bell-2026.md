Jacob Bell pleaded not guilty to murder. The Crown relied on his statements to police on 29 March 2024.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Were three statements lies? | Yes |
| Unfair prejudice outweighs probative value? | Yes — would reveal recent offending, prison and parole |
| Result | Excluded under s 137 |
