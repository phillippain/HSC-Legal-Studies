A man subject to a harmful proceedings order asked to revive an appeal deemed abandoned after he missed a transcript deadline. The appeal attacked a costs order of $11,791.40.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Seven grounds | No error in any |
| An appeal with no reasonable grounds | Vexatious — must be dismissed |
| Result | Application dismissed on the papers; no new costs |

## Caution

Procedural; the underlying family dispute is not decided.
