A car dealer, a related company and their director ran consumer car loans and collected repayments without an Australian credit licence, charging interest on the original amount. They made admissions.

## What the court decided

| Respondent | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Diamond Wheels Pty Ltd | $2.5 million |
| Keo Automotive | $3.5 million |
| Ken Keomanivong (director) | $750,000 (agreed) and a five-year ban from consumer credit |

Capacity to pay moderated the amounts but did not control them.

## Use it for

- **Year 11** — civil penalties as a remedy enforced by a regulator; how civil and criminal law differ (dis.2.1, dis.2.7).

## Caution

Year 11 only; civil, not criminal. Payment terms and costs were left undecided.
