In the murder trial of Beaumont Lamarre-Condon, two Crown witnesses asked not to give evidence in the courtroom.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Declare one witness unavailable or excuse her? | No |
| Evidence by audio-visual link from a remote room? | Yes, with a support person |
| Practical unfairness to the accused? | None |

## Caution

A ruling during the trial; witnesses not named.
