A system error from 2012–13 used a wrong provisional income for the father; the mother was overpaid **$3,563.25** and was repaying it
at $26.90 a fortnight after Child Support corrected the error. The error affected **over 47,000** cases.

| Question | Finding |
|---|---|
| Should the Registrar's s 75 discretion to amend the father's 2011–12 provisional income be exercised? | **No** |
| Weight | Greatest weight to the father's much better finances against the mother's reliance on the disability support pension |
| Splitting the overpayment | Not an option |

The decision under review was **set aside**; the mother's application succeeded.

- **Just outcomes** — a discretion exercised by weighing each parent's circumstances.
