Mr Warren is on parole for recklessly inflicting grievous bodily harm in company. The State applied for an extended supervision order.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Test at the preliminary hearing | Would the State's material, at its highest, justify an order? |
| Likelihood of further violence | High |
| Result | Two examiners appointed; final hearing February 2027 |
