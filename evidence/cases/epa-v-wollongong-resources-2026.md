Wollongong Resources held the licence for the dormant Russell Vale Colliery. A faulty turbidity sensor let about **807 kilolitres** of coal-laden water into Bellambi Gully Creek; it pleaded guilty to two environmental offences.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Objective seriousness | Low for both |
| Discount and totality | 25% plea discount; totality applied |
| Penalty | $60,000 each, ordered as payments to a council restoration project (s 250), not fines |
