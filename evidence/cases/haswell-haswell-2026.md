A wife applied under s 79A to set aside consent property orders made in December 2020.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| The husband's conduct | Knowingly lied about a serious illness and his business's prospects; threats and repeated calls; understated bank funds |
| Most weight | A business valuation far above the $150,000 disclosed |
| Result | Orders set aside; a new division at a later hearing |
