A mother appealed three of 43 parenting orders made on 28 May 2026. The primary judge had barred overseas
travel until age 18 without both parents' consent and set a joint-consent rule for extra-curricular
activities. Neither party nor the Independent Children's Lawyer had sought either. Names are pseudonyms.

## Held — Campton J

- A judge must give notice of a course "materially outside the range of orders sought or reasonably
  anticipated"; both orders denied procedural fairness. The flight-risk and reasons grounds failed.
- The Court re-made the orders: no overseas travel until late 2031, Hague countries on 28 days' notice,
  other countries with AUD150,000 security, and each parent free to run activities in their time.
- Costs certificates were ordered for the mother, father and ICL.
