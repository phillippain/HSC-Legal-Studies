A New Zealand citizen who arrived aged four was jailed for eight years in 2018 for a group attack. A Tribunal restored his visa, but in 2026 the Assistant Minister cancelled it again under s 501BA(2).

## What the court decided

| Argument | Held |
|---|---|
| Illogical to note 'increasing seriousness' yet give earlier offending no weight | No — s 501M allows criminal history to be used, and the earlier offence was only a comparator |
| Result | Dismissed with costs |
