A social housing tenant of about 22 years lost her rent subsidy (*Housing Act 2001* (NSW) s 57(1)) from 16 June 2025 and was charged market rent of $410, later $440 a week. Arrears built up and on 16 January 2026 she agreed in a short NCAT hearing to consent orders ending the tenancy and ordering $12,654.28 in arrears. She appealed on 17 February 2026, the day possession was due.

## Held

The Appeal Panel **refused an extension of time and leave** and **dismissed the appeal**. A firm landlord who refused a payment plan was not **duress, undue influence or unconscionable conduct**; the recording showed she consented and there was no medical evidence of impairment; the r 37 vulnerable person argument failed. It still **suspended possession until 28 August 2026** because her age and long residence were **exceptional circumstances** under s 154G.

## Use it for

- **Social housing**: a public landlord, a cancelled subsidy, and the 28-day limit on suspending possession (`shel.3.4`).
- **Dispute resolution**: same-day conciliation and consent orders, and when a negotiated order can be set aside (`shel.2.5`, `shel.2.7`).
- **Homelessness and affordability** as background: the family said she would be homeless; subsidised rent became market rent (`shel.3.3`, `shel.3.1`).

## Caution

This is a consent termination, not an allocation case, and no discrimination claim was made. The relief is a suspension of possession, not a reversal. The tenant's daughter and granddaughter appeared as her agents. No costs order is in the file.
