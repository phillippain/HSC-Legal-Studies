Datona bought a rare quad-axle fuel tanker trailer for $223,927.95. In April 2024 its third axle tore from the chassis.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Defect or poor maintenance? | Defect |
| Breach of the acceptable quality guarantee? | Yes — a major failure |
| Remedy | Refund less scrap value, plus lost profits (as a chance) to 30 June 2028 |
