## Held

- Supply of 316.39 g of cocaine (commercial quantity) and dealing with $1,750 of proceeds; offending below the mid-range, no sophistication (*Melikian*: role matters more than quantity).
- **25% plea discount**; an aggregate sentence; rehabilitation and community protection given **particular weight**.
- Served by an **Intensive Correction Order** from 11 September 2026 to 1 July 2027, because a return to custody would interrupt his progress.

- **crime.4.3** — objective seriousness, subjective factors and aggregate sentences.
