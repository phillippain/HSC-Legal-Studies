Cabezz sued Mr McShane to enforce his guarantee of loans to his company, Velocity, claiming over **$1.3 million**. He said he signed the 2024 deed only for Velocity and had no legal advice, and admitted applying his brother-in-law's e-signature as witness without permission.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| His evidence | Rejected |
| Adverse inference against Cabezz | Refused |
| Result | He is bound; judgment for Cabezz on liability |
