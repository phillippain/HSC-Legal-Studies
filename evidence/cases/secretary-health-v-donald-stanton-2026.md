A NSW Health employee with a physical disability and anxiety won a discrimination claim over workstation, medical and fitness-for-work steps, and was awarded $55,000. The employer appealed.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Correct hypothetical comparator? | No — error |
| Employer's safety defence? | Still failed |
| Result | Orders set aside; liability and remedy remitted |

Liability is reopened; this is not an employer win.
