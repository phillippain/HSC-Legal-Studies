Mr Anthonige did cleaning work for Drip N Dry's clients in 2022 and claimed he was an employee. The South Australian Employment Court said he was not.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Delay explained? | Yes — wrong advice about where to appeal |
| Arguable grounds? | Two |
| Prejudice or abuse of process? | No |
| Result | Extension granted; no costs order |
