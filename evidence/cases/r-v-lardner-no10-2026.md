After a judge-alone trial, Mr Lardner was found guilty of the manslaughter of David Vale by excessive self-defence (s 421), not murder, for stabbing his cousin twice on 1 March 2023.

## What the court decided

| Sentencing fact | Finding |
|---|---|
| Did the deceased use the knife? | Not found; the knife fell from the deceased |
| Seriousness | Well above the low end of manslaughter |
| Culpability | Reduced by a deprived childhood (Bugmy) |
| Discount | 25% (an earlier plea offer had been rejected) |
| Sentence | 8 years 3 months, non-parole 5 years 6 months (15 January 2026 to 14 July 2031) |

## Use it for

- **crime.3.8 / crime.4.3** — excessive self-defence as a partial defence, and sentencing facts found by the judge.
- **Moral culpability** — disadvantage weighed against the loss of a life.
