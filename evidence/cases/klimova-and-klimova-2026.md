A mother with **no lawyer at trial**, assisted by an interpreter, appealed final parenting orders
(26 March 2026) under which her 12-year-old son lives with his father and spends three nights a
fortnight with her. Riethmuller J dismissed every ground on 22 October 2026 and fixed costs at
**$25,000**.

## Held

- **Interpreter:** NAATI accreditation is "a quality control process used by the registry"; the test
  is whether the interpreting was sufficient for a fair hearing. The Magistrate found her English good.
- **Section 102NA:** protects alleged victims of family violence from cross-examination by the
  alleged perpetrator; it is not a legal aid scheme, and vague evidence of a foreign charge did not
  engage it (a "family violence order" is one under a State or Territory law, s 4).
- **Judicial help (*Re F*):** a judge may help a litigant in person but "not appropriate for a judicial
  officer to take the role of an advocate".
- Findings and use of the single expert's report were open on the evidence; any error had to affect the
  final result (*Gerlach*).

## Use it for

- **Dealing with domestic violence** (fam.2.4) and **courts and dispute resolution** (fam.2.5): the
  legal aid gap, accessibility and cost as effectiveness criteria.
- **Year 11 access and fairness** (inv.1.4, inv.1.6) for people who cannot afford a lawyer.
- Care: unproven allegations of family violence and a child in a high-conflict dispute; the Family
  Report's discussion of a belief in "black magic" is best left out.
