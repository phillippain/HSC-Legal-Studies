**Roxanne Tickle**, a transgender woman, was removed from **Giggle for Girls**, a women-only social app,
after its founder Sall Grover judged from her photo that she was male. She sued under the *Sex
Discrimination Act 1984* (Cth), which since **2013** prohibits discrimination on the ground of **gender
identity** (s 5B).

## First instance v Full Court

| | Bromwich J (2024) | Full Court (2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Discrimination | **Indirect** (s 5B(2)); direct claim failed | **Direct** (s 5B(1)), two acts |
| Special measure for women (s 7D) | Rejected | Rejection upheld |
| Knowing she was transgender | Weakened the direct claim | Not needed |
| Reasonableness | Not pleaded or proved | Cannot justify direct discrimination |
| Damages | $10,000; aggravated refused | **$20,000**, reflecting the respondents' conduct |
| Final | — | High Court refused special leave, 10 Sep 2026 |

## The enforcement pathway

Written complaint to the **AHRC** (free, no lawyer) → investigation → conciliation → termination →
application to the Federal Court or Federal Circuit and Family Court, generally within **60 days**. The
Commission cannot sue for the complainant; it notes significant delays.
