After the death of their infant son, two former de facto partners disputed who should keep his cremated remains. The mother sought sole ownership; the father wanted interment with equal access or rotating custody.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Are the urn and ashes property? | Yes |
| The father's pre-proceedings police reports | Disentitling conduct |
| Result | Mother declared sole owner under s 90SL |

An infant's death — teach with care. Names are pseudonyms.
