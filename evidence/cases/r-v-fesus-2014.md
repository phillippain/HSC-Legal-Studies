**Steven (Steve) Fesus** was charged in July 2013 with murdering his wife, Jodie, who went missing from their Shellharbour home in 1997; her remains were found about five weeks later in a shallow grave near Gerroa.

## Held

- On **16 June 2014** Adams J granted bail in the Supreme Court, after a two-hour application (Illawarra Mercury, 16 June 2014). Conditions included reporting to Wollongong police twice daily and not approaching prosecution witnesses.
- He had been refused bail earlier under the **Bail Act 1978**, where murder required exceptional circumstances (s 9C): two earlier Supreme Court applications, which the Daily Telegraph reported as "three prior refusals".
- Under the **Bail Act 2013** (commenced 20 May 2014) there is no offence-based presumption. New forensic evidence about the cause of death satisfied **s 74** (repeat applications), which Adams J said has a low threshold, so the application was heard under the **unacceptable risk** test (ss 17, 20).
- It was the **first murder case to test the new Act**.

## The reaction

The Telegraph headline, "New laws allow accused wife killer Steven Fesus to be released after three prior refusals", is the media half of the reform cycle. About a month after the Act began, Premier Mike Baird ordered a review by John Hatzistergos, and the **Bail Amendment Act 2014** (show cause, in force 28 January 2015) followed. The Attorney General said that "a number of bail decisions made under the new Act have caused concerns in the community" (13 August 2014).

## Use it for

- **Responsiveness and law reform:** a decision made correctly under a new law, followed by rapid amendment.
- **Balancing rights:** the presumption of innocence against community concern.

## Caution

- Fesus was later **convicted of murder** at a third trial (reports give October 2017 or February 2018) and sentenced to 22 years, non-parole 16 years 6 months (Johnson J). Teach it as a decision that was lawful when made, not as proof the law was wrong or right.
- One secondary source dates the killing to 2007; the Illawarra Mercury and Goulburn Post say 1997.
- The date of the judgment itself was not checked against the judgment text; the 16 June 2014 date is the Illawarra Mercury's report of the bail decision.
