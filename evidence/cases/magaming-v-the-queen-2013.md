## Facts

**Bonang Magaming**, a 19-year-old Indonesian fisherman, steered a boat carrying **52 asylum seekers** intercepted near Ashmore Reef on 6 September 2010. He pleaded guilty to aggravated people smuggling (s 233C, *Migration Act 1958* (Cth)) and received the **five-year mandatory minimum** with a three-year non-parole period (s 236B).

## Held — 6 to 1 (Gageler J dissenting)

- The offences in ss 233A and 233C are not coextensive: five or more people is an extra element.
- A prosecutor chooses the charge but **not the punishment**, so the choice is not judicial power.
- A mandatory minimum is not on that account inconsistent with Chapter III: fixing minima is a policy choice for Parliament (Keane J).
- Appeal dismissed. The request to reopen *Fraser Henleins* was refused.

## Use it for

- **crime.6.1**: people smuggling as transnational crime.
- **crime.4.1**: mandatory minimums against judicial discretion, and the prosecutor's role.
- **Effectiveness**: a minimum aimed at organisers but falling on a young crew member; a 2012 Attorney-General direction softened this.

## Caution

The case tests the **validity of the scheme**, not whether the sentence was right. No hearing or sentencing dates in the file. Gageler J dissented on Chapter III.
