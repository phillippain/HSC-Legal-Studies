Daniel Kalandyk pleaded guilty to damaging property by fire (domestic violence) after filming threats and setting fire to his parents' home.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Intent | Accepted he meant to do it |
| Moral culpability | Substantially reduced by mental illness |
| Starting point and plea | 4 years less 25% |
| Sentence | 3 years, non-parole 1 year 11 months; special circumstances found |
