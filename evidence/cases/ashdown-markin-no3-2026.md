A mother alleged very serious violence by the father of their five-year-old daughter; she and the Independent Children's Lawyer opposed any time with him. After a ten-day hearing (March 2026), Wilson J applied the civil standard, requiring cogent proof for serious claims.

## What the court decided

| Question | Finding |
|---|---|
| Were the serious allegations proved? | One allegation proved; the mother found unreliable |
| Is the father an unacceptable risk? | No |
| Decision-making | Sole decision-making on major long-term issues to the mother (she must tell the father within seven days) |
| Time | Four phases: contact centre, family present, longer days, then unsupervised Wednesdays and Sundays |
| Refused | The ICL's no-time order and a harmful proceedings order |

Parents communicate through AppClose; s 68B injunctions manage the risk.
