## Facts

**Petr Klomfar**, 41, carried suitcases hiding **4.653 kg of pure cocaine** from Las Vegas to Sydney for a promised AUD6,000. He pleaded guilty and received **7 years 8 months (non-parole 5 years)** after a 30% discount (Zahra SC, 18 May 2018). He appealed on disparity with his co-offender (6 years 3 months) and manifest excess. Heard 11 March 2019.

## Held — unanimous

Leave granted; appeal **dismissed**. The co-offender's lesser criminality and severe mental illness explained the difference, and general deterrence carries paramount weight for drug importation, whatever the offender's place in the hierarchy.
