A first jury convicted DL of two counts, for which another judge imposed **8 years**; on other counts the jury could not agree.
After a **retrial**, a second jury found him guilty of **five** child sexual offences, including maintaining an unlawful sexual
relationship with a child (s 66EA — the judge need not identify each act).

## How the sentence was built

| Factor | Finding |
|---|---|
| Aggravating | Breach of trust; harm to the victims (s 3A) |
| Mitigating | Good character; low risk of reoffending |
| Plea | None — no discount, no remorse |
| Facilitating justice (s 22A) | **2.5%** for an efficient trial |
| Totality | Indicative sentences of 12 months to 7 years combined into one aggregate |

**Sentence**: aggregate **9 years**, non-parole **4 years 6 months**, starting **29 October 2028** (after the earlier sentence);
non-parole ends 28 April 2033, sentence ends 28 October 2037. A registrable person order with a 15-year reporting period.

## Caution

DL is identified by initials only, and no detail of the acts is given. Count 6 is labelled two ways, and an 8-year sentence is
said to start before the verdict date; neither is resolved in the judgment.
