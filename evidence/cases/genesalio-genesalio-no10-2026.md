Under 2024 consent orders a husband and his brother were jointly liable to pay the wife $4,000,000. The brother sought a stay of May 2026 enforcement orders pending appeal.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Stay pending appeal? | Refused — issues already rejected by the Full Court and High Court |
| Balance of convenience | Favoured the wife |
| Costs | $35,000 indemnity costs |
