Mr Wang, 41, was refused a Partner (Temporary) visa under s 501(1) because of a 2019 sentence of 22 months for tobacco offences. He accepted he failed the character test.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Risk of reoffending | Low |
| His children and family | Heavy weight |
| Result | Refusal set aside and remitted |
