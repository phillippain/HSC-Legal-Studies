Ms Mikhaiel was convicted of aggravated sexual offences against a 15-year-old in her care. The Crown used her phone searches as tendency and coincidence evidence.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Tendency framed as an interest in massaging | Not supported by the evidence |
| The narrower tendency | Lacked significant probative value |
| Coincidence use | Admissible |
| Result | Leave granted; all convictions quashed; a new trial (listed 24 November 2026) |

## Use it for

- **crime.3.7** — tendency and coincidence evidence: probative value against unfair prejudice.
- **crime.4.5** — the appeal system correcting how evidence was used. A retrial is not an acquittal.

## Caution

Unanimous orders, but Roberts J differed on one point (tendency evidence). Facts are kept non-graphic.
