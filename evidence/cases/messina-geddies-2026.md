A woman claimed a property alteration after an 11-year de facto relationship. Her partner died in 2024 and his executors became respondents.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Join the trust's corporate trustee? | Yes |
| Join the executor personally? | No |
| Preservation and disclosure orders? | Refused — no evidence of risk |
| Valuation and records | Single expert valuation and delivery of records ordered |
