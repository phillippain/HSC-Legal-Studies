In three appeals from South Australia, young men were convicted of murder after a group assault in
which one of them killed the victim. They argued the High Court should abandon **extended joint
criminal enterprise** (from *McAuliffe v The Queen* (1995)): liability for a crime a participant
foresaw as a possible result of the agreed crime.

**Held** (6:1, Gageler J dissenting): the doctrine stays. Changing it was a matter for Parliament.
Gageler J would have required more than foresight of a possibility for murder.

## Use it for

- **Parties to a crime** (`crime.1.7`): how liability spreads beyond the person who did the act.
- **Balance**: community protection from group violence against individual justice, especially for
  young or peripheral members of a group.
- **Law reform**: the Court said reform belongs to Parliament; England changed course in
  *R v Jogee* (2016).
