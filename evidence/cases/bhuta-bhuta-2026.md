## Held

- s 48 satisfied: separation under one roof from mid-2024; no reasonable likelihood of resuming cohabitation.
- The wife had actually received the orders by email, and the portal is a shared 'letterbox'.
- Costs: general rule is each pays their own (s 114UB), departed from because she was wholly unsuccessful and put him to substantial expense: $5,297, stayed.

Divorce changes status only: the Court said property rights are unaffected. The file has garbled brackets and gives the adult child's birth year as 2005; avoid it. No cases are cited. Handle neutrally: the wife was absent and unrepresented.
