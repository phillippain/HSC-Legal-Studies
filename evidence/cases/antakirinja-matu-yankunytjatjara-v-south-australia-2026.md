The **Antakirinja Matu-Yankunytjatjara (AMY)** native title body (AMYAC, the prescribed body corporate) applied under **s 61 of the *Native Title Act 1993* (Cth)** to vary the **11 May 2011** consent determination (*Lennon v State of South Australia* [2011] FCA 474) in **four limited ways**. A respondent, **Ms Brown**, who appeared for herself, asked for leave to issue subpoenas and sought to raise the description of who the native title holders are. The hearing was on 4 November 2026; reasons were published on **5 November 2026**.

## What the applicant asked to change

1. Delete two paragraphs that assumed no native title in pastoral improvements, after *Western Australia v Brown* (2014) 253 CLR 507.
2. Add land parcels to the extinguishment schedules (to help compensation).
3. Add parcels to the surrender schedules (under an agreement).
4. A correction to the determination.

The 2011 **description of the native title holders** was **not** touched.

## Held — O'Bryan J, 5 November 2026

| Issue | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Does the variation put the description of the holders in issue? | **No** — "AMYAC does not seek to vary that description" (at [20]) |
| Can a respondent apply to vary that description? | **No** — a respondent "has no entitlement to apply to vary the description of the native title holders" (at [21]) |
| Do the proposed subpoenas have a legitimate forensic purpose? | **No** — the documents "are not relevant to the issues that arise in the proceeding" (at [24]) |

Both of Ms Brown's applications were **refused**. Directions were made for affidavits, mediation and a hearing on **28 January 2027**. The Court did **not** decide whether her account of her ancestry or her family's normative system is correct — only that this proceeding does not raise it.

## Authorities

*Lennon* [2011] FCA 474 (the determination being varied); *Western Australia v Brown* (2014) 253 CLR 507 (native title can exist in pastoral improvements); *Seven Network v Fairfax Media* [2023] FCAFC 185 and *Wong v Sklavos* [2014] FCAFC 120 (a subpoena needs a legitimate forensic purpose). Also *Native Title Act* s 84(3) and Federal Court Rules 2011 r 24.01.

## Use it for

- **chg.5.2 / chg.5.5 (native title overview and outcomes)** — a determination is a living order that courts vary as law develops (here, after *Brown*), and a limit: disputes about *who holds* title cannot be reopened through a variation.
- **chg.5.4** — a later judicial decision feeding into variation of a determination.

## Caution

A **Year 11 class summary** of a short procedural judgment; there is **no HSC edition**. The merits of Ms Brown's claim were **not decided** — do not suggest she is wrong about her own ancestry. *Western Australia v Brown* is unrelated to Ms Brown. The case concerns Aboriginal group identity and family membership: handle respectfully. Check quotations ([20], [21], [23], [24]) against the judgment.
