A Malaysian woman, estranged from her family, applied for a protection visa; a delegate refused it for lack of detail
(November 2022). Malaysia criminalises same-sex conduct.

## What the Tribunal decided

| Question | Finding |
|---|---|
| Credibility | Credible despite inconsistencies, a missed letter, an agent's drafting errors and delay |
| Convention reason | Lesbian women in Malaysia are a **particular social group** (*Applicant S395 of 2002*; s 5L) |
| Concealment | She cannot be expected to hide her sexual orientation (s 5J(3)(c)(vi)) |
| Real chance of serious harm | Yes; relocation and state protection not available |

The refusal was **set aside** and **remitted** with a direction that she meets s 36(2)(a). Complementary protection was not
decided, because she is already a refugee.
