A carer sought an adjournment the night before a guardianship hearing, with no medical certificate and
after ignoring directions. The Tribunal proceeded. All names are pseudonyms.

## Held — the Tribunal (decision 18 August 2026)

- Adjournment refused: Kathy's welfare is the paramount concern (s 4, *Guardianship Act 1987*), and the carer
  "has been afforded opportunities to present her case ... though has not availed herself".
- Kathy needed a guardian: Alzheimer's dementia leaves her partly incapable of managing her person.
- The **Public Guardian** was appointed for three years because no suitable private person was proposed
  (s 15(3)), with functions including accommodation, health care and consent to restrictive practices
  only as a last resort and under a behaviour support plan.

## Use it for

- **dis.2.5 and inv.1.6** — tribunal procedure and a fair hearing weighed against delay and welfare.

## Caution

Year 11 only. Not a care and protection of children case. Add no identifying detail. Dementia and family
conflict appear in the file; keep to the legal test.
