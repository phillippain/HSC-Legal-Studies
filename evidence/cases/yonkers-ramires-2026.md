A divorcing wife asked for the family home to be sold before trial. The husband wanted to keep it and finish building works.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Is equity erosion a legitimate worry? | Yes |
| Should it be sold now? | No — irreversible and would defeat his claim |
| Result | Dismissed; costs reserved |
