Enteka, a company controlled by Ms Aravanopoulos, borrowed from Funding Solved to refinance a defaulting loan; she guaranteed it and mortgaged two properties. The new loan defaulted too.

## What the court decided

| Defence | Held |
|---|---|
| Unconscionable conduct | Rejected — she was experienced and took legal advice |
| Unjust contract (Contracts Review Act) | Did not apply — the loan was for business |
| Result | Judgment for the lender on liability; amount, interest and costs to be fixed (listed 27 November 2026) |
