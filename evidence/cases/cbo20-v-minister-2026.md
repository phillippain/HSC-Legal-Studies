A protection visa applicant did not attend his 11 March 2020 AAT hearing, which dismissed his review.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Did emailing count as receipt? | Yes |
| COVID-19 lockdowns? | Began after the hearing |
| Result | Appeal dismissed with costs |
