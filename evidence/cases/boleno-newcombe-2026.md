A father appealed final parenting orders (29 May 2026) limiting his time with his young daughter to
professionally supervised time until she turns 10. The primary judge found an unacceptable risk of
psychological harm. Names are pseudonyms.

## Held — joint judgment

- The unacceptable-risk finding is evidence-based, not discretionary: "the predictive exercise of risk
  assessment cannot be undertaken other than by reference to past events".
- All four grounds failed: the risk assessment, the adequacy of reasons, an inference about his beliefs,
  and the end date. Supervision until 10 was open because indefinite supervision was never proposed.
- The father pays costs of $14,879.78 (mother) and $5,662.80 (Independent Children's Lawyer).
