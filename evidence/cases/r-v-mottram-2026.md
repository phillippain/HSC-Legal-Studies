## Held

- Common assault (a bite on the cheek in a prison kitchen, 11 December 2024) at the **lowest range**; guilty plea, **25% discount**.
- The s 5 threshold was **just met**, against the joint submission: a bond to behave in custody is meaningless when the offender must behave anyway.
- **General deterrence** decided it: a fixed term of **one month** from 18 April 2029, when the murder non-parole period expires, to 17 May 2029.

## Use it for

- **crime.4.2 / crime.4.7** — deterrence against a community-based order, and why the alternative did not work here.
- **crime.t.discretion** — a judge departing from a joint submission and choosing the structure and start date.

## Caution

The judge only **assumed** a CCO can be served in custody; do not teach it as a rule. The Act for 's 56' and the CCO section are not in the file. Care: a murder sentence and an assault; keep to the legal test.
