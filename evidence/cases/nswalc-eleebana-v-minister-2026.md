The NSW Aboriginal Land Council appealed the refusal of a 2010 land claim at Mount Hutton. The Minister said the land was a Crown road and so not claimable.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Was the land proved to be a road? | No — a 1971 Crown plan was unreliable |
| Alternative holding | Reserved land stays claimable even if another Act blocks its sale |
| Result | Appeal upheld; land to be transferred |

## Use it for

- **hr.2.9** — statutory land rights for Aboriginal peoples.
- **Effectiveness** — a 16-year wait for one claim.

## Caution

The judgment discusses only the Act's remedial purpose, not rights instruments; a duplicate para 180 in the file.
