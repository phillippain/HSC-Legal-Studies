Dismissed by Eastern Health in 2022 for misconduct linked to her vaccination status, the applicant (self-represented) sought
declarations that s 165AI of Victoria's *Public Health and Wellbeing Act* was inconsistent with the *Fair Work Act* and so
**inoperative under s 109** of the Constitution. The matter came to the Federal Court from the High Court.

## The questions

| Issue | Held |
|---|---|
| Jurisdiction (ss 71, 77) | Yes, the Federal Court had jurisdiction |
| Inconsistency under s 109 | None — the State and Commonwealth laws can operate together |
| Utility of declarations | None |
| Standing | No |

All claims failed; she was ordered to pay **costs**.
