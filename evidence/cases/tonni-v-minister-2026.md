A Bangladeshi student visa applicant and her husband, without lawyers, sought judicial review of the Tribunal's finding that she was not a genuine temporary entrant.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Misread why she enrolled in a new course | No — the finding was open |
| Inconsistent; ignored her study record | No |
| Result | Dismissed with fixed costs of $5,400 |
