A wife seeks to set aside a declaration of trust and the transfer of a house to her husband's mother so it returns to the asset pool. The mother asked to be removed as a party.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Prima facie case under s 106B? | Yes |
| Could the pool be fixed yet? | No |
| Age and health a reason to remove her? | No |
| Result | Application dismissed; she stays a party |

- **fam.2.3** — transactions defeating property claims.
- **Effectiveness** — third parties drawn into family disputes.

## Caution

Interim; no finding that the transfer will be set aside.
