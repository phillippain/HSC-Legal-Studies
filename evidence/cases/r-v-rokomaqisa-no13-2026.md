The defence sought to exclude a recorded conversation of 8 June 2021 between three other men: not relied on, irrelevant and hearsay.

## What the court decided

| Objection | Held |
|---|---|
| Not relied on | The Crown relies on it for Count 2 (conspiracy) |
| Irrelevant | Relevant (s 55) |
| Hearsay | s 66A removes the objection |
| Result | Admissible |
