A jury convicted Tyron Givens of sexual intercourse without consent and of intentionally choking a person rendering them unconscious. He sought leave to appeal.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Unreasonable verdicts? | No, despite the long delay in formal complaint |
| An 'Ewen direction' required? | No — in substance a barred 'Murray direction' |
| A s 165B (delay) ground not raised at trial | Leave refused |
| Result | Appeal dismissed |
