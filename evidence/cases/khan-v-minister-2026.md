Mr Khan applied for a skilled regional visa in 2021 with his then wife as secondary applicant. In 2024 his agent lodged a withdrawal notice naming both.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Is withdrawal judged objectively? | Yes |
| Did his application end? | Yes, on 1 August 2024 |
| Result | Mandamus refused; dismissed with costs |
