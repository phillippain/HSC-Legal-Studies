On the first day of a final parenting and property hearing, the wife's counsel and then her solicitor sought to withdraw over unpaid fees and no instructions.

## What the court decided

| Question | Finding |
|---|---|
| The solicitor's conduct | Let the debt build for months, did not properly cease to act and gave no help with a funding scheme — a 'cavalier attitude' |
| Order | Referral to the NSW Legal Services Commissioner to consider investigation |
| Names | The solicitor's and wife's names redacted before publication |
| Not decided | Whether the Uniform Law or Conduct Rules were breached |

The Cross-Examination of Parties Scheme (s 102NA) protects parties where family violence is alleged — another reason representation matters.

## Use it for

- **fam.2.5** — courts supervising the quality of representation; professional regulation as a non-court check.
- **Just outcomes** — unaffordable fees and delay can block a fair hearing.

## Caution

Procedural: no breach was found, and the referral is not a finding of misconduct.
