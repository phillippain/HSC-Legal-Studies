568 paragraphs, with the trial run in tranches from May 2023 to July 2024 and lay evidence heard
**on country**. Mediation between the two groups failed.

South-western Victoria has been recognised as native title country in bands running west to east —
*Lovett* 2007 (Gunditjmara), *Lovett* 2011 (Gunditjmara and Eastern Maar, distinct and coexisting),
*Austin* 2023 and 2024 (Eastern Maar). **Area C** — the gap between the Shaw and Hopkins Rivers,
taking in Warrnambool, Port Fairy, Allansford and Penshurst — was claimed by both.

**The claim changed mid-trial.** The Gunditjmara applicant began by claiming the full suite of
rights it held in the earlier areas; by closing submissions it accepted the Eastern Maar are the
"land holding" group for Area C and claimed only non-exclusive rights to access and take resources.

## Held — the Gunditjmara claim was not established

Whether the Eastern Maar hold native title in Area C was **not decided**; only the Gunditjmara claim
was before the Court.

## The law worth quoting

**s 223** *Native Title Act 1993* (Cth): communal, group or individual rights in land or waters
possessed under traditional laws and customs, where the holders have a connection with the land and
the rights are recognised by the common law. **"Traditional"** means rooted in the normative system
that existed at sovereignty and passed substantially uninterrupted — **adaptation is permitted;
abandonment is not**.

## Use it for

- **Law reform in action, thirty years on.** *Mabo* recognised native title and the *Native Title
  Act* built a process; this is what that process costs in 2026 — a trial in tranches over fourteen
  months, competing claims within one language society, and a claim that narrowed as the evidence
  came in.
- **Resource efficiency and accessibility** in a native title answer, with something concrete under
  it rather than the usual assertion that claims are slow and expensive.
- **The burden of proof falls on the claimants**, and the "substantially uninterrupted" requirement
  asks them to prove continuity through the very dispossession the law is meant to answer. That is
  the strongest critical point available at ls.5.
