After about eight years of litigation — through Division 1, the Full Court and the High Court — a husband sought final spousal maintenance from his former wife; she sought a harmful proceedings order.

## What the court decided

| Question | Finding |
|---|---|
| Spousal maintenance | Dismissed — his inability to support himself came from spending a $968,849 payout, and she was not reasonably able to pay |
| Harmful proceedings order (s 102QAC, 2023 reform) | Made — he cannot start proceedings against her without leave; she is not told of a leave application unless leave is granted |
| Intention to harm | Not found; the Court looked at the effect |

He had not paid a $70,000 costs order.

## Use it for

- **fam.2.5** — a new statutory tool against repeat litigation (law reform in action).
- **fam.2.3** — maintenance after a financial agreement is set aside.

## Caution

Names are pseudonyms; the litigation history is long and partly in earlier judgments.
