A mother alleged ten contraventions of 2020 parenting orders and sought imprisonment of the father. His counsel applied orally to strike out the counts.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Were the counts properly pleaded? | No — they did not say what he should have done |
| Did some orders bind the children, not parents? | Yes |
| Result | Application dismissed; leave to amend refused |
