Refused a further student visa because she was not shown to be a genuine temporary entrant, she lost before the Tribunal (2021) and on judicial review (2025), then appealed.

## What the court decided

| Ground | Held |
|---|---|
| Tribunal ignored her evidence | Rejected |
| Delay | Rejected |
| Unfairness | Rejected |
| Reasoning by guesswork | Rejected |
| Result | Appeal dismissed with costs |
