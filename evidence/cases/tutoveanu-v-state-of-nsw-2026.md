After the Australian Human Rights Commission terminated his complaint (20 March 2026, s 46PH(1)(c)), Mr
Tutoveanu, who appeared for himself, sought leave under s 46PO(3A)(a) of the *AHRC Act 1986* to sue the State
of NSW under the *Sex Discrimination Act 1984* (ss 22, 28AA, 28G). He said a police report misgendered him
and no investigation followed.

## Held — Markovic J

- Leave is a filter, not a high bar, but the applicant must show a **reasonably arguable** case.
- No evidence of intersex status; no less favourable treatment shown; one apparently unintentional,
  later corrected error was not harassment.
- Police writing a report and deciding whether to investigate are **not services** (*Bickle*).
- Leave refused, application dismissed, no order as to costs.

- **hr.2.9 / hr.2.11** — how a statute is enforced through the Commission and the Court, and how
  effective that pathway is for a self-represented person.
- **hr.3.1** — a contemporary issue: recognition of gender identity and intersex status.

## Caution

A leave decision, not a finding that no discrimination occurred. The file lists ss 5B, 5C, 7B and 106
inconsistently and the date the pronoun was corrected is not given. The underlying allegation is not
described here.
