Malaki Russell pleaded guilty to common assault (domestic violence) after grabbing his ex-partner by the chest and neck in her bedroom.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Objective seriousness | Below midrange |
| Culpability | Reduced by cognitive impairment, PTSD, youth and deprived childhood |
| Plea discount | 25% |
| Sentence | 6-month fixed term, backdated — free to go |
