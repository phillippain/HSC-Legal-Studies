After nine years in prison for supporting Islamic State, the respondent was placed on an interim control order (23 March 2026,
varied 9 July). A senior AFP member sought confirmation. He did not give evidence, so his claimed change and his psychologist's
report were untested.

## The test

| Step | Held |
|---|---|
| Is there a real risk to the public that the order would address? | Yes — a real, unacceptable risk |
| Is each condition reasonably necessary, appropriate and adapted? | Most, with variations; other conditions loosened |
| Electronic monitoring | **Removed** as disproportionate |

The order was **confirmed** under s 104.14(7) until **22 March 2027**.

## Use it for

- **crime.4.8** — post-sentence control: liberty after a completed sentence against community safety.
- **Discretion** — conditions tailored one by one; supportive conditions can serve rehabilitation and protection together.

## Caution

The reasons for confirmation are a procedural step in a control-order regime; the file has some inconsistent Act references.
