The only **appeal** in the 2026 set. De facto relationship mid-2015 to late 2021; child X born 2019
— autism spectrum disorder, global developmental delay, **non-verbal** [4]. Unchallenged findings of
family violence by the father across the relationship and afterwards, **and against two other
intimate partners** [34]. Ordered in May 2023 to inform himself about the child's disabilities, he
made "no enquiry whatsoever" [35].

**Trial outcome under appeal:** supervised time of four hours every alternate Saturday, indefinite.
Net pool $3,251,131; contributions 70/30 to the father; a **17.5% adjustment** under s 90SM(5)
(worth $568,948) → final division **52.5/47.5**, cash payment $1,564,549, lump sum spousal
maintenance $220,000 under s 90SE.

## Held — appeal dismissed

- Ground 1 "entirely without merit" [45]. **"The Act does not contain an 'unacceptable risk test'"**
  [29] (*Isles & Nelissen*). Supervision's artificiality "cannot displace evidence of safety
  concerns" [39]. *Slater v Light* offers guidelines, not binding law, and indefinite supervision has
  never been prohibited (*Lainhart & Ellinson*). It is not the judge's role to build in an
  opportunity to vary [44].
- Grounds 2 and 3 rejected — implied error under *House v The King* is "notoriously difficult to
  prosecute" [77].
- Two real drafting errors were corrected under the **slip rule** (r 10.13), and costs of
  **$34,650.21** ordered against the father because the corrections "could have been made by
  agreement or by application to the primary judge" [100].

## Use it for

- The best vehicle in the set for the **2024 parenting reforms**: it applies the *Family Law
  Amendment Act 2023* (Cth) framework, in force 6 May 2024, which repealed the equal shared parental
  responsibility presumption and rebuilt s 60CC around safety.
- **Domestic violence shaping both the parenting and the property outcome** — rare and valuable.
- **Courts and dispute resolution**: appellate jurisdiction, the slip rule, the ICL, and the rule
  that first-instance remedies must be exhausted before appellate jurisdiction is invoked [25].

## Teaching caution

The appeal was **dismissed** — do not write that he "won on two grounds". The corrections came via
the slip rule at the respondent's invitation. Note the case-name flip: *Hyde & Bergsma* at trial,
*Bergsma & Hyde* on appeal.
