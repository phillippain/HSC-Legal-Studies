After a property judgment of 18 June 2026 gave the wife 58 per cent, she asked for $500,000 in costs of two abandoned trials (June and October 2023) and the final trial (October 2025). On 24 September 2026 Gill J held the husband’s late disclosure caused both losses and ordered $67,715.06, offset against what she owed him. This is a final costs ruling, not a property decision.

## Use it for

- Divorce and separation: costs after a property settlement following the end of a relationship.
- Courts: case management, vacated trials, a compliance and readiness hearing and the costs power.
- Compliance: the husband’s duty of disclosure was breached, with real financial consequences.
- Effectiveness: timeliness and cost; two lost trials and a two-year delay.

## Cautions

This is a final costs ruling by a single judge after a property judgment, not a rehearing of the property split, and the amount awarded is far below the $500,000 sought. The file labels paragraphs twice (‘1. 3’); the real number is the second. Para 59 says the matter was ‘listed for trial on 6 July 2023’, but [46] says that date is when orders listing the October trial were made. Para 71 says junior counsel’s rate is ‘per hour’ though the sum is a daily rate. Colgate Palmolive is cited as 1992 in [22] and 1993 in the header. Care line: legal debt, borrowing and financial stress; keep to the legal test.
