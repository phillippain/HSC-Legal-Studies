A 12-year-old boy who plays sport on most days lives with his mother. His parents disputed his time with his father and whether sport should come first.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Approach | The Independent Children's Lawyer's proposal adopted |
| Time | Alternate weekends in term and holiday blocks |
| Decision-making | Mother keeps sole decision-making; consultation before long-term decisions; notice of overseas travel |
