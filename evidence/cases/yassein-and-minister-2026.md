A Syrian refugee in Australia since 2016 applied for citizenship in 2023 but did not sit the test, claiming a permanent mental incapacity from PTSD and depression.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Weight on the psychologist's report? | Little — did not attend despite summons |
| Enduring incapacity linked to his English? | Not shown |
| Result | Refusal affirmed |
