A partner visa holder was sentenced to five years three months for drug offences and his visa mandatorily cancelled. The Tribunal refused to revoke, rating his reoffending risk low to moderate.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Jurisdictional error? | No |
| Denial of procedural fairness? | No — he raised the issue himself |
| Result | Appeal dismissed with costs |
