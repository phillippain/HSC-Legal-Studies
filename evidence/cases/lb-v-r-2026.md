At 1.30am on 6 June 2025 an intruder took a woman's car keys and drove off in her car; about six minutes later a speed
camera photographed it, and a matching balaclava was later found inside. The young person (initials only, s 15A) was
convicted in the Children's Court of aggravated break and enter and taking a conveyance. **Identity** was the only issue.

## The three questions

| Issue | Held |
|---|---|
| Was the photo admissible (s 55), or should it be excluded or limited (ss 137, 136)? | Admissible — facial features clearly visible; warnings (including on unconscious racial bias) reduced the danger of unfair prejudice |
| Could the Children's Court be satisfied beyond reasonable doubt that LB was the driver? | Yes, on the whole circumstantial case (photo, fingerprints, body-worn video) |
| Was the idea that another person did it excluded? | Yes — the alternative theory was fanciful |

Appeal **dismissed**; convictions confirmed. The first hearing was adjourned because only a photocopy of the photo was available.
