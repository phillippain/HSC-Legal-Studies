An Afghan Hazara man, already found to be a refugee, was refused a Safe Haven Enterprise visa because a 3 year 9 month sentence for a sexual offence meant he failed the character test.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Character test | Failed |
| Direction 123/123A | Community safety and expectations outweighed family ties and hardship |
| Removal to Nauru | Lawful under the Refugees Convention |
| Result | Refusal affirmed |
