Adrianne Jones complained to ADNSW in January 2025 about NSW Police conduct towards them between
June 2024 and January 2025. It was identified as **disability discrimination in the provision of
goods and services** — ss 49A, 49B, 49C, 49M and 53 of the *Anti-Discrimination Act 1977* (NSW).

ADNSW declined it as lacking in substance in July 2025, holding among other things that:

- the allegations **do not identify the service the police provide and refused** — police being
  "dismissive and unsupportive of their vulnerable circumstances after being told they have PTSD"
  does not establish a refusal, or a service on less favourable terms;
- **no comparator** was identified or implied;
- questioning or considering a person's possible mental health does not establish a refusal;
- **"The police determining whether to continue with the investigation of an alleged crime or
  failing to act in a manner sought, does not show a refusal of a service for the purposes of the
  Act"**;
- the **manner** of service is not covered by the Act;
- the police gave reasons in line with their statutory obligations.

Four hearing days across September 2025 to February 2026. **Leave refused** under s 96(1). The
applicant was self-represented; the Commissioner was represented by a national firm.

## Use it for

- **Crime and Human Rights in the same case.** Whether to continue investigating is police
  discretion, and the discretion is essentially unreviewable through this route. A student writing on
  *police powers* or *reporting crime* can use it to show what a person has when they think that
  discretion was exercised badly — which, through the *Anti-Discrimination Act*, is very little.
- **Disability, the CRPD and access to justice** — arts 12–14 in an Australian tribunal, with four
  hearing days and no hearing of the merits.
- The **asymmetry of representation** is worth one sentence: a self-represented applicant with PTSD
  against the Commissioner of Police and a national law firm, over four hearing days.

## Caution

The Tribunal refused **leave**; it made no finding that the police discriminated or did not. *IW v
The City of Perth* (1997) HCA is cited among the authorities on the scope of "services", and is
worth knowing as the leading case on that phrase.
