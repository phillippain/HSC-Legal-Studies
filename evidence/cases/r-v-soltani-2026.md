Eight offenders were convicted of detain for advantage offences (maximum 25 years); nine took part and one has died.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Range of head sentences | 2 years 3 months to 7 years (Soltani) |
| Non-parole periods | All eight set below the 75% ratio |
| Factors | Role, plea, health, prospects, parity |
