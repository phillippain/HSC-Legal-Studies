A resident of a registered general boarding house (14 bedrooms, 15 occupants) received a termination notice on 23 January 2023 after complaints from other residents. He sued the managing agent in the Supreme Court, alleging **racial discrimination** and fraud. Button J had refused an injunction on 6 February 2023.

## Held

Schmidt AJ **dismissed the proceedings with costs**. Under the *Anti-Discrimination Act 1977* (NSW) complaints go to the Anti-Discrimination Board and then NCAT, and NCAT also decides whether the *Boarding Houses Act 2012* or the *Residential Tenancies Act 2010* applies, so the Court should not let **Parliament's arrangements be circumvented**. In any event less favourable treatment because of race was not put or proved (*Purvis* comparator test).

## Use it for

- **Discrimination**: eviction because of race is unlawful (s 20), and the pathway that must be used (`shel.3.2`).
- **Other shelter types**: a registered boarding house and the boarder/lodger line to the tenancy Act (`shel.2.4`, `shel.2.2`).
- **Dispute resolution**: investigation and conciliation resolve many disputes before litigation (`shel.2.5`, `shel.2.7`).

## Caution

The Court did not decide that discrimination did not occur; it decided the forum and proof points. It accepted the agent's evidence of repeated complaints. It says nothing about homelessness beyond an offer of other accommodation.
