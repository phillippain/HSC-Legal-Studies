Rodrigo Duterte ran a **'war on drugs'** as mayor of Davao and then as President of the Philippines
(2016–22). Police counted over 6,000 deaths; human rights groups estimate up to 30,000.

## Timeline

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| March 2019 | Philippine withdrawal from the Rome Statute takes effect; the ICC keeps jurisdiction over Nov 2011 – Mar 2019 |
| March 2025 | Arrested in Manila on an ICC warrant and flown to The Hague |
| 23 April 2026 | Three counts of **crimes against humanity** (murder, attempted murder) confirmed |
| 8 October 2026 | Found **fit to stand trial**; release refused (Al Jazeera, 8 October 2026) |
| 30 November 2026 | Trial listed to open |

The charges are **confirmed, not proved**: he is presumed innocent.

## Use it for

- **Crimes against humanity** (art 7): a systematic attack on civilians in peacetime.
- **Enforcement depends on politics**: the arrest came after a split between Duterte and the new
  government, a contrast with the Putin warrant.
- **Withdrawal does not erase jurisdiction** (art 127).
- **Fair trial rights** at the international level, linking to `crime.3`.
- Check the trial's progress before the exam.
