By 4:3, the High Court held that the grant of a **pastoral lease** does not necessarily extinguish
native title: the two can coexist, and where they conflict the rights under the lease prevail.

Parliament responded with the *Native Title Amendment Act 1998* (Cth) — the "10 point plan" — which
confirmed extinguishment in defined categories and narrowed the right to negotiate.

## Use it for

- The **mechanisms of reform** interacting: a court decision, then a legislative response that
  reduces its effect. That sequence is worth more in an answer than either step alone.
- **The relationship between legal institutions**: this is the case that shows what parliamentary
  sovereignty means in practice.
- **Effectiveness of law reform in achieving just outcomes** — the question the syllabus actually
  asks at ls.5. The honest answer has to account for 1998 as well as 1992 and 1996.
