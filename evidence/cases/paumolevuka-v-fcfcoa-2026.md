A man facing removal asked a court for more time to challenge a bridging visa refusal. A Tongan interpreter was promised but did not appear, and the application was dismissed.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Was he disadvantaged? | Yes, materially |
| Jurisdictional error? | No — urgency, no right to an interpreter, and the case was bound to fail |
| Result | Application dismissed; injunction discharged |
