Home owners paid a builder partly in cash. The builder claimed $201,200 under the Security of Payment Act; the owners served no payment schedule and an adjudicator allowed the claim in full. The owners sought certiorari and declarations.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Did overstating the amount defraud the adjudicator? | No — the Act lets owners correct a claim through a payment schedule |
| Fraud | Not proved on the balance of probabilities (stronger evidence needed for a serious allegation) |
| Result | Summons dismissed with costs; NCAT will decide how much is really owing |
