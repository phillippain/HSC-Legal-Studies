Kutti appealed an aggregate sentence imposed in the District Court at Albury on 12 February 2026.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Effect of reduced culpability on sentencing purposes considered? | No — error |
| Punished for uncharged conduct (De Simoni)? | Yes — error |
| Result | Appeal dismissed: no lesser sentence warranted |
