Quoc Hoang Tran was convicted by a jury of attempting to possess a commercial quantity of an unlawfully imported border controlled drug.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Recklessness correctly explained? | No |
| Direction on his silence? | None given |
| Direction on the driver's flight? | None given |
| Result | Conviction and sentence quashed; new trial |

The orders were made on 28 October 2026; bail pending the retrial was decided the same day in *Tran v R* [2026] NSWCCA 187 (a separate card).
