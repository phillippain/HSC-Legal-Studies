Mr Devine did not lodge tax returns for six financial years. Where a parent does not lodge, the
child support system does not wait — it applies a **provisional income**, indexed from an earlier
year. In December 2024 he lodged all six returns across three days, out of chronological order.

| Year | Actual taxable income | Provisional income | Used in the assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2016–17 | $292,764 | $126,203 | **$292,764** |
| 2017–18 | $209,433 | $129,056 | **$209,433** |
| 2018–19 | $351,966 | $136,130 | **$351,966** |
| 2019–20 | $134,916 | $360,765 | **$360,765** |
| 2020–21 | $34,615 | $370,972 | **$370,972** |
| 2021–22 | $12,258 | $381,883 | **$381,883** |

The three high returns replaced the estimates retrospectively, and the $351,966 figure cascaded
forward into indexed provisional incomes for the following three years. The later returns showed
real incomes as low as $12,258 — a **31× gap** for 2021–22 — but were lodged too late to displace
the higher figures. Arrears: **$39,298.36**.

## Held — decision affirmed in full

The income figures were the ones the legislation required and the arithmetic was correct; the
Tribunal checked it itself. The father's argument that lodging over three days rather than one had
inflated the arrears by $15,000–$20,000 was rejected.

## Use it for

- **The legal rights and obligations of parents** — child support is the obligation most students
  forget, and this is what enforcement of it actually looks like.
- **Merits review** as a mechanism: the Tribunal stands in the shoes of the decision-maker and can
  affirm, vary or set aside. Here it affirmed, and that is still the system working.
- **Compliance and non-compliance**, from the other direction: the non-compliance was with the tax
  system, and the child support consequence was automatic.
- **Is the outcome just?** He is paying on incomes he did not earn in three of the six years,
  because of when he lodged. Defensible as a rule that makes estimates workable; hard to call
  substantively fair. That tension is the answer.
