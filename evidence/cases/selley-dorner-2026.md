A girl of 11 with ADHD and a boy of 8; parents who married in 2013 and separated in March 2021, both
"active participants in parenting" before that. Family violence was found — against a parent and
against a child — and the father's drinking produced a **very high hair test**. Time went from
unsupervised, to nothing for four months, to supervised, and the question at trial was how to
rebuild it.

## Held — a staged return, with testing hung over every step

- **Joint decision-making** on all major long-term issues, with **28 days' written notice** of a
  proposed decision and 7 days to object in writing. The mother's bid for sole decision-making on
  the elder child's health was refused.
- The children live with their mother and **build to four nights a fortnight** across three
  twelve-week stages, plus seven-day holiday blocks.
- **Hair follicle testing** at the father's cost, at least every three months for at least a year, at
  a **20 pg/mg** threshold. A positive result **suspends his time and reverts it to Stage 1**.
- No more than **two standard drinks** on a day the children are in his care, and at least two
  further years with his psychologist.
- Mutual restraints on abuse, denigration and physical discipline, **enforceable by arrest without
  warrant**; all communication through a parenting app. Overseas travel only to Hague Convention
  countries, in alternate years. Family dispute resolution by 31 December 2027 on whether the two
  blocks become one.

Refused: the father's and the ICL's five nights in a single block; the mother's total alcohol ban in
the twelve hours before care; her proposal to postpone overseas travel to 2030.

## Use it for

- **fam.2.4** — family violence **measured rather than assumed**: the Court gave the consideration
  "moderate weight" because it was not as widespread as alleged. That is a more useful line for an
  evaluation question than a case where everything alleged was accepted.
- **Compliance by design** — the clearest example in the collection of orders built so that breach
  answers itself: a fixed testing threshold, automatic reversion, and arrest powers behind the
  restraints.
- **fam.3.2** — joint decision-making kept, but engineered: notice, objection period, and a refused
  "carve-out" for health.
- **Legal and non-legal together** — orders set the frame; psychologists, play therapy and courses do
  the work the orders cannot.
- **fam.1.5** — the children's views were considered and given little weight because of masking, and
  a neurodivergent child's needs shaped what was realistic.

## Caution

About **thirty of the forty-two orders were made by consent**, so this is not a case where a judge
imposed everything over objection. The catchwords say five nights a fortnight; the reasons and orders
say **four**, and the summary follows the orders — do not quote the catchwords. The 2024 best-interests
framework applies. Summary prepared for class from the judgment; quotes checked against the judgment
text.
