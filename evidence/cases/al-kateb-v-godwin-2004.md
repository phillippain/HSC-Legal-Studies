Ahmed Al-Kateb, a **stateless Palestinian** born in Kuwait, arrived in Australia in December 2000. His
protection visa application was refused at every level. He asked to be sent to Kuwait or Gaza, but no
country would accept him.

## Held (6 August 2004, 4:3)

- The Migration Act requires an unlawful non-citizen to be detained until removed, deported or granted a
  visa. The words were clear, so detention continued **even if removal might never be possible**.
- The provisions were **not invalid**: because a purpose of the detention was eventual removal, it was not
  punishment forbidden by the Constitution.

## Use it for

- **exp.4.3 / exp.6.2** — the issue of indefinite detention, and a barrier that lasted 19 years.
- **hr.2.8 / hr.2.14** — the limits of constitutional protection of rights; often cited in the Charter of
  Rights debate because the Court could not read the Act down to protect liberty.
- Pair it with *NZYQ* (2023) to show the law changing through the courts.

## Caution

The **constitutional** holding was overruled in *NZYQ* (2023). The holding about what the words of the Act
mean was not. Do not cite *Al-Kateb* as current law on validity.
