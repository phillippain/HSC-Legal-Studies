In June 2020 **Citigold**, a mining company, placed 150 million shares at $0.005; the price later rose to $0.012. In 2026 a
family partnership and a trustee sought **leave under s 237** of the *Corporations Act* to sue directors and others **on behalf
of the company**.

## Held — application dismissed

- They were **not members**, or persons entitled to be registered, so had **no standing** (s 236(1)(a)).
- An affidavit prepared over lunch on the hearing day was **refused** as unfairly prejudicial to the respondents.
- In any case there was **no serious question to be tried** (s 237(2)(d)). Costs are reserved.

## Use it for

- **Year 11 (Disputes under the law — civil)** — standing, procedural fairness in evidence, and the burden of proof in civil
  proceedings. Not an HSC topic.

## Caution

A **class case summary**; insider trading was never proved and costs are still to be decided.
