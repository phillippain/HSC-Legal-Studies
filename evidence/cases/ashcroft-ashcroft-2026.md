About four years after separation, the wife sought property orders. The husband's lawyers under the s 102NA scheme withdrew.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Contributions | 60:40 to the husband before the family violence adjustment |
| Current and future circumstances | +10% to the wife |
| Orders | Sale of the home; 50:50 split |
