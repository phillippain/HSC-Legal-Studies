## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Risk | A strong likelihood she would reunite with a violent former partner |
| Supervision | No suitable service; a risk she would miss visits |
| Benefit to the child | Little |
| Result | Father has sole parental responsibility; no time unless the child wishes and the father agrees |
