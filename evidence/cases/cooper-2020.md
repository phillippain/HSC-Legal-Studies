The Court of Appeal held that a by-law imposing a blanket prohibition on keeping animals in a strata
scheme was **harsh, unconscionable or oppressive** within s 139(1) of the *Strata Schemes Management
Act 2015* (NSW), because it prohibited conduct that had no effect on any other occupant's use and
enjoyment of their lot.

The reform followed: **s 137B** SSMA now prevents a by-law from unreasonably prohibiting the keeping
of an animal.

## Use it for

- **Other types of shelter** — strata living is how a large share of NSW households are housed, and
  by-laws are private law-making that binds them.
- The **courts → parliament** reform sequence in miniature, and a very short one to write: a court
  struck down a by-law, and Parliament generalised the result. Good as the second example beside the
  longer *Brookfield → s 37 → Pafburn* chain.
