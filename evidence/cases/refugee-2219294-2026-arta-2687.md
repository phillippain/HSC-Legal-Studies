A man from China applied for a protection visa in 2021, citing family pressure to marry. In March 2025 he added claims that he is
homosexual and celibate.

| Question | Finding |
|---|---|
| Is he a gay man? | **Not satisfied** — the claim emerged late, his accounts of telling his agent conflicted, and he gave no personal detail |
| Disapproval for not marrying | Not serious harm |
| A further hearing | Not needed — about two days of evidence; he did not respond |

The refusal of the Subclass 866 visa was **affirmed**. The ART replaced the AAT in 2024, and the duty to invite a hearing (s 425)
was repealed.

## Use it for

- **hr.2.11 / hr.3.1** — credibility, interpreters and country information decide refugee claims.
- **Contrast** with 2217075 (Malaysia), where a sexual orientation claim was accepted.

## Caution

**Do not present him as a gay refugee** — the Tribunal was not satisfied he is gay. Year 11 links are conditional and the link is
not close.
