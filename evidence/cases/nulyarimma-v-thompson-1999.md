## Facts

Aboriginal activists (Wadjularbinna Nulyarimma, Isobel Coe, Billy Craigie and Robbie Thorpe) tried to have the Prime Minister and three parliamentarians charged with **genocide** over the Commonwealth's native title "Ten Point Plan". In a linked proceeding (Buzzacott v Hill) an applicant sued Ministers over the World Heritage listing of Lake Eyre. The appeal came from the ACT Supreme Court (Crispin J).

## Held — three separate judgments, both proceedings dismissed

- All three judges accepted that the prohibition on genocide is a **peremptory norm** of customary international law.
- **Wilcox and Whitlam JJ**: without legislation, genocide is **not an offence** the Australian courts can try.
- **Merkel J** (on that point only): genocide **is** part of the common law, but the appellants still failed on other grounds.
- Orders were unanimous: the appeal and the proceeding were dismissed.

## Use it for

- **crime.6.1**: genocide as a crime against the international community, and the gap between an international norm and an Australian prosecution.
- **Incorporation**: international law does not become Australian law automatically. Link to *International law and Australian law*.
- **Law reform**: legislation, not the courts, closed the gap. Check the later Australian genocide legislation before teaching it, as it is not in this file.
