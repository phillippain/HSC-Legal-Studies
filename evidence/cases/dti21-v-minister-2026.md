A Malaysian man refused a protection visa applied for judicial review three days out of time.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Was the delay a problem? | Minor, and no prejudice to the Minister, but poorly explained |
| Did the case have merit? | No — five template grounds without particulars |
| Result | Extension refused; application dismissed |

## Use it for

- **hr.3.1** — access barriers for unrepresented asylum seekers.
- **Effectiveness** — time limits and template grounds.

## Caution

The Court looked only at whether the case had arguable merit.
