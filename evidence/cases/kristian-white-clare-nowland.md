At about 2am on 17 May 2023, in a Cooma aged care home, Clare Nowland, 95, with undiagnosed dementia,
walking with a frame and holding a knife, was tasered by Senior Constable Kristian White about three
minutes after he arrived. She fell, struck her head and died on 24 May 2023.

- Charges upgraded to manslaughter (29 November 2023).
- **15 October 2024**: application for a **judge-alone trial refused** — media coverage had not been
  shown to make a jury trial unfair.
- **27 November 2024**: jury verdict, guilty of manslaughter after five days' deliberation.
- **28 March 2025**: 2-year community correction order with 425 hours of community service (maximum
  for manslaughter: 25 years).
- **30 July 2025**: the Court of Criminal Appeal dismissed the DPP's appeal that the sentence was
  manifestly inadequate.

## Use it for

- **Police use of force** (LEPRA s 231) and accountability: police are not above the law.
- **Juries** — the contrast with *Dawson*, where judge-alone was granted.
- **Sentencing and Crown appeals** — objective seriousness, general deterrence of police.
