## Facts

An Animals Australia investigator illegally filmed alleged **live-baiting of rabbits** at **Kadir's** greyhound property and gave the footage to the RSPCA, which obtained a warrant. She also elicited admissions. Heard 15 October 2019; appeal from the NSW Court of Criminal Appeal ([2017] NSWCCA 288).

## Held — unanimous (one joint judgment)

Applying s 138 of the *Evidence Act 1995* (NSW):

- The **recordings were excluded**: they were obtained in contravention of an Australian law, and difficulty of getting evidence lawfully does not favour admitting it.
- The **search warrant evidence and admissions** were admitted. Each item is weighed separately.
- Order: appeal allowed in part, restoring the trial judge's exclusion of the surveillance evidence.

## Use it for

- **crime.2.3**: improperly or illegally obtained evidence, including evidence obtained by a private person.
- **Balance** of the public interest in convicting offenders against the interest in not condoning illegality.
- The statutory successor to *Bunning v Cross* and *Ridgeway*.

## Caution

The decision turns on the facts of the greyhound live-baiting prosecution. The appellants' request for remittal was refused and the file notes the text is subject to revision before publication in the CLR.
