A Malaysian woman's protection visa was refused in 2015; her Tribunal review was dismissed in 2016 when she did not attend.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Was the fraud claim against the agent arguable? | Reasonably arguable |
| Was the delay explained? | No — extraordinary and unsatisfactorily explained |
| Result | Extension refused; application dismissed |
