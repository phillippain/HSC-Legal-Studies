Three children (two with health conditions) whose parents separated in April 2021. The children began living with
their mother in Sydney in September 2025; in 2026 **the mother died** and they lived with the paternal aunt. On
**1 July 2026** interim orders let them relocate to Victoria with the maternal grandmother and aunt. On **24 August
2026** the paternal aunt applied again after subpoenaed material showed the grandmother had a long-treated mental
illness she had not disclosed. Hearing 11 September; **orders and reasons 22 September 2026**. The father is
overseas. (Pseudonyms.)

## Held

- **Interim orders are "only a waypoint on the road to a final solution"** (at [24]).
- **Safety first, on evidence**: s 60CC puts safety first after the 2024 amendments, but a risk must be found on
  evidence, not assumed (*Barnabas & Phineus*; *Fitzwater*; *Isles & Nelissen*).
- **The non-disclosure**: the grandmother "should have known about the importance to the Court" of adequate
  information (at [39]), but her illness was long stable and treated, with no recent relapse.
- **Stability**: continuity of care is "integral to the safety and wellbeing" of the children (at [46]); the maternal
  aunt is, "in effect, a safety net" (at [48]).

## The orders

| Safeguard | Detail |
|---|---|
| Treatment reports | Letters from the grandmother's psychologist on four set dates; she stays engaged with her psychiatrist and psychologist |
| Notice | Lawyers told within **24 hours** of any hospital admission; the aunt must remove the children if the grandmother deteriorates |
| Expert | A **single expert psychiatrist** to prepare a family report; costs shared 50/50 |
| Father | Electronic contact only |
| Not ordered | Moving the children to Sydney, or travel overseas |
