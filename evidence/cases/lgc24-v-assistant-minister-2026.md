Two Indonesian parents with criminal convictions were each refused a Protection visa by the Assistant Minister, 80 days apart. Their young child has a speech delay and speaks no Bahasa.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Did the Minister overlook the child's claim? | Yes — recorded but never answered |
| Did that apply to the husband too? | Yes — the Minister knew of it |
| Was the error material? | Yes |
| Result | Both refusals quashed and remitted |

## Use it for

- **hr.3.1** — children's best interests in migration decisions.
- **hr.2.11** — courts enforcing lawful decision-making.

## Caution

Remitted for a fresh decision; the visas were not granted.
