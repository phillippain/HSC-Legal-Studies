An Indian citizen without a substantive visa since 2009 applied in 2017 for a Medical Treatment (Visitor) visa. The Tribunal affirmed the refusal after she did not attend its hearing.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Did hardship change the criteria? | No |
| Any error material? | No |
| Natural justice denied? | No |
| Result | Appeal dismissed with costs |
