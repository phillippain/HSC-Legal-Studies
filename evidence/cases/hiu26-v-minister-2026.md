An Iranian refugee's visa was mandatorily cancelled after a prison sentence for drug offences. The Tribunal reinstated it, but **537 days** later the Minister personally cancelled it again under s 501BA.

## What the court decided

| Ground | Held |
|---|---|
| The child's best interests | Rejected |
| His good conduct since release | Rejected |
| An implied time limit on s 501BA | Rejected — the judge followed a binding Full Court decision |
| Result | Application dismissed with costs |

## Use it for

- **Year 11** — ministerial power to override a tribunal, and precedent binding a single judge (inv.3.2).

## Caution

Year 11 only; a character cancellation — no protection merits decided.
