## The operation

**Operation Ironside (2018–21)**: the FBI and AFP secretly ran **ANOM**, an encrypted phone app sold to
organised crime. Every message was copied to police. Hundreds of people were charged in Australia and
across more than 100 countries.

## The case

Accused people argued that ANOM messages had been **intercepted** in breach of the *Telecommunications
(Interception and Access) Act 1979*, and so were inadmissible. Parliament then passed the
*Surveillance Legislation (Confirmation of Application) Act 2024*, confirming that information obtained
under the AFP's warrants was **not intercepted** and was **lawfully obtained**. The plaintiffs argued the
Act was invalid because it interfered with judicial power. On **8 October 2025** the High Court
**unanimously rejected** the challenge.
