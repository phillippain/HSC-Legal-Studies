A man born in Myanmar lost that citizenship on becoming Taiwanese. He applied for protection in 2018 and was refused in 2023. At review he said his gang and debt claims were not true.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Country of nationality | Taiwan |
| Fears about Myanmar relevant? | No |
| Inability to sponsor family persecution? | No — normal immigration control |
| Result | Refusal affirmed |
