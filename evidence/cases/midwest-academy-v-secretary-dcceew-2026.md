Mr Akkari and Midwest Academy pleaded guilty in the Local Court to four counts of unlawfully clearing native vegetation, and appealed the fines as manifestly excessive.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Nature of the appeal | Rehearing of sentence |
| Offending | Reckless, harmful, partly for commercial gain |
| Fines | Modest |
| Result | Both appeals dismissed; costs for Midwest open |
