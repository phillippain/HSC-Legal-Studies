A motor mechanic applied for a permanent employer-nominated visa. The job was no longer available after COVID-19 lockdowns hurt his employer. The Tribunal sympathised but had no discretion.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Procedural fairness given? | Yes |
| Can the Court fix an unfair result? | No — legality only |
| Result | All four grounds failed; costs $5,900 |
