A Baulkham Hills tenant paying $520 a week lived with water penetration and mould from November 2021 that made about **a third of his flat uninhabitable**. The parties negotiated a 25% rent cut and then $200 a week from November 2022, with a condition that he make no further NCAT application. On 20 February 2023 the landlord served a 90-day no-reason termination notice.

## Held

NCAT **capped rent at $200 a week** (s 44(1)(b), facilities withdrawn), ordered **$760 refunded** and awarded **$1,500 for breach of quiet enjoyment** (s 50). It **rejected** the retaliatory notice claim (s 115), the $650 labour claim (not an urgent repair), nil rent, $11,000 and the Europe trip claim.

## Use it for

- **Leasing and protection** (`shel.2.2`, `shel.2.3`): habitability duties, excessive rent, quiet enjoyment and the limits of the retaliation rule.
- **ADR** (`shel.2.7`): the parties negotiated the $200 rent themselves, and the Tribunal gave weight to their agreed figure.
- **Effectiveness**: the remedies were real but partial, and the cure depended on the owners corporation.

## Caution

This is a mixed result, not a tenant victory. The file has slips (para 22 says 2021 for 2022; order 3 refers to "order 1" for the rent order). s 44(5)(h) excludes the tenant's ability to pay, so this is not an affordability case.
