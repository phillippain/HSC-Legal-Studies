**Brodie Brooker** pleaded guilty to **ongoing supply** (19 supplies, May 2024), possessing a stun gun and attempting to
receive **280 g** of methylamphetamine, with **11 offences on Form 1s**. In December 2025 he received an aggregate **4
years 3 months** (non-parole 2 years 7 months).

## Held — leave granted, appeal dismissed

- He argued that calling the attempt "**not an isolated aberration**" wrongly used other offending to raise **objective
  seriousness**. The phrase has two readings; an appeal court will **not infer error** where the remarks are reasonably
  read as lawful.
- Calling the quantity "just into the commercial range" but "still a very large amount" was not an error either.
