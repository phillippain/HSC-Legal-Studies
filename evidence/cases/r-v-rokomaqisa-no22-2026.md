The Crown wanted to prove a phone found at the accused's home was a second encrypted device. The defence objected late, though it had accepted the point in the first trial; the expert relied on a partial extraction the Crown served only on the day.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Who was at fault? | Mainly the Crown — late disclosure |
| The defence's late objection | Did not save the evidence |
| Result | Evidence excluded |
