Two parents sought final parenting orders for children aged **10 and 8**. In 2023 the father was found guilty of
intimidation and a two-year AVO protected the mother, but in early 2025 the **District Court quashed the conviction and
dismissed the AVO**. The trial ran over four days in September 2025.

## Held — Judge Suthers

- The father was a **perpetrator of family violence** (including controlling behaviour at a doorway incident) — the
  family court decides on the **balance of probabilities**, so a quashed criminal conviction does not decide the issue.
- Unsupervised time posed an **unacceptable risk** to the children.
- **Orders (38 parts):** children live with the mother, who has **sole parental responsibility**; time with the father
  begins **supervised** and graduates, with safe changeovers; a neutral expert explains the orders to the children,
  whose wish to see their father is respected.
- The judge criticised **both** parents for escalating the conflict.
