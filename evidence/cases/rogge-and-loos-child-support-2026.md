A 2016 parenting plan recorded care of a girl (born 2012) as **65% mother, 35% father**. In 2024 the mother told Child
Support that care had changed from 14 August 2022; on objection, Child Support decided **75/25**. The father sought
review. Both parents were self-represented; the hearing was by video with an interpreter.

## Held — decision set aside

- Neither parent had kept a calendar. The Tribunal accepted the father had not provided all the care the plan expected,
  but could not fix the exact nights.
- It **split the difference**: 254 nights to the mother, 111 to the father — **70/30** from 14 August 2022, with the old
  65/35 revoked as of 13 August 2022.

## Use it for

- **fam.2.2** — care percentages drive child support; small changes in nights change who pays.
- **fam.2.5 / tribunals** — informal, accessible review for self-represented parents, and its limits when evidence is
  missing.

A **class case summary** of a single-member decision (pseudonyms). The decision has no paragraph numbers, and the
night arithmetic and the mother's 2024 overseas claim are loosely reasoned.
