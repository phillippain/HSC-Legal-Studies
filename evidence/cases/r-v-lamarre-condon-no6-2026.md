When the Crown asked for the full note, the defence claimed client legal privilege.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Was privilege waived (s 122)? | Yes |
| Why? | Redacted parts covered the same subjects as parts used |
| Result | Crown gets the whole note |
