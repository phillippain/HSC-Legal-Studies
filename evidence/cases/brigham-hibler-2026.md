Seven years of **private collect** — the parents paying each other directly — ended when Child
Support began collecting on 1 January 2025. The father said he "always paid a month ahead", so his
December payment covered January, and sought credit for a $649 payment recorded online as made on
4 February 2025. The mother said no such payment was made. Credit refused; objection disallowed;
review in the Tribunal, both parents unrepresented, one in person and one by phone.

## Held — decision affirmed

- **s 71** — a direct payment counts as child support only if **both** parents intended it in lieu;
  **s 71A** the same for third-party payments; **s 71C** credits prescribed payments (including,
  under **reg 19**, the payee's rent, utilities and home loan) once 70% is paid, up to 30%.
- All three presuppose an **actual payment**. At the hearing both parents agreed no $649 was paid.
  Nothing to credit.
- The father's real grievance — that collection should have started on 1 February, not 1 January —
  was a **separate decision**, not before the Tribunal.

## Use it for

- **fam.1.5** — the legal obligation to pay child support, and exactly when a payment outside the
  system counts.
- **fam.2.5** — the administrative pathway: Child Support decision → internal objection → merits
  review in the ART, and the strict scope of that review.
- **Effectiveness** — accessible and cheap (no lawyers, a phone link), but it answered the question
  asked, not the problem the father had.
- **Shelter link** — reg 19 lets payments towards the payee's rent or home loan count as child
  support.
