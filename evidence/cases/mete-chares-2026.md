Final orders in April 2023 gave the mother sole parental responsibility and left the father's time with three children to written agreement. In 2024 he sought reconsideration.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Threshold under s 65DAAA | A significant change in circumstances must be shown first |
| Shown here? | No — continuations of earlier circumstances |
| Result | Application dismissed |
