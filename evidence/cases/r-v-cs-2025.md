CS, aged 16 and a half, was convicted by a jury of murder and armed robbery causing wounding in Nowra on 4 January 2022. The Crown could not prove who stabbed Mr Kerr. He is the co-offender of CL (see *R v CL* [2026] NSWSC 101).

## What the court decided

| Factor | Finding |
|---|---|
| Youth, deprivation, impairments | Reduce culpability; rehabilitation carries more weight |
| SNPP (20 years) | Not applied — a juvenile |
| Special circumstances | Found |
| Sentence | Murder 14 years 6 months, non-parole 9 years 6 months (to 23 July 2032); robbery 2 years 6 months concurrent |
