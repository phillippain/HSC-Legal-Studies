Xycom contracted on 6 December 2013 to sell Mr Raymond Wu an off-the-plan unit at Hurstville with a **sunset date of 31 December 2015**. Jobema bought the site in 2014-15 subject to the contract and assumed Xycom's obligations. After **s 66ZL of the *Conveyancing Act 1919* (NSW)** began on 2 November 2015, Jobema served notice on 1 December 2015 and applied for leave to rescind, saying construction costs and prices had risen.

## Held

Black J **dismissed the application** on 12 January 2016. The court may permit rescission only if it is **just and equitable in all the circumstances** (s 66ZL(6)). Jobema was bound by Xycom's contract and delay, its claims about costs and finance were **bare assertion**, legislative change was a foreseeable business risk, and it had treated buyers **selectively**.

## Use it for

- **Purchasing** (`shel.2.1`): protection for off-the-plan buyers and the court's role under a new statutory test.
- **Law reform**: s 66ZL from the *Conveyancing Amendment (Sunset Clauses) Act 2015*, applied to existing contracts (`shel.t.reform`).

## Caution

The decision covers **only Mr Wu**; other buyers' applications were deferred. The file has **no paragraph numbers**, no price or deposit, no costs order, and no case law on s 66ZL (it was new). Mr Wu did not appear. The masthead reads "upreme Court" and one affidavit is dated after the judgment, both file errors.
