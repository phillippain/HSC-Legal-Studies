The Tribunal dismissed a Malaysian man's protection visa review when he did not attend a hearing, then confirmed the dismissal.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Was notice validly given? | Yes — emailed to the address he gave |
| Confirmation one day early? | An error, but immaterial |
| Result | Dismissed with $5,400 costs |
