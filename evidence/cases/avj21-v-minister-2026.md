A Malaysian mother, her son and her young daughter were refused protection visas, and the Tribunal affirmed in 2021. The daughter relied on her parents' claims.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Did the Tribunal breach s 424A (putting adverse information)? | No — a historical fact differs from the conclusion drawn from it |
| Did deciding the parents first show apprehended bias? | No |
| Result | Both applications dismissed |

- **hr.3.1** — how far a child's own claim is assessed in refugee law.
- **inv.1.6** — procedural fairness and the bias rule.

The file's dates conflict in places; the decision is on judicial review, not the merits.
