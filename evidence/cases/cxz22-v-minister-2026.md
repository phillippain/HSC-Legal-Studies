A Malaysian woman's protection visa was refused in 2017. Her review was dismissed when she did not attend, and in 2021 the Tribunal held it had no jurisdiction to hear her again. She sought judicial review **245 days** late.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Explanation for the delay | Inadequate |
| Grounds | Unarguable |
| Result | Extension refused; application dismissed with $4,000 costs |
