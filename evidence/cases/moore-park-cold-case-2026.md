- **1991–93:** women were attacked at knifepoint in Moore Park, the eastern suburbs and the inner
  west; the task force disbanded in 1993.
- **2024:** a familial DNA match led police to collect the man's fork and glass at Sydney Airport
  (January); he was arrested in February 2024.
- **23 Sept 2025:** guilty pleas to 13 charges in the Local Court, 14 more offences on a Form 1, nine
  withdrawn.
- **19 Oct 2026:** at the sentence hearing a victim read her impact statement to him; the defence
  relied on his deprived upbringing and drug use; he apologised. Sentence is due in December 2026.

## Use it for

- **Historical offences** (`crime.4.1`, `crime.4.3`) — since 18 October 2022 (s 21B) courts use
  today's sentencing patterns, but the maximum penalty is the one in force when the offence was
  committed.
- **Victim impact statements** (`crime.4.4`) — harm described three decades on.
- **Discounts and Form 1** (`crime.3.4`) — s 25D (25% for a Local Court plea, unless reduced for
  extreme culpability, s 25F(2)); Form 1 offences cannot be prosecuted later.
- **Investigation** (`crime.2.4`) — familial DNA and covertly collected samples.

## Caution

A news report of a hearing, not a judgment: no sentence had been imposed at 20 October 2026, and the
defence arguments are submissions. Published charge counts do not fully reconcile. Check the remarks
on sentence in December.
