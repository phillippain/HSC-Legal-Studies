MTH sued the estate of **Geoffrey Croft**, convicted in 2019 of sexual offences against her. The trial judge wrongly thought s 91
of the *Evidence Act* barred use of the convictions and dismissed her claim. The Court of Appeal awarded **$1,346,000** by assuming
a certificate of conviction.

## What the High Court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Can a conviction prove the facts in a civil claim? | Yes — s 92(2) allows it, replacing the old *Hollington* rule (from an ALRC report) |
| Was the Court of Appeal's assumption about a certificate right? | No |
| Remedy | Appeal allowed in part; Court of Appeal orders (3) and (5) set aside; **new trial** of the claim against the Estate |

The appellant pays the costs of the amici curiae.
