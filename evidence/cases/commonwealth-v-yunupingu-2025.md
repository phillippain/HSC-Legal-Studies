The late Dr Galarrwuy Yunupingu, for the Gumatj clan of the Gove Peninsula — the same country as *Milirrpum* — claimed compensation for Commonwealth grants over Gumatj land between 1939 and 1969 (the bauxite leases among them), before the *Racial Discrimination Act 1975* made such acts compensable under the *Native Title Act*.

## Held (12 March 2025)

- Section 51(xxxi) of the Constitution — acquisition of property only on just terms — applies to laws made under the territories power, s 122. Earlier authority to the contrary (*Teori Tau* 1969) was wrong.
- Native title rights and interests are "property" for s 51(xxxi).
- A grant inconsistent with native title can be an "acquisition" of property, so laws authorising such grants without just terms were invalid to that extent, and compensation may be payable.

The Commonwealth's appeal from the Full Federal Court was dismissed. The claim now returns to the Federal Court to assess compensation.

## Caution

The judgment decides questions of law; no compensation has been assessed. Media estimates of the Commonwealth's liability are commentary.
