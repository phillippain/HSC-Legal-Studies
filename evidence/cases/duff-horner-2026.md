Jurisdiction was contested: the **s 90SB(a)** two-year threshold was satisfied and a declaration
made under **s 90RD** (June 2020 – June 2023).

Then the division: 58/42 on initial contributions, equal during the relationship, +1% for
post-separation improvements, +1% for future needs → **60/40 of a $2,670,031.52 pool**. Per
*Shinohara & Shinohara* [2025] FedCFamC1A 126, no notional add-backs.
