A Mexican woman applied for a protection visa in 2020 and was refused in 2022. At the 2026 hearing she gave new evidence that, as
the daughter of a senior figure in the **Light of the World Church**, she had been sexually abused by its inner circle.

| Question | Finding |
|---|---|
| Credibility | Credible |
| Convention reason | Membership of a particular social group |
| Real chance of serious harm | Yes |
| State protection, changing her behaviour, relocation | All failed — a weak justice system in Mexico |

The delegate's decision was **set aside** and **remitted** with a direction that she meets s 36(2)(a); complementary protection was
not decided.

- **hr.3.1 / hr.2.9** — protection where a state cannot or will not protect; Convention concepts in the *Migration Act*.
- **Effectiveness** — merits review corrected the refusal, but six years passed from application to decision.
