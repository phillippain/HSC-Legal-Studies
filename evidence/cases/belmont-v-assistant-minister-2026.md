A New Zealand citizen's visa was cancelled after domestic violence order convictions, then reinstated by the Administrative Review Tribunal. The Assistant Minister used s 501BA(2) to overturn it in the national interest.

## What the court decided

| Ground | Held |
|---|---|
| Ignoring immigration detention | Rejected |
| Refusing to treat stress as lessening seriousness | Rejected |
| Inferring he hid convictions on a passenger card | Rejected |
| Result | Dismissed with costs |
