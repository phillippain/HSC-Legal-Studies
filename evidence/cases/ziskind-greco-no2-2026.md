Cohabited 1999, married 2005, separated 2024 — a 25-year relationship with two children, aged 11
and 8. The central factual issue was the father's drug use: he claimed occasional past cocaine use,
but 2022 drug-rehabilitation intake records identified **methamphetamine as his primary drug of
concern for around nine years**, and repeated 2024–25 hair-follicle tests returned positive results
the single expert could not attribute to his claimed (illegally sourced) ADHD medication.

## Findings

| Issue | Outcome |
|---|---|
| The father's credibility on drug use | **Not accepted** — he blamed the rehabilitation records on error and changed his account between expert reports and trial |
| Unacceptable risk of harm | **Upheld** — a "significant" risk of relapse made unsupervised care an unacceptable risk |
| Family violence | **Partly upheld** — derogatory language, gaslighting, **covert surveillance** and threats to withhold the children met the definition; alleged physical altercations were not proven on the civil standard |
| Joint decision-making sought by the father | **Rejected** — sole responsibility to the mother, given the absence of trust and his past threats to breach orders |
| Contributions over a 25-year relationship | **52%** to the mother, by "instinctive synthesis", rejecting both 50% and 55% |
| s 79(5) future needs | **+8%**, rejecting 3% and 10% |

Pool: **$9.7 million**, mostly the proceeds of the family home. The parties also disputed ownership
of the family dog.

## Use it for

- **What family violence means after the reforms.** No proven assault, and family violence was still
  found — on conduct that is recognisable as coercive control. That is the single most useful
  correction to the assumption that domestic violence means physical harm.
- **The civil standard doing visible work**: the same judgment upheld one category of allegation and
  rejected another on the evidence, in one case. Pair with *Billings & Clowes (No 2)*, where the
  allegations failed but were not found to be false.
- **"Instinctive synthesis"** — a court rejecting both parties' percentages and choosing its own is
  what discretion in property division actually looks like.
