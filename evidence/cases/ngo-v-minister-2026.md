A man's Bridging C visa was mandatorily cancelled after drug trafficking and proceeds-of-crime convictions. The Tribunal refused to revoke the cancellation.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Parole arguments | Rejected |
| Finding on risk of reoffending | Illogical — error |
| Expert evidence on the children | Wrongly said to be absent — error |
| Result | Decision quashed |
