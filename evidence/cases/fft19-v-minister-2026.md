A protection visa applicant said he learned of his refusal only after the 28-day review period ended. He argued the Department had to notify him again after a phone call.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Did s 66 require a second notice? | No — a single duty, done by posting |
| Procedural fairness from the call? | Not reviewable |
| Result | Dismissed with costs |
