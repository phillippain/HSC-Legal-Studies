Property orders gave the wife **25%** of the pool and the husband **75%**. Both parties jointly asked for the appeal to be allowed,
by a Minute of Consent Orders.

| Issue | Held |
|---|---|
| Were the reasons for the percentage split adequate? | No |
| Was the wife's health weighed under s 79(5)? | No weight, or plainly wrong weight |
| Remedy | Remitted to Division 2 for rehearing by a different judge — the self-represented husband wanted to lead further evidence |

The wife received a costs certificate. The new split is for the rehearing.

## Use it for

- **fam.2.3** — property after separation: a percentage must be explained, and health is a s 79(5) factor.
- **fam.2.5** — an appeal in Division 1 correcting a Division 2 decision; consent and joint submissions shortened the appeal.
