Ms Chinacalle held a subclass 485 visa only as her then de facto partner's secondary applicant. They separated; his visa was cancelled at his request and hers followed under s 140(1).

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Did it matter she had left his family unit? | No — the section looks to the basis of grant |
| Result | Appeal dismissed with costs |
