Ms Gavin began as a claims agent on **13 April 2026** with six months' probation. During a performance improvement plan
she disclosed **endometriosis** and asked for adjustments (19 August); the employer decided to terminate (4 September).
She complained to **Anti-Discrimination NSW** and asked NCAT for **interim orders** reinstating her. She appeared for
herself; the employer had counsel.

## Held — application dismissed (17 September; reasons 4 November 2026)

- The s 105 interim power is guided by the principles for an **interlocutory injunction**: a **prima facie case** and the
  **balance of convenience**.
- No prima facie case on the evidence, and the balance favoured the employer.
- The **complaint itself is undecided**.

## Use it for

- **hr.2.11 / hr.2.9** — how state anti-discrimination law is enforced: complaint to Anti-Discrimination NSW, then NCAT.
- **Access to justice** — a self-represented worker against a represented employer; interim relief is hard to win.
