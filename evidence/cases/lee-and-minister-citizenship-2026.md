A delegate refused an application for Australian citizenship (s 21(4)(e), *Australian Citizenship Act 2007*)
in June 2025 and sent the decision to the email address the applicant had nominated. She asked the
Tribunal for an extension of time on 15 June 2026, 347 days after the review period ended.

## Held — the Tribunal

- Section 19 of the *Administrative Review Tribunal Act 2024* allows an extension if "reasonable in all
  the circumstances", applying the *Hunter Valley Developments v Cohen* factors.
- The delay was extreme and unexplained; she "bore the responsibility of updating her contact details".
- The merits were weak on an impressionistic view; no prejudice was not enough; she could reapply.
- The application for an extension of time was refused.
