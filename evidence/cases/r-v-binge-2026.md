**Kohen Binge**, 22, joined a group attack on an inmate in his cell and wounded him with a home-made knife. He pleaded guilty
early to **reckless wounding in company** (s 35(3), maximum 10 years; standard non-parole period 4 years).

## The questions

| Issue | Held |
|---|---|
| Objective seriousness | Well above midrange |
| Moral culpability | Substantially reduced by childhood deprivation and trauma — *Bugmy* applies even without a causal link |
| Does reduced culpability mean a lower sentence? | Not automatically (*Harris*); deterrence carries weight for violence in prison (*Jeremiah*) |
| Presumption against concurrency for a convicted inmate (s 56) | Held not to apply |

**Sentence**: 5 years 9 months (after a 25% plea discount) from 12 July 2026 to 11 April 2032; parole eligibility after 3 years
2 months (11 September 2029).
