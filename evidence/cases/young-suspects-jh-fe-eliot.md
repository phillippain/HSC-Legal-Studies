- ***JH v R*** [2019] NSWSC 192: a 16-year-old was arrested for smoking on the Manly Corso. The arrest was not reasonably necessary (LEPRA s 99), so the resist-arrest charge was set aside.
- ***R v FE*** [2013] NSWSC 1692: a 15-year-old was not properly cautioned, and her mother, who had limited English, was used as the support person; both interviews were excluded.
- ***R v Eliot (No 1)*** [2022] NSWSC 327: police took a 14-year-old's laptop from hospital without a warrant, and his mother was not told she could refuse; the evidence was excluded under Evidence Act s 138.

## Use it for

Arrest (s 99), rights in custody (Children (Criminal Proceedings) Act s 13), use of evidence (s 138), and young offenders.
