A dance academy in St Leonards leased premises in 2022 with a licence for **17 car spaces**. The new landlord terminated the
licence in September 2025, saying customers used more than the academy's seven rooftop spaces, relying on **boom-gate access
records** from the parking operator. The academy sought a declaration that the termination was void.

## Held — breach not proved

- The **landlord bore the onus** of proving breach, even though the tenant brought the case.
- The ordinary **balance of probabilities** applied (*Evidence Act* s 140; *Briginshaw* did not raise it here).
- The access records — with known reader faults — were **unreliable** and not proof of where cars parked. The licence remains;
  the academy gets its costs.
