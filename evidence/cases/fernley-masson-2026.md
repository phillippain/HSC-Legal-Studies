X, 17, is permanently and severely disabled (NDIS level seven — he cannot be left alone and needs
24-hour care). His parents separated in 2019; from about 2021 the father chose to have no contact,
and all care fell to the mother. Child support stops at 18. The mother and X's stepfather applied
for **adult child maintenance**. All three parties were self-represented; the father would not
produce his bank statements, and subpoenas were needed.

## Held — $1,300 a month, CPI-indexed, until 2041

- **s 66L** — maintenance after 18 is available where "necessary … because of a mental or physical
  disability". Satisfied: the father accepted he had to contribute.
- **s 66J** — X's needs outside the NDIS were $1,870.83 a month, unchallenged. His disability
  support pension and his NDIS package ($119,866.97 for the year, "not intended to meet his day to
  day living expenses") were put aside.
- **s 66K** — the father pleaded poverty, but his business grossed $213,678.81 (2024) and
  $320,466.51 (2025); earning capacity found at $264,000. "It is simply unclear where all the money
  the father earns has gone" [56].
- Term: the mother asked for no end date; the father for three years. The court fixed **15 years**,
  to his pension age of 67, because a short term would only bring them back to court.
- *Cosgrove & Cosgrove* (1996): a warm relationship is not a precondition. The father's choice not
  to see X "should not be a bar" to an order [66].

The order is registered with the Child Support Agency for collection within 14 days.

## Use it for

- **fam.1.5** — the obligation of parents to maintain their children in its clearest form, and
  its reach past 18.
- **fam.3.2** — parental responsibility defined by the child's need, not the parent's involvement.
- **Compliance** — about $14,000 in arrears cleared only in 2024–25, non-disclosure, subpoenas, and
  agency collection as the law's answer.
- **Law reform** — the gap between an 18-year child support scheme, NDIS funding that excludes daily
  living costs, and lifelong disability.

## Caution

The order ends at the father's retirement, not when X's needs end — the court's own compromise
between finality and fairness. Summary prepared for class from the judgment, with quotes checked
against the judgment text.
