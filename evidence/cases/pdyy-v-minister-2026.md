A refugee-visa holder with a long record of offending, including repeated family violence against his partner, was refused a protection visa because he was a danger to the community.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Unreasonable to discount his partner's evidence? | No — the reasons, read as a whole, rested on expert risk evidence and unmet rehabilitation needs |
| Result | Application dismissed with costs |

- **hr.3.1** — refugee protection limited by community safety; state sovereignty.
