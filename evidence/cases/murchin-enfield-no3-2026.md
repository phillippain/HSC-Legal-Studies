X, five. Grave allegations both ways, an attempted removal from a contact centre, and a social media
campaign about a private case. Repeated child protection notifications against the father were all
unsubstantiated. X was made available six times out of seventeen; testing was not done and reports
were not attended. Her allegations of strangulation, forced sex and a threat with a gun were **never
sworn to in her trial affidavit and never put to him in cross-examination**, so they could not be
accepted. Four hearing days on circuit; 375 paragraphs.

## Held

X lives with his father, who has **sole decision-making responsibility with no obligation to consult
or notify**. X spends **no time with his mother**; she may send cards, letters or gifts four times a
year, subject to content conditions the father may inspect. A **recovery order under s 67U**
authorises police to recover X if he is removed, in force for twelve months — the maximum the Court
held was available. Injunctions restrain both parents from denigration and from posting about X
online, and the father from caring for X above 0.05 BAC; he must complete a men's behaviour change
program. The mother pays **$9,322.47** in costs.

## Use it for

- **fam.2.4** — family violence defined by conduct, not by assumption: here the mother is the
  perpetrator, and the campaign itself is the violence. Social media as a modern form of it.
- **fam.3.2** — decision-making stripped back as far as the Act allows.
- **Compliance** — the spine of the case, with a costs order "protecting the public purse" as the
  sanction.
- **Effectiveness** — supervised contact, intervention orders and a recovery order are all deployed,
  and the ceiling of each is visible, including the twelve-month cap.
- **Rights** — a parent's freedom to post online against a child's privacy and protection, resolved
  by injunction.

## Caution

Untested allegations are not disproved allegations — they were not established because of how the
case was run. Four cards a year is a deliberate minimal thread kept open for later, not a finding
that contact is worthless. Summary prepared for class from the judgment; quotes checked against the
judgment text.
