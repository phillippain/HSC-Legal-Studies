During the final hearing of a long de facto property case worth about $65–67 million, the couple's three adult children applied to be joined. The wife had just dropped her claim to the husband's shares.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Do the children have an arguable case? | Yes |
| Necessary parties under r 3.01? | Yes — joined; hearing vacated |
| Costs | Wife to pay $57,200 indemnity costs |
