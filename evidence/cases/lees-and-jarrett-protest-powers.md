- **February 2025**: a move-on power for protests near places of worship. *Lees v State of NSW* (Mitchelmore J, 16 October 2025): **invalid**. The Crimes Act offence of harassing worshippers remains. (*The Australian*, 16 October 2025.)
- **December 2025**: after the Bondi Beach terrorist attack (14 December 2025), the Terrorism and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2025 let the Commissioner make **Public Assembly Restriction Declarations**; one covered central Sydney from 24 December and was extended in January and February 2026.
- *Jarrett v State of NSW* [2026] NSWCA 62 (16 April 2026): the PARD provisions were **invalid**. 'Social cohesion' was not a legitimate purpose and every assembly was restricted regardless of any threat.
- LEPRA s 19A (removing face coverings at protests on reasonable suspicion) was not challenged and remains.

## Use it for

- **Accountability of police powers**: courts as a check on Parliament and police.
- **Human Rights**: with no bill of rights, the implied freedom is one of the few constitutional protections (ICCPR arts 19, 21).
