A woman refused membership of the Russian Club complained of several kinds of discrimination and victimisation; the Board's President referred the complaints, and she needed leave under s 96.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Each complaint | Lacked substance or was speculative |
| Re-opening the case | Refused |
| Result | Leave application dismissed |

## Caution

Leave only — no findings on the allegations.
