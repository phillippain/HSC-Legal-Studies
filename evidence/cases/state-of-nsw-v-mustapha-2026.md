**Mejid Mustapha**, 37, has a violent record and past extremist beliefs (in 2018 he assaulted a police officer and made
threats referring to Islamic State) but has **never been convicted of a terrorism offence**. He was placed on an extended
supervision order in 2022 and a second, relaxed one-year order in November 2025. The State sought a **third**.

## Held — Schmidt AJ

- The test is an unacceptable risk of a **serious terrorism offence**, not violence in general.
- Despite his compliance and progress, lingering beliefs and recent conduct meant, if the allegations were proved, a further
  order could be justified.
- **Two examiners** appointed and a **28-day interim order** from 13 November 2026.
