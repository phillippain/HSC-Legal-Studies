All five contraventions proven; no reasonable excuse under **s 70NAD(3)**. Applying *Stamp & Stamp*
[2014] FCCA 1269, "token words of encouragement" placed the burden of compliance on a **14-year-old**
rather than on the parent.

**No penalty.** Orders suspended under s 70NBC, time cut to four hours a fortnight, counselling
ordered, six-month review.

## Use it for

The single cleanest example in the Family option of **compliance and non-compliance**: the court
found every breach established and then declined to punish any of them, because punishing the parent
would not produce the relationship the orders exist to protect.

Beyond Family, it is a **Crime core** point too: enforceability is not the same as enforcement, and
a finding without a consequence is a limit on the law's effectiveness rather than proof of it.
