The Tribunal refused a Subclass 500 (student) visa because the applicant was not enrolled in a course when it decided.

| Ground | Held |
|---|---|
| Should hardship have been weighed? | No — cl 500.211(a) is a **binary criterion of fact** with no discretion |
| New procedural fairness grounds | No merit |
| Result | All grounds failed; **$4,500** costs |

- **Justice** — law applied as written, even where hardship is real.
