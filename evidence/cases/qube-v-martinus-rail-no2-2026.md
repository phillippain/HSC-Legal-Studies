Martinus Rail owed Qube about $26 million, enforced from an arbitral award, and asked to pay monthly over 18 months.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Who carried the onus? | Martinus |
| Did it prove it could not pay another way? | No — assets, related-party loans and borrowing ignored |
| Result | Dismissed with costs |
