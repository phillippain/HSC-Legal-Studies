Consent orders gave a wife 90 days to take over the family home, refinance and pay her husband 38% of the equity, or the home would be sold. She was late, and a magistrate extended time and changed the payment and sale triggers.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Was the transfer date only machinery? | Yes — it could be extended |
| The changed payment and sale triggers | Altered the husband's rights — struck out (Order 4 set aside; words deleted from Order 3) |
| Result | Appeals otherwise dismissed; no costs application |
