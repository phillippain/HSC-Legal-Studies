**Quoc Hoang Tran**, 49, had his conviction for attempting to possess a commercial quantity of a border controlled drug quashed,
with a new trial likely in mid-2027. He applied for bail.

## The two tests

| Test | Held |
|---|---|
| Show cause (a show cause offence) | Shown — the **ceiling principle** (a retrial sentence cannot exceed the first) and time already served |
| Unacceptable risk (s 19) | Risks of flight and reoffending acceptable with conditions |

**Bail granted** on **14 conditions**, including reporting to Redfern Police each Monday and Friday and surrendering his passport;
he must appear in the District Court on 6 November 2026. Guilt is not decided.

## Use it for

- **crime.2.5** — bail after a successful appeal; liberty against the risks of flight and reoffending.
- **Compliance** — passport, phone and drug-testing conditions secure appearance.

## Caution

A short bail judgment (18 paragraphs); the retrial outcome is unknown.
