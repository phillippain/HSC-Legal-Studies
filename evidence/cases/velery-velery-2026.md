A Family Court of WA judge divided a **$1.25 million** pool 60% to the wife and 40% to the husband (contributions 55/45). She appealed, saying her tax debt and her mother's $303,000 loan were ignored and the husband's inheritance over-weighted.

## What the court decided

| Ground | Held |
|---|---|
| Reasons | Adequate |
| Findings on debts and contributions | Open on the evidence |
| Cryptocurrency error | Favoured her |
| Result | Appeal dismissed; no order as to costs (s 114UB presumption not displaced) |

- **Effectiveness** — legal costs nearly matched the asset pool.

Names are pseudonyms; child support departure orders were not challenged.
