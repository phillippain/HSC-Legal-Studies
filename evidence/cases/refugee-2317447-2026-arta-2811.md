The husband claimed political torture in Nepal; the wife feared family violence in India; their two sons were born in Australia.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Husband's account | Shifting and unsupported |
| Wife at risk? | Not accepted |
| Sons | A hard adjustment, not serious harm |
| Result | All refusals affirmed |
