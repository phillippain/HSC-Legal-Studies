The defence tried to tender a police still from council CCTV whose timestamp was 12 seconds different from the footage. A police inspector checked the original, and the gap came from making the still.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Cause of the discrepancy | The process of making the still |
| Section 135(b) | Misleading and confusing |
| Result | Still excluded |

## Use it for

- **crime.3.7** — the discretion to exclude evidence (s 135), applied to defence evidence too.

## Caution

An interlocutory ruling.
