A former tenant appealed an NCAT decision dismissing her second set of claims against her landlord.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Unfairness | None found |
| Fence and garden claims | Already decided |
| Other grounds | Leave refused |
| Result | Allowed in part: three undecided claims remitted |
