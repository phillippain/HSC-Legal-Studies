After a successful appeal against convictions on other offences, Mr Haouchar was re-sentenced on his guilty pleas: a police pursuit, an unauthorised pistol, commercial drug supply (with two Form 1 offences taken into account) and dealing with $22,250 of proceeds.

## What the court decided

| Factor | Finding |
|---|---|
| Plea discount | 25% |
| Addiction and trauma | Modestly lessened culpability |
| Special circumstances | Found — non-parole about 60% |
| Sentence | Aggregate 6 years, non-parole 3 years 7 months, from 28 December 2022 |
