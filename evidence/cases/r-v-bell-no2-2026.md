A jury found Jacob Bell guilty of murder after a dispute with a neighbour ended in a stabbing.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Planning? Knife brought? | No |
| Intent | Grievous bodily harm, not to kill |
| Subjective factors | Deprived background, mental health, youth |
| Sentence | 20 years 10 months total, non-parole 13; 5% discount; special circumstances |
