The High Court held that the statutory duty in s 37 of the *Design and Building Practitioners Act
2020* (NSW) is **non-delegable**. A developer or head builder is therefore responsible for the work
of those it engages and cannot reduce its liability by apportioning it among subcontractors under
the proportionate liability scheme.

## Use it for

On the **provider** side, the decisive levers sit outside housing law: this is a case about
negligence and statutory construction that determines who bears the cost of defective housing. The
market response — decennial liability insurance — is the practical consequence, and naming it turns
a description of a case into an evaluation of a reform.
