A mother asked to relocate permanently to 'Country B' with her five-year-old daughter, X. The father opposed.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Benefits to the mother real? | Yes |
| Risk to X's relationship with her father? | Yes — underdeveloped |
| Result | X stays in Australia; father's time grows in stages; mother has sole decision-making |

## Use it for

- **fam.2.2** — relocation and the best interests of the child.
- **Values** — a parent's freedom of movement versus the child's relationships.

## Caution

Holidays abroad are allowed; the relocation itself was refused.
