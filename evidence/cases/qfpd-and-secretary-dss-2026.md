A man injured in 2019 received a $400,000 settlement in April 2024. Services Australia imposed a compensation preclusion period from February 2019 to March 2022.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Preclusion period | Calculated under the 50% rule |
| Treat compensation as not made? | Declined |
| Waive the $58,163.44 debt? | Refused |
| Result | Decision affirmed |
