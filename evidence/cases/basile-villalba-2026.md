Two parents could not agree interim arrangements before a July 2027 final hearing.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Living arrangements | Children live with the father; 5:9 with the mother plus holidays |
| Mother's changeover rules, first refusal, make-up time? | Refused |
| Principle | Best interests paramount; equality no substitute for cooperation |
