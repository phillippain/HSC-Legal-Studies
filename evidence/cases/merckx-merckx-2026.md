After nearly 20 years of marriage, a couple arbitrated their property dispute, producing two 'interim awards' and a 'final award' (21 March 2025). The husband asked the Court to register it under s 13H.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Was there a written arbitration agreement meeting reg 67F? | No |
| Was a reg 67G notice given? | No — so it was not family law arbitration |
| The interim awards | Lacked finality |
| Result | Registration refused; costs reserved; the property division is still to be decided |

## Use it for

- **fam.2.5** — arbitration as a non-court method, and the legal footing it needs.
- **Effectiveness** — a long, costly process ended with an award that could not be enforced.

## Caution

Registration only; the property dispute continues.
