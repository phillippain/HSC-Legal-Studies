A father appealed parenting orders keeping his time with two children supervised. The mother listed 25 allegations of family violence, but the primary judge never said which he accepted.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Were the reasons adequate? | No — the judge did not say which allegations he accepted |
| What is family violence under s 4AB(1)? | Behaviour that coerces, controls or causes fear; *Pickford* was plainly wrong |
| Result | Appeal allowed; rehearing before a Division 1 judge; the 19 June 2026 orders stay in force |

## Use it for

- **fam.2.4** — the statutory definition of family violence, interpreted by a five-judge bench.
- **Year 11 Changing the law** — judicial methods: interpreting statutes and overruling an earlier decision (chg.3.3).

## Caution

All family violence claims are allegations; which are true is for the rehearing.
