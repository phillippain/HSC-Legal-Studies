A Nigerian husband, wife and three daughters sought protection visas, fearing harm as Christians (the husband a convert from Islam) and later gender-based violence. The Tribunal affirmed the refusal.

## What the court decided

| Ground | Held |
|---|---|
| The conversion claim was ignored | No — it was dealt with, though against the family |
| Reliance on s 36(2B)(c) | Superfluous; no error |
| Result | No jurisdictional error; dismissed with costs of $9,097.93 (not against the minor) |

## Use it for

- **hr.2.11** — judicial review tests legality, not whether the family is in fact safe.
- **Year 11** — a dispute with the state in a court (dis.3.4).

## Caution

Whether the family would be safe in Nigeria was not decided.
