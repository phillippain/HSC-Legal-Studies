A husband inherited most of a deceased employer's estate, which the wife had not expected.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Approach | Two pools |
| The estate | 85:15 for the husband |
| The rest | 55:45 for the wife, partly because of his controlling conduct after separation |
| Result | She receives $610,157 plus a share of his superannuation |
| Release from the Harman undertaking (to give his admission to police) | Refused |
