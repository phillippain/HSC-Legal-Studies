A student visa applicant did not reach court when his judicial review was called, so it was dismissed. He asked to reinstate it.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Arguable jurisdictional error? | No — 39 not 42; study exemption did not apply |
| Explanations satisfactory? | No |
| Result | Reinstatement refused; $1,200 costs |
