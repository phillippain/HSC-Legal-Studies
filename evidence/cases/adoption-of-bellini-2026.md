Foster carers who had raised two young children since 2023 asked the NSW Supreme Court to adopt them. The Minister held parental responsibility until age 18, the birth mother opposed, and the birth fathers could not be identified. On 29 October 2026 McGrath J held adoption was clearly preferable to the alternatives, dispensed with consent and notice, approved a name change and registered an adoption plan with twelve contact visits a year.

## Use it for

- Parents and children and adoption: an adoption order, consent dispensed with and a registered contact plan.
- Care and protection: the Minister’s parental responsibility to 18, risk reports and the alternatives to adoption.
- Parental responsibility: adoption moves it to the carers and ends the birth parents’ legal role.
- Courts: Children’s Court care orders, a Supreme Court adoptions list and a mediation.
- Effectiveness: permanence and security for the children, set against loss of the birth mother’s legal status.

## Cautions

This is a final, contested decision made on the evidence, not an appeal, with orders in the same terms for each child. Inconsistencies in the file: [115] relies on s 67(1)(c) but the orders use s 67(1)(d); [81] dates a directions hearing 1 April 2025 (should be 2026); the contact list at [61] places 20 June 2026 and 8 August 2026 among 2025 dates; [62] says visits mainly ended early at the birth mother’s claim of illness while the Secretary’s submission at [198] says all did. Names are pseudonyms and some slip. Care line: neglect, family violence, drug use and mental health appear; teach the legal test only.
