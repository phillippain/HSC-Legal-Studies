A Turkish citizen feared harm over anti-government views, Gulen-linked schooling and a family dispute. The Administrative Review Tribunal affirmed the refusal.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Did the Tribunal ignore relevant matters? | No — it considered each claim and the updated report |
| Was the decision legally unreasonable? | No — open on the evidence |
| Result | Amended application dismissed |
