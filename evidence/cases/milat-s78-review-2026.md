**Matthew Milat** was about **17 years 11 months** old at the time of the 2010 offence; his co-offender was 18. He pleaded
guilty in the Children's Court and was sentenced in 2012 to **43 years** (non-parole **30**). The CCA dismissed his appeal
in 2014 (and reduced the co-offender's sentence); the High Court refused special leave. In 2026, self-represented, he applied
under **s 78** for an inquiry or a referral to the CCA.

## Held — refused on the papers

- An order needs a **doubt or question** about guilt, mitigating circumstances or evidence (**s 79(2)**); the test is unease or
  disquiet. **Section 78 is not another appeal.**
- His complaints — parity with the co-offender, the plea discount, drugs, risk, his surname, premeditation, and being
  "treated as an adult" though a child — had largely been dealt with before and raised no such doubt.

## Use it for

- **crime.5.4** — sentencing a child offender as an adult for the most serious crime; CROC's "shortest appropriate time".
- **crime.4.8 / crime.4.5** — the review mechanism after appeals are exhausted.

A **class case summary**. The killing is not described; no birth date is in the file.
