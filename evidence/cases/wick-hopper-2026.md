A family living in regional Queensland separated after a police protection notice and a temporary protection order for the
mother (December 2025). The mother moved to Canberra with the youngest child. On 8 May 2026 a Division 2 judge made interim
orders on the day — "the least worst decision" — placing the children (16, 14 and 10, pseudonyms) in two homes.

## The three grounds

| Ground | Held |
|---|---|
| Were same-day (ex tempore) reasons for splitting the siblings inadequate? | No — adequate for an interim hearing |
| Did the mother's unilateral move change parental responsibility (s 61C), making Canberra a "baseline"? | No — both parents keep parental responsibility after separation and a move |
| Could the judge rely on untested allegations, and did he breach s 102NH? | Risk can be weighed without findings; no breach |

Appeal **dismissed**; the father pays **$4,250** costs. The Court admitted his drug-test results on appeal.

## Caution

The family violence allegations are **untested**, and the primary judge's reasons are not in the file.
