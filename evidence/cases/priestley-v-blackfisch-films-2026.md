**Matthew Priestley** (a director from 2018) and **Mika Tyson** worked with **Blackfisch Films**, run by Ms Rutter, who also
has Gomeroi ancestry. After an argument over office cleanliness (September 2024) the locks were changed, after a plan to divert
a film project was found. The Fair Work Commission found no dismissal (January 2026). The applicants alleged race
discrimination in pay, remarks (including being called "too black") and exclusion.

## Held — claims dismissed (s 102)

- They were **contract workers** (s 10), not employees (s 8), which set the comparators.
- The alleged slurs were **not found**; the "too black" remark was about activism, not race; the lock-out followed the
  project-diversion plan.
- Estoppel from the Fair Work findings was not decided.

## Use it for

- **hr.2.11 / hr.2.9** — enforcing race discrimination law through NCAT, and the importance of proof and categories
  (employee v contractor).

## Caution

A **class case summary**; the slurs are alleged, not found.
