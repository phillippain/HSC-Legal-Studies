Mr Gamage was convicted in the Local Court of three ICAC Act offences and appealed to the District Court. Before that appeal was heard he sought judicial review of two District Court orders dismissing his motions.

## What the court decided

| Argument | Held |
|---|---|
| The charges were invalid | An earlier decision had already upheld them |
| cl 46 | Irrelevant |
| Unfairness and bias | Failed |
| Result | Summons dismissed with costs; the conviction appeal stays adjourned part-heard in the District Court |

## Use it for

- **crime.4.5 / crime.3.1** — appeals from the Local Court, and judicial review for jurisdictional error.
- **Effectiveness** — finality and the limits on re-arguing settled points.

## Caution

Procedural — judicial review of interlocutory orders; guilt is not decided.
