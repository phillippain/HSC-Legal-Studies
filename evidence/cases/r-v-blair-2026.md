On 11 January 2025, in a cell at Clarence Correctional Centre (a privately run maximum-security prison
near Grafton), Blair joined a group attack in which another inmate used a knife and Blair stomped twice
on the victim's head, on CCTV. He pleaded guilty in the Local Court to **reckless wounding in company**
(*Crimes Act* s 35(3): maximum 10 years, SNPP 4 years). He was represented by the Aboriginal Legal
Service.

## The sentence — 21 October 2026

- **Objective seriousness: well above mid-range** — the confined cell, the knife and the head stomps.
  Record of violence and offending in custody aggravated (s 21A(2)(d)).
- **Bugmy not engaged**: a difficult childhood was accepted, but profound deprivation was not proved.
- **De La Rosa**: schizophrenia partly caused the offending (less culpability) but also raised danger to
  the community — "the two cancel out". Psychosis at the time was not proved.
- Indicative 7 years 6 months, less **25%** = **5 years 7 months, non-parole 3 years** (special
  circumstances for longer supervised parole and treatment).
- **s 56** *Crimes (Sentencing Procedure) Act*: a sentence for an offence by an inmate is consecutive
  unless the court directs otherwise. None was made, so it runs from 18 October 2026, when his
  earlier parole eligibility began, to 17 May 2032.
