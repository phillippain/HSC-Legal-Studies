Sef Gonzales, convicted of three murders in 2004, had five failed s 78 applications against his convictions. His sixth, about sentence only, led McNaughton J to refer the 'whole case' to be dealt with as an appeal against sentence. He then sought to list a conviction appeal.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Did the referral allow a conviction appeal? | No — the order's words limit the appeal to sentence |
| The other reading | Would be absurd |
| Result | The Registrar was right to refuse to list a conviction appeal |
