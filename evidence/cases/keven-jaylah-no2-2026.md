X, born 2020. Final orders in 2024 generated three sets of contravention proceedings by 2026. Six
breaches between May and September 2025 — five refusals of time and one missed phone call — all
committed while the mother was **already on a bond** from the first round. Decided entirely on the
papers.

## Held — two 24-month bonds, one with a $4,000 surety

The bonds are served concurrently and conditioned on good behaviour and compliance with any current
or future parenting orders; she must attend the registry to sign them within 30 days. **Make-up
time** in two blocks in the September and December 2026 school holidays. Communication is now by
text about parenting issues only, with changeover at school or a named public restaurant.

Refused: the father's $8,000 surety — **halved to $4,000 because of her mortgage commitments** —
the full 15 days 9 hours 40 minutes of make-up time sought, and his application to remove the
address disclosure order; and every variation the mother sought, for want of evidence.

## Use it for

- **Compliance** — the enforcement ladder in one family: a bond, a finding with no penalty, then
  longer bonds with a surety. The rungs are visible and so is their failure.
- **Effectiveness** — the honest measure: the 2024 bond did not prevent the 2025 breaches, and the
  Court's answer is **proportionality**, weighing the penalty's effect on the child.
- **fam.3.2** — making a child available and facilitating a phone call are enforceable duties of
  equal status.
- **Shelter link** — housing cost shaping a legal penalty: the mortgage is why the surety was
  halved.

## Caution

An apprehended violence order was asserted but never tendered — untested claims cannot ground
findings, even about violence. Decided on the papers, so no evidence was tested orally. Summary
prepared for class from the judgment; quotes checked against the judgment text.
