## Held

- Orders can still be made after a partner's death (s 90SMA(8), s 90SM(2)); the pool is assessed at trial.
- The wife's untested affidavit was admitted (Evidence Act s 67(4)); **family violence was not established**, so no s 90SM(4)(ca) adjustment.
- Contributions **53% / 47%**, then **+15%** for age, health, no superannuation and rehousing: **68% / 32%**. The farm is sold.

## Use it for

- **fam.2.3** — the steps in property division and how a survivor's needs are weighed.

## Caution

The file is inconsistent on the relationship length, the estate's final position and the death date: avoid them. Care: death, illness and violence allegations (not established). Not in file: costs.
