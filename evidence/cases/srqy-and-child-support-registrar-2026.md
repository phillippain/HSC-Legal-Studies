Two high-school boys moved to **boarding school** (2022 and 2024), with fees paid by their **maternal
grandfather**; the parents shared holidays and alternate weekends. The mother asked Services Australia to
change the existing **50/50** care percentages. It decided **77% mother / 23% father**, back-dated to the
start of boarding, creating large debts for the father. A first review produced percentages totalling
under 100% (valuing the grandfather's contribution at 34%). The mother sought a second review.

## Held — decision set aside, 50/50 restored

- A care percentage can be **revoked only if the care actually taking place no longer matches it**
  (s 54F). Counting nights is permissive, not mandatory (s 54A).
- Boarding-school nights were **split equally**: nothing separated the parents' roles and **neither** paid
  the fees, so the *Polec* factors did not favour one parent.
- The earlier objection process had "fundamentally miscarried".
