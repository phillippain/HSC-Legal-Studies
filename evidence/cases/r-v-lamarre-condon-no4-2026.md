The defence wanted the jury told the accused had no history of violence.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Say he has no convictions? | Yes, without raising good character |
| Crown reply witnesses and taser footage? | Not allowed |
| Good character on violence? | No — controlling conduct evidence was unchallenged |
