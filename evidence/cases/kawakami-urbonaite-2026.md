X, 13, and Y, 8. A decade of family violence between the parents, with the **mother identified as
the predominant aggressor** and a Community Corrections Order for breaching intervention orders. In
2023 X alleged sexual abuse by her father; the department investigated and closed the case, and X
later said the allegations were false and that her mother had encouraged them. The mother was
diagnosed with an episode of psychosis in 2024 and returned **thirteen positive drug screens out of
fourteen**. She had filed nothing substantive for over two years, then emailed the Court that she
would not return. The hearing went ahead undefended, with the **Independent Children's Lawyer** as
contradictor, and judgment was delivered ex tempore the same day.

## Held

The children **live with their father**, who has sole decision-making responsibility for all major
long-term issues. Time with their mother: up to **two hours on the first Saturday of every third
month**, supervised at a contact service at her cost, plus calls at the children's wish. She is
restrained from approaching the children, their schools and the father's home; both parents are
restrained from denigration, family violence, physical discipline and posting about the case. The
father may obtain passports without her consent. Everything she had sought in 2023 was refused.

## Use it for

- **fam.3.2** — decision-making responsibility *removed* rather than shared, in the post-2024
  language (the judge notes the family report writer's "sole parental responsibility" is outdated).
- **fam.3.4** — state and federal systems meeting: departmental removal, an interim accommodation
  order, placement by the Children's Court, then s 67ZBE reports feeding the federal case.
- **fam.1.5 / rights of the child** — the children were heard and then outweighed: participation
  (CROC art 12) against protection (art 19), with best interests paramount (art 3) — reaching them
  through ss 60CA and 60CC, not the Convention itself.
- **Effectiveness** — removal, placement and expert reporting worked; a decade of intervention
  orders and a corrections order did not change the parent's behaviour.

An **undefended** hearing: only one side's evidence was tested, and the judge says so. Summary
