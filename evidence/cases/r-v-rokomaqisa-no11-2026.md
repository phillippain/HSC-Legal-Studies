Before the retrial, the defence subpoenaed the DPP's files about Witness A.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Conference notes and emails | Client legal privilege (s 119) |
| Limited disclosures | Did not waive privilege (s 122) |
| Other material | Could not bear on any fact in issue |
| Result | The Director excused from producing them |
