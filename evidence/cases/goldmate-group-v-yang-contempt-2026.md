Freezing orders stopped Yuchen Yang from dealing with assets worth up to **$2,516,162**.

## What the court decided

| Charge | Held |
|---|---|
| Charges 2 and 3: directing $22,000 and $5,500 of borrowed money to liquidators (about 4 June 2026) | Proved beyond reasonable doubt |
| Charge 4: documents | Dismissed — a notice to produce is not a court order |
| Penalty and costs | Reserved |
