## Facts

**Justin Soyke**, 18 at the offending (November 2013 to May 2014), pleaded guilty to four offences under ss 477.1 and 477.2 of the *Criminal Code* (Cth) and asked for **17 attempts** under s 478.1 to be taken into account under s 16BA. He hacked an ISP hosting 129 websites, an ACT Government account and a Telstra server (using Heartbleed), working with Australian and Danish associates; the attempts included NASA, NATO, UN and US Department of Defense servers. Culver DCJ gave **3 years**, released after 12 months (15 October 2015).

## Held — appeal dismissed

- Two expert reports were **not admitted**: one was available but unused (new evidence) and the other could have been obtained with reasonable diligence.
- Serious offending was well open on risk alone, and general deterrence matters for computer offences.
- A sentence of 3 years was not manifestly excessive, with the s 16BA offences and an earlier bond.

## Use it for

- **crime.6.1**: cybercrime, with an international dimension, under Commonwealth law.
- **crime.4.2**: general deterrence as a purpose of punishment.

## Caution

A **sentence appeal**: the foreign servers appear only as attempts, and the file says nothing about extradition or foreign cooperation. Arrest and plea dates and later history are not in the file.
