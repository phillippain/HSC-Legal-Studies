Reginald Woolmington, a 21-year-old farm labourer, was charged with murdering his 17-year-old wife
Violet, who had left him three weeks earlier and was shot at her mother's house on 10 December 1934.
He said the gun went off by accident while he was threatening to shoot himself. The first jury could
not agree. At the second trial the judge told the jury that once the killing was proved **the law
presumed murder unless the prisoner satisfied them it was an accident**. He was convicted and
sentenced to death; the Court of Criminal Appeal applied the proviso and dismissed his appeal.

## Held — Viscount Sankey LC, for a unanimous House of Lords

- "Throughout the web of the English Criminal Law one golden thread is always to be seen, that it is
  the duty of the prosecution to prove the prisoner's guilt."
- The prosecution must prove **every element**, including intent, **beyond reasonable doubt**; if the
  accused's evidence raises a reasonable doubt, he is entitled to be acquitted even if the jury does
  not believe his explanation.
- Only two exceptions: **insanity** (where the onus is "definitely and exceptionally" on the accused —
  *M'Naghten*, and in Australia *R v Porter*) and **statutory** reversals Parliament writes in.
- The proviso was refused: the Lords could not say a properly directed jury would inevitably have
  convicted. The conviction was quashed.

## Use it for

- **crime.3.6** — the burden and standard of proof, and the presumption of innocence (ICCPR art
  14(2)); NSW now writes the standard into *Evidence Act 1995* s 141.
- **The exceptions** — pair with *Porter* (insanity: the accused proves it on the balance of
  probabilities) and *Zecevic* / *Rowan* (once a defence is raised, the Crown must *disprove* it).
- **crime.4.5 (appeals)** — a misdirection on who bears the burden is itself a miscarriage of justice.

## Caution

An English decision, adopted throughout Australian law rather than binding here. The outcome after
the House of Lords quashed the conviction is not in the report. The library summary was built from
the report itself.
