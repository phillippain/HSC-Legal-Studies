Reginald Woolmington was charged with murdering his wife, who was shot. The trial judge told the jury that, once the killing was proved, it was for the accused to show it was an accident. The House of Lords quashed the conviction. Viscount Sankey: "Throughout the web of the English Criminal Law one golden thread is always to be seen, that it is the duty of the prosecution to prove the prisoner's guilt."

## Use it for

The burden of proof (crime.3.6) and the presumption of innocence (ICCPR art 14(2)); NSW codifies the standard in Evidence Act s 141.
