A jury convicted Mr Kinnaird of three Commonwealth offences of using a carriage service for child abuse material, in messages with a co-offender. He received an aggregate 5 years 10 months, non-parole 3 years 6 months.

## What the court decided

| Ground | Held |
|---|---|
| Disparity with the co-offender | No unjustified disparity |
| General deterrence misstated | No |
| Objective seriousness | Open |
| Manifest excess | No — leave granted, appeal dismissed |

## Use it for

- **crime.4.2** — general deterrence for offences that feed demand for abuse material.
- **crime.4.3** — parity between co-offenders and the aggregate sentence.

## Caution

Whether an indicative sentence can be appealed on its own was not decided.
