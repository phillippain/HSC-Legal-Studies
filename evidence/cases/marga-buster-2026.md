A mother stopped a nine-year-old child’s court-ordered time with the father from November 2022, and the child came to believe in abuse allegations the mother later abandoned in written submissions. On 17 September 2026 Brasch J found no abuse, found three acts of family violence by the father against the mother, held the mother an unacceptable risk of emotional harm, and made final orders: the child lives with the father after a 12-week moratorium on time with the mother.

## Use it for

- Compliance and non-compliance: the mother stopped court-ordered time and did not follow the August 2023 and August 2025 orders.
- Separation and children: a final decision on where a child lives and how time is shared after parents separate.
- Parental responsibility: sole decision-making given to the father because the parents could not co-parent.
- Domestic violence: three acts of family violence by the father found; s 60CG and an injunction under s 68B.
- Courts and dispute resolution: an Independent Children’s Lawyer, Family Reports, re-unification therapy and a contact centre.
- Effectiveness: non-legal supports and earlier orders failed to restart time over almost four years.

## Cautions

This is a final parenting decision after a contested hearing, not an interim one. Care line: it concerns abuse allegations that were not proved, family violence and a parent-child relationship the court found unhealthy; teach the legal test only. The header gives the hearing as 4–6 August 2025 and 2026 dates, yet [47] says the trial stopped on 6 August 2025 for consent orders. [15] says the ICL and father both proposed a three-month moratorium, but [225] says the father proposed six weeks. The header lists s 68R, while the orders rely on ss 68B and 68C. Labels are doubled; the second number is real. Later events are not in the file.
