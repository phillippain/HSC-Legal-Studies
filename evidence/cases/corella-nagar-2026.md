A man now 67 claimed a de facto relationship of 25 years — June 1998 to July 2023 — with a woman now
85, and a property adjustment. She said he was a friend. They met while she was still married to
and living with her unwell husband; a casual sexual relationship followed. In 2001 her solicitors
drafted a financial agreement (under both the *Property (Relationships) Act 1984* (NSW) and s 90B of
the *Family Law Act 1975*); he refused to sign it. Across twenty years she bought and sold three
homes in her own name without consulting him, and from 2011 to 2017 she was in a relationship with
someone else.

## Held — declaration that there was never a de facto relationship

Judge Colquhoun tested all nine **s 4AA(2)** circumstances and found almost every one against him:
no common residence at any of five addresses, "we do our financials separately", nothing owned
jointly, never registered, no witnesses, and a commitment that was affectionate "at certain times"
but not a shared life. A declaration under **s 90RD** that the parties have never been in a de facto
relationship; his application dismissed — and with it any property order under **s 90SM**.

- *Fairbairn v Radecki* (2022) 275 CLR 400: "living together" means **sharing life as a couple**;
  a common residence is not essential.
- *Mayson & Wellard* (2021): where both sides seek a declaration, **each carries its own burden**.
- His failure to call "a single witness" was "especially influential" [167]. Mail sent to her
  addresses was "insufficient evidence" that they lived together [121].

## Use it for

- **fam.1.4** — the boundary of an alternative relationship. The law recognises couples who never
  share a roof (*Fairbairn*), but not an "intermittent friendship, which was intimate and romantic
  at times". Pair with *Duff & Horner*, where the same gateway opened.
- **fam.2.3** — de facto property rights on separation exist only once the relationship is proved;
  the declaration is the whole case.
- **Effectiveness** — the process protected an older woman's assets from an unfounded claim, with
  evidence by video link while she was being treated for cancer. It took nearly two years from
  filing and a two-day trial.
- **Cooperation** — the unsigned 2001 agreement: a binding financial agreement exists to prevent
  exactly this litigation.

## Caution

A decision on its facts, largely on **credit** — he was "evasive" and "inconsistent", she "direct,
logical and candid". It does not narrow the definition of de facto; it applies it. Summary prepared
for class from the judgment, with quotes checked against the judgment text.
