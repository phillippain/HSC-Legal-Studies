After a relationship of over 26 years, a wife on a disability support pension asked for $250,000 for legal costs, spousal maintenance and valuations of a family farm run through two trusts. On 26 August 2026 McNab J, at an interim stage, ordered a $150,000 part property payment, $500 a week in maintenance (rising to $1,500 if she leaves), joint valuations and mediation, and left the trusts for the final hearing.

## Use it for

- Divorce and separation: interim property payment and spousal maintenance under ss 72, 75(2) and 79.
- Courts and dispute resolution: an interim hearing, joint single experts and ordered mediation with written offers.
- Cooperation and conflict resolution: both sides took extreme positions; the court pushed them to mediate.
- Effectiveness: an interest-bearing lender debt and costs the court says will significantly impact on each party.

## Cautions

This is an interim (interlocutory) decision, not a final property settlement: the judge made no finding about the trusts and said there was little evidence they were a sham. Order 9 sets mediation ‘not later than 30 October 2025’, before the judgment date, so the date looks wrong. The orders are numbered unevenly; para 80 and order 8(b) word the maintenance step slightly differently; para 78 says ‘father’ where it means the husband; the husband’s affidavit is dated 3 May at [26] but 8 May 2026 at [6]; the figures at [66] are muddled. Care line: disability, chronic pain and mental health; keep to the legal test.
