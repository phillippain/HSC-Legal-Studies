A Sri Lankan woman in Australia since 2008 applied for protection in 2018. A private video made in 2005 was released without her consent in 2007.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Abuse and mental health diagnoses? | Accepted |
| Would she still be recognised and harmed? | Not after about 19 years |
| Four trips back and long delay | Undercut her fear |
| Result | Refusal affirmed |
