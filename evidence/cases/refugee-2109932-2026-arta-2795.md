An elderly Indian couple, with all three children in Australia, applied for protection in 2019. Too unwell to take part, they had the review decided on the papers.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| 2014 home attack? | Accepted |
| Real chance of future harm? | No — long returns to India |
| Hardship without family significant harm? | No — not intentionally inflicted |
| Result | Refusals affirmed |
