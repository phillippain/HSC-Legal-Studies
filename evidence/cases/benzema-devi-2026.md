After a relationship of about 20 years, the father ignored directions and filed nothing for trial. Jarrett J decided parenting and property on the mother's unchallenged evidence and the family reports.

## What the court decided

| Issue | Order |
|---|---|
| Family violence | Coercive control and post-separation surveillance found; unacceptable risk of emotional harm to three children |
| Parenting | Mother has sole decision-making; children live with her; father has no time — only letters, presents and calls a child asks for |
| Communication | AppClose only; mother keeps travel documents |
| Property | Adjusted 67.5/32.5 to the mother for non-disclosure; father transfers a car and pays $347,532.90 |

## Use it for

- **fam.2.4** — coercive control recognised as family violence (s 4AB), with s 68B restraints.
- **fam.2.3** — non-disclosure in property proceedings.

## Caution

Decided on one side's unchallenged evidence after a default; names are pseudonyms.
