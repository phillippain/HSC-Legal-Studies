Four children died between 1989 and 1999. Folbigg was arrested in April 2001 and in 2003 a jury
convicted her of the manslaughter of Caleb, grievous bodily harm to Patrick, and the murders of
Patrick, Sarah and Laura. Sentence: 40 years (non-parole 30), reduced on appeal in 2005 to **30 years
(non-parole 25)**. An appeal on juror misconduct failed (2007).

## Correction

- **2019** inquiry: no reasonable doubt.
- **2021** petition by scientists; second inquiry (Tom Bathurst KC): reasonable doubt, based on a
  CALM2 genetic variant and a different reading of the diaries.
- **5 June 2023** pardoned and released after about 20 years; **14 December 2023** the Court of
  Criminal Appeal quashed all convictions.
- **August 2025** $2 million ex gratia payment, criticised as inadequate. NSW has no statutory right
  to compensation (contrast ICCPR art 14(6)).

## Use it for

- **Juries and evidence** — circumstantial evidence and expert evidence limited by the science of the time.
- **Appeals** (crime.4.5) — appeals consider only the trial evidence; Part 7 inquiries reached what appeals could not.
- **Effectiveness** of the trial process: the system corrected itself, but slowly.
