Mr Dut was sentenced to 3 years 4 months for supplying a large commercial quantity of methylamphetamine and 6 months for supplying cocaine. The judge backdated the start by three months to credit refused bail, strict bail conditions and a residential detox.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Is quasi-custody a finding of fact? | Yes |
| The weight given to it | Discretionary; no error shown |
| Result | Leave granted; appeal dismissed; start date 20 December 2025 stands |

- **crime.4.5** — appeals against the exercise of discretion.
