A child born in Cambodia in June 2024 applied for Australian citizenship by descent through his registered mother, an Australian citizen aged 51 who said she gave birth after free IVF with donor eggs and sperm. A delegate refused.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Birth and antenatal records | Unreliable |
| Evidence of IVF | None beyond her account |
| Photographs | Unverifiable |
| Result | Not satisfied she was his parent at birth |
