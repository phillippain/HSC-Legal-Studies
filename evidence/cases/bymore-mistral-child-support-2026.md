A father challenged Child Support's decision on how much care he gives his young son (recorded as 72% mother, 28% father).

| Question | Finding |
|---|---|
| Actual care since 5 February 2025 | Most likely **62% mother, 38% father** |
| Evidence | No care calendars; the parents' evidence conflicted |
| Date of effect | Affirmed as **23 July 2025** — his objection was late and he gave no evidence of special circumstances (s 87AA) |
| A claimed 2024 change of care | Not considered — no objection decision covered it |
