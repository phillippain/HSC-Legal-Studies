The parties married in 1986 and emigrated in 1992. The husband filed for property orders in February 2025 seeking **55%**
of the net property; the wife had dropped her own claim. After interim orders (a $50,000 partial settlement and a
disclosure deadline) he missed the trial date, filed his affidavit and financial statement late, and made an unsuccessful
recusal application.

## Held — application dismissed

- The husband breached his duty of **full and frank disclosure**: his overseas company interests and bank accounts were
  never identified or valued.
- Without that, the court could not find it **just and equitable** to alter property interests; the husband bore the
  **onus** and had not shown the facts for the orders he sought. The wife held nearly all the known assets.
- Costs were reserved.
