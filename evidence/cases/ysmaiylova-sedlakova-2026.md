A stepfather and the child's mother asked for leave under s 60G(1) to start adoption proceedings for the mother's 13-year-old son, X. The biological father supported the application on oath.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Is leave merely procedural? | No — adoption alters legal relationships |
| The child's wishes | Given substantial weight |
| Result | Leave granted to commence proceedings under the Adoption Act 2009 (Qld) |
| Not decided | The adoption itself — a Queensland court decides it |
