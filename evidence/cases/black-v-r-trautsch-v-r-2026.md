On 22 January 2023 two on-duty NSW police officers attended a woman in distress; they assaulted her and used OC spray, and
Black also sent protected video. Both pleaded guilty (10% discount) to common assault and unauthorised use of a prohibited
weapon. The District Court sentenced Black to **5 years 9 months** and Trautsch to **5 years 6 months** (8 August 2025).

## The three questions

| Issue | Held |
|---|---|
| Can an assault be aggravated by **gratuitous cruelty** (s 21A(2)(f))? | Not the assaults here; the factor needs pain as an end in itself |
| Was it an error to find both **abuse of trust and abuse of authority** (s 21A(2)(k))? | Yes — counting both without explanation risked double weight |
| What sentences on resentencing? | Black **3 years 2 months** (non-parole 2 years 1 month); Trautsch **3 years** (non-parole 2 years) |

Sentencing is an "instinctive synthesis" (*Markarian*); the appeal court corrects errors in how discretion is exercised.
