AB sued his former partner and a news company over an article said to reveal data from his laptop. When she ignored court orders he began contempt proceedings and served a notice to produce on the media defendants.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Abuse of process? | No |
| Journalist privilege (s 126K) | Privileged parts can be redacted |
| Result | Media defendants' motion dismissed with costs |
