The parents were not *ad idem* on any donor agreement, and in any event "the court is not bound by a
private contract between parents about the care of a child".

Decided on **CROC arts 7, 8 and 9** through s 60B: waiting until the child turns 18 to learn his
identity would mean "he is already an adult". Twelve supervised sessions, graduated time to
alternate weekends at 9, and registration on the birth certificate under ss 18 and 20 of the
*Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration Act 1995* (NSW).

## Use it for

- **Rights and obligations of parents and children**, and informal donor conception under
  **birth technologies**.
- Beyond Family: **Human Rights** — the best example in the project of **CROC incorporated and doing
  real work** in an Australian court, rather than being cited decoratively. When a question asks how
  international law is enforced domestically, this is the answer with a judgment attached.
- Law reform the judgment itself invites at [119]: extending clinical governance, written agreements
  and independent advice to known-donor conception.
