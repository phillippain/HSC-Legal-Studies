WE was convicted in 2020, at his fourth trial, of jointly planning a terrorist act. On day 21 he withdrew his instructions and represented himself. Later reports diagnosed schizophrenia present during the trial; the Crown conceded.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Was there a real and substantial question about fitness? | Yes (the RTI test) |
| Result | Leave granted to reinstate the appeal and appeal out of time; conviction quashed |
| Retrial | Div 6 fitness questions addressed first; mention on 11 December 2026 |
| Not decided | That WE was in fact unfit; the charge stands |

The trial judge was not criticised.

- **crime.3.5** — self-representation, and the moral standard that a person who may be unfit should not be tried.

## Caution

WE is identified by initials; fitness is still to be determined.
