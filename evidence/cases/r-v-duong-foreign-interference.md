## Facts

In 2020 **Di Sanh Duong**, a Melbourne community figure, organised a donation to a Melbourne
hospital during the COVID-19 pandemic and sought to involve the then federal minister **Alan Tudge**.
The prosecution case was that he was preparing to influence the minister for the benefit of the
**Chinese Communist Party**. He denied it.

## Outcome

- **19 December 2023**: a County Court of Victoria jury convicted him of **preparing for or planning a
  foreign interference offence** (Criminal Code s 92.4(1)).
- **29 February 2024**: sentenced to **2 years and 9 months**, to be released on a recognisance
  release order after 12 months.

## The law

The *National Security Legislation Amendment (Espionage and Foreign Interference) Act 2018* created
Division 92 offences for covert or deceptive conduct on behalf of a foreign principal. Other offences
against the sovereign: treason (s 80.1), urging violence (s 80.2, which replaced sedition in 2010)
and espionage (Div 91).

## Use it for

- **Offences against the sovereign** (`crime.1.5`): a rare, recent prosecution.
- **Preliminary offences**: a conviction for *preparing*, before any influence was exerted.
- **Political factors** (`crime.1.8`) and **rights**: freedom of political communication and
