## Held

- Murder with intent to kill formed during an unplanned attack; **no provocation**; not a typical domestic homicide.
- Moral culpability **reduced principally by mental illness**; general deterrence given less weight; custody more onerous for him.
- **25% plea** and **about 5% assistance** (Ellis-style, s 23): 19 years becomes **13 years 4 months** from 22 November 2024; non-parole period 10 years.
