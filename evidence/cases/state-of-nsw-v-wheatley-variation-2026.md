A young man was subject to an interim supervision order under the *Terrorism (High Risk Offenders) Act
2017* (NSW), made on 27 May 2026 and suspended while he was in custody. Facing possible extradition to
Queensland on unrelated charges, he was the subject of a State motion to vary the conditions.

## Held — Emmett J (22 October 2026)

- "A supervision order made under the THRO Act continues to bind an offender while that offender is
  outside the territory of NSW", subject to its terms. His Honour decided only what was necessary.
- Most conditions are suspended while he is lawfully in Queensland so that he is not put in involuntary
  breach; supervision, interviews, information sharing and place and travel conditions remain.
- The State's condition 20C (seek a transfer of any Queensland parole or sentence to NSW) was **refused**.

## Use it for

- **crime.4.8** — post-sentencing considerations: supervising a high risk offender in the community.
- **Balancing community safety and individual rights** — avoiding conflicting obligations.

## Caution

A variation ruling, not a sentence or a finding on risk. The holding on territorial reach is limited
because argument was limited, and officers' powers in another State are left open. The *Hudson*
citation is inconsistent in the file. Allegations are untested; keep to the legal test.
