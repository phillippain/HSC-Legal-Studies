## Held

- The overseas case raised the same controversy as unappealed 2025 orders: a challenge 'by stealth', vexatious and oppressive (*CSR v Cigna*; *Agau & Coemans*).
- A good-faith purchaser was prejudiced and the husband delayed a year.
- Interim injunction until further order; undertaking as to damages reserved; back before the Court on 31 August 2026.

## Use it for

- **fam.2.3 / fam.2.5** — enforcing property orders across borders and non-compliance.

## Caution

Interim and ex parte: the judge said her mind remains open. Orders and reasons conflict in places (order 6(b), order 7); follow the reasons. Care: the husband's illness; countries and towns are anonymised.
