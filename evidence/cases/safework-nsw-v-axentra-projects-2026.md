A formwork subcontractor pleaded guilty under s 32 of the *Work Health and Safety Act 2011* (NSW) after a
16-year-old trainee fell about 3.4 metres through a formwork deck on 19 May 2022. The maximum penalty
at the time was $1,782,579.

## Held — Strathdee DCJ

- The risk of falls through formwork decks was "well known, and as such, foreseeable"; the controls were
  relatively straightforward, and the failures were "manifold and systemic".
- The fine was set at **$100,000**, reduced by **25%** for the plea to **$75,000**. No deduction for
  capacity to pay.
- Half the fine goes to the prosecutor, the company pays costs, and a **public notice** (s 236) is
  published in the Sunday Sydney Morning Herald.

## Caution

The judgment cites s 21A(2)(g) and s 21A(2)(ib) for the same finding, and the notice size and cost text
is garbled. The amount of costs is not in the file. A child worker was injured; keep to the legal test.
