## Held

- A review of a Senior Judicial Registrar's interim orders is **de novo**; untested evidence is weighed by what is undisputed.
- Conduct 'constitutes coercion or control'; the risk is **not unacceptable**, so indefinite supervised time is not warranted.
- From 20 September 2026: three hours each week; weekly call; App Close; three courses; s 68B injunctions, a 100 metre restraint and a s 68C arrest declaration.

## Use it for

- **fam.2.4 / fam.3.2** — coercive control and interim parenting decisions.

## Caution

Not a finding that the father is safe. The file has errors (the child's birth year in para 1, the call day, a later-dated order). Care: family violence and a young child.
