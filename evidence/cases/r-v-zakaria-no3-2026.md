Masood Zakaria applied for a stay of proceedings about his removal to Australia. The Crown and accused argued the Commonwealth had waived privilege over legal advice.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Was privilege waived? | No — neither witness disclosed the advice |
| Assistant Commissioner's answers | Excluded as unfairly prejudicial |
| Status | A pre-trial ruling; the stay is undecided |
