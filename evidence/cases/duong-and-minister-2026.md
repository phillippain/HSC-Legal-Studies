A man from Vietnam, in Australia since 2002, had his resident return visa mandatorily cancelled after cannabis cultivation (three years) and attempted possession of methylamphetamine with intent to supply (five years six months).

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Character test | Failed |
| Family and children | Weighed but outweighed |
| Community protection | Heavy weight |
| Result | Refusal to revoke affirmed |
