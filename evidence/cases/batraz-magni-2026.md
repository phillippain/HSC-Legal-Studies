A father and mother disputed final parenting orders for their nine-year-old son, who had not seen his father since March 2024. On 16 September 2026 Jarrett J ordered that the child live with the mother, that she have sole parental responsibility and that he spend no time and have no communication with his father. The risk came from a deliberate pattern of threats and accusations, not one incident. Protective injunctions and a harmful proceedings order were also made.

## Use it for

- Parental responsibility: sole responsibility ordered because joint decision-making under s 61DAA(1) could not work.
- Separation and children: final orders on where the child lives and whether any time is safe, under s 60CC(2).
- Compliance: protection orders were contravened and an interim order was breached, so the court added injunctions.
- Domestic violence: protection orders, s 4AB family violence and a s 68B injunction for the mother and child.
- Courts and dispute resolution: a family consultant, a contact service, an Independent Children’s Lawyer and a harmful proceedings order.
- Effectiveness: parenting programs and supervised time (non-legal measures) did not change the conduct.

## Cautions

This is a final first-instance decision; the trial began before a Division 2 judge who recused himself. Para 23 says four contraventions of the protection order but names three dates. Para 143 describes wider injunctions (to age 18), and para 147 requires 28 days’ travel notice, but the orders as made say neither; para 147 also cites the ‘Australian Passports Act’ where the orders cite the Passports Act 2005. Care line: family violence, mental health and a child’s fear; keep to the legal test.
