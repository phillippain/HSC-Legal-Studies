GQN18, a Sri Lankan Tamil man who arrived by boat in 2013, was refused a Safe Haven Enterprise Visa. The Immigration Assessment Authority affirmed in 2018, and a lower court dismissed his review in 2025.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| He did not appear | The Court decided the appeal on the merits |
| Grounds | Unparticularised; no error shown |
| Result | Appeal dismissed with costs |
