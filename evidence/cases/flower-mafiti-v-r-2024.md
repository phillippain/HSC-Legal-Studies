## Facts

Two former Qantas baggage-handling friends pleaded guilty to **importing about 228 kg of pure cocaine in 12 shipments** from South Africa (2016-2019) and dealing with proceeds of crime. In the District Court (11 February 2022) Flower got **28 years (non-parole 17)** and Mafiti **23 years (14)**.

## Held — unanimous, appeals allowed

The sentencing judge required "exceptional" hardship to a family under *Crimes Act 1914* (Cth) **s 16A(2)(p)**, a gloss rejected in *Totaan*. Resentenced: **Flower 21 years 6 months (non-parole 13)**, **Mafiti 16 years (9 years 6 months)**, with general deterrence and parity stressed. Remaining grounds were not decided.
