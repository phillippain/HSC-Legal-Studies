Risk found **significant but not unacceptable**, so: sole decision-making to the mother, a
three-stage graduated time order (1 → 2 → 3 nights, tied to X turning 8 then 10) and a s 68B
injunction over **both paternal grandparents**.

An injunction against "fear based beliefs and practices" was **refused as insufficiently precise** —
a useful illustration that an order must be capable of enforcement to be made at all.

## Use it for

- **Care and protection of children**; **dealing with domestic violence** where the violence is
  religious and psychological rather than physical.
- Beyond Family: **Human Rights** — **ICCPR art 18** (freedom of religion) against **CROC art 19**
  (protection from harm), and the enforceability of an order that tries to regulate belief.
- The distinction between *significant* and *unacceptable* risk is the hinge of the judgment and is
  worth quoting precisely.
