Mr Chernyi complained that NSW Police discriminated against him on the ground of race in responding to emergency calls and an interpreter request in September 2025. The Anti-Discrimination Board declined the complaint as lacking substance.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Is leave needed under s 96(1)? | Yes — the complaint was declined |
| A comparator and link to race? | None shown |
| Result | Leave refused |
