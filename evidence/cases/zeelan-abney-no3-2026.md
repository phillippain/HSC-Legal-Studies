A casual relationship from September 2016; she moved into his apartment in **November 2017** (the
start of the de facto relationship); an assault on 4 November 2017 (her account preferred though
never reported); a daughter in 2018; separation after an altercation on **19 April 2019** — about
18 months. A provisional ADVO in 2021 and his conviction for contravening it; spousal maintenance of
$747 a week for six months in 2023; final parenting orders in 2024. A two-day hearing in August 2026
"almost entirely about credibility".

## Held — 9 September 2026

- **The pool**: about **$12,547,825**, almost all built by him before they met. The Suburb LL house is
  his — no writing for a trust (*Conveyancing Act 1919* s 23C) and no joint endeavour with his mother
  (*Muschinski v Dodds*).
- **The liabilities**: four claimed loans totalling **$6,825,703** are **not legal debts** —
  undocumented, statute-barred (*Limitation Act 1969* s 14) or presumed social (*Pearce & Pearce
  (No 3)*). They still count as **moral obligations** he will honour, weighed at the discretionary
  stage (*Halstron & Halstron*).
- **Contributions**: "The assets on the balance sheet are the product of the significant initial
  financial contribution of the husband" (at [64]); she brought no assets and did the full-time
  care.
- **Family violence**: found on both sides, his "more significant" — but no clear link to her
  capacity to contribute or her future needs, so **s 90SM(4)(ca) and (5)(a) produced no measurable
  adjustment**. "The wife's failure to report the incident does not mean it did not occur" (at [84]).
- **No percentage at all**: a holistic lump sum of **$600,000** — $400,000 in 60 days, $200,000 in
  120 — secured by a power of sale over the house on default, a registrar's signing power and a
  restraint on dealing. She asked for 25% (about $3.1m). Refused: spousal maintenance (she has
  earning capacity) and a caveat — a s 90SM order "does not create an interest in land" (at [145]).

## Use it for

- **fam.2.3 (property)** — the numbers are the argument: $12.5m seen, $6.8m of unenforceable
  obligations weighed anyway, $600,000 ordered, 18 months together. Under five per cent of the pool,
  seven years after separation.
- **fam.1.4 (alternative relationships)** — de facto recognition in action: the Court had to fix when
  the relationship began and ended before any property power existed; a de facto partner then
  reached the same statutory power as a spouse (s 90SM).
- **fam.2.4 / fam.t.reform** — family violence is now a **mandatory** consideration in property cases
  (the 2024 amendments), and this is what happens when the causal link cannot be proved: found as a
  fact, changing no figure.
- **Compliance by design** — sale, a registrar's signature and a restraint written into the orders
  before any default; set beside *Charis* for what happens when a party simply will not pay.
- **shel.2.1 / shel.1.1** — a mother living in her son's house with nothing in writing; a home used as
  security for a family law debt.
