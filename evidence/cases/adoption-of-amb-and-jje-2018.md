Carmen is the birth mother of A and J, who had lived with Mr and Mrs Fam as full-time carers since
**2015**. The evidence was of drug use, post-natal depression, and domestic violence from a past and
a present partner, with **no capacity to protect the children or to separate from the perpetrators**.
Caseworkers considered the children at risk of **emotional harm** if restored to their mother. The
carers had the capacity and desire to provide a stable home, and the children had **thrived** with
them. Carmen did **not consent** to the adoption.

## Held

The adoption was granted. Under the ***Adoption Act 2000* (NSW)** a court may **dispense with
consent** where adoption is in the **best interests of the child**. The judge also approved the change
of surname, as creating **uniformity** and helping to stabilise the family.

## Use it for

- **Adoption** (`fam.1.6`) — the mechanism, not just the concept. Consent is the norm; dispensing with
  it is the exception, and "best interests" is the test that governs both.
- **Adoption out of out-of-home care**, which is the live policy debate in NSW: permanency and
  stability for the child against the permanent severing of a legal relationship with a parent whose
  circumstances might change. Read it beside the **Family Is Culture** material, where the same
  argument runs the other way for Aboriginal children.
- **Identity** — the surname decision is a small point that shows the court weighing belonging in the
  new family against continuity with the old.
