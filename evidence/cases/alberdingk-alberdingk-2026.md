The parents lived together from 2015 and separated in **January 2024**; sons **X born 2017** and
**Y born 2019**. The children live with the mother in regional NSW. The father is a tradesman who
**worked away two weeks in three**. After two work cycles the mother **withheld the children**,
alleging he was an **unacceptable risk of harm**.

She sought **sole parental responsibility** and only "**identification time**" — **four supervised
visits a year**. He sought shared decision-making and a **graduated increase to five nights a
fortnight**.

The issues: allegations of **sexual, physical, verbal and psychological abuse and coercive control**;
**risk from firearms**, through the children's exposure to **recreational shooting and hunting**;
whether the children benefit from time with their father; and each parent's **parenting capacity**.

## Held — the allegations were not made out

Mansfield J found the mother's case of **unacceptable risk was not made out**.

- **Parental responsibility** — **neither parent loses it**. Major long-term decisions are **joint**,
  **except health and education**, which the **mother decides after consulting** the father.
- **Living and time** — the children continue to live with the mother and spend meaningful,
  **unsupervised** time with the father, **graduated up to five nights a fortnight**.
- **Firearms** — exposure to firearms is declared a **major long-term issue**, and an **injunction**
  bars the father from exposing the children to firearms **without joint agreement**.
- **Best interests** — identification time alone would cause "**loss, grief, long-standing
  psychological harm**" and was not in the children's best interests.

## Use it for

- **fam.2.2 — the case that balances the library.** Almost every other parenting record here involves
  a parent found to be a risk: *Melzer & Powles* (time severed), *Escarro & Kopp* (restraints),
  *Hemswold* (supervision). This one is the opposite — **allegations not made out, and unsupervised
  time ordered**. Without it, an answer reads as though allegations are always accepted, and they are
  not.
- **"Unacceptable risk" is the standard, and it has to be proved.** Pair it with *Morse & Duarte* and
  with *Taggart & Gowden* on *Isles & Nelissen*: risk assessment is predictive and separate from
  fact-finding, **but somebody still has to establish the factual foundation**. Here the mother did
  not, and the consequence was unsupervised time.
- **The firearms injunction is the most teachable order (fam.2.5)** — rather than restricting time,
  the Court restricted **one activity**, declared it a **major long-term issue** so it cannot be
  decided unilaterally, and backed it with an **injunction**. Tailored, checkable and proportionate.
  Compare the 200-metre exclusion and 12-hour sobriety condition in *Escarro & Kopp*.
- **"Identification time" is a term worth knowing** — four supervised visits a year, enough for a
  child to know who their parent is and no more. The Court's rejection of it, in the language of
  "**loss, grief, long-standing psychological harm**", is the clearest statement in the library that
  **the benefit of a relationship is itself a protected interest** under s 60CC.
- **The post-2023 allocation (fam.3.2)** — no presumption of equal shared parental responsibility;
  joint decision-making **except** health and education, where the mother decides **after
  consulting**. Line it up with *Cerinza & Moreau* (sole on schooling), *Hemswold* (28-day notice) and
  *Sullivan & Warren* (7/14 days): four different splits, same Act.
- **Withholding children before a hearing (fam.2.5)** — she withheld them on a belief of risk that
  was later not made out. A hard, honest question: what should a parent who genuinely fears for their
  children do, and what does the law expect of them in the meantime?
- **Work patterns and parenting** — two weeks away in three. Useful and unglamorous: parenting orders
  have to fit a roster.

## Caution

**Pseudonyms**; Pt XIVB applies. The library document is a **case study card** with a syllabus-linking
section, not the judgment — **verify the quoted phrases and the orders against the decision**, and
cite the judgment. A **Division 2, first-instance** decision on its own facts.

Handle the finding carefully. "**Unacceptable risk not made out**" means the **evidence did not
establish** the allegations to the required standard; it is **not** a finding that the mother lied or
that nothing happened, and the card does not record what evidence was led or what the Court made of
her credit. Do not write it up as a vindication or as a false-allegation case — that framing is
exactly what the Family Law Council's report on **disincentives to disclosing family violence**
identifies as the fear that silences victims, and it would be unsafe to teach from a one-page
summary. The allegations included **sexual abuse**; flag the content before use and keep to the legal
standard. The orders are **graduated**, so the father's time increases over time and the final
position is not the position on day one.
