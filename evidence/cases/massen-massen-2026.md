The couple (both 41, pseudonyms, three children) married in 2009 and separated in early 2024. Net assets of
**$56,170,382** came almost entirely from the husband's parents: after the family business sold for $250m, **$23,000,000**
was paid into a trust in January 2019. Final orders (26 May 2026) gave the husband 67% and the wife 33%, and vested a
property in the wife (Order 22(a)).

## The three questions

| Issue | Held |
|---|---|
| Were the reasons for treating the $23m as both parties' contribution adequate, given earlier findings? | No — "opaque"; parties must be able to see why |
| Was the finding open on the evidence? | Not decided separately once the reasons failed |
| Rehear, or decide on appeal? | Remit the s 79 applications to a **different judge** |

Appeal **allowed**; Order 22(a) set aside.

## Use it for

- **fam.2.3** — gifts from extended family in the property pool under s 79.
- **Just outcomes** — adequate reasons as part of procedural fairness; a long, costly case ends in a rehearing.

## Caution

The Court did not decide whose gift the $23m was. The file gives two dates for the mother giving up control of the trust.
