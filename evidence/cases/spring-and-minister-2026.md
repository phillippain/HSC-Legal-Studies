A New Zealand citizen, in Australia since he was a teenager, had his visa cancelled under s 501(2) after more than **40** convictions (mostly driving offences) and reoffending after a written warning.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Character test | Failed |
| Direction 123 | Community protection and expectations outweighed his family, children and ties |
| Non-refoulement | No real risk of harm in New Zealand (speculative) |
| Result | Cancellation affirmed; cancellation is not a punishment |
