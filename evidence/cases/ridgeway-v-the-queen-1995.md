## Facts

**Ridgeway**, an ex-prisoner, arranged to buy heroin from an informer, Lee. Unknown to him, **Australian Federal Police** helped import it illegally in a "controlled delivery" and arrested him on receipt. He was tried in the South Australian District Court on two offences under s 233B(1) of the *Customs Act 1901* (Cth). The Full Court dismissed his appeal (Legoe J dissenting). Heard 22-23 August 1994.

## Held — 6 to 1 on the orders (McHugh J dissenting)

- **Entrapment is not a substantive defence** to a criminal charge.
- But the importation was an offence committed by the police, so the court should not allow the prosecution to benefit from it: conviction quashed and a **permanent stay** of both charges.
- The majority differed in their reasoning: Brennan J would have entered an acquittal and Toohey J a verdict of not guilty. The orders as printed record the stay.

## Use it for

- **crime.2.3**: the limits on police investigative methods such as controlled deliveries, informers and undercover work.
- **Balance**: effective investigation of drug importation against the rule of law.
- Pair with **Kadir; Grech** (s 138) for the modern statutory discretion.
