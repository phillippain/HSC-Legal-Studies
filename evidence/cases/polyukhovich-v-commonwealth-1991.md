## Facts

Ivan Polyukhovich, an Australian citizen in South Australia, was charged under the ***War Crimes Act 1945* (Cth)**, as amended in 1988, with **murders in Ukraine in 1942-43**, when no Australian law covered them. He argued the Act was beyond power and a retrospective usurpation of judicial power. The question was reserved to the Full Court. The subject matter is the Holocaust: keep class discussion at the level of the law.

## Held — 4 to 3 that s 9 of the Act is valid

- **Mason CJ, Dawson, Toohey and McHugh JJ**: valid under the **external affairs power** (s 51(xxix)). Mason CJ and Dawson J held that the externality of the conduct was enough; Toohey J required that it touch Australia. A retrospective criminal law that leaves guilt to the court does not offend **Chapter III**.
- **Brennan J** dissented on the external affairs power. **Deane and Gaudron JJ** dissented on Chapter III: a law declaring guilt for a past act usurps judicial power.
- Answer: **No**, s 9 is not invalid. Seven separate judgments, no order as to costs.
