Mr Kuot, 35, a South Sudanese citizen who arrived as a refugee child in 2005, had his humanitarian visa mandatorily cancelled a second time. The Federal Court ordered a rehearing.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Direction 123 | Applied |
| Risk of reoffending | Unacceptable |
| Offending | Very serious |
| Result | Decision not to revoke affirmed |
