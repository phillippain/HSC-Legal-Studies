## Facts

SASR soldier **Ben Roberts-Smith** sued newspapers for defamation over 2018 articles alleging unlawful killings of Afghan prisoners. The hearing ran from **7 June 2021 to 27 July 2022**. The judgment has 2,618 paragraphs.

## Held

**Besanko J** found the respondents proved the **substantial truth** of the murder imputations (Whiskey 108, Darwan and Chinartu), plus bullying and assaults, applying the civil standard (**balance of probabilities**, s 140 *Evidence Act* with *Briginshaw*). The domestic violence and hypocrisy imputations failed, but contextual truth succeeded. Each proceeding was **dismissed** on 1 June 2023 and damages were not assessed.

## Caution

This is **not a criminal verdict**. Roberts-Smith has since been charged (April 2026: five counts, per the Afghanistan record), and the criminal case is undecided: do not describe him as convicted. Later history from outside the judgment: the High Court refused special leave to appeal on 4 September 2025 (High Court dispositions list). A named, living person: keep to the law.
