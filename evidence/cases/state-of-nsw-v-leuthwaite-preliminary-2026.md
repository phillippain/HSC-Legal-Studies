**Matthew Leuthwaite**, 30, was sentenced on **13 February 2020** for a serious offence committed in September 2018. His non-parole period expired on 5 August 2025 and he was released to parole on **24 September 2025**; parole was revoked on **14 January 2026** after an ADVO breach. With his sentence about to expire, the State filed a summons on **7 August 2026** for a **three-year extended supervision order (ESO)** under ss 5B and 9(1)(a). A risk assessment report (13 July 2026) found him high risk. He cut off his monitoring tag in early October 2026 and had not been located by the preliminary hearing (6 October). Rigg J gave judgment on **5 November 2026**. **The final ESO has not been decided.**

## The preliminary question and the orders

| Issue | Finding |
|---|---|
| If the allegations are proved, would they justify an ESO? | **Yes** — "a high degree of probability" of an unacceptable risk of a further serious offence if not supervised (at [50]); risk need not be more likely than not (s 5D) |
| Should an interim supervision order (ISO) be made? | **Yes** — community safety is paramount (at [11]); no reason against |
| Which conditions are proportionate? | **37 conditions** imposed, including a curfew and drug non-association; others refused |

**Orders**: two qualified experts to assess him and report (s 7(4) — mandatory once the test is met); an **ISO for 28 days from 5 November 2026**. **Refused** as disproportionate or oppressive: a schedule of movements (at [56]), visitor notices, financial information and a licensed-premises ban; proposed condition 13 was also held "disproportionate to risk and oppressive" (at [64]).

## Use it for

- **crime.4.8 (post-sentencing considerations)** — supervision that continues after the sentence has ended; compare with detention-style orders.
- **Balancing community safety and liberty** — safety is paramount, yet each condition must be proportionate to the risk.
- **Compliance** — removing a monitoring tag and breaching an ADVO on parole count as evidence of risk.
- **Discretion** — the court chooses which conditions fit; the expert-assessment step is mandatory.

## Caution

A **class case summary** of a **preliminary** decision: the three-year ESO is undecided and the final hearing date is not in the file. The judgment cites **no case law** — it applies the Act and Practice Note SC CL 12 to the evidence. The index offence is not described here; do not add detail. Treat the statement that the allegations are "if proved" as essential — he has not yet been found a high risk offender.
