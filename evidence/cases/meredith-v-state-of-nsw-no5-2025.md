## Facts

Raya Meredith, 27, was strip searched at the **Splendour in the Grass** festival on **20 July 2018** after a drug dog indicated; **nothing was found**. She was the lead plaintiff in a representative proceeding under the *Civil Procedure Act 2005* (NSW). The State conceded the search was unlawful.

## Held — judgment for the plaintiff

- *Law Enforcement (Powers and Responsibilities) Act 2002* **s 31(b)** needs separate suspicion of necessity, seriousness and urgency: that a drug offence is serious does not itself supply the grounds.
- Breaches of **ss 32 and 33** (conduct of the search) matter, and police cannot direct body movements.
- **$93,000** plus interest: battery $3,000, assault $25,000, false imprisonment $15,000, aggravated damages $50,000 (including for how the State conducted the case). A right to **exemplary damages** was found, but quantifying them was deferred. 21 common questions were answered.
