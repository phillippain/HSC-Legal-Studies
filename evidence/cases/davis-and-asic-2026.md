Kirk Davis applied to ASIC to be a registered company auditor. The Tribunal dismissed his review because ASIC had made no reviewable decision.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Is reinstatement available after a contested hearing? | Yes |
| Can 'error' include a Tribunal finding? | Yes |
| Was a finding wrong? | One — but not causative |
| Result | Reinstatement refused |
