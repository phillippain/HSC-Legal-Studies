South Africa brought proceedings on **29 December 2023**, alleging that Israel was breaching the
**Genocide Convention 1948** in Gaza. In 2024 the Court made several **provisional measures orders**,
directing Israel to prevent acts within the Convention and to allow humanitarian assistance. The Court
has **not decided** whether genocide has occurred, and Israel denies the allegation.

The written pleadings continue: under the current timetable South Africa's Reply is due in
**November 2027** and Israel's Rejoinder in **May 2029**, before any hearing on the merits.

## ICJ or ICC?

| | International Court of Justice | International Criminal Court |
|---|---|---|
| Who is a party | **States** | **Individuals** |
| Outcome | State responsibility, orders | Conviction and sentence |
| Basis | UN Charter; treaties such as the Genocide Convention | Rome Statute |
