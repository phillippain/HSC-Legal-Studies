In January 2001 SLD, aged 13, abducted a three-year-old girl from her bed on the Central Coast and
killed her. He had been in foster care from age 2, was intellectually impaired and had been
expelled from several schools.

Murder is a serious children's indictable offence, so he was sentenced 'according to law' in the
Supreme Court. Wood CJ at CL imposed **20 years with a 10-year non-parole period**, describing the
background as 'almost as bleak and disturbing as his crime'. He suggested children convicted of
murder be detained and **resentenced at about 21**, when their risk could be better assessed. The
appeal was dismissed in 2003.

- **Law reform not taken**: the review-and-resentence idea, newly relevant as the SCIO list grows (55
