Scicluna was convicted in the District Court (17 March 2025) of a firearm discharge offence and two possession offences. A co-accused had already succeeded on appeal.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Summing up balanced? | No — unfair, a miscarriage of justice |
| Crown position | Conceded, matching the co-accused's appeal |
| Result | Three convictions quashed; retrial ordered |
