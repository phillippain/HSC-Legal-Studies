Parties who lived together from January 2019 and separated finally on **6 January 2024**; interim parenting orders
were made by a registrar on 14 November 2024. In mid-2026 the father was **formally charged**, and at the end of July
the mother suspended his time. The mother (unrepresented) applied to suspend or supervise his time; the father
applied to be released from the **Harman undertaking** for two family reports. Hearing 4 September; **reasons 22
September 2026**. (Pseudonyms.)

## Two decisions

| | 1. Time with the father | 2. Harman release |
|---|---|---|
| Question | Should time stop or be supervised because he is charged? | Are there "special circumstances" under Family Law Rules 2021 r 15.26? |
| Held | No. A charge is a **procedural step, not proof**; the allegations were known in 2024; no risk shown | Yes. Same events underlie both cases; the reports "have the real potential to be important" to the criminal case |
| Detail | Mother's account of the agency's (DFFH) advice "is not supported by the evidence" (at [65]); school changeovers avoid conflict | Release is for his **criminal proceedings only** |

The father "does not pose a risk to the children arising from formal charges being laid" (at [81]); the parents
"should not have to see each other at changeovers" where avoidable (at [79]).

## The orders

- Alternate Tuesdays and other set times with the father from 8 September 2026; changeovers at school or
  kindergarten, otherwise an agreed public venue.
- The father is released from the Harman undertaking (r 6.04(1), r 15.26) for two family reports, for his criminal
  proceedings only.
- Not granted: the mother's applications to suspend or supervise his time. (The orders were corrected under the
  slip rule, r 10.13.)
