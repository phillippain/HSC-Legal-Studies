A student visa applicant did not meet a Tribunal deadline to give evidence of enrolment, and the Tribunal decided his review without a hearing.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Was he denied a hearing? | No — not responding ended his statutory right to one |
| Could the Tribunal decide otherwise? | No — with no current enrolment it made the only decision open |
| Result | Application dismissed |
