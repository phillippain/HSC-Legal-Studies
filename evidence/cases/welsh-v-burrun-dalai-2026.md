An administration officer with a heart condition was made compulsorily redundant in April 2025 in a cost-cutting restructure.

## What the court decided

| Question | Held |
|---|---|
| Disability and age discrimination by inference | Not established |
| The restructure | Genuine, driven only by finances and operations |
| Result | Complaint dismissed |

## Use it for

- **hr.2.9 / hr.2.11** — anti-discrimination law at work and the burden of proving discrimination by inference.

The header says age and sex, the order says age and disability; her age is given as 54 and 57.
