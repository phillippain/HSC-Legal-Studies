## Affordability

Restrictive zoning and built-form controls — height limits, minimum lot sizes, floor-space ratios —
limit supply where demand is highest. Approval delays raise financing and holding costs, which end
up in the price.

The responses, all within the *Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979* (NSW) and the
policies made under it:

- **Housing SEPP in-fill affordable housing bonuses** — height and floor-space ratio bonuses of
  **20–30%** where at least **10–15%** of gross floor area is affordable housing.
- **Transport Oriented Development** chapter of the Housing SEPP — a state framework for apartments
  near stations, expressly including an affordable housing aim.
- **s 7.32 EP&A Act** — councils may impose conditions requiring land or money for affordable housing.
- **SEPP 70** — affordable housing contribution schemes tied to upzonings in nominated precincts.

## The case worth citing

***LFD Homes Pty Ltd v The Council of the City of Sydney* [2025] NSWLEC 1492** — the Land and
Environment Court dismissed an appeal to replace a **32-room boarding house** with four dwellings,
finding an unacceptable loss of affordable rental boarding-house accommodation.

That is planning law protecting the **least protected tenure in the option** — boarders and lodgers,
whom s 8(c) of the *Residential Tenancies Act 2010* excludes. Tenancy law offers them nothing; the
planning system kept the building.

## Discrimination, homelessness and social housing

Zoning produces **place-based exclusion**: where local controls resist group homes, supportive
accommodation or crisis housing, people with disability and people experiencing disadvantage lose
access to well-serviced suburbs and are concentrated elsewhere. That is an equity effect the
*Anti-Discrimination Act 1977* (NSW) will not reach, because no one is refused anything — the
housing simply is not there.

## Use it for

Planning law is the missing half of the Shelter option. A response that can name the Housing SEPP,
s 7.32 and *LFD Homes* is arguing about how shelter is actually produced, not only about how a
tenancy is ended.
