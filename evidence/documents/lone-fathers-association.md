The **Lone Fathers Association of Australia Inc.** was established in **1975** as an advocacy group
for separated parents, out of a perception that custody decisions disproportionately favoured mothers
and left fathers with little or no contact. It runs counselling, free legal advice and child support
services, and acts as an **advisory body to government** on family law, child support, parental
responsibility, superannuation and property.

Its most consequential contribution was the ***Family Law Amendment (Shared Parental Responsibility)
Act 2006* (Cth)**, which introduced the **presumption of equal shared parental responsibility** —
presented at the time as a step toward just outcomes for all family members, with both parents legally
responsible for decisions in the child's best interests.

## Why this is the best NGO example in the option

Because you can follow it to the end. The **Family Law Amendment Act 2023 (Cth)** **removed that
presumption from s 61DA**, on evidence that it was producing arrangements that placed children at risk
in cases involving family violence. The *Daily Telegraph* put the criticism bluntly as early as 2010:
the presumption "led to children being left in abusive homes."

So the sequence is: an NGO identifies a real injustice → it secures a legislative change → the change
produces a different injustice → Parliament reverses it seventeen years later.

## Use it for

- **The role of non-government organisations** (`fam.2.6`) argued rather than described. An NGO's
  effectiveness is usually measured by whether it changed the law. This one did, and the question that
  earns marks is whether that made the law better.
- **Law reform as a cycle, not a ratchet** — pairs directly with the law reform record and with the
  2023 Act.
- **Whose interests an NGO represents.** The LFAA advocates for a class of parents; the 2023 reform
  was made in the interests of children. When those diverge, the syllabus's "best interests of the
  child" principle decides — which is the honest way to evaluate an advocacy group's influence.
