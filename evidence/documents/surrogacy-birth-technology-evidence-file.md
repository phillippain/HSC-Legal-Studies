An evidence file organised the way the syllabus asks — legislation, case law, media and
international instruments — with a rule attached: **use at least one piece from each category in an
extended response**, and evaluate each against a criterion rather than describing it.

## The framework

- **Surrogacy Act 2010 (NSW)** — governs the *arrangement*
- **Assisted Reproductive Technology Act 2007 (NSW)** — governs the *clinical process*, providers,
  consent and donor information
- **Status of Children Act 1996 (NSW)** and **s 60H** *Family Law Act 1975* (Cth) — determine legal
  parentage for children born through assisted reproduction
- **CRC art 7** — the child's right to identity; **HCCH Parentage / Surrogacy Project**; the Council
  of Europe instrument on ART standards

Case law named: **Bernieres v Dhopal** [2017] (an overseas arrangement, and the inadequacy it
exposed), **Masson v Parsons** (2019) HCA — **a sperm donor recognised as a legal parent** — and
**Baby Gammy**, the child with Down syndrome left with a Thai surrogate.

## The exam traps the file names

1. Do **not** confuse the *Surrogacy Act* (the arrangement) with the *ART Act* (the clinical process).
2. The **CRC is not directly enforceable** in Australian courts — it is persuasive unless implemented
   by statute.
3. **Altruistic** surrogacy is lawful in NSW; **commercial** surrogacy is prohibited in every
   Australian jurisdiction.
4. ***Masson v Parsons* (2019) changed the law on donors.** Any note saying a donor can never be a
   legal parent is out of date.

## The five arguments it sets up

- **Legal recognition against social reality** — the law lags behind how Australians actually form
  families.
- **Rights in conflict** — the child's right to identity against a donor's expectation of anonymity.
- **Federalism** — eight frameworks producing unequal treatment for the same arrangement.
- **Globalisation of reproduction** — overseas commercial surrogacy exposing the limits of domestic
  regulation.
- **Technology outpacing law** — IVF, pre-implantation testing and mitochondrial donation.

## Use it for

The spine of any surrogacy answer, and the place to check a citation before using it. Its case law
sits behind the 2026 judgments already filed here: *Daniels & Rooney* (a surplus embryo as property
because no statute says otherwise) and *Wilkerson & Croxford* (CROC arts 7, 8 and 9 deciding a
known-donor case).
