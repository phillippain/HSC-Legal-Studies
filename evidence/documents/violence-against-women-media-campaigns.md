Domestic violence was long treated as private — closed courtrooms and reporting restrictions kept it
out of the news, and out of public awareness. That changed deliberately.

- **2016** — the federal government's first domestic violence campaign, ***Violence against women:
  Let's stop it at the start***, shared hundreds of thousands of times and viewed approximately
  **43 million times**.
- **2018** — further campaigns targeting the **"boys will be boys"** attitude and the normalisation of
  male violence.
- **Our Watch**, an independent not-for-profit, runs **#BecauseWhy** on gender roles and stereotyping,
  and **"Doing Nothing Does Harm"** — the three S's, *show, support, speak up*.

## The numbers, and the limit

**One in six** Australian women and **one in sixteen** men have experienced physical or sexual
violence from a live-in partner since the age of fifteen (ABS 2017, via AIHW). Awareness campaigns
coincided with **more reporting**: about **31,435 ADVOs** issued in 2018 alone. And still,
**approximately one woman a week** dies as a result of domestic violence, and about **20% of ADVOs are
breached**.

## Use it for

- **The role of the media** (`fam.2.7`) as a *non-legal response* with a measurable effect on
  **reporting** and no effect on **compliance**. That distinction is the whole paragraph: the media
  changes what people are willing to say, the law has to change what people do.
- **Changing community values** — a government paying to change attitudes is itself evidence that the
  law understood its own limits.
- **More reports is an ambiguous statistic.** Rising ADVO numbers can mean more violence or more
  confidence in reporting it; say which you think and why, using the breach rate to argue that the
  enforcement end is where the failure sits.

## Caution

The figures are from the teaching series and date from around 2018–19. Where a current number is
needed, use the AIHW and BOCSAR records in this finder instead: 58 women and 15 men killed by a
current or former partner in 2024–25, and 2.3 million Australians affected in the last year.
