Once a suspect is arrested, **Part 9 of LEPRA** turns police discretion into a timetable and a list of
entitlements, administered by a **custody manager** who is separate from the investigating officer.

## The clock

| Provision | Rule |
|---|---|
| ss 114–121 | Detention for investigation for a **reasonable time, initially 6 hours** |
| s 117 | **Time-outs** — the clock stops for rest, meals, medical attention, contacting a lawyer |
| s 118 | Longer than 6 hours needs a **detention warrant** from an authorised officer |
| s 204 | Detention only for the time **reasonably necessary** |

## The entitlements

- **s 122** — caution and a summary of rights, given by the custody manager
- **s 123** — the right to contact a friend, relative, guardian or Australian legal practitioner
- **s 124** — consular contact for foreign nationals
- **s 128** interpreter · **s 129** medical attention · **s 130** refreshments and facilities
- **s 131** — a **custody record**, which is what makes the rest auditable
- **s 231A** — spit hoods prohibited; **ss 108B–108G** — mandatory in-car video recording

## Children are different

Admissions by a person **under 18 are inadmissible** unless made in the presence of a support adult
(s 13 *Children (Criminal Proceedings) Act 1987*). A strip search of a 10–17 year old requires a
support person (s 33 LEPRA), and no one under 10 may be strip searched at all.

## Use it for

- **Detention and interrogation** as a *balance* question: the state needs time to investigate; the
  suspect is still presumed innocent. Part 9 resolves it by rationing time rather than by granting
  a right of silence in the American sense.
- **Enforceability** — the sanction is not automatic. A breach exposes the evidence to the
  **s 138 *Evidence Act 1995* (NSW)** discretion to exclude improperly obtained evidence, which is
  weighed, not applied. Civil action (trespass, false imprisonment) and the **LECC** sit behind it.
- The contrast between **rights on paper and rights in the moment**: the custody manager, the record
  and the video are all attempts to make s 202–204 checkable after the fact.
