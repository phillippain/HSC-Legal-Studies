- About **40,000** bail decisions a year are made in the Local Court; **5,732** people were on remand (March 2025).
- Remand costs over **$105,850** per person a year.
- Bail decisions are predictions about risk; some people granted bail will reoffend, and blaming the judge for that is 'wrong and unfair'.
