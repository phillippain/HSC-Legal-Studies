Eneasi Taumoefolau moved to Australia from Tonga as a child in 1989 and was **deported in October
2022** after repeated drug and firearm offences. In 2024 he imported more than 2 kg of methamphetamine
into Tonga from the United States and tried to establish a **Comancheros** chapter; police raids in
August 2024 arrested 17 people and seized gang patches and merchandise. He was sentenced to **life**
for the importation in January 2026. In October 2026 Justice Elisapeti Langi sentenced him to **12
years**, concurrent, for **participating in an organised criminal group** under s 66 of Tonga's
*Counter Terrorism and Transnational Organised Crime Act 2013* (maximum 25 years). He says he will
appeal.

## Intended v observed

| Australia's aim | What happened |
|---|---|
| Community safety is the "highest priority" in visa cancellation (*Migration Act* s 501, Ministerial Direction 110, 2024) | The offender built a gang chapter and drug network in Tonga |
| Responsibility passes to the country of citizenship | Tonga has no law or program for deportee reintegration |
| Offender faces justice in the receiving state | Life plus 12 years, under transnational organised crime law |

## Use it for

- **crime.6.1 / crime.6.2 (international crime)** — transnational organised crime and a Pacific state
  using its own UNTOC-style law against an Australian-born gang structure.
- **Evaluation** — whether s 501 deportation reduces crime or exports it; Pacific governments have long
  argued the second. Australia's $600 million Waqa Moana Initiative targets Pacific drug trafficking,
  not deportation.
- **hr.2.1 / ls.2.11 (sovereignty)** — each state polices its own territory; the problem crosses them.

## Caution

A class **evidence summary of one news report**. The 12-year sentence was reported only by the ABC at
the time and the sentencing remarks were not yet published; the exact Migration Act power used to
deport him was not confirmed (s 501 is the usual pathway). Tongan media (Kaniva, Matangi Tonga) confirm
the January 2026 life sentences.
