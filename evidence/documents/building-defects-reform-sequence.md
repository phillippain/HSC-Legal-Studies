## The sequence

| When | What | What it did |
|---|---|---|
| 2014 | *Brookfield Multiplex v Owners Corporation SP 61288* | The High Court held **no duty of care** was owed by a builder to an owners corporation for pure economic loss in that commercial context — the gap |
| Nov 2014 | **Lacrosse** cladding fire, Melbourne | Combustible cladding becomes a national issue |
| Dec 2018 | **Opal Tower** evacuated, Sydney Olympic Park | Cracking; hundreds of residents displaced at Christmas |
| Jun 2019 | **Mascot Towers** evacuated | Residents left with an unsellable, unlivable asset and a repair bill |
| 2020 | ***Residential Apartment Buildings (Compliance and Enforcement Powers) Act 2020* (NSW)** | **Prohibition orders** and **building work rectification orders**; the regulator can stop an occupation certificate issuing and stop the sale |
| 2020 | ***Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020* (NSW), s 37** | A **statutory duty of care** to exercise reasonable care to avoid economic loss from defects, owed to owners **including subsequent owners** — filling the *Brookfield* gap |
| 2024 | ***Pafburn v Owners – SP 84674* [2024] HCA 49** | The s 37 duty is **non-delegable**; a developer or head builder cannot shift responsibility to subcontractors |

## Use it for

- **Initiating and responding in one sequence.** The Acts **responded** to the evacuations; s 37
  **initiated** a duty the common law had declined to recognise; *Pafburn* then made it unavoidable.
  That is the highest-scoring move available in this option, and it is all one story.
- **Prevention as resolution** — the Building Commission's power to stop a sale resolves a defect
  before a purchaser ever becomes an owner with a claim. Compare the years *Brookfield* and *Pafburn*
  each took to litigate.
- **What triggers a fast legislative response**: a visible building failure, and a visible encampment
  (the *Sydney Public Reserves (Public Safety) Act 2017*), each within weeks or months. The social
  housing waiting list, which is larger and slower, has produced nothing comparable. Responsiveness
  tracks visibility, not scale — that argument is worth a paragraph on its own.
