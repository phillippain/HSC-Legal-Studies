## Disability Discrimination Commissioner (Australian Human Rights Commission)

- A statutory office under the *DDA* since 1993, held since 2024 by Rosemary Kayess, who had chaired the UN Committee on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities.
- Functions: the Commission receives and conciliates complaints under the *DDA* (a complainant cannot go to court without first going through the AHRC); the Commissioner runs inquiries, reports to Parliament, advises on legislation and promotes the CRPD. Findings and recommendations are not enforceable.

## NSW Ageing and Disability Commissioner

- Created by the *Ageing and Disability Commissioner Act 2019* (NSW) after the Royal Commission's early hearings on abuse in group homes; an independent statutory body with a helpline, investigation powers over abuse, neglect and exploitation of adults with disability and older people in their homes and communities, and the Official Community Visitors scheme for people in supported accommodation.
- Can investigate and refer to police, the NDIS Commission or NCAT (for guardianship) but cannot itself order a remedy.

The NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission (Cth) regulates NDIS providers and is a third body; do not merge the three. Office-holders change — check names before citing them.
