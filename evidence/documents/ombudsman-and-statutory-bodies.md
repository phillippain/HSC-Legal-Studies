A reference note.

| Body | Established by | What it does | What it cannot do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NSW Ombudsman** | *Ombudsman Act 1974* (NSW) | Investigates complaints of wrong conduct by NSW agencies; free, informal, own-motion powers | Cannot overturn a decision or award compensation |
| **Commonwealth Ombudsman** | *Ombudsman Act 1976* (Cth) | The same for federal agencies, plus specialist roles (immigration detention, overseas students) | As above |
| **Australian Human Rights Commission** | *AHRC Act 1986* (Cth) | Receives and **conciliates** discrimination and human rights complaints; national inquiries; reports to Parliament | Cannot determine a complaint — an unresolved matter must go to the Federal Court, with costs exposure |
| **ICAC** | *ICAC Act 1988* (NSW) | Investigates corrupt conduct in the NSW public sector; public hearings; findings and recommendations | Does **not** prosecute — briefs go to the DPP |
| **LECC** | *LECC Act 2016* (NSW) | Police and Crime Commission misconduct | Investigates and reports only |
| **Royal commissions** | Letters patent | The strongest investigative powers available — compel evidence, override some privileges, run for years | Recommend only |

## The pattern

These bodies trade **coercive power for reach**. They are free or cheap, they are not bound by the
rules of evidence, they can start without a complainant, and they can look at systems rather than
single decisions — and at the end they publish a report.

Whether that is enough is the evaluative question, and the finder holds both answers. The **Royal
Commission into Institutional Responses to Child Sexual Abuse** produced the *Evidence Amendment
(Tendency and Coincidence) Act 2020*. The **Family Is Culture** review produced **12 of 126**
recommendations implemented in five years. Same mechanism, opposite outcomes.

## Use it for

- **Ombudsman and statutory bodies** (`ind.2.7`) — group them by what they can *do*, not by who they
  belong to, and the answer writes itself.
- **Enforceability** — the recurring criticism is that a recommendation is not an order. The
  counter-argument worth making is that these bodies reach conduct no court could: a pattern, a
  culture, a department's whole practice.
- **Access** — no fee, no lawyer, no standing requirement. For most people this is the only realistic
  way to challenge a government body at all.
