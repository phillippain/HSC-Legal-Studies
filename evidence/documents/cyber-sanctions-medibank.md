The **2022 Medibank breach** exposed the personal and health data of about **9.7 million** customers.

## The sanctions

| Date | Target |
|---|---|
| January 2024 | **Aleksandr Ermakov**, a Russian national, for his role in the Medibank breach: Australia's first cyber sanction |
| February 2025 | **ZServers**, a Russian 'bulletproof' hosting provider (with the US and UK) |
| November 2025 | **Media Land**, another bulletproof host |

A sanction makes it a crime to deal with the person's assets or give them funds, and bans them from
entering Australia.

## Use it for

- **Where extradition fails**: Russia does not extradite its nationals, so sanctions are the measure
  that is available.
- **Symbolic or effective?** They signal disapproval and disrupt infrastructure, but rarely punish an
  offender who never leaves Russia. Ransomware payments must now also be reported within **72 hours**
  (Cyber Security Act 2024).
