## Conditions that give rise to reform

**Changing social values**, **new technology**, **new concepts of justice**, and **failure of existing
law** — the last usually made visible by a single case.

Worked examples from the records in this finder:

| Condition | Example |
|---|---|
| Failure of existing law, exposed by one case | *R v Singh* (2012) → **Crimes Amendment (Provocation) Act 2014** |
| A victim left out of the process | *R v Koch* (2009) → **s 35A** victim consultation in charge negotiation |
| An inquiry finding | Royal Commission → **Evidence Amendment (Tendency and Coincidence) Act 2020** |
| Changing values and market failure | 45% of leases ending with no reason → **no-grounds evictions abolished, 2024** |
| New concepts of justice | Shared parental responsibility placing children at risk → **Family Law Amendment Act 2023** |
| New technology | Secret GPS tracking of partners → NSW criminalisation |

## Agencies of reform

**Law reform commissions** (ALRC, NSWLRC) — independent, thorough, consultative, and with no power to
implement. **Parliamentary committees** — closer to the decision, less independent. **The media** —
sets the agenda and supplies the pressure, and is the reason the *Singh* and *Koch* reforms happened
in the years they did. **NGOs and interest groups** — sustained expertise and organised constituency,
from the Tenants' Union to AbSec. **Royal commissions and inquiries** — authority and public record.

## Mechanisms of reform

**Courts** change law case by case, within the dispute before them, and cannot choose their subject
matter. **Parliaments** can change anything within power, at any time, and are the only mechanism
that can act prospectively and comprehensively. **The United Nations and IGOs** set standards that
reach domestic law only through the incorporation route.

## The stage that is measured least

Recommendation is not implementation. **Family Is Culture**: 126 recommendations, **12 fully
implemented five years on**. Any evaluation of the law reform process that stops at "a review was
commissioned" is unfinished — the question is what happened to the recommendations, and how long it
took.
