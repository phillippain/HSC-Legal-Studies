## Pressure from outside

**Executive Order 14203** (February 2025) authorised US sanctions on ICC officials. By **August 2026**
the Prosecutor, both Deputy Prosecutors and **9 of the Court's 18 judges** (including its President) had
been sanctioned: assets frozen, US travel and banking cut off.

## Pressure from inside

On **24 July 2026** the Assembly of States Parties removed Prosecutor **Karim Khan** for serious
misconduct (arts 42 and 46), the first Prosecutor removed. He denies the findings.

## Membership

|---|---|
| States parties (2026) | **125** (some materials wrongly say 193, the UN's membership) |
| Withdrawals lodged | Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger (June 2026); Venezuela (July 2026), effective a year later (art 127) |
| Withdrawal reversed | Hungary (May 2026) |

## The Court still works

Convictions: **Ali Kushayb** (Darfur, 20 years, December 2025); **Yekatom and Ngaïssona** (Central
African Republic, July 2025). Duterte's trial is listed for 30 November 2026.

## Use it for

- **Politics as a limitation**: an international court can be sanctioned by the state most able to
  enforce its warrants.
- **Legitimacy**: misconduct inside and attacks from outside both weaken compliance.
- **Balance**: convictions and a head-of-state trial in the same period.
