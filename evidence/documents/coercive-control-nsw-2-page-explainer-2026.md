A two-page class explainer, linking the Crime core and the Family option:

- **What it is:** a *pattern* of dominating behaviour (monitoring, financial abuse, threats, shaming, isolation, property damage, harming animals, restricting freedom), not a single act. A stand-alone crime in NSW from **1 July 2024**.
- **The offence (s 54D):** the accused is an adult; current or former intimate partners; a course of abusive conduct (s 54F); intent to coerce or control; and a reasonable person would see it as likely to cause fear of violence or a serious effect on day-to-day life. Up to **7 years**; indictable but a **Table 1** offence, usually heard in the Local Court. Defence: the conduct was reasonable in all the circumstances (**s 54E**).
- **Link to Crime:** actus reus = the course of abusive conduct; mens rea = intent to coerce or control.
- **The numbers (BOCSAR, data to 30 June 2026):** **682** incidents recorded, **42** charges, **4** proven (all guilty pleas), 6 withdrawn, 32 not finalised; regional NSW **15.2** v Greater Sydney **4.3** incidents per 100,000.
- **The response:** ADVOs ("domestic abuse", s 6A), a show-cause bail test with electronic monitoring, victim impact statements and s 4A sentencing; campaigns, police and specialist-worker training, and support services.
- **Evaluation:** strengths, weaknesses and open questions (intimate partners only, regional gap, the untested "reasonable" defence, the 2026 statutory review).
