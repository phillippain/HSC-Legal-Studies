Built around the syllabus criteria, one heading at a time, with a verdict on each.

## Responsiveness and society's wishes — effective

*Family Law Act 1975* (Cth) and the **best interests of the child**. The
*Family Law Legislation Amendment (Family Violence and Other Measures) Act 2011* (Cth) broadened the
definition of family violence to include **children witnessing violence**. The *Crimes Legislation
Amendment (Coercive Control) Act 2022* (NSW) commenced **1 July 2024**, with **$245.6 million** in the
2024–25 NSW Budget for domestic, family and sexual violence victim-survivors. **AIHW (2025)**:
emotional abuse, including exposure to family violence, was the most common primary type of abuse in
substantiated child protection cases — **57%, about 24,000 children**, in 2023–24.

## Rights and international law — effective

The *Family Law Reform Act 1995* (Cth) changed **"custody" to "residence"** and **"access" to
"contact"**, enshrining the values of the **CROC**, art 3(2). **s 60CA** makes the child's best
interests the **paramount** consideration. The *Family Law Amendment Act 2023* (Cth) and the
*Family Law Amendment (Information Sharing) Act 2023* (Cth) commenced **6 May 2024**, replacing
equal shared parental responsibility with **joint decision making on major long-term issues**.
Where the Act departs from the CROC, **the Act prevails** — the Convention is not directly enforceable.

## Enforceability and resources — ineffective

Federal law sets the guidelines; **enforcement is a residual state power**. The *Children and Young
Persons (Care and Protection) Act 1998* (NSW) imposes **mandatory reporting** by professionals on
reasonable grounds to suspect risk of significant harm. *The Guardian* (2013): only **30%** of the
state's most serious child abuse cases were fully investigated with a caseworker visit, because of
staff shortages. **AIHW (2025)**: notifications rose from **49 to 53 per 1,000** children between
2019–20 and 2023–24, while substantiations slightly fell — the gap between reporting and response.

## Enforceability — court powers — effective

Under the *Minors (Property and Contracts) Act 1970* (NSW) a court can authorise treatment parents
refuse. *Director Clinical Services, Child & Adolescent Health Services and Kiszko* [2016] — a
six-year-old denied brain cancer treatment; the court ordered chemotherapy, though the parents'
appeal ultimately succeeded. *Hurt v R; Delzotto v R* [2024] HCA 8 upheld Commonwealth mandatory
minimum sentences for child sexual abuse offences as **yardsticks for the least serious case**.

## Law reform — from parental rights to a child-centred approach

The *Family Law Amendment (Shared Parental Responsibility) Act 2006* (Cth) presumed equal shared
responsibility, pushed by the Lone Fathers' Association; the *Daily Telegraph* (2010): "this led to
children being left in abusive homes". The 2011 amendment defined abuse more clearly; the **2023 Act
removed the presumption from s 61DA** and elevated **child safety** above shared time.
*Pickford & Pickford* [2024] FedCFamC1A 249 recognised that **coercive control is about the impact on
the victim, not the perpetrator's intent**.

## Non-legal mechanisms — effective

**Luke Batty** (2014), and **Rosie Batty** as Australian of the Year 2015 — *The Guardian* (2015):
"Rosie has put domestic violence on the national agenda". 2025 figures: **1 in 6 women and 1 in 16
men** have experienced domestic and family violence since the age of 15; in 2024–25, **58 women and
15 men** were killed by a current or former partner. The **Domestic, Family and Sexual Violence
Commission Yearly Report (2024)** called for a multi-sectoral approach and more frontline funding
under the National Plan 2022–2032.

## Caution

A teaching compilation, and the citations in it vary in precision — *Pickford* is printed as
"FedCFamCIA 249" (read: FedCFamC1A) and *Kiszko* has no judgment number. Check any case on AustLII or
NSW Caselaw before quoting it in an exam-conditions answer.
