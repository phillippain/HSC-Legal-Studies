A redesigned class poster (flow chart, colour-coded tests, NEW/UPDATED badges) setting out the *Bail Act 2013* (NSW):

- **Why bail:** presumption of innocence, right to liberty, equality before the law.
- **Who decides:** police; Local Court judges (called Magistrates until 28 March 2026); District and Supreme Courts, which
  hear bail **de novo**. Registrars and other authorised justices no longer decide bail (**s 70A**, from 14 March 2025).
- **Three tests:** automatic release (s 21), **show cause** (s 16A — never for an accused under 18 at the time of the
  offence), and the **unacceptable risk** test (ss 17–20).
- **2024 DV reforms:** show cause for serious DV offences (s 16B(1)(c1)) and **electronic monitoring** (s 28B).
- **Stay of release (s 40):** to the earliest of a Supreme Court decision, a Crown notice, or 4 pm on the third business day
  — first bail decisions only.
- **Youth bail (s 22C):** a "high degree of confidence" test for 14–17-year-olds charged with motor theft or serious break
  and enter while on bail for one; extended to **4 April 2028**.
- **Cases:** *Williams v R* [2026] NSWCCA 17 (firearm show cause, bail granted) and *R v TB* [2025] NSWSC 38 (s 22C, a
  14-year-old Aboriginal boy granted bail; tension with s 6 of the *Children (Criminal Proceedings) Act*).
