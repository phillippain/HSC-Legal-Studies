The NSW Drug Summit (late 2024) produced 56 recommendations. The government's response
(October 2025):

- **Supported** fully or in principle **51 of 56**.
- Backed a **12-month drug-checking trial** at music festivals, but **did not** support suspending
  drug detection dogs or strip searches during it.
- Supported **expanding eligibility** for the Early Drug Diversion Initiative (criminal infringement
  notices for small quantities, from February 2024).
- Supported more investment in alcohol and drug services, the workforce and education; will consider
  a defence for drivers with a medical cannabis prescription.
- Only **noted** the recommendation to lift restrictions on supervised injecting centres.

## Use it for

- **Drug offences** (`crime.1.5`): possession, supply and deemed supply under the *Drug Misuse and
  Trafficking Act 1985* remain crimes; the response adjusts how they are enforced.
- **Factors and prevention**: treatment and harm reduction as social crime prevention.
- **Moral and ethical standards**: a community still divided between health and criminal responses.
