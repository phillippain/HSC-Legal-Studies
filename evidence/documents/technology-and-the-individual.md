## The impacts, and the legal response

| Impact | Where the law sits |
|---|---|
| **Personal data collected at scale** | *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles; mandatory **data breach notification** since 2018. Small businesses under $3m turnover are largely **exempt**, and there is no general right to sue for a breach of privacy |
| **Surveillance of individuals** | *Surveillance Devices Act 2007* (NSW); NSW moved to criminalise **secret GPS tracking** after reporting on devices bought by domestic violence offenders |
| **Automated decisions** | Rental application scoring (AHURI FR454, 2026) — weightings nobody outside the platform can see. Discrimination law attaches to grounds and to decisions a person makes; a weighting inside a model is neither |
| **Computer crime** | *Crimes Act 1900* (NSW) Pt 6 — unauthorised access, modification and impairment of data |
| **Police powers over devices** | LEPRA **ss 76AA–76AQ** digital evidence orders: a court can compel a password, and non-compliance is an offence |
| **Online harm and speech** | The eSafety Commissioner's removal powers; defamation reaching social media posts |

## Why enforcement is the hard part (`ind.3.3`)

- **Jurisdiction.** The server, the company and the harm are often in three countries.
- **Anonymity.** A remedy needs a respondent who can be identified and served.
- **Speed.** Material spreads faster than any takedown order issues.
- **Cost.** The individual's remedy is a civil action against a company with better lawyers.
- **Invisibility.** A person scored by a model, or tracked by a device, usually does not know it
  happened — and a right you do not know has been breached is never enforced.

## Future directions (`ind.3.4`)

Privacy Act reform (a direct right of action, and narrowing the small-business exemption), statutory
regulation of automated decision-making, national rules on tenant data following **"A Better Deal for
Renters"**, and the standing question of whether to legislate for AI specifically or to apply existing
law to it.

## Use it for

- The **contemporary issue** the syllabus requires, with the structural reason it is hard: the law
  governs **what the state may do to you** far better than **what a company may do with what it knows
  about you**.
- **Responsiveness** measured against a moving target — every reform above is a response to a harm
  that had already occurred at scale.
