- Charges should **reasonably reflect the criminality** of the conduct and give the court adequate sentencing power.
- Before agreeing to charge negotiation, prosecutors must **consult victims and police**; under Crimes (Sentencing Procedure) Act s 35A, the court requires a certificate that this was done.
- The Director of Public Prosecutions is independent of government (Director of Public Prosecutions Act 1986).

## Use it for

Prosecutorial discretion in charge negotiation — guided, but largely unreviewable by courts (*Greta*, *Koch*).
