When an Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander person is detained, the **custody manager must notify** the Aboriginal Legal Service's Custody Notification Service, which gives legal advice and checks on the person's welfare. Notification has been **mandatory since 2016**.

It responds to the **Royal Commission into Aboriginal Deaths in Custody (1991)**, which found that many deaths followed failures of care in police cells.

## Use it for

Rights of suspects in custody (LEPRA Part 9), and a legal measure with a non-legal service (the ALS) doing the work.

Check the current regulation number and funding arrangements before quoting them.
