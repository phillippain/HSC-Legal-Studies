The Youth Koori Court (Parramatta 2015; Surry Hills 2019; Dubbo 2023) runs within the Children's Court
under a practice note. Aboriginal young people who have pleaded or been found guilty meet Elders,
family and services to agree an Action and Support Plan before sentence.

BOCSAR compared **151 participants** with **2,883** similar Aboriginal young people in the ordinary
Children's Court:

- **40% less likely** to receive a custodial sentence at finalisation;
- **84% less likely** to receive custody on reconviction;
- **no statistically significant reduction** in reoffending.

## Use it for

- **Children's Court procedures** (`crime.5.3`) and **effectiveness**: success on custody, not on
  reoffending. Saying both is stronger than claiming it 'reduces crime'.

## Caution

It operates at three sites, under a practice note rather than its own Act, so access depends on
where a child lives.
