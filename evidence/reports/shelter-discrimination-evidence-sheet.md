## The protections

| Law | Jurisdiction | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| *Anti-Discrimination Act 1977* (NSW) | NSW | Discrimination in **accommodation** on grounds including race, sex, disability, marital or domestic status, homosexuality and age |
| *Racial Discrimination Act 1975* (Cth) | Commonwealth | Racial discrimination in housing and accommodation |
| *Disability Discrimination Act 1992* (Cth) | Commonwealth | Discrimination against people with disabilities in accommodation |
| *Sex Discrimination Act 1984* (Cth) | Commonwealth | Sex, pregnancy, family responsibilities and marital status in housing |

## The evidence that they are not enough

| Indicator | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Renters reporting discrimination when applying | **27%** (2023) | AHURI |
| Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander renters reporting discrimination | **32%** (2022) | AIHW |
| People with disability reporting discrimination in housing access or tenancy | **21%** (2021) | ABS |
| Complaints to the AHRC | **1,426** under the DDA and **476** under the RDA (2023) | AHRC |

## Case law

- **Huntley v State of New South Wales** (ADT, 2007) — an Aboriginal applicant successfully
  challenged discriminatory treatment in housing allocation.
- **Lamb v Samuels Real Estate Pty Ltd** (ADT, 2011) — an agency discriminated by refusing
  accommodation because of a **guide dog**.

## Use it for

The gap between **prohibition and enforcement** is the whole argument. Four statutes prohibit it;
between a fifth and a third of renters in the affected groups report experiencing it; and the AHRC
receives complaints in the hundreds, not the hundreds of thousands. A complaints-based enforcement
model asks the person discriminated against — often while they are looking for somewhere to live —
to run the case.

## Caution

The survey figures are **self-reported perception** of discrimination, which is not the same as a
proven contravention, and the three surveys use different years and methods. Complaint counts
measure complaining, not discrimination.
