The Commission reviewed the *Crimes (Sentencing Procedure) Act 1999* as a whole. It found the Act
complex and the s 21A lists of aggravating and mitigating factors confusing, and recommended
simplifying them, keeping judicial discretion and the guideline judgment scheme, and replacing the
patchwork of community-based orders.

## Use it for

- **Law reform agencies** (`inv.1.8`) and **responsiveness**: the report preceded the 2017 Act that,
  from 24 September 2018, replaced suspended sentences, home detention, community service orders and
  bonds with ICOs, CCOs and CROs (`crime.4.6`).
- **Statutory guidelines** (`crime.4.1`) — an independent body arguing for discretion over formula.

## Caution

The government did not adopt every recommendation; say 'many recommendations fed the 2018 reforms',
not that the reforms implemented the report.
