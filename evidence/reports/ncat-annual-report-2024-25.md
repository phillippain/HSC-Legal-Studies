## Use it for

- **Courts and tribunals** (`shel.2.6`) — the structural fact that most answers miss is that the
  courts have almost no role in ordinary shelter disputes and the tribunal has almost all of it.
  Over 45,000 of the Division's applications are shelter matters.
- **Accessibility**, with the caveat that matters more: the forum is equally open to both parties,
  and the consequences of using it are not symmetrical. The tenancy and social housing applications
  are overwhelmingly brought **by providers** seeking termination or arrears, and defended by tenants
  who are usually unrepresented.
- **Resource efficiency** — a 96.9% clearance ratio is a genuine achievement and worth conceding
  before criticising anything else.
- Set the 50,963 matters NCAT finalised against the handful of shelter appeals decided by the Court
  of Appeal: **extent measured by caseload and extent measured by consequence give opposite answers**.
