58 pages, 13 major offences, 62 offence types, 28 SA4s. Published quarterly — the September
Quarter 2026 update lands around December 2026.

## Ten-year trends (Table 1.6, AAPC, June 2017 → June 2026)

| Offence | 2017 | 2026 | 10-yr |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sexual assault | 5,703 | 13,308 | **+9.9%** |
| Steal from retail store | 24,147 | 32,835 | +3.5% |
| Domestic violence related assault | 28,755 | 38,844 | +3.4% |
| Break and enter dwelling | 29,020 | 17,941 | −5.2% |
| Steal from motor vehicle | 40,092 | 24,596 | −5.3% |

Retail theft is the only property offence rising, and the fastest riser over two years.

## The clearance gap (Fig 3.8, proceedings within 90 days)

Murder **74%** · DV assault **73%** · robbery 43% · non-DV assault 30% · break and enter dwelling
15% · **sexual assault 5%** (13,194 recorded, 693 proceedings). The fastest-rising offence over ten
years has the lowest clearance rate of the thirteen — and it fell from 6% to 5%. This is the
sharpest single contrast in the report.

## Discretion has moved, not shrunk

| Activity | 2016–17 | 2025–26 |
|---|---|---|
| Persons scanned / wanded | — (power from Dec 2024) | 43,445 |
| Taser use | 642 | 2,290 |
| ADVO compliance checks | 11,655 (2018) | 115,355 |
| Person searches | 236,944 | 100,430 |
| Move-on directions | 106,629 | 44,117 |
| Strip searches | 4,459 | 1,802 |

Discretionary street powers (LEPRA s 21 searches, s 198 move-on, Pt 4 Div 4 strip searches) are in
sustained decline; statutory, targeted and technologically mediated powers — wanding, which needs
no warrant and no suspicion — are rising.

## Two-year movers

Up: blackmail and extortion +35.4% · **coercive control +25.1%** (303→379) · breach bail +6.9%
(56,820→60,714) · breach ADVO +6.5% (26,781→28,515). Down: fail to appear −57.6% · fraud −7.2%.

## The caveats BOCSAR publishes about its own data

Willingness to report; offences *detected* rather than reported track police activity; visitor
populations distort rates; LGAs under 3,000 residents are unreliable. "Stable" means no significant
trend, not no change. "Proceeded against" counts persons of interest, not convictions.

Quoting a caveat is the cheapest way to show source evaluation in an extended response.

## Gap

BOCSAR is NSW-only, so it says nothing about **international crime** — use AFP or AUSTRAC there.
