BOCSAR compared offenders given an intensive correction order with similar offenders given a prison
sentence of up to two years, using matching to control for differences between the groups. ICO
offenders had between **11% and 31% lower odds of reoffending**, depending on the model.

## Use it for

- **Types of penalties** (`crime.4.6`) — the evidence for supervised community sentences, published
  the year before NSW replaced suspended sentences and home detention with the new ICO (2018).
- **Purposes** (`crime.4.2`) — rehabilitation and specific deterrence: prison is not the most
  effective deterrent for these offenders.

## Caution

The study covers the old ICO (before 2018) and short sentences only; it says nothing about serious
violent offenders. Matching reduces but cannot remove differences between the groups.
