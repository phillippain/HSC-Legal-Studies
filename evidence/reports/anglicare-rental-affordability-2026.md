Anglicare surveys every listing advertised on a single weekend and tests each against income
support and minimum wage budgets. In 2026: **one** of roughly **49,000** listings nationally was
affordable for a single person on JobSeeker.

## Use it for

- **Affordability** — the single most quotable figure in the option, and a non-government
  organisation acting as both researcher and advocate (shel.2.9).
- Against **ICESCR art 11(1)** and CESCR General Comment No 4's affordability criterion: Australia has
  ratified the covenant and this is the measured result.

## Caution

A one-weekend snapshot by an advocacy organisation, and affordability is defined against income
support rates that are themselves a policy choice. Name the method — it is a strength, not a
weakness, and naming it is what source evaluation looks like.
