The Department publishes this summary every month.

| Measure, 30 September 2026 | Figure |
|---|---|
| People in immigration detention facilities | 1,182 (1,092 men, 85 women, 5 children) |
| Average time in detention | 432 days |
| Detained more than 5 years | 62 (5.2%) |
| Children in immigration detention centres | 0 (5 in alternative places of detention) |
| Living in the community under a residence determination | 96 |
| Largest nationality groups | New Zealand 159, Iran 99, India 71 |

## Use it for

- **exp.4.3 / exp.6.2** — detention has no time limit: an average of more than a year.
- **exp.6.1** — very few children are now detained.

## Caution

Most people in detention are **not** asylum seekers: many are there after a visa cancellation, which is
why New Zealand is the largest group. Do not use the total as "the number of asylum seekers detained".
The figures change monthly — check the latest release.
