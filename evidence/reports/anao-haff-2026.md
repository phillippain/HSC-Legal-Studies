As at May 2026, **1,432** of a targeted **40,000** Housing Australia Future Fund homes had been
delivered — **under 4%**. Against the National Housing Accord, **307,635** homes were completed to
March 2026 where roughly **420,000** were required to be on track.

NSW has about **250** registered community housing providers managing **70,747** properties, against
roughly **2.3 million** individual investors, about 70% of whom own a single property.

## Use it for

- **Social housing** and the **obligation to provide shelter** — a commitment, a target and an
  audited result, which is exactly the shape of evidence an effectiveness question wants.
- The **ANAO** itself as a mechanism of accountability (Preliminary ind.2.7, statutory bodies) —
  an independent auditor publishing a number the government would not have chosen to publish.
