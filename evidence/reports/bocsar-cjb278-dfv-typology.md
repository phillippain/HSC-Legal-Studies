BOCSAR linked records from **seven** NSW government systems — courts, police victimisation, child
protection, out-of-home care, homelessness services and housing — for **196,701** people with a
domestic and family violence offence finalised in court between 2008 and 2023. **77.8%** were male;
**27.2%** identified as Aboriginal.

Clustering was run on the 71,896 people born from 1987, whose childhood records are complete, and
separately for men and women. Reoffending and revictimisation were deliberately **kept out** of the
clustering, then used to describe the groups — so the groups are not defined by the outcome they
are being used to explain.

## Eight groups

Four male, four female. The largest male group (M4, 15,669) is moderate offending with recent DFV
charges and elevated child protection contact; **M3** (14,744) has the highest lifetime offending,
the highest childhood maltreatment score and the highest DFV reoffending rate. Among women, **F3**
(5,212) has the highest lifetime offending *and* the highest victimisation history, with **28.7%
first in court for DFV before turning 18**.

Whole-sample 12-month rates: general reoffending **21.7%**, DFV reoffending **9.6%**, general
revictimisation **17.8%**, DFV revictimisation **7.1%**.

## Use it for

- **Factors affecting criminal behaviour**, with something better than a list: childhood
  maltreatment, disadvantage, out-of-home care and homelessness service use separate the groups,
  and they separate men and women differently.
- **Why a single response fails.** If there are eight profiles, one perpetrator program is being
  asked to do eight jobs. That is a law reform argument with evidence under it.
- **Victim–offender overlap** — the female groups are victim-offenders. Use it wherever a question
  asks about balancing the rights of victims and offenders as though they were different people.

## Caution

The clustering is descriptive, not causal, and the cohort born from 1987 is younger than the full
sample. Group sizes are of the 71,896 clustered, not of the 196,701.
