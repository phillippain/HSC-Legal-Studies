**122,494** people were counted as experiencing homelessness on Census night 2021. About **289,000**
clients used Specialist Homelessness Services nationally in 2024–25.

## Use it for

**Homelessness** as a contemporary issue, and the point that the person with the greatest need has
the least legal protection: no tenure, so no *Residential Tenancies Act*, no Torrens title, and a
relationship with public space governed by trespass, move-on directions and public-order offences.

## Caution

The Census counts on one night and uses a statistical definition that includes severely crowded
dwellings — a large share of the total. The service figure counts **clients**, not people without
housing. Both undercount rough sleeping. Quote the definition with the number.
