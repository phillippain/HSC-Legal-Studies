Youth on Track provides case management for 10–17-year-olds at risk of reoffending. In the trial:

- 12 months: about 48% of the comparison group and 51% of Youth on Track participants reoffended;
  **no statistically significant difference** at 12 or 24 months.
- At 24 months participants were 3.2 points less likely to reoffend and 3.5 points less likely to
  enter custody, neither significant.
- Participants were **6.2 points more likely to be employed** at exit (significant).

## Use it for

- **Early intervention** as a non-court alternative (`crime.5.5`), with honest evidence.

## Caution

Some subgroups had much larger reductions; the program's own snapshot figures are more positive than
the trial.
