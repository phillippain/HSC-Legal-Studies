156,256 defendants in finalised appearances, up **21.0%** on 2021. **86.4%** were found guilty of at
least one charge (90.7% in 2021). **8.9%** had bail refused at finalisation, up from 7.5%.

## The numbers worth carrying into an answer

| | 2025 |
|---|---|
| Median arrest to finalisation, guilty plea | **76 days** |
| Median arrest to finalisation, defended hearing or trial | **247 days** — 3.3× longer |
| All charges withdrawn | 12,772, **+126%** on 2021 (5,644) |
| Custodial sentences, Supreme / District / Local / Children's | 89.8% / 71.8% / 8.4% / **5.3%** |
| District Court appeals finalised | 7,285, **61.6% upheld** in whole or part; 79% on severity |
| Children's Court guilty rate | **67.4%**, down from 80.8% in 2021; withdrawals up 154% |
| Proven domestic violence defendants | 26,407, **+29.7%**; custody 13.6% against 9.5% overall |
| Bail refused, Aboriginal defendants | **14.9%** against 8.9% for all defendants |
| Imprisonment share, Aboriginal defendants | **16.0%** against 9.4% for all defendants |

## Use it for

- **Court delay as a trade-off, not a failure.** 247 against 76 days is the cost of contesting a
  charge. An answer that says delay proves the system ineffective has not noticed that the delay
  buys the defended hearing.
- **Appeals as evidence of review and correction** — 61.6% upheld is a striking figure, and it cuts
  both ways: the system corrects itself, and it got 61.6% of those sentences wrong the first time.
- **Equality before the law.** Aboriginal defendants are refused bail and imprisoned at roughly
  1.7× the rate of all defendants. That is the clearest statistical statement of inequality in the
  Crime topic.
- **The withdrawal figure (+126%)** is prosecutorial discretion, visible.

## Caution

BOCSAR says it plainly: finalised court appearances are **not** crimes reported to police, and
associations do not establish causes. The Aboriginality figures use a revised derived method from
2025, so they cannot be compared with older releases. BSOC 2023 offence groups are not comparable
with pre-2025 reports.
