Recommendations reported on release (Justice Reform Initiative, 18 Sept 2026; Sydney Criminal
Lawyers, 25 Sept 2026):

- **National minimum standards** for youth justice, developed by the Attorney-General with the
  states and territories; reported to include a minimum age of criminal responsibility of 14,
  detention as a last resort, and bans on spit hoods, solitary confinement and holding children in
  adult prisons;
- a National Child and Community Safety Taskforce; a public education campaign on youth crime;
  implementing OPCAT inspections; a further inquiry into the causes of youth incarceration.

## Use it for

- **Effectiveness** across Part 5, and the limits of national action when youth justice is a state
  responsibility.
