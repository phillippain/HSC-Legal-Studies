From **29 February 2024**, NSW police may give an adult found with a small amount of a prohibited drug a **$400 Criminal Infringement Notice**, waived if the person completes a health intervention, instead of a Court Attendance Notice.

- **BOCSAR CJB 272**: only about **22%** of eligible events received a CIN; the rest were charged.
- **SMH, 9 August 2025 ('postcode lottery')**: 7.3% of 18,702 people caught with drugs were fined rather than charged (to mid-2025); 49 of 58 Police Area Commands charged 90% or more; Auburn diverted 32%, 20 commands under 2%. MDMA 29% and cocaine 23% diverted, but methamphetamine 3% and heroin 2%.

## Use it for

Police discretion (charge, divert or caution), equality before the law, and diversion in Sentencing and Young Offenders.

## Caution

The SMH and BOCSAR figures cover different periods and definitions. Library worksheet: *Application in Action – Drug diversion*.
