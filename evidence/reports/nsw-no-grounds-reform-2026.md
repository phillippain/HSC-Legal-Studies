Before the reform, **45%** of NSW leases ended with **no reason given**. One year after the
no-grounds ban, **84%** of tenancies ended **at the renter's choice**.

## Use it for

Evidence that a tenancy reform **worked**, which is rarer and more valuable in an answer than
evidence that one failed. It also lets you take a position on the contested core of the option:
does regulating providers reduce supply? Industry transaction data covering a single month during an
announcement effect says yes; UNSW City Futures and AHURI research on completed reform cycles says
no. A band 6 response **resolves** that dispute rather than reporting it.

## Caution

A government reporting on its own reform. Say so, and pair it with independent research.
