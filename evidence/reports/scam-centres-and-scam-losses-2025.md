## The losses

Australians reported losing **$2.18 billion** to scams in **2025**, up 7.8%, across **481,523
reports**. Investment scams cost the most, and older Australians lost the most.

## The compounds

Many scams are run from compounds in **Myanmar and Cambodia** where trafficked workers are forced to
defraud victims; UN estimates put **120,000** people in Myanmar's compounds alone. In **October 2025**
the US and UK sanctioned Cambodia's **Prince Group** and its chairman **Chen Zhi**; in **January 2026**
he was arrested in Cambodia and sent to **China**, not the US.

## Australia's response

- AFP partnership work and victim warnings; support for Thai police.
- The **Scams Prevention Framework Act 2025** places duties on banks, telcos and digital platforms.
- Australia had **not** sanctioned the scam networks by October 2026.

## Use it for

- **Two transnational crimes in one**: fraud on Australians and trafficking of the workers.
- **Limitations**: offenders offshore, corruption protecting the compounds, victims who rarely recover
  money.
