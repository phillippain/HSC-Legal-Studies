- **art 12** — equal recognition before the law and legal capacity.
- **art 13** — access to justice, including procedural and age-appropriate accommodations.
- **art 14** — liberty and security of the person; disability alone never justifies detention.

Australian implementation runs through the *Disability Discrimination Act 1992* (Cth), the *National
Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013* (Cth) and, in NSW, s 14 of the MHCIFPA 2020 — with the
Disability Royal Commission as the reform agency.

## Use it for

A Human Rights contemporary issue that is **also** a Crime answer: the JAS Diversion evaluation
(BOCSAR CJB 279) shows what art 13 access to justice looks like when it is funded, and what it looks
like when it is not — six court locations out of more than a hundred.
