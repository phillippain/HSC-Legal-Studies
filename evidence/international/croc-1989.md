The articles that recur in this course:

| Article | Substance | Where it bites |
|---|---|---|
| **art 3(1)** | Best interests of the child a primary consideration | s 60CA FLA; every parenting judgment |
| **art 9(3)** | Contact with both parents | *Billings & Clowes (No 2)* |
| **art 12** | The child's right to be heard | *Billings* — where X was **never interviewed** |
| **arts 7, 8, 9** | Identity, name, and knowing one's parents | *Wilkerson & Croxford*, decided on these |
| **art 19** | Protection from all forms of violence | *Attar & Chawd*, against ICCPR art 18 |
| **art 37(b)** | Detention a last resort, for the shortest appropriate period | "adult time" for young offenders |
| **art 40** | A distinct juvenile justice system | Children's Court; *Young Offenders Act 1997* |

The UN Committee on the Rights of the Child has recommended Australia raise the minimum age of
criminal responsibility to **14**; NSW remains at **10**.

## Use it for

CROC is the one international instrument in this course with judgments attached. *Wilkerson &
Croxford* shows it **doing the deciding**; *Billings & Clowes (No 2)* shows art 12 being defeated in
practice because a parent would not permit an interview. Cite both and the answer stops being
decorative.
