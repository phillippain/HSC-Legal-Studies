Adoption is a **state** matter (the Commonwealth has no power over it), administered in NSW by the
Department of Communities and Justice and ordered by the **Supreme Court**. The 2000 Act replaced the
*Adoption of Children Act 1965* after a NSW Law Reform Commission review and a parliamentary inquiry
into the forced adoptions of the 1950s–70s, and it reversed the old model of secrecy.

## Key principles and rules

| Topic | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Best interests** | s 8 — the child's best interests are the paramount consideration; adoption is a service for the child, not a right of adults; the child's wishes must be considered and a child over 12 must consent |
| **Consent** | Part 5 — the birth parents' consent is required, given no earlier than 30 days after birth with a 30-day revocation period; the Supreme Court may **dispense with consent** where the parent cannot be found, is incapable, or where the child has been in care and adoption is clearly in their interests |
| **Who can adopt** | ss 26–28 — a couple (married, de facto, including **same-sex since the *Adoption Amendment (Same Sex Couples) Act 2010***) for two years or more, or a single person; step-parents in limited cases |
| **Open adoption** | Part 4 and **adoption plans** (s 46) — agreed arrangements for contact and information between the child, birth family and adoptive family; the Register of Reunion Information and access to birth records at 18 |
| **Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander children** | ss 35–39 — the **Aboriginal child placement principles**: adoption is the last resort; consultation with Aboriginal community; placement within kin, community or other Aboriginal family in that order |
| **Intercountry adoption** | Part 4 Div 2 — under the Hague Convention (1993), given effect by Commonwealth regulations; the *Korea adoption inquiry* record shows the current scrutiny |
| **Permanency from care** | 2018 amendments to this Act and the *Care and Protection Act* set permanency planning timeframes (6 months for under-2s, 12 months otherwise) and made adoption a preferred option after restoration and guardianship — the *Adoption of AMB and JJE* record |

## Use it for

- **fam.1.6** — the statutory framework; students should know consent, best interests, openness
  and the placement principles.
- **fam.3.4** — adoption from out-of-home care, the policy that made NSW the state with the most
  care adoptions, and the controversy for Aboriginal children (the *Australian* 2025 record).
- **ls.2.14 / croc-1989** — CROC art 21 incorporated through state law; the Hague Convention
  through Commonwealth regulation.
- **fam.t.values** — from closed to open adoption, and from excluding to including same-sex
  couples: two reversals in one Act.

## Caution

Adoption numbers are small (around 100–150 a year in NSW, most from care) — take current figures
from the AIHW *Adoptions Australia* report. Section numbers are indicative; cite the principle.
Surrogacy is not adoption — a parentage order under the *Surrogacy Act 2010* is the route there.
