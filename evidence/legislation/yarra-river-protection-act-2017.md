*Wilip-gin Birrarung murron* is Woi-wurrung for "keep the Birrarung alive". The Act opens with a preamble in Woi-wurrung and English written by the Wurundjeri, recognising the river as the lifeblood of the Wurundjeri and their "intrinsic connection" to it.

## What it does

- Declares the Yarra and its parklands **one living and integrated natural entity** to be protected as a whole rather than lot by lot.
- Sets out **Yarra protection principles**, including that "Aboriginal cultural values, heritage and knowledge" must be acknowledged and the Traditional Owners' role as custodians recognised.
- Creates the **Birrarung Council**, an independent statutory voice for the river that must include at least two Wurundjeri members (originally; now Traditional Owner members), to advise the Minister and report to Parliament.
- Requires a 50-year **Yarra Strategic Plan** (*Burndap Birrarung burndap umarkoo*, 2022) across the councils and agencies that manage the river.

## Use it for

- **inv.2.3** — interconnectedness of Country, Peoples and Cultural Practice written into statute: the river, the people and their obligations treated as one thing.
- **inv.2.4** — responsibilities for caring for Country given a legal form (water and land management), and a model other jurisdictions have cited.

## Caution

Victorian law, with no NSW equivalent; the Council advises and cannot stop a development. It is recognition of custodianship, not ownership or a water entitlement.
