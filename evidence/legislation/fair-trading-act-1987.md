Most of the Shelter statutes say what landlords, agents, operators and builders must do; the *Fair
Trading Act* and the agency it creates are how NSW finds out whether they did. **NSW Fair Trading**
(within the Department of Customer Service) administers the *Residential Tenancies*, *Strata*,
*Land Lease*, *Retirement Villages*, *Boarding Houses*, *Home Building* and *Property and Stock
Agents* Acts, holds the **Rental Bond Board**, and runs the complaint and mediation service that
sits in front of NCAT.

## What the Act provides

- **The Commissioner (Part 2)** — a statutory office with functions to investigate, conciliate
  complaints, take proceedings, publish warnings and advise the Minister on consumer law.
- **Investigation powers (Part 2 Div 3 and Part 6)** — authorised officers may enter business
  premises, require documents, and issue **penalty notices**; the Commissioner may prosecute offences
  under the Acts Fair Trading administers and seek **undertakings** and **public warnings**.
- **Consumer protection** — the Act applies the **Australian Consumer Law** as a law of NSW (s 28),
  so misleading conduct by agents and traders is actionable in NSW courts and NCAT.
- **Property services** — the Commissioner also licenses real estate agents (*Property and Stock
  Agents Act 2002*), which is the lever over underquoting and rent bidding.

## The Rental Commissioner and the Taskforce

The **NSW Rental Commissioner** (appointed 2023) is an administrative appointment within Fair
Trading, not a statutory office — created by government decision to drive the 2024 reforms and
advise on their implementation. The **$8.4 million Rental Taskforce** (announced February 2025)
sits under the Commissioner with inspectors targeting no-grounds evictions, rent bidding, bond
breaches and non-compliant agents; its first enforcement figures came in 2025–26.

## Use it for

- **shel.2.8 (government organisations)** — the one agency that touches every Shelter tenure, and
  the statute it acts under.
- **shel.2.5** — Fair Trading's free complaint service as the first rung of dispute resolution;
  mediation before NCAT.
- **Evaluation of the 2024 reforms** — the Tenants' Union's point that "renters shouldn't have to
  carry the load of enforcing tenancy laws": whether the Taskforce changes that is the test of
  enforceability.
- **shel.2.9** — Fair Trading funds TAAP (via the Rental Bond Board) and works with the Tenants'
  Union: cooperation between a regulator and the NGO sector.

## Caution

The Act is a framework; the **powers used in a tenancy case** are usually in the *Residential
Tenancies Act* itself (penalty notices, offences) and this Act supplies the officer and the
procedure. Section numbers above are indicative. Rental Commissioner and Taskforce figures should
be taken from Fair Trading's own releases (the nsw-rental-taskforce-2025 record).
