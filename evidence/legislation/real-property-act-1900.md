Under the Torrens system, registration confers title. **s 42** makes the registered proprietor's
title **indefeasible**, subject to limited exceptions (most importantly fraud). Interests are
protected by registration and by caveat, not by possession of a document.

## Use it for

The top rung of the tenure ladder. In an evaluation question, the contrast does the work: the state
guarantees the title of a person who can afford to buy, and offers a person who cannot a statutory
tenancy that can be ended, or, under s 8(c) of the RTA, nothing at all. **Effectiveness tracks
tenure, not need** — and s 42 is the evidence for the top of that claim.
