- Registrable persons report personal details to police: address, vehicles, employment, online
  identities and unsupervised contact with children, and report changes and travel.
- Reporting periods are generally **8 years, 15 years or life** for adults, depending on the
  offences; shorter for juvenile offenders. Failing to comply is an offence.
- The **Child Protection Register** is held by police. NSW has **no public register**. Registered
  persons are barred from child-related work.

## Use it for

- **Sex offender registration** (`crime.4.8`) — community protection against privacy and
  reintegration.

## Caution

Registration follows automatically from conviction for a registrable offence; it is not part of the
sentence the court imposes.
