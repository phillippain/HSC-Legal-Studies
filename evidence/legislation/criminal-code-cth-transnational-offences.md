| Crime | Law | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Importing a commercial quantity of a border-controlled drug | Part 9.1 (s 307.1) | Life |
| Slavery, servitude, forced labour, **forced marriage** | Div 270 | Slavery 25 years |
| Trafficking in persons | Div 271 | 12–25 years |
| Money laundering | Div 400 | Up to life (amounts of $10m+ from crime) |
| Unauthorised access, ransomware | Part 10.7 | Up to 10 years |
| Genocide, crimes against humanity, war crimes | Div 268 | Life (most) |
| People smuggling | Migration Act s 233A (aggravated ss 233B–233C) | 10–20 years |

The Commonwealth relies on the **external affairs power** (Constitution s 51(xxix)) to implement treaty
obligations. Several offences apply **extraterritorially**, to conduct overseas.

In July 2026 the government announced a **'failure to prevent' modern slavery offence** for companies.

## Use it for

- **Domestic measures** (`crime.6.2`): name the Division, not just 'the law'.
- Most transnational crime is tried in **domestic courts**; international bodies help find and return
  the offenders.
