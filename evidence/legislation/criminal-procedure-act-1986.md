| Provision | What it does |
|---|---|
| Court Attendance Notices | Police start most prosecutions with a CAN instead of an arrest |
| Charge certification and case conferencing (2018) | The DPP certifies the charges; prosecution and defence must confer about a plea before committal |
| ss 132–132A | Judge-alone trials: ordered if the prosecution agrees; otherwise only in the interests of justice; not without legal advice (s 132(6)) |
| s 281 | Admissions about indictable offences must generally be recorded |
| ss 292–292E | Jury directions in sexual offence trials on common misconceptions (from 2022) |
| s 294A | An unrepresented accused cannot personally cross-examine a sexual offence complainant |
| s 294CB | Limits on evidence of a complainant's sexual experience |
| Part 6 | Vulnerable witnesses: CCTV, recorded evidence, support persons |

## Caution

The Victim Experience Reforms Bill 2026 would amend several of these provisions; check its status.
