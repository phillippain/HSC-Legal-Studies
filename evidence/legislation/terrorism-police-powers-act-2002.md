- **Part 2A — preventative detention (2005)**: a court order can detain a person aged **16+** for up to **14 days** to prevent an imminent terrorist act or preserve evidence after one. Not for questioning.
- **Part 2AA — investigative detention (2016)**, inserted after the Parramatta shooting of Curtis Cheng: a person aged **14+** arrested in connection with a terrorist act may be detained for questioning for an initial **4 days**, extendable by an eligible judge to **14 days** in total.
- **September 2026**: Parliament extended the preventative detention powers (Law Society Journal, 25 September 2026).

Compare the ordinary limit under LEPRA Part 9: up to 6 + 6 hours before charge or release.
