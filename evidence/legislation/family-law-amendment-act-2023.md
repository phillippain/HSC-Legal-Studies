In force **6 May 2024**. The presumption of equal shared parental responsibility — and the
consequential requirement to consider equal or substantial and significant time — was **repealed**.
Section 60CC was rebuilt around a shorter list of factors with **safety first**, and decision-making
for major long-term issues is now **allocated** by the court rather than presumed (ss 61C, 61D).

## Use it for

- The **law reform** answer in the Family option, with two 2026 judgments applying it:
  *Bergsma & Hyde* (parenting, appellate) and *Billings & Clowes (No 2)* (sole decision-making with
  a duty to consult).
- **Law reform initiated by evidence**: the presumption was widely reported to have been read by
  parents as a right to equal time, and to have operated against safety. The amendment is a response
  to how a law was understood, not only to how it was written — a distinction worth a sentence in a
  Band 6 answer.
