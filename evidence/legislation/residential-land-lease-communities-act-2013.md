A **land lease community** (once called a residential park or caravan park) is a place where
residents own their home — a relocatable or manufactured dwelling — and pay **site fees** to the
operator who owns the land. NSW has around 500 communities and more than 40,000 residents, mostly
over 55 and concentrated on the coast; for many it is the only form of home ownership within
reach. The Act commenced on **1 November 2015** and is administered by NSW Fair Trading.

## Key features

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Site agreement** (Part 4) | Must be in writing, in the standard form, with a **disclosure statement** 14 days before signing; may be fixed-term or ongoing |
| **Site fees** (Part 7) | Increased either **by notice** (90 days' written notice, no more than once a year) or by a **fixed method** written into the agreement; a home owner (or 25% of those affected) can apply to **NCAT** for an order that an increase is **excessive** |
| **Community rules** (Part 6) | Operators may make rules about the use of the community; residents can challenge unreasonable rules in NCAT — the over-55s rule at Brooms Head is a 2026 example |
| **Utilities** (Part 8) | Operators in embedded networks can charge only within statutory limits (recast in 2024) |
| **Termination** (Part 11) | Grounds include non-payment, breach, and the operator's change of use of the land; notice of **12 months** (change of use) with **compensation** for relocation, and a Tribunal order required |
| **Residents' committees** (Part 9) | A statutory right to form a committee the operator must consult |

## Use it for

- **shel.2.4 (caravan parks)** — the current name and law for the tenure students still call
  "caravan parks"; pair with the 2024 amendment record for the reform story and the Brooms Head
  NCAT case for a dispute.
- **shel.1.4** — a split in the "bundle of rights": ownership of the structure, a lease of the
  land — which is why residents are exposed when the land is redeveloped.
- **shel.3.2 (older people)** — a tenure built on the retirement savings of asset-rich,
  income-poor residents; fee and utility caps are protection for a vulnerable group.
- **shel.2.5 / shel.2.6** — NCAT's Consumer and Commercial Division has a dedicated list.

## Caution

**Long-term casual occupants** (holiday vans) are outside this Act — see the *Holiday Parks
(Long-term Casual Occupation) Act 2002*. Part numbers are approximate — cite the topic, and check
the consolidated Act, which was substantially amended in 2024 with more changes foreshadowed for the
remaining 27 review recommendations.
