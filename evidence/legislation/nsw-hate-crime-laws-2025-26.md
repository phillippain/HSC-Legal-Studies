## 2025: inciting racial hatred

**s 93ZAA** (in force **15 August 2025**): intentionally and publicly inciting hatred on the ground of
race, maximum 2 years. A sunset clause (s 93ZAC) means it must be reviewed: the **Sackar Review**
recommended changes, and Portfolio Committee No 5 took submissions in **September 2026**. The Law
Society warned the offence is complex to enforce but said repeal now could send a mixed message.

## December 2025: after the Bondi Hanukkah attack

On **14 December 2025** an attack on a Hanukkah celebration at Bondi Beach killed 15 people. On
**24 December** Parliament passed the *Terrorism and Other Legislation Amendment* Bill:

- **s 93ZB**: displaying a prohibited terrorist organisation's symbol in public (2 years).
- **Public assembly restriction declarations**: police could prohibit public assemblies in an area for
  up to 90 days after a terrorism declaration.
- Tougher firearms laws.

In **April 2026** the **Court of Appeal** (Bell CJ, Ward P and Free JA) unanimously held the
protest-restriction scheme invalid because it impermissibly burdened the **implied freedom of
political communication**.

## June 2026: the Hate Crimes Act

Passed **2 June 2026** after attacks on LGBTQIA+ people:

- 'Post and boast' offences extended to assault and robbery.
- **Luring** offences: drawing a person to a place (including through dating apps) intending to
  commit a serious offence, even if no attack occurs.
- Threatening or inciting violence on a protected attribute (s 93Z): maximum raised from 3 to 5
  years, 7 if violence results.
- The *Crimes (Sentencing Procedure) Act 1999* amended to make hatred or prejudice easier to prove as
  a motive.

## Use it for

- **Public order offences** and **preliminary crimes** (luring) in `crime.1.5`.
- **Balance of rights**: free speech and assembly against protection from hatred, with a court
  enforcing the constitutional limit.
- **Law reform after an event**: fast, reactive and partly invalid.

## Caution

Section numbers for the 2026 offences and commencement dates were not checked against the enacted
Act. Check the s 93ZAA review outcome and any High Court appeal.
