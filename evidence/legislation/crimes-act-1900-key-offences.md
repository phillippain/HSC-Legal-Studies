The *Crimes Act 1900* (NSW) defines most indictable offences in NSW. Minor offences sit in the
*Summary Offences Act 1988*; drug offences in the *Drug Misuse and Trafficking Act 1985*;
Commonwealth offences in the *Criminal Code Act 1995* (Cth).

## Key offences

| Offence | Section | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Murder: intent to kill or inflict grievous bodily harm, or reckless indifference to human life | s 18 (penalty s 19A) | Life |
| Manslaughter: voluntary (partial defence) or involuntary (unlawful and dangerous act; criminal negligence) | s 24 | 25 years |
| Assault causing death (one punch) | s 25A | 20 years |
| Wounding or GBH with intent | s 33 | 25 years |
| Coercive control (from 1 July 2024) | s 54D | 7 years |
| Assault occasioning actual bodily harm | s 59 | 5 years |
| Common assault | s 61 | 2 years |
| Publicly threatening or inciting violence on a protected attribute | s 93Z | 5 years (from 2026) |
| Inciting racial hatred (from 15 August 2025) | s 93ZAA | 2 years |
| Displaying a prohibited terrorist symbol (December 2025) | s 93ZB | 2 years |
| Larceny | s 117 | 5 years |
| Fraud | s 192E | 10 years |
| Concealing a serious indictable offence | s 316 | 2–5 years |
| Attempt: same penalty as the full offence | s 344A | — |

## Parties

- **s 345**: principals in the second degree and accessories before the fact to a serious
  indictable offence may be tried and punished as principals.
- **s 347** and **s 349**: accessories after the fact (up to 25 years where the offence is murder).
- **s 351A**: recruiting a child to carry out criminal activity (12 years; 15 if the child is under
  16, from the June 2026 reforms).
- **s 351B**: aiding and abetting summary offences.

## Use it for

- **Name the section.** Markers reward 'murder (s 18 *Crimes Act 1900* (NSW))' over 'murder'.
- **Elements**: the s 18 definition shows the three mental states that can make a killing murder.
- **Law reform**: ss 54D, 93ZAA, 93ZB and 351A were all added or changed in 2024–26.

## Caution

Maximum penalties change: check the current text at legislation.nsw.gov.au before an assessment.
