| Defence | Section | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Self-defence | ss 418–420 | Complete; not available to cause death to protect property (s 420) |
| Excessive self-defence | s 421 | Murder to manslaughter |
| Extreme provocation | s 23 (2014) | Murder to manslaughter; deceased's conduct must be a serious indictable offence |
| Substantial impairment by abnormality of mind | s 23A | Murder to manslaughter; **accused proves it** on the balance of probabilities |
| Infanticide | s 22A | A mother who kills her child under 12 months while her mind is disturbed |
| Mental health or cognitive impairment | MHCIFPA 2020 | Special verdict: 'act proven but not criminally responsible' (replaced 'not guilty by reason of mental illness'); **accused proves it** |

Manslaughter carries a maximum of 25 years (s 24); murder, life (s 19A).

For other defences, once raised on the evidence the prosecution must disprove them beyond reasonable doubt.
