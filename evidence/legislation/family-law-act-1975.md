## Use it for

- **State and federal jurisdiction**: family law is Commonwealth, but domestic violence orders,
  child protection and the criminal law are state — the division of powers doing visible damage in
  *Fairleigh*.
- **Changing community values**: no-fault divorce in 1975, de facto access to the property regime
  from 1 March 2009, marriage equality in 2017, the parenting reforms of 2024.
