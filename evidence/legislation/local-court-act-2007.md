- The Local Court hears summary offences, indictable offences dealt with summarily, first appearances, bail and charge certification. Sentencing limit: 2 years for one offence, 5 years in total (Crimes (Sentencing Procedure) Act s 58).
- **Title change**: amendments introduced in September 2025 retitled magistrates as **judges** (including the Chief Magistrate and Children's Magistrates). In effect by April 2026; powers, functions and protections unchanged.
- Reasons given by Attorney-General Michael Daley: the court makes more than **90%** of NSW judicial decisions; criminal matters up **34%** since 2014; magistrates have had to be legally qualified since **1955** (LSJ, 2025).

## Caution

The syllabus still says 'magistrate'; both terms are acceptable. Check the exact commencement date.
