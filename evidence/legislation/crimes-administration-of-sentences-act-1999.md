- **Classification:** Corrective Services NSW classifies inmates by risk, from minimum to maximum
  security, with special classes such as 'extreme high risk restricted' (Regulation cl 15). This
  decides where they are held, programs, visits and work. **Protective custody** separates inmates at
  risk, such as former police and informants.
- **Parole:** for sentences of 3 years or less, release at the end of the non-parole period is
  automatic (court-based parole). For longer sentences the **State Parole Authority** decides;
  since the 2017 reforms, the safety of the community is the paramount consideration. Victims on the
  Victims Register can make submissions.
- **'No body, no parole' (2022):** the Authority must refuse parole to a person serving a sentence for
  murder who has not satisfactorily cooperated in identifying the location of the victim's remains.
  Passed while Chris Dawson was on trial for the murder of Lynette Dawson, whose body has never been
  found.

## Use it for

- **Post-sentencing considerations** (`crime.4.8`) — every one of security classification, protective
  custody and parole sits here. Loveridge (paroled April 2024), Skaf (paroled 2021) and Fairleigh
  (paroled April 2026, reoffended within weeks) test whether parole protects the community.

## Caution

Parole is supervision in the community for the balance of the sentence, not early release from it.
