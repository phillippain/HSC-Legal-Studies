| Section | Rule |
|---|---|
| s 55 | Relevance: could it affect the probability of a fact in issue |
| s 59 | Hearsay rule (exceptions include admissions) |
| ss 76, 79 | Opinion rule; exception for experts |
| s 89 | No adverse inference from silence during police questioning |
| s 89A (2013) | Serious indictable offences: inference allowed if a special caution was given and a lawyer was present |
| ss 97–98, 97A | Tendency and coincidence; s 97A (2021) presumes significant probative value of a child sexual offence accused's sexual interest in children |
| ss 84, 85, 90 | Admissions obtained by violence, unreliably or unfairly |
| s 138 | Improperly or illegally obtained evidence excluded unless the desirability of admitting it outweighs the undesirability (*R v White*; *R v Eliot (No 1)*) |
| s 141 | Criminal standard: beyond reasonable doubt |

Admissibility is argued at a **voir dire**, without the jury.

## Use it for

Investigation (s 138 as the check on police) and the trial (what the jury hears).
