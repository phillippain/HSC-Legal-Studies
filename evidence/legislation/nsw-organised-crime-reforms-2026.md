The context: ABC reporting (September–October 2026) attributes **41 murders since 2020** to
Sydney's underworld conflict over the drug trade, with at least six innocent people killed in
mistaken-identity attacks in 18 months. Two 16-year-olds and a 14-year-old were charged over the
Glen Alpine murder of Marco Tapia (September 2026). Taskforce Falcon reports **800+ arrests and
2,100+ charges**.

## June 2026 — Organised and Gang-related Crime Reforms (passed 4–5 June)

- A "kill car" offence: destroying a vehicle used in an aggravated offence (12 years).
- Firing at a building or vehicle: maximum 16 years, or 18 in public disorder or for organised crime.
- Recruiting a child to crime: 12 years, or 15 if aggravated (child under 16).
- Destroying business premises by fire or explosives (14 years).
- Bail: courts must consider organised crime links and breaches of SCPOs or firearms prohibition orders.
- Five years, not one, to seek forfeiture from people under drug trafficker declarations.

## September 2026 — Further Organised Crime Reforms (passed 24 September)

- **Interim serious crime prevention orders**: police can restrict a high-risk suspect's associates,
  movements, devices and internet use immediately while applying to a court for a final order.
- **Digital evidence access orders** for organised crime investigations; the Crime Commission can
  require devices on summons (refusal up to 4 years); penalties for defying the Commission doubled to 10 years.
- Four more offences, including shootings and firebombings, added to the **Serious Children's
  Indictable Offence** scheme (55 in total): children tried and sentenced as adults.
- Life imprisonment for planning a murder; new offences for running platforms used for organised crime.
- Unredacted toll-camera images; NSW joins the Commonwealth face-matching database.

## Use it for

- **crime.2.1** — the modern replacement for discretionary targeting (STMP, ended 2023): statutory,
  and mostly court-supervised, but interim SCPOs act before any hearing.
- **crime.5** — children tried as adults; the ALS, NATSILS and the Greens say it punishes exploited children.
- **Law reform** — fast, reactive reform after mistaken-identity killings.
