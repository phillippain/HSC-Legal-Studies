Summary offences are less serious offences heard by a **magistrate alone** in the **Local Court**.

| Offence | Section |
|---|---|
| Offensive conduct | s 4 |
| Offensive language | s 4A (often dealt with by penalty notice) |
| Custody of a knife in a public place | s 11C |
| Obstructing traffic; unauthorised public assemblies | Part 4 (ss 23–27B authorise assemblies) |

The Local Court can impose up to **2 years' imprisonment** for one offence and **5 years** in total
(*Crimes (Sentencing Procedure) Act 1999* s 58).

## Use it for

- **Summary offences** (`crime.1.6`): what they are and who hears them.
- **Public order offences** (`crime.1.5`).
- **Discretion**: offensive language charges have long been criticised as falling most often on
  Aboriginal people and young people.
