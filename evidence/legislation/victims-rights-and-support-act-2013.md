- **Charter of Victims Rights:** courtesy, compassion and respect; information about services and
  about the investigation and prosecution; protection from contact with the accused; to make a
  victim impact statement; to be told about the offender's parole and release through the Victims
  Register.
- **Victims Support Scheme:** counselling, financial assistance and recognition payments.
- **Commissioner of Victims Rights** handles complaints about Charter breaches.

## Use it for

- **The role of the victim** (`crime.4.4`) — the statutory basis for the victim's place.
- **Post-sentencing** (`crime.4.8`) — the Victims Register and parole submissions.

## Caution

The Charter is not enforceable by legal action: a breach gives no right to sue or to overturn a
decision. Molly Ticehurst's father learned of Billings' appeal from the media (ABC, 13 Oct 2026).
