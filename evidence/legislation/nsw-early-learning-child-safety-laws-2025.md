Passed on 22 October 2025, after alleged abuse in childcare and the June 2025 independent (Wheeler)
review of the old regulator inside the Department of Education, the Act legislated **more than 30
reforms**, several bringing forward changes Education Ministers agreed nationally in August 2025. On
23 October 2026 the Deputy Premier reported the first year's results.

## Before and after

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Regulator | Regulatory Authority inside the Department | Independent **NSW Early Learning Commission** (1 Dec 2025), ~6,100 services |
| Guiding duty | None paramount | Children's **safety, rights and best interests paramount**, above provider profits |
| Penalties | Lower; 15 fineable offences | **Tripled** (up to 900% for 25+ services); 30 fineable; court maximum $51,600 / $154,800 |
| Unreported incidents | Fine only via prosecution | **On-the-spot fines** |
| Individual staff | No suspension power | Commission can **suspend educators and volunteers** |
| Personal devices | $1,100 policy penalty, service only | $3,420 individual; $17,200 service |
| Transparency | Quality rating displayed | Compliance history at premises and online |

## First year (government figures)

**53** educators, staff or volunteers suspended; **8,776** visits since December 2025, **71%**
unannounced; **876** compliance actions in April–June 2026 (up 108%); **$324,290** in fines; 8 services
cancelled and 10 suspended. Affinity Education was fined $67,770 and capped at 100 NSW approvals.

## Use it for

- **fam.3.4 / fam.1.5** — the state protecting children in non-parental care through regulation rather
  than child-protection orders; best interests made paramount by statute.
- **Law reform** — media scandal → review → fast legislation → a new independent regulator; pair with
  the SMH Affinity reporting.

## Caution

A **class evidence summary of a ministerial media release** — a government reporting its own results,
with no critics or sector voices. Only the 876 figure could be matched to published Commission data,
and it covers one quarter. Not-for-profit providers had urged a pause on the reforms in 2025.
