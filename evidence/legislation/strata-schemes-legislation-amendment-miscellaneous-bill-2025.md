About **1.2 million** people in NSW live in strata, across **87,000+** schemes (up from about 70,000 in
2015); **over 30%** of schemes are two-lot schemes such as duplexes. The Bill implements **28
recommendations** of the 2021 statutory review of the 2015 strata Acts and is the government's
"final" response to it. Introduced 19 November 2025, passed the Assembly with amendments on
11 February 2026 and the Council without amendment on **15 October 2026**; the ministerial release
followed on 16 October.

## What changes

| | Current law | After commencement |
|---|---|---|
| Two-lot schemes | AGMs, meetings and a committee as for any scheme | **Exempt** (new s 7A); decisions by written resolution of **both** owners; no 10-year capital works plan or Strata Hub reporting |
| Bonds and fees on occupiers | Pet bonds banned; others largely unregulated | Bonds limited to the damage risk, fees to reasonable cost (**s 105AA**) — e.g. swipe-card bonds far above replacement cost |
| EV charger on your own lot | Ordinary renovation approval | Owner gives notice; committee has **3 months** to object reasonably; **silence is consent** (**s 132D**); owner pays and indemnifies (s 132E); a by-law that unreasonably prohibits it has no effect |
| Building manager term | Up to **10 years** | Up to **3 years** (**s 68**), regulations may vary |
| Ending a manager's agreement | Existing NCAT grounds | New ground: breach of a duty prescribed by regulation, e.g. undisclosed commissions (**s 72(3)(d1)**) |
| Fair Trading enforcement | Compliance notices and undertakings | Secretary may **publish** them, protected from defamation (**s 188ZD**) |

An EV dispute runs notice → objection → **Fair Trading mediation** (compulsory for most strata
disputes, s 227) → **NCAT** order for a no-objection notice.

## The sequence

2021 statutory review → **Dec 2023** first tranche → **Sep 2024** agents' conflicts and commissions
→ **Feb 2025** payment plans, levy certification, solar/EV by-law limits → **Oct 2025** building
manager duties and Fair Trading powers commence → **15 Oct 2026** fourth bill passes.

## Use it for

- **shel.2.4 / shel.2.3** — strata as a tenure where the neighbours make the rules, and the law
  stepping in on three power imbalances: owners corporation v lot owner (EV, bonds), owners v
  building managers (terms, commissions), and regulator v scheme (publication).
- **ls.4.1–4.3 (law reform)** — a textbook sequence: statutory review → four tranches over three
  years → regulations still to come. The Opposition supported the Bill while criticising the pace;
  crossbenchers pressed on insurance commissions, which remain under review, not in the Bill.
- **shel.t.values / responsiveness** — EV charging and sustainability as the social change the law
  is answering; set against lawyers' warnings that electrical upgrade costs may land on owners
  corporations and that embedded-network protections cover only future contracts.

## Caution

A **class-made summary** built around a **government media release promoting its own reform**. At
20 October 2026 the Parliament's bill page listed the Bill as **awaiting assent**; a practitioner
note of 16 October says it has since received assent — check the Act number and the
**commencement proclamation**, because most provisions start by proclamation and key details (what
is a reasonable EV objection, which bonds are banned) depend on **regulations not yet made**. The
Hansard speeches were read on the minister's website, not the parliamentary transcript. The claim
that an earlier tranche requires second quotes for work over $30,000 was not verified.
