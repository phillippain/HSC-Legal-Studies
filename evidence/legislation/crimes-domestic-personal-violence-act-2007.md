**s 14** makes contravening an apprehended domestic violence order an offence — maximum 2 years
and/or 50 penalty units. **s 14(4)** requires imprisonment where the contravention was an act of
violence, unless the court orders otherwise and, under **s 14(6)**, gives reasons.

## The compliance evidence

BOCSAR, 12 months to June 2026: **28,515** breach-ADVO incidents, a two-year rise of **6.5%**, and
**115,355** police ADVO compliance checks — up 19.2% in two years from 11,655 in 2018.

## Use it for

**Compliance and non-compliance**: the system's own protective orders are breached at record
levels while enforcement activity rises sharply. That pairing — more checking, more breaching — is
better evidence than either figure alone.
