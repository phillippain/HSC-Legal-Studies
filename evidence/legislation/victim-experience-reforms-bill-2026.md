**Status: introduced 22 September 2026; not passed at 20 October 2026.**

- **Pre-recorded evidence-in-chief** for all complainants and tendency witnesses in prescribed sexual offences (now limited to children under 16 and people with cognitive impairment).
- **Ground rules hearings**: a $4.8 million pilot at Parramatta, Wollongong and Dubbo District Courts, 31 March 2027 to 30 June 2030.
- **Domestic violence evidence-in-chief** usable in standalone ADVO proceedings; recordings not handed to the defence.
- Complainants not required at committal hearings without special reason; **court closed** while DV victim impact statements are read.
- Complainant evidence reusable in Crown appeals and retrials; **court leave** needed to access protected confidences (counselling records).

## Caution

A Bill, not law. Check whether it passed, and in what form.
