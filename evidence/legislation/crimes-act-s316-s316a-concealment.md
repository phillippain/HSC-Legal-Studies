- **s 316**: an adult who knows or believes a serious indictable offence has been committed, and has information that might be of material help to police, commits an offence by failing without reasonable excuse to bring it to police attention.
- **s 316A** (inserted 2018, after the Royal Commission into Institutional Responses to Child Sexual Abuse): any adult who knows or believes a child abuse offence has been committed must report it; failure is an offence. Religious confession is not an excuse.
- **Mandatory reporters** (teachers, doctors, police) also have duties to report children at risk of significant harm under child protection law.

## Use it for

**crime.2.2 (reporting crime)** — the rare cases where reporting is compulsory, set against the 'dark figure' of unreported crime.
