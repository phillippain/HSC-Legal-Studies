Policing is a **state** power. The Commonwealth has no general police power and the AFP exists for
Commonwealth laws, Commonwealth places and the territories. The syllabus asks what the agencies are
and how a citizen deals with them; these Acts answer the first.

## Police Act 1990 (NSW)

- **s 6** — the **NSW Police Force**: its mission is to work with the community to reduce violence,
  crime and fear; functions include preventing and detecting crime, protecting persons and property,
  and traffic.
- **The Commissioner** (Part 4) — appointed by the Governor; in charge of the Force, responsible to
  the Minister for Police but with operational independence by convention; the Minister cannot
  direct an investigation.
- **Oath of office** (s 13) — to "cause Her/His Majesty's peace to be kept and preserved" and act
  "without favour or affection, malice or ill-will".
- **Discipline** (Part 9) — the Commissioner's power to remove an officer for loss of confidence
  (s 181D), reviewable by the Industrial Relations Commission.
- **Powers** are not in this Act — they are in **LEPRA**, the *Crimes Act*, the *Bail Act*, the
  *Road Transport Act* and others.

## Australian Federal Police Act 1979 (Cth)

- Created after the 1978 Hilton bombing, merging the Commonwealth and ACT police.
- **Functions (s 8)** — Commonwealth offences (drug importation, fraud against the Commonwealth,
  terrorism, cybercrime, child exploitation, foreign interference), community policing in the ACT,
  protection of Commonwealth office-holders and places, and **international liaison** (officers in
  30+ countries; Operation *Ironside*/ANOM with the FBI).
- Works alongside the **Australian Criminal Intelligence Commission** (*Australian Crime Commission
  Act 2002*, coercive hearing powers), **AUSTRAC**, **Border Force** and state forces in joint
  taskforces.

## Oversight — where a citizen complains

| Force | Body | Act |
|---|---|---|
| NSW Police | **Law Enforcement Conduct Commission** — investigates serious misconduct; most complaints referred back to police internal investigation with LECC oversight | *LECC Act 2016* |
| NSW Police | **NSW Ombudsman** no longer oversees police (since 2017); the **Coroner** for deaths; the **Supreme Court** for civil actions (*Hamzy*, *Jones*) | |
| AFP | **Commonwealth Ombudsman**; **National Anti-Corruption Commission** (2023) for corruption; the AFP's own Professional Standards | *Ombudsman Act 1976* (Cth); *NACC Act 2022* |

## Caution

Officer numbers are from the NSW Police annual report. The Police Act was heavily restructured in
2001–02 — section numbers for discipline may differ in older notes. The National Anti-Corruption
Commission covers the AFP but not NSW Police (that is LECC and ICAC).
