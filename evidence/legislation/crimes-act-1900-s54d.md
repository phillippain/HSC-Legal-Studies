Inserted by the *Crimes Legislation Amendment (Coercive Control) Act 2022* (NSW) and commenced
**1 July 2024**. The offence requires a **course of conduct** engaged in repeatedly or continuously,
consisting of abusive behaviour (defined in s 54F), with an **intention to coerce or control**, and
judged against a reasonable-person test. Maximum penalty **7 years**. Section 54E provides a
reasonableness defence.

## Use it for

- **Law reform driven by changing values** — conduct that was once treated as a private matter
  became a criminal offence, and the offence describes a pattern rather than an incident.
- **Enactment is not enforcement.** BOCSAR recorded **379** coercive control incidents in the
  12 months to June 2026 and only **10** adults proceeded against to court.
- The **moral and ethical standards** theme: the law now names harm that leaves no physical trace.

## Caution

Commenced 1 July 2024, so it cannot apply to conduct before that date. Pair it with a statistic —
the offence's existence proves possibility, never typicality.
