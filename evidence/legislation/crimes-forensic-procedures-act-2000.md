- Police must first seek the suspect's **informed consent**.
- **Non-intimate** procedures (for example a self-administered mouth swab) on a suspect under arrest can be ordered by a **senior police officer**; **intimate** procedures, or procedures on a suspect not under arrest, need a **magistrate's order**. Reasonable force may be used to carry out an order.
- The Act governs procedures carried out **on** a person, not material they leave behind: hence *R v White* (2005), DNA from a cigarette butt.
- Risks: contamination and over-reliance (*Farah Jama*); privacy in mass screening (Wee Waa, 2000).
