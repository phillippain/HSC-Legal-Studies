- **s 5** — the age of criminal responsibility in NSW is **10**; *doli incapax* is presumed for
  children aged 10–13.
- **s 3** — defines a **serious children's indictable offence** (SCIO). An SCIO must be dealt with
  "according to law" in the District or Supreme Court; everything else is normally heard summarily
  in the Children's Court.
- **s 14** — **prohibits** recording a conviction against a child under 16, and gives the court a
  discretion for 16–17 year olds.
- **s 33** — the penalty ladder: dismissal with caution, good behaviour bond, fine, probation,
  community service order, and a **control order of no more than 2 years for a single offence**.

## Use it for

- **Age of criminal responsibility**, against the UN Committee on the Rights of the Child's
  recommendation that the minimum age be raised to 14.
- The "charged 94 times, never convicted" headline: s 14 means that describes the law operating
  **as Parliament designed it**, not a failure of it. This is the single most useful correction a
  student can make to a tabloid argument.
- **Jurisdiction as the reform lever** — adding offences to the SCIO list in s 3 moves children
  into the adult courts without changing a single penalty.

## Updates to October 2026

- **s 5 (from 1 March 2026)**: doli incapax codified: the court must consider the conduct, the circumstances, the child's development and education, and upbringing; rebuttal is open on the facts of the offending alone; s 5(8) stops diversion being used to prove capacity. See *Doli incapax and diversion reforms (2025–26)*.
- **s 6** principles: equal rights and a right to be heard; responsible but needing guidance; education and employment uninterrupted; live at home where possible; no greater penalty than an adult.
- **s 8**: proceedings against children should start by court attendance notice rather than arrest.
- **s 13**: a child's statement is inadmissible unless a responsible person (or, from 14, a chosen adult, or a lawyer) was present, or there was a proper reason for their absence.
- **s 15A**: no publication of the name of a child involved in criminal proceedings.
- **ss 17–19**: SCIOs are dealt with 'according to law'; s 18(1A) lets the court choose for related offences; s 19 lets a sentence be served in a youth justice centre. In September 2026 four shooting and firebombing offences were added: **55 SCIOs**.
- **s 33**: dismissal or caution; a conference outcome plan; a bond (up to 2 years); a fine (up to 10 penalty units); probation; a community service order; a suspended control order; a control order (2 years per offence, 3 years aggregate under s 33A).
