A three-rung hierarchy of alternatives to court — **warning**, **caution**, **youth justice
conference** — administered by police and the courts.

## The numbers that make it examinable

In the 12 months to June 2026 (BOCSAR): **3,166** warnings, **6,740** cautions and **947** youth
justice conferences, against **11,238** young people proceeded against to court. That is a
**45.6%** diversion share, against **3.9%** for adults — and adult diversion has fallen **7.0% a
year** for ten years while youth conferencing has risen 7.0%.

Discretion narrows as seriousness rises: for cannabis possession **14.2%** of young people are
proceeded against to court, against **62.2%** of adults; for domestic violence assault it is
**34.6%** of young people against **99.9%** of adults; for murder, 100% of both.

## Use it for

Any question on **discretion**, on **alternatives to court**, or on whether the criminal justice
system deals effectively with young offenders. The contrast with adult diversion is the strongest
single statistic in the topic.
