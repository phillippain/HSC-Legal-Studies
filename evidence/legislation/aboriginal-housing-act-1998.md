Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people are about 3.4% of the NSW population and roughly a
quarter of its homeless population (Census 2021), and AHURI's long-term analysis finds **one in
eight** Aboriginal households has unmet housing need — 81% of them in unaffordable rentals, 14%
in severe overcrowding. The *Aboriginal Housing Act 1998* is NSW's structural response: a
dedicated, Aboriginal-governed housing body.

## What the Act does

- **The Aboriginal Housing Office (AHO)** — a statutory body (now within Homes NSW) that owns about
  6,000 homes, funds **Aboriginal Community Housing Providers** (including Local Aboriginal Land
  Councils) to manage several thousand more, and plans Aboriginal housing across the state.
- **The Board** — all members Aboriginal, appointed to represent regions and communities; the Act
  requires the AHO to act on the Board's advice — self-determination written into the structure.
- **Functions** — to ensure Aboriginal people have access to affordable, quality housing; to build
  the capacity of the Aboriginal community housing sector; to administer Commonwealth and state
  funding (historically the Aboriginal Rental Housing Program and the 2008–18 National Partnership
  on Remote Indigenous Housing, which largely bypassed NSW).
- **Policy** — the *AHO Strong Family, Strong Communities* strategy (2018–28) and the **Aboriginal
  Housing Standards** for the sector; tenancies are under the *Residential Tenancies Act* like any
  social housing.

## Use it for

- **shel.3.2 (discrimination — Aboriginal people)** — a law that targets disadvantage rather than
  prohibiting discrimination: the "special measures" model.
- **shel.3.4 (social housing)** — a separately governed slice of the social housing system, and
  the overcrowding and repairs data that show its resourcing.
- **shel.2.8 / shel.2.9** — the AHO (government) and Aboriginal community housing providers (NGOs)
  as a cooperation model; the AHRC's YiSSA record shows what happens when an Aboriginal service
  loses its funding.
- **inv.2.1 / inv.2.2 / exp.4** — alongside land rights and native title, housing as a practical dimension of
  self-determination.

## Caution

Stock and waiting-list numbers are from AHO and DCJ reports and change — check the AHO annual report
before quoting. The Act creates an agency and a board; it contains **no entitlement** to housing.
Closing the Gap target 9 (88% of Aboriginal people in appropriately sized housing by 2031) is the
national benchmark to cite for effectiveness.
