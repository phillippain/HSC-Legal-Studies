## What happened

ASIO assessed that Iran's **Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC)** directed two attacks on
Australia's Jewish community: **Lewis' Continental Kitchen** in Sydney (October 2024) and the **Adass
Israel Synagogue** in Melbourne (December 2024). In August 2025 Australia expelled Iran's ambassador.

## The law

The 2025 Act created a framework for listing **state sponsors of terrorism**. On **27 November 2025** the
IRGC became the **first entity listed**. It is now an offence to direct its activities, be a member,
recruit, train, fund or support it, each carrying up to **25 years' imprisonment**.

## Use it for

- **Terrorism between the categories**: transnational in its networks, not an ICC crime.
- **Responsiveness**: Parliament legislated within months.
- **Limitations**: the people who ordered the attacks are beyond reach; the law targets anyone in
  Australia who helps them.
