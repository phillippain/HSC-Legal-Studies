Section 37 imposes a duty on any person carrying out construction work to **exercise reasonable care
to avoid economic loss caused by defects**, owed to owners and to each subsequent owner — including
owners corporations — and it applies retrospectively to work in the ten years before commencement.

## Why it exists

*Brookfield Multiplex* (2014) had held that a builder owed no such duty in negligence to a
subsequent owners corporation for pure economic loss. Section 37 is Parliament **reversing a High
Court decision**, and *Pafburn* [2024] HCA 49 then held the duty **non-delegable**, so a developer
cannot apportion it away to subcontractors.

## Use it for

The cleanest law reform chain on the **provider** side of the Shelter option: common law failure →
statutory duty → apex court construction → market response (decennial liability insurance). It also
answers the "initiating or responding?" discriminator directly — s 37 *responded* to *Brookfield*
and *initiated* the insurance reform that followed.
