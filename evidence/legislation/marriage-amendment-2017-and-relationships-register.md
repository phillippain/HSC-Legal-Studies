The recognition of same-sex relationships is the syllabus's standard case study of law responding to
changing values, and it happened in **stages**, each a separate statute.

| Year | Law | What it did |
|---|---|---|
| **1999** | *Property (Relationships) Legislation Amendment Act 1999* (NSW) | NSW recognised same-sex de facto couples for property division and other state laws (the first state to do so) |
| **2004** | *Marriage Amendment Act 2004* (Cth) | Inserted "a man and a woman" into the definition of marriage and barred recognition of overseas same-sex marriages |
| **2008** | *Same-Sex Relationships (Equal Treatment in Commonwealth Laws — General Law Reform) Act 2008* (Cth) | Removed discrimination against same-sex couples in **84 Commonwealth laws** — tax, superannuation, social security, Medicare, immigration, veterans' affairs — following the AHRC's *Same-Sex: Same Entitlements* report (2007) |
| **2009** | *Family Law Amendment (De Facto Financial Matters and Other Measures) Act 2008* (Cth) | From 1 March 2009, **de facto couples** (opposite- and same-sex) could use the Family Court for property and maintenance (FLA Part VIIIAB), after the states referred the power; before this, NSW de facto property disputes went to the state Supreme and District Courts |
| **2010** | *Relationships Register Act 2010* (NSW) | A state register giving **registered relationships** conclusive proof of de facto status — no need to prove two years' cohabitation |
| **2013** | *Commonwealth v Australian Capital Territory* (HCA) | Struck down the ACT's *Marriage Equality (Same Sex) Act 2013*: only the Commonwealth can legislate for marriage, and "marriage" in s 51(xxi) includes same-sex marriage |
| **2017** | *Marriage Amendment (Definition and Religious Freedoms) Act 2017* (Cth) | After the postal survey (7.8 million responses, **61.6% yes**), marriage became "the union of 2 people"; overseas same-sex marriages recognised; religious ministers and celebrants may refuse to solemnise |

## Use it for

- **fam.3.1** — the whole chronology in one place; the point students most often miss is that
  **de facto recognition came first** and marriage last.
- **fam.1.4** — de facto relationships: the two-year rule (FLA s 4AA), the NSW register, and why
  the 2009 referral matters.
- **chg.1 / chg.3** — conditions for reform (AHRC inquiry, changing attitudes, a plebiscite) and
  mechanisms (High Court ruling out the states, Parliament acting).
- **exp.4 / hr.2.9** — statute as the vehicle for ending discrimination against a group.

## Caution

The 2017 Act's religious-freedom provisions remain contested and the Commonwealth's broader
religious discrimination bills (2021–22) lapsed — don't overstate what the 2017 Act settled.
Section numbers for the de facto provisions are in the *Family Law Act* (Part VIIIAB), not the
amending Act. The 61.6% figure is the ABS survey result; the vote in Parliament was 128–4 in the
House.
