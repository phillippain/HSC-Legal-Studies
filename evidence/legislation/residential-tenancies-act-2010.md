Sets the rights and obligations of landlords and tenants, and routes disputes to **NCAT**.

**s 8(c) excludes boarders and lodgers** from the Act. That single exclusion is the sharpest
illustration of the principle that runs through the whole option: **protection tracks tenure, not
need**. A registered proprietor is protected almost perfectly (*Real Property Act 1900* (NSW) s 42);
a tenant is protected substantially; a boarder or lodger is protected barely; a person sleeping
rough is governed by trespass and public-space law only.

## Use it for

- **Leasing**, and **protection for tenants, landlords, boarders and lodgers** — where the phrase
  "boarders and lodgers" in the syllabus dot point is pointing at an exclusion, not a protection.
- **Law reform**: the 2024 NSW tenancy reforms, including the end of no-grounds termination. See the
  2026 outcome data.
