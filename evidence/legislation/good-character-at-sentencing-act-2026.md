- **What changed:** s 21A(3)(f) ('the offender was a person of good character') is removed, as is the
  narrower rule in s 21A(5A) for child sexual offences. Evidence led only to show good character
  cannot be considered; references can still be relevant to another factor (remorse, prospects of
  rehabilitation).
- **What did not:** a lack of prior convictions (s 21A(3)(e)) can still mitigate.
- **Why:** victim-survivors Harrison James and Jarad Grice campaigned as 'Your Reference Ain't
  Relevant'. The matter was referred to the **NSW Sentencing Council** in 2023, which recommended
  removing the factor as vague and subjective.
- **Opposed by** the NSW Bar Association and the Aboriginal Legal Service. A review is required two
  years after commencement.

## Caution

Check the commencement date before teaching; reports say it applies to proceedings on foot when it
commences. Courts reported the change at nswcourts.com.au (2026).
