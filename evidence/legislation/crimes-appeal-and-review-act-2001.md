| Route | Law |
|---|---|
| Local Court to District Court: appeal as of right, a rehearing on the transcript (*R v Higgins*) | Crimes (Appeal and Review) Act Part 3 |
| District or Supreme Court to the Court of Criminal Appeal: conviction or sentence; Crown appeals against inadequate sentences | Criminal Appeal Act 1912 (s 5D Crown appeals) |
| Inquiry into a conviction after appeals are exhausted (*Folbigg*, 2019 and 2022–23) | Crimes (Appeal and Review) Act Part 7 |
| Retrial after acquittal only for very serious offences with fresh and compelling evidence (2006) | Crimes (Appeal and Review) Act Part 8 |

BOCSAR (2025): 61.6% of 7,285 District Court appeals were upheld in whole or part.
