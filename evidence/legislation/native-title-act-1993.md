Enacted in response to *Mabo (No 2)*. It establishes a process for claiming and determining native
title, creates the **National Native Title Tribunal**, provides a **right to negotiate** over certain
future acts, and validates past grants that might otherwise have been invalid.

Amended by the *Native Title Amendment Act 1998* (Cth) after *Wik*.

## Use it for

- **Parliament as a mechanism of reform**, completing the sequence begun by the High Court.
- **Accessibility and resource efficiency**: claims are long, expensive and evidence-heavy, which is
  the standard criticism and the easiest place to make an evaluative judgement rather than a
  descriptive one.
- The **agencies of reform** that produced it — law reform commissions, land councils as NGOs, the
  media, and international pressure.
