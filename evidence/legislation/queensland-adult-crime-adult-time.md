| Jurisdiction | Minimum age (2026) | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Queensland | 10 | Adult penalties for 13 offences (2024), 33 from 2025; detention no longer a last resort for them; the Human Rights Act 2019 expressly overridden |
| ACT | 14 | 12 from Nov 2023, 14 from 1 July 2025; a short list of serious violent offences still chargeable at 12–13; most under-14s referred to therapeutic support |
| Victoria | 12 | Raised in 2025; then adult sentencing for some violent crimes by children 14 and over (passed Dec 2025) |
| NT | 10 | Raised to 12 (Aug 2023), lowered back to 10 (Oct 2024) |
| NSW | 10 | Doli incapax codified (2026); 55 SCIOs (Sept 2026) |

Queensland's statements of compatibility conceded the laws limit children's rights. In NSW the
Opposition pledged 'adult crime, adult time' laws on 13 September 2026, to be introduced within 100
days if it wins the March 2027 election.
