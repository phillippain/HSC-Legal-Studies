The Independent National Security Legislation Monitor, Jake Blight, made seven recommendations in his *Defining terrorism* report. The Conversation argues for the key one: removing 'religious' from the motive element of a 'terrorist act' in s 100.1. About two in three people charged with terrorism offences in 2026 are children.

## Current definition v the INSLM proposal

| Current law (s 100.1) | INSLM proposal |
|---|---|
| Advance a political, religious or ideological cause | Political or ideological cause; religion removed |
| Conviction rates differ by ideology | Aims at consistent treatment |
