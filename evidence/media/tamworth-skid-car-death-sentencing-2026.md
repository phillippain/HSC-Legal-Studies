On 10 March 2025 a modified "skid car" carrying three passengers crashed in Tamworth at **at least 92 km/h**
in a 50 km/h zone, killing **Logan Power**. The driver pleaded guilty in the Local Court (27 May 2026) to one
count of **dangerous driving occasioning death**; the Crown withdrew three other charges. Bail had been revoked
in September 2025.

## The benchmarks v this case

| Law or guideline | This case |
|---|---|
| s 52A(1): max **10 years**; aggravated (more than **45 km/h** over) max **14 years** | About 42 km/h over — charged as the basic offence |
| *R v Whyte* guideline: high culpability, custody generally appropriate | **3 years 2 months** full-time, parole eligibility **29 February 2028** |
| Aggravating factors: speed, people at risk, showing off | High speed, three passengers, car modified for burnouts |
| Plea at committal: **25%** discount (s 25D) | Discount not reported |
| Licence: automatic 3-year disqualification after release | 3 years |

Logan's mother read a victim impact statement; a psychiatrist reported on remorse. 355 people died on NSW roads
in 2025, 134 in speed-related crashes. The NSW Law Reform Commission has recommended reviewing the 45 km/h
threshold.
