CEO Dom Rowe released Cotality data: Bourke median unit rent rose from $208 to $346 a week; national annual unit rent growth was 4.9%. Tenants must prove an increase is excessive at NCAT, which cannot consider ability to pay.

## NSW v ACT rent rules

| NSW | ACT |
|---|---|
| Once a year; no limit on amount | Once a year; CPI-linked benchmark |
| Tenant must prove excessive at NCAT | Increases above benchmark presumed excessive |
