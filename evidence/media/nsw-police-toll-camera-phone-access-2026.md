Ministers Yasmin Catley and Jenny Aitchison announced the toll camera change; the bill that followed also widened compelled phone access. The first toll sites are the Sydney Harbour Bridge and Tunnel. Police can apply for an organised crime order within 14 days of seizure; an order lasts seven business days, with up to three extensions.

## Police powers before and after

| Before | After (bill as passed) |
|---|---|
| Toll images redacted; driver identity unavailable | Unredacted images for serious indictable offences and missing persons |
| Refusing a digital evidence access order: up to 5 years | Up to 7 years |
