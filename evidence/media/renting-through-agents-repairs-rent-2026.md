Agent-managed renters were **2.6 times** as likely (adjusted) to report delayed repairs or unfair rent rises; about **75%** of private rentals were agent-managed in 2016, up from earlier decades.

## Landlord v agent

| Renting from a landlord | Renting through an agent |
|---|---|
| 18.7% reported delayed repairs | 37.8% |
| 16.1% reported restrictions such as pets or pictures | 30.4% |
| 9.7% reported an unjustified rent rise | 20.9% |
| Deals with the owner, who holds the legal duties | Deals with an agent chosen by, and owing duties to, the landlord |
