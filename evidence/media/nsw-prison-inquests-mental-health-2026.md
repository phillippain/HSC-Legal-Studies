The ABC reviewed **249** coronial inquests into NSW prison deaths since 2015. **48** found failures in mental health
care — screening (20), supervision (14), clinical care (27), waits for care (29); **14** of those 48 deaths involved
Indigenous people. Corrections did not fully support recommendations in **10 of 32** inquests. The story centres on
**Bailey Mackander**, who died in November 2019; after the 2021 inquest, Corrections refused five recommendations on
risk intervention team cells.

## What a coroner can and cannot do

| Can | Cannot |
|---|---|
| Hold a **mandatory inquest** into a death in custody, led by a senior coroner (s 22(2)) | Find guilt — findings must not suggest an offence (s 81(3)) |
| Record identity, date, place, manner and cause of death (s 81(1)) | Prosecute — only refer papers to the DPP (s 78) |
| Make recommendations to prevent similar deaths (s 82) | **Compel** an agency to act |
| Expect a response within six months (M2009-12) | Enforce that deadline — the memorandum is not mandatory |

The RCIADIC asked for responses within **three** months; NSW chose six. A 2023 statutory review (Rec 40) proposed
legislating the response duty and publishing responses on the Coroners Court website.

## Follow-up (ABC, 8 November 2026): "Corrections should not run prison mental health"

Forensic psychiatrist **Dr Kerri Eagle** says Justice Health, not Corrective Services NSW, should lead mental health care:
the risk intervention team (RIT) — usually two CSNSW staff and one general nurse — is run by Corrections and lacks evidence
as a health intervention. The Minister declined an interview.

| Recommendation | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Mackander (2021) rec 1: psychologist input on RIT reviews | Rejected by CSNSW, the ABC reports |
| Safer RIT assessment cells | Five cell-safety recommendations rejected |
| Inspector of Custodial Services (2021): more acute and step-down beds | **109** forensic beds promised in October 2023, about 50% more |
| Cartwright (2024): use s 24 transfers to outside hospitals when no forensic bed is free | Response not checked |

- **Simon Cartwright** waited **17 days** for a mental health bed at the MRRC in 2021 and died of an untreated illness; the
  coroner called it a gross systemic failure and found a s 24 transfer was available but unused.
- The **Mandela Rules** make prison health care a State responsibility and leave clinical decisions to health professionals.
- In 2022 the government supported 15 of 35 coronial system inquiry recommendations and noted the rest.
