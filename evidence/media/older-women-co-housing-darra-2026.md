Five women moved into the first project of the charity **Sharing with Friends** (Older Women Co-Housing Association Qld). Each
paid **$120,000** for a **50-year lease** of a private studio, refunded with CPI on leaving or death (less renovation costs);
weekly contributions are about $180–200. Census night 2021 counted **7,325** homeless women aged 55+, up from 5,234 in 2011;
58.8% of 288,970 specialist homelessness clients in 2024–25 were female.

## Boarding house v the Darra model

| | Boarding house / rooming accommodation | Darra co-housing |
|---|---|---|
| Planning label | Rooming accommodation; boarding houses listed | No co-housing category exists |
| Right to occupy | Rooms, with shared facilities | 50-year lease of a self-contained studio, plus shared spaces |
| What residents pay | Rent | $120,000 buy-in, refunded plus CPI |
| Who runs it | A provider, possibly accredited | Charity owns the land; residents manage the community |
| Resale | — | Cannot be sold on the open market, keeping it affordable |

Anti-discrimination law allows women-only, age-based housing as a special measure (SDA s 7D; ADA s 33) but does not create or
fund it. NSW's Housing SEPP separates affordable boarding houses from co-living, and neither suits a buy-in leasehold model.
The next project needs about **$200,000** per resident, excluding women with fewer savings.

A [second class summary of the same report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/12svJBmpQ7bEtMQ9orCPDtLI8ALfmmLOz/view) compares the model
with the *Residential Tenancies and Rooming Accommodation Act 2008* and the *Retirement Villages Act 1999* (Qld).
