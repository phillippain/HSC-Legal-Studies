Combined capital city values fell **8.1%** over seven months to October; CBA forecasts a record peak-to-trough fall of about **15%**. The RBA's stress test puts borrowers in negative equity at under 1% now and about 5% after a further 20% fall.

## Buyers v sellers in a falling market

| Buyers | Sellers |
|---|---|
| Vendor discounts widened to 4.5% | Median time on market rose to 41 days |
| Price-guide rules paused to 1 December 2027 | Agents free to cut advertised prices |
| Five business days cooling-off (0.25% forfeit); none at auction | Bound once cooling-off ends or at auction |
| Lender must assess the loan as not unsuitable | Each 25bp rise cuts buyers' borrowing |

## Caution

Forecasts differ widely; the negative equity figure is a stress test, not a forecast.
