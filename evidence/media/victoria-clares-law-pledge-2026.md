Premier Ben Carroll and Attorney-General Sonya Kilkenny pledged a scheme letting people ask police about a partner's history of
violence, modelled on **South Australia's**. The Opposition pledged a scheme in July 2026. No bill exists.

## NSW pilot v SA scheme

| | NSW pilot (2016–2019) | SA scheme (since October 2018) |
|---|---|---|
| Coverage | Four police commands (Oxley, Shoalhaven, Sutherland, St George) | Statewide; person at risk 17+ |
| Who can ask | Person at risk, family, friend or professional | Person at risk or a third party |
| Demand | Limited use; just over 50 disclosures in two years | Applications rose from 256 (2018–19) to 662 (2023–24) |
| Cost and speed | About **$3,959** per application | Median **21 days** to disclosure (target 14) |
| Support | Linked to a crisis support program | A specialist worker at every disclosure |
| Outcome | Not continued — "evidence base is not there yet" | Ongoing; a flat budget is causing delays |

SA received **2,581** applications and held **1,064** disclosure meetings from 2018 to 2024; it runs under information-sharing
guidelines, not an Act.
