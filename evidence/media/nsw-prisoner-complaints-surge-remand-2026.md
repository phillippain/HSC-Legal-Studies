Official Visitors received **32,168** complaints in adult centres in 2025–26, up about **60%** on 20,062 the year before (itself nearly double 2023–24). Remand reached **46%** of adults in custody (June 2026).

## What changed

|  | 2024–25 → 2025–26 |
|---|---|
| Adult complaints | 20,062 → 32,168 |
| Complaints per 100 inmates | 152.89 → 230.43 |
| Adult inmates | 13,122 → 13,960 (up 6.4%) |
| Top category | Medical issues in both years |
| Official Visitors | 92 → 93 |

Youth justice centres recorded a further 561 complaints.
