NSW Fair Trading announced that **Leader Properties Investment Pty Ltd** and its director **Wan Hao Cai** were disciplined for the unlawful lockout of a tenant under the *Residential Tenancies Act 2010* (NSW). The company was penalised **$22,000** and the director **$11,000**, with training and reprimands also imposed.

## Use it for

- **Government organisations**: Fair Trading as the regulator that enforces tenancy law (`shel.2.8`).
- **Protection and compliance**: a landlord cannot lock out a tenant, and non-compliance has penalties (`shel.2.3`).
- **Pairing**: set it beside *Fimmano v Anderson*, where the tenant's Fair Trading complaint started the dispute.
