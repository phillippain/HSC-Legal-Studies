"Chloe" (a pseudonym) says her former partner's income fell on paper from more than **$300,000** to under
**$20,000** through income estimates, so that from November 2026 she — the main carer — must pay him. Financial
abuse is family violence under the *Family Law Act* (s 4AB(2A)).

## Meant to work v can be misused

| | How it should work | How it can be misused |
|---|---|---|
| Income estimates | Allowed only if income has fallen **15%+** | About **half** of 70,000 estimates a year are incorrect |
| Reconciliation | Replaced by actual income; 10% penalty if 110%+ | Needs tax returns — 200,000+ payers had not lodged for two years |
| Collection | Agency Collect enforces | Private Collect assumed paid; 70% of financially abused women on it not paid in full |
| Challenging income | Change of assessment (s 98C) | Burden falls on the victim-survivor to prove the other parent's income |

**$2.0 billion** in Agency Collect debt is owed by 229,235 paying parents (March 2026). The **$182.6m** 2026–27 reform
package mostly starts on **1 July 2027** — too late for Chloe — and ends mandatory information exchange between
parents.
