KPMG compared house prices and births across Greater Sydney neighbourhoods in 2019 and 2024. Neighbourhoods with median
prices of **$400k–$900k** fell from **199 to 86**; Greater Sydney had **58,122** births in 2024, a reported 20-year low.
Sydney's fertility rate (**1.4**) compares with **1.98** in NSW outside Sydney (ABS gives NSW overall as 1.46). The
Sydney median house price is **$1.66m** (September quarter 2026), 7.9% below its peak. One family moved to Newcastle to
afford a home.

## The legal levers (not discussed in the article)

Planning (the Housing SEPP's low- and mid-rise reforms), stamp duty concessions for first home buyers, the
Commonwealth 5% Deposit Scheme and the National Housing Accord.
