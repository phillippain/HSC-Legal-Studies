Western Sydney Wanderers chair **Paul Lederer** said police treatment of fans had reached a "tipping point". The Red
and Black Bloc (RBB) held a **20-minute silent protest** at the 31 October match. As reported by the club and fans:

| What police did | What the club and fans say |
|---|---|
| Directed the RBB to surrender drums, a megaphone and banners two hours before kick-off | Fans want equal treatment and common sense |
| Up to 20 public order and riot squad officers behind supporter bays | No justification; intimidating for families |
| Hundreds held outside the Sydney derby (16 October) for up to 30 minutes | Repeated issues all season |
| No reason or legal power published | The club does not condone anti-social behaviour |

**Possible powers:** LEPRA Part 14 **move-on directions** (reasonable belief of obstruction, harassment, fear or
violence), **s 21 searches** (reasonable suspicion of a weapon or prohibited item). **Accountability:** complaints to NSW
Police under the *Police Act* Part 8A, overseen by the **LECC**.
