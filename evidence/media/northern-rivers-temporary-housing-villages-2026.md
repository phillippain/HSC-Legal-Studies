After the 2022 floods the government built **546** dwellings across **11** villages; **1,021** people lived in them in July 2023.
Five closed in 2025. The remaining six were extended to December 2026; the 9 November release sets a different outcome for each.

| Village | After 9 November 2026 |
|---|---|
| Ballina, Mullumbimby | Homes NSW keeps managing for 12 more months |
| Brunswick Heads | Offered to Byron Shire Council, which sought a community housing provider |
| Coraki | Offered to Richmond Valley Council; 56 pods considered for worker accommodation |
| Lismore, Wardell | Close early 2027; decommissioned for future housing |

## The pathway

Emergency accommodation (peaking at 1,440 people in April 2022) → a temporary village under an occupancy agreement → an
income-tested licence fee from late 2024 → individual support and up to **$10,000** to move on → a permanent home. Villages must
be demobilised within five years under the Housing SEPP. **0 of 4,382** planned flood-free homes or lots had been delivered by
31 March 2026; about 270 residents had moved to longer-term housing by August 2024.
