A snapshot of an NGO at work in the gap between a law passing and commencing. The *Residential
Tenancies Amendment Act 2024* had passed in October 2024; by February 2025 the once-a-year rent
increase cap and the ban on background-check fees were in force, but the eviction and pet reforms
were "anxiously" awaited while the Government drafted the Regulations.

## What the bulletin records

- **No-grounds evictions continuing** — "renters are still being evicted for no reason. The Tenants'
  Union is hearing of ongoing (and possibly increasing!) numbers of no-grounds notices being served"
  — a last rush before the ban. The TU asked renters to "share your eviction story".
- **What the Regulations must do** — "attach strong evidence requirements to the 'reasonable grounds'
  landlords will be able to use to end a tenancy" (the requirement later removed: see the rollback
  record).
- **Rental Taskforce welcomed** — the $8.4 million taskforce under the Rental Commissioner is "a good
  step towards raising the standards of the industry, but further resources will be needed". Renters
  "shouldn't have to carry the load of enforcing tenancy laws – like going to Tribunal just to get
  basic repairs done".
- **Selwyn Street, Paddington** — 28 boarding-house residents facing eviction to make way for four
  luxury apartments; a rally at Whitlam Square; the NSW Government and City of Sydney had agreed to
  buy the property but the developer refused to sell, with a week until the eviction date.
- **Aboriginal housing** — an AHURI long-term study: one in eight Aboriginal and Torres Strait
  Islander households has unmet housing need; 81% of those are in unaffordable rentals and 14% in
  severe overcrowding.
- Factsheets updated; free webinars for renters and community workers on the new laws.

## Caution

A **membership newsletter** — advocacy, undated anecdotes ("hearing of"), and no figures for the
no-grounds surge. Its claims about commencement dates were forecasts; the reforms commenced
19 May 2025. The Selwyn Street outcome is not recorded here. Quote it as the Tenants' Union's
position, not as fact.
