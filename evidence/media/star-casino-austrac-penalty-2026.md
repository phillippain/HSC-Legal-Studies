AUSTRAC began investigating The Star in 2021 and filed **civil penalty proceedings** in November 2022, alleging a
non-compliant AML/CTF program, inadequate transaction monitoring and customer due diligence, and weak board
oversight — letting **1,200+ high-risk customers**, including through overseas **junkets**, gamble through high-risk
channels. On **28 October 2026** Justice Moore ordered **$260 million** ($195m Sydney, $65m Queensland), paid in
four instalments over 19 months.

## Star v Crown

| | Crown (2023) | The Star (2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Penalty | $450m | **$260m** |
| How set | Agreed with AUSTRAC | **Contested**: AUSTRAC $400m, Star $100m (more would mean insolvency) |
| Lessons | No Bergin findings in advance | Failed to act despite the Bergin inquiry's findings |

The court weighed deterrence against capacity to pay, remediation and **$200m** in earlier state penalties for
overlapping conduct. The largest AUSTRAC penalties remain Westpac ($1.3bn) and CBA ($700m). Reforms from 31 March
2026 cut the casino due-diligence threshold to under $5,000. A separate ASIC case penalised former executives.
