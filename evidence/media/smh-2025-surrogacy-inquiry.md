Jennifer McCloy carried a baby for her best friend, Edwina Peach, after multiple miscarriages. She
handed Angus to his parents at birth — and then **remained his legal guardian for the next year**
while the required paperwork for the parentage order was processed. She thinks that should change.
A NSW parliamentary inquiry examined how government can make the path to parenthood easier.

The article also follows Dimity Smith in **Tamworth**, whose IVF is slowed by distance: repeated
travel to Sydney because even blood test results are slow in the bush.

## Use it for

- **Surrogacy** (`fam.3.3`) from the side students rarely write about: **altruistic, lawful, and
  still hard**. Most answers argue about commercial surrogacy overseas; the domestic process is where
  the law's own design shows.
- **The parentage gap** — the child lives with his parents while the law recognises someone else as
  his guardian. Name the delay and what it means: medical consent, travel, school enrolment, and
  inheritance all sit with the wrong person for a year.
- **Accessibility as geography** — regional access to ART is a resource question the syllabus criteria
  ask about, and this is a concrete instance.
- **Reform in motion**: a parliamentary inquiry is an agency of reform (`ls.4.2`) with a named
  subject, so it pairs with the law reform record rather than sitting alone.
