A front-section exclusive in the last weeks before the 2025 federal election. The **Housing
Australia Future Fund** — a $10 billion investment fund under the *Housing Australia Future Fund Act
2023* (Cth), whose returns finance social and affordable housing — had, almost 18 months after
legislation, agreed funding for **about 800 homes** and completed none. In his 2021 budget reply
Anthony Albanese had promised **30,000** social and affordable homes in five years (20,000 social,
10,000 affordable "for frontline workers").

| Government | Opposition and industry |
|---|---|
| "Up to 13,700 homes are in the pipeline" under round one; Penny Wong: 24,000 through direct Commonwealth investment | Senator Andrew Bragg: "Labor has built bureaucracies, not houses"; the Coalition would scrap the fund and spend $5bn on enabling infrastructure for up to 500,000 homes |
| Housing Minister Clare O'Neil: the Coalition "caused the delay" by resisting the legislation for a year; "Labor is building 55,000 over the next five years" | Urban Taskforce (Tom Forrest): 30,000 is achievable but progress "threw the number into doubt" — yet "this is not the time to stop the process" |
| | HIA (Simon Croft): "the legislation and mechanisms have taken longer than we would have liked" |

## Caution

A **partisan news frame** ("costly flop") with the Government's reply included; the pipeline figures
are the Government's and the 500,000-home Coalition claim is a policy assertion. The article predates
the May 2025 election, after which the Coalition's plan lapsed. Use the ANAO record for audited
numbers. A second copy of this article sits in the Desktop folder under "The Telegraph Shelter
Government Housing grant.pdf".
