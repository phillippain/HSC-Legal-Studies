In August 2014 a group of teenagers abused Jewish primary school children on a school bus in Sydney's
eastern suburbs. One young person attended a **youth justice conference** in December 2014. His
outcome plan required a guided tour of the **Sydney Jewish Museum**, where he met a Holocaust
survivor, and the NSW Jewish Board of Deputies' 'Respect, Understanding, Acceptance' harmony program
(he missed his first session and completed a later one). The Board's CEO, Vic Alhadeff, said its
approach 'has always been focused on rehabilitation'.
