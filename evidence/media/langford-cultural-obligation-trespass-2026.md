Ruth Langford, arrested at a January 2025 forestry protest, represented herself and argued Aboriginal law obliged her to protect Country. Chief Magistrate Catherine Geason accepted her obligations were genuine and that she could perform ceremony, but found her entitlement ended once a forestry officer directed her to leave. One trespass count was proven; sentencing is listed for 18 December 2026.

## Defence argument v the court's reasoning

| Defence | Court |
|---|---|
| Aboriginal law obliges her to protect Country | Accepted she holds genuine obligations |
| Presence was cultural practice, not protest | Entitled to perform ceremony |
| Stayed and accepted arrest | Entitlement ended when directed to leave |
