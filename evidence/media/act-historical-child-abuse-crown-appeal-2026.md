Survivor **Mike Worsman** reported in 2019 to SA Police; because the offending happened in Canberra, the matter took **18 months**
to reach the AFP. The offender, aged 15 at the time (the victim was 12), pleaded guilty in 2024 to five charges.

## Original sentence v re-sentence

| | Original (2025, Mossop J) | Re-sentence on Crown appeal (11 November 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Head sentence | 2 years 7 months | **4 years 7 months** |
| Suspension | Remainder suspended after 16 months (s 12) | Not suspended until June 2028 |
| Survivor's view | The process added to trauma | Extra time, but still falls short; hoped for life in custody |

ACT law requires **current sentencing practice** for child sexual offences, capped at the maximum at the time of offending (s 34A),
while the offender's youth at the time still matters. The case took about **seven years** from report to re-sentence.

Told mainly from the survivor's perspective; no DPP, police, defence or court comment, and the appeal reasons were not yet published,
