Referred by Social Services Minister Tanya Plibersek and adopted on 28 October 2025, the House committee inquiry
received **230+ submissions**, held its last hearing on 16 October 2026 and expects to report in **early 2027**. A
NSW 2016 pilot found **39%** of women who died by suicide were known to police for DFV (police records only).

## Current law v proposed reform

| | Now | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Suicide after abuse | No homicide offence | **Victoria**: a new form of manslaughter (election pledge, Premier Ben Carroll, September 2026) — an Australian first |
| Coercive control (NSW s 54D, from 1 July 2024) | **473** incidents, **22** charges, **1** proven (to December 2025) | Build police and prosecutor capacity first |
| Death data | ABS has no DFSV coding; relies on coronial documents | National coronial standards to flag DFV-related suicides (ANROWS) |
| Death reviews | NSW review team studies homicides; one suicide pilot (2017) | DFSV prioritised in suicide case review |
| Services | DV and mental health services work in isolation | Coordinated suicide prevention pathways |

The terms of reference also cover perpetrators' **threats of suicide** as coercive control.

## Caution

A **class evidence summary of a news report** built around one victim-survivor's account; no government voice. The
Victorian offence is a promise, not a bill. The committee examines systemic issues, not individual cases.
