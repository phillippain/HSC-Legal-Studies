The Herald profiles 'Emily', who says she has spent **$500,000** over five years, and 'Lauren'. **83%** of 2023–24 final parenting applications alleged family violence; victim-survivors in a 2026 study reported legal costs of $80,000 to more than $250,000.

## Protections on paper v in practice

| The law on paper | In Emily's and Lauren's cases |
|---|---|
| Five subpoenas per interim application without leave | Emily received more than double in a month and could not afford objections |
| Harmful proceedings orders (s 102QAC) | None reported; five years of proceedings |
| Costs orders | Emily's unpaid after two years |
| Economic abuse in the definition from 10 June 2025 | Lauren's 2015 consent orders predate it; $165,000+ quoted to reopen |
| $3.9bn legal assistance partnership | Emily earns too much for Legal Aid and self-represents |
