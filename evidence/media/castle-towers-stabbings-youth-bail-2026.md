Two 16-year-old boys were stabbed in the torso at Castle Towers, Castle Hill, on 21 November 2026; one was critical. A 17-year-old was charged with wounding with intent to cause grievous bodily harm (s 33, maximum 25 years). The charge is unproven.

## Same charge, different process: adult v young person

| Adult accused | Accused under 18 |
|---|---|
| Wounding is show cause only with a prior serious offence | Show cause never applies under 18 |
| Local Court, then District or Supreme Court | Children's Court; may be dealt with at law |
| Name can be published | Name cannot be published |

Under the s 22C youth bail test (repeat car theft and break and enter only), 69% of appearances in 2024–26 ended in remand and 79% involved Aboriginal young people — the wider youth bail climate, not this charge.
