Anthony Pele, 25, faces four outstanding NSW warrants, including alleged murder over a Greenacre shooting (July 2023),
and allegedly left Australia in August 2023. He has not been convicted and is presumed innocent. Australia has sent
Vietnam **19** extradition requests since the 2012 treaty; the first person was only arrested in December 2024.

## Domestic v international tools

| | Domestic (NSW / Australia) | International |
|---|---|---|
| Authority to arrest | NSW warrants, valid until arrest | Interpol red notice — not an arrest warrant |
| Finding him | $250,000 reward; Crime Stoppers | AFP liaison; location unconfirmed |
| Getting him back | No power to arrest overseas | Request under the 2012 treaty, with the Attorney-General's authority (s 40) |
| Evidence | Strike Force Helston | Mutual legal assistance treaty (2014) |

Vietnam applies its own law and the treaty: **dual criminality**, refusal grounds (including "unjust or oppressive"), and
either state may refuse to surrender its own nationals. If returned, he can be tried only for the extradition offences
(**speciality**, s 42).
