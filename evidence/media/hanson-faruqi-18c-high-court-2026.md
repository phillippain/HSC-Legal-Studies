Senator Mehreen Faruqi complained to the AHRC about Senator Pauline Hanson's 9 September 2022 post. Hanson declined
conciliation, Faruqi sued, and Stewart J found a breach of s 18C, rejected the s 18D defence and ordered deletion and costs
(2024). The Full Federal Court (Perry, Kennett and Bennett JJ) dismissed the appeal **3–0** on 27 July 2026. The High
Court granted special leave on **5 November 2026**; the Commonwealth Attorney-General is a respondent. No hearing date is set.

## What s 18C prohibits v what the treaties require

| | Section 18C (RDA 1975) | ICCPR art 20(2) / CERD art 4 |
|---|---|---|
| Conduct | Public acts reasonably likely to offend, insult, humiliate or intimidate | Advocacy of racial hatred that incites discrimination, hostility or violence |
| Focus | How the targeted person or group would reasonably feel | Whether others are incited |
| Seriousness | Includes offending or insulting | Very serious acts only |
| Free speech | s 18D exempts reasonable, good-faith acts | Due regard to freedom of expression |
| Full Court's answer | Catching lesser conduct "nips in the bud" later incitement (*Toben v Jones*, 2003) | Treaties set the general course; Parliament chooses the means |

## Two constitutional questions

1. **External affairs (s 51(xxix))** — is s 18C a law implementing the treaties, when it reaches further than they do?
2. **Implied freedom of political communication** — does s 18C burden political speech beyond a legitimate purpose?
   Twomey points to *Farmer* [2025] HCA 38 (the Candace Owens visa case) on protecting groups from intimidation.

The Senate rejected changes to 18C **31–28** in March 2017.

## Use it for

- **hr.2.8 / hr.2.9** — the Constitution protects rights only indirectly; statute implements treaties.
- **hr.2.14** — courts, not a Charter, balancing freedom from racial vilification against freedom of speech.

## Caution

An **academic explainer** leaning to the view that "nips in the bud" stretches the external affairs power; no voice for
Faruqi or the Commonwealth. The outcome is unknown.
