The *Daily Telegraph* reported in March 2022 that the family of an eight-month-old boy was suing the State after the baby was allegedly strip searched before a visit to a jail in Kempsey in 2018.

## Use it for

Strip searches (LEPRA ss 31–34: never under 10), alongside the LECC inquiry (2020) and *Meredith v NSW* (2025).

## Caution

Allegations reported in the media, from the department's Crime Evidence Maps; the outcome of the claim is not recorded here. The search may have been conducted by corrective services rather than police; check before using it as a police example.
