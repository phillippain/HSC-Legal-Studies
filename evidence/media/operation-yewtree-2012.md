London's Metropolitan Police began Operation Yewtree in October 2012 after a television documentary exposed sexual abuse by the late BBC presenter Jimmy Savile. Large numbers of people came forward about abuse that had happened decades earlier, and the investigation widened to other well-known figures.

## Use it for

- **Why crime goes unreported** — fear of not being believed, power imbalances and the status of the offender; reporting surged once victims saw others believed.
- **Late reporting** as a problem for evidence at trial (crime.3.7).

## Caution

A United Kingdom example: use it to illustrate reporting behaviour, not NSW law.
