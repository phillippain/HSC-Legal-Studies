**50,079** NSW properties were registered for short stays in 2024, almost 32,000 non-hosted. Register and scraped-data counts for the Sydney LGA differ (2,500 against 5,500). Victoria has levied **7.5%** on short-stay bookings since 1 January 2025.

## NSW rules v the New York model

| NSW now | New York City |
|---|---|
| Hosted stays allowed all year | Stays under 30 days need the host present |
| Whole home, host away: up to 180 days in Greater Sydney (60 in Byron) | Generally prohibited for stays under 30 days |
| Bookings of 21+ days not counted toward the cap | Rentals of 30+ days exempt from registration |
