MacRae received four life sentences for murders in Victoria and South Australia in 1985, with a 36-year non-parole period. Commissioner David Lovell KC affirmed the Parole Board's grant. He had 17 negative urine tests in prison.

## Case against release v the decision

| Attorney-General | Commissioner / Board |
|---|---|
| AG must show cogent reasons to depart from the Board | Not persuaded; Board decision affirmed |
| Inadequate foundation on relative risk | Board fully considered his risk |
| Declined a violence program | Completed all relevant programs |
