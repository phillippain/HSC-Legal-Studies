About **12 people**, believed to be Chinese nationals, came ashore near **Pennefather Beach** on Cape
York around 3 am in late June 2026. Campers saw the group; some went into Weipa. A **34-year-old
Taiwanese man** was arrested in a supermarket car park after a community member reported them, and was
charged with an **aggravated people smuggling offence** (at least five people), which carries up to
**20 years' imprisonment**. A second man was detained while inquiries continued. The arrivals were
detained by the Australian Border Force. (A local MP said two had been charged; AAP reported one.)

## Use it for

- **People smuggling, not trafficking**: the passengers paid for and consented to the journey.
- **Law**: Migration Act 1958 s 233A, with aggravated offences in ss 233B–233C.
- **Effectiveness**: the border was crossed undetected; the arrest came from a member of the public.
