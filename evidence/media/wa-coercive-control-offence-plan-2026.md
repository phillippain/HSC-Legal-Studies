Premier Roger Cook, Attorney General Tony Buti and Minister Jessica Stojkovski announced a bill for a standalone offence in 2027, within a $121m 2026 family and domestic violence package ($66.1m new money). WA has not released the offence's elements.

## WA now v the NSW offence

| WA now | NSW s 54D (since 1 July 2024) |
|---|---|
| No standalone offence; bill promised for 2027 | Abusive behaviour towards intimate partners |
| Restraining order definition covers patterns (2024 Act) | Course of conduct; no minimum number of incidents |
| NSW results to December 2025 | 473 incidents, 22 charges, 1 proven |

The ABS 23% figure is national; NSW charges were mostly still before the courts.
