Police were called to a **Baulkham Hills** home about **5.15am** on 31 March 2025 after reports that
multiple people had been stabbed. Three children aged **between 10 and 16** had stab wounds — a boy
with two, a girl with three, and a girl **who uses a wheelchair** with one wound and several
lacerations. Paramedics treated them at the scene and took them to Westmead Hospital in a **stable**
condition.

The **47-year-old mother** was charged at Westmead Hospital, where she was being treated under police
guard for **suspected self-inflicted** wounds. The Hills Police Area Command commander, Detective
Superintendent **Naomi Moore**, said police believed the children were **asleep** when they were
attacked, and that their father, woken by screams, "has secured the weapon, and he's contacted
police".

At **Parramatta Local Court** the next day her lawyer made **no application for bail** and bail was
**formally refused**; she was to remain in custody for at least two months. An **interim apprehended
domestic violence order** taken out by police **to protect her husband** was to be considered at her
next appearance. Her defence lawyer said the case was "tragic for all involved".

## Use it for

- **fam.2.4 — family violence that does not fit the pattern.** Almost every other record in this part
  of the library involves a man harming a woman or a child. This is a mother, her children, and an
  ADVO protecting the **husband**. Use it to show that the *Crimes (Domestic and Personal Violence)
  Act 2007* is drafted around a **domestic relationship**, not around a gender, and that the law must
  work in the cases that do not match the campaign.
- **The ADVO as a parallel, civil protection (fam.2.5)** — a criminal charge and an **interim ADVO**
  running at the same time, one punitive and one protective, on different standards of proof. The
  clearest small example in the library of the two systems operating together.
- **Bail, where nobody applied (crime.2.7)** — "no application for bail and it was formally refused".
  A useful corrective to the assumption that bail is always fought: the *Bail Act 2013* requires the
  court to address it even when the accused does not, and a show-cause offence makes release
  unlikely.
- **Disability as a vulnerability the law recognises (exp.4.1)** — one of the children uses a
  wheelchair. Relevant to both the objective seriousness of an offence at sentence and to care and
  protection.
- **The father's intervention** — a family member disarming the attacker and calling triple zero.
  Worth a line on how often family violence is interrupted by a family member rather than by the
  state.
- **Care and protection follows (fam.3.4)** — three injured children, one parent in custody, the
  other the protected person on an ADVO. Ask what happens next under the *Children and Young Persons
  (Care and Protection) Act 1998*, which the article does not address and which is the real
  consequence for the children.

## Caution

**An untested allegation, reported four days in.** The mother was **charged, not convicted**; every
account of what happened is **police belief** or the **prosecution case** as reported at a first
mention, and she is entitled to the presumption of innocence. Write "allegedly" throughout, and do
not state that she stabbed the children as a fact. The outcome of the charges, of the ADVO
application and of her mental health — there is a strong suggestion of a mental health crisis, and
the article reports **suspected self-inflicted wounds** without any assessment — is **not recorded
here**; check what happened before using it, because a mental health defence or a diversion under the
*Mental Health and Cognitive Impairment Forensic Provisions Act 2020* would change the legal analysis
entirely, and that possibility must not be presented as a finding either.

The **children are living, identifiable minors** described by age, sex, injury and disability, and
their mother is accused. **Do not name the family or reproduce the injury detail** in any resource,
and think hard before using this in a classroom at all: it involves attempted filicide, a disabled
child and apparent parental self-harm. If used, flag it and pair it with support information
(**1800RESPECT 1800 737 732**, Lifeline **13 11 14**). A short, early news report is a thin basis for
legal analysis; the value here is the **structure** — charge, bail, ADVO, care and protection — not
the facts.
