The NHMRC evidence review covered **15** studies (12,756 people) with low overall certainty. Melbourne's largest clinic prescribed blockers to **64** young people in eight years; Queensland's pause on new starts runs until UK trial results (2031).

## State bans v national advice

| Queensland and NT (public system) | NHMRC interim advice (16 November 2026) |
|---|---|
| No new starts for under-18s | Can be considered once puberty starts and suitability is assessed |
| Government direction binds public health services | Shared decision: young person, parents and clinicians |
| Pause applies to minors with gender dysphoria | Gender incongruence criteria; dysphoria not required |
| Vine review: limited, scarce long-term evidence | Low certainty; possible benefit; serious harms appear rare |
| Binding state policy, challenged in court | Non-binding; states decide |
