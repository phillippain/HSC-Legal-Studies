The story follows refugee **Aziza Anwari**'s search for a home. The Hunter (Newcastle, Lake Macquarie) has almost **6,400**
households waiting, more than **765** on the priority list; need rose **12%** in a year, and Newcastle's rental vacancy rate was
**1.5%** at the end of 2025.

## Demand v supply

| | Demand | Supply |
|---|---|---|
| Statewide | **72,232** households waiting; 14,155 approved as priority (30 September 2026) | 5,175 social, affordable and key worker homes since early 2023 |
| Hunter | ~6,400 waiting | 150+ homes since April 2023; 77 more due |
| Existing stock | 30,000 public homes targeted for repair | 25,752 upgrades (July 2024 to June 2026) |
| Private market | Lake Macquarie three-bedroom rents up almost 7% in six months | Rent rises once a year; no-grounds evictions banned |

CHIA NSW proposes a **$26bn** fund for 50,000 social and affordable homes by 2050. Demand counts households at one date; supply
counts homes over several years, so the columns are not directly comparable.
