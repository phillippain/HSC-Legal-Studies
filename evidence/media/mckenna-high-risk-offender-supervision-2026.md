**Dennis McKenna** has served **15.5 years** in prison since 2011 for child sex offences. The State applied for a
restriction order; on 4 November 2026 Justice Solomon found him a **high risk serious offender** but made a
**supervision order** with **41 conditions** (including no contact with his 29 victims, per the ABC) rather than a
continuing detention order. Release is expected within weeks.

## Continuing detention v supervision (WA, s 48)

| | Continuing detention order | Supervision order (McKenna) |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Stays in custody after the sentence ends | Released under conditions |
| Duration | Indefinite, until rescinded (s 26) | For the period stated (s 27) |
| Test | State proves an unacceptable risk to a high degree | Court must be satisfied supervision adequately manages the risk |
| Paramount | **Community protection** | Community protection |

*Fardon* (2004) upheld post-sentence preventive detention; *Garlett* (2022) upheld WA's scheme. Victims' interests enter
only through conditions.
