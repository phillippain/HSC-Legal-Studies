Insulation could save $3.8bn in health spending over 20 years. NSW sets seven minimum standards under s 52(1A); 54% of NSW rentals are unhealthy against 22% of owner-occupied homes.

## NSW v Victoria and the ACT

| NSW (s 52) | Victoria / ACT |
|---|---|
| Mould not a listed standard | Vic: free of mould and damp |
| No heating standard | Vic: fixed heater; ACT: ceiling insulation |
