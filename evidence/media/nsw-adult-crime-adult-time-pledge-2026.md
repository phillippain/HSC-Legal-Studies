A law firm explainer sets out current NSW treatment of young offenders: Children's Court control orders of up to two years for one offence (three in total), rehabilitation focus, and serious children's indictable offences dealt with at law. About 4,700 Queensland children have been charged under adult-time laws.

## NSW now v the pledge

| NSW now | Pledge (Queensland model) |
|---|---|
| Children's Court maximum 2 years per offence | Adult penalties for listed offences |
| Rehabilitation emphasised | Punishment and deterrence emphasised |
