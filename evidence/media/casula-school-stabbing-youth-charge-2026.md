A 15-year-old was allegedly stabbed outside Casula High School at about 8.30am on Monday 9 November; teachers and tradies
tried to intervene. A 16-year-old was arrested and charged at Liverpool Police Station with **wounding with intent to cause
grievous bodily harm** (s 33(1), maximum 25 years); police refused bail. He has not been convicted.
