Philip Young admitted 48 offences against his wife, including 11 rapes and 11 counts of administering a substance. A jury convicted four men; one more pleaded guilty. The men argued they believed she consented because her husband said so.

## England's reasonable belief test v NSW affirmative consent

| England and Wales (SOA 2003) | NSW (from 1 June 2022) |
|---|---|
| Guilty only if belief in consent is not reasonable | Accused taken to know of non-consent if belief not reasonable (s 61HK) |
| No explicit 'say or do something' rule | No reasonable belief unless the accused said or did something to find out |
