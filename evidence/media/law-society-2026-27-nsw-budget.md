- Courts received a 'meagre' share of the $116.7 billion infrastructure program to 2030.
- **$3 million** for court technology, against **$108.8 million** for NSW Police technology.
- Domestic and family violence funding up 50% to $184 million; the South West Sydney Justice Precinct left out.
- The article's headline reports a $23.8 million cut to Legal Aid NSW, but the body does not give the figure.
