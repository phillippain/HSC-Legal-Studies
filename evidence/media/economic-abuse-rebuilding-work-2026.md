'Cat' was earning $10,000 a month on paper but could not leave the house. Partner financial abuse costs victims and the economy an estimated $10.9b a year. The Leaving Violence Program gives up to $5,000 support ($1,500 in cash).

## Legal v non-legal responses

| Legal | Non-legal / gaps |
|---|---|
| NSW coercive control offence (s 54D) | 473 incidents, 1 offence proven to Dec 2025 |
| Paid family violence leave (employees) | Excludes contractors and sole traders |
| Leaving Violence Program | Up to $5,000 |
