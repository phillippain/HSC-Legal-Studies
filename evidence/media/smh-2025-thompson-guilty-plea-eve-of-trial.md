**Mackenzie Anderson** was killed in her Newcastle home in **March 2022**, not quite 22 years old.
**Tyrone Thompson**, her former boyfriend, had served **just over four months** for assaulting and
threatening her. He contacted her as soon as he was released, **despite an AVO**, and killed her
**less than two weeks** after that release.

On **Tuesday 1 April 2025** he **pleaded guilty to murder** — after maintaining his innocence for
**three years**, and **on the eve of his trial** in the NSW Supreme Court.

She was one of **16 NSW women** to die in domestic violence–related homicides over the **two years
to June 2022**.

## Use it for

- **crime.3.4 (pleas and charge negotiation) — the timing is the whole point.** A plea entered the
  day before a trial, after three years of denial, is the clearest illustration of how the plea
  system actually operates: the **sentencing discount** under the *Crimes (Sentencing Procedure) Act
  1999* and the *Criminal Procedure Act 1986* is largest for an early plea and smallest for a late
  one, and he took the smallest. Read it with
  `cowdery-charge-negotiation-criminality-principle` and
  `early-appropriate-guilty-plea-reforms-2018`: the 2018 reforms exist precisely to pull pleas
  earlier, and here they did not.
- **What a late plea saves and costs (resource / exp.5.3)** — the Crown had briefed a trial, the
  witnesses had been prepared, and the family had waited three years. The plea spares them the
  **trial** but only after the full cost of preparing for one. A precise resource-efficiency
  argument, and a precise argument about victims' families.
- **crime.2.6 / fam.2.4 — the AVO and the release.** He was convicted of assaulting and threatening
  her, served **four months**, and contacted her **immediately** on release in breach of an AVO. The
  sequence — conviction, short sentence, release, breach, homicide — is the single most
  concentrated piece of evidence in the library on the **limits of a protection order** and on what
  happens at the point of release. Pair it with `bocsar-cjb278-dfv-typology` and
  `smh-2025-lilie-james-inquest`, where separation was also the trigger.
- **Separation as the risk point (fam.2.4)** — she had told him to leave her alone and to stop
  harassing her mother. Domestic violence death reviews identify the period **after** a victim ends
  the relationship as the most dangerous, and this is that pattern.
- **16 deaths in two years** is the figure to use for scale, attributed as reported.
- **crime.4 (sentencing)** — a guilty plea to murder, entered late, with an extensive prior history
  against the same victim. Useful for the interaction of the **plea discount**, **prior record** and
  **objective seriousness**, though the sentence itself is not in this article.

## Caution

**Extremely graphic, and about a real young woman who was murdered.** The article is labelled
"Graphic content" and recounts the killing and the offender's words in detail; this record
deliberately omits most of it, and **so should any classroom resource**. Flag it before use, keep to
the legal sequence, and do not reproduce the detail of the injuries or the quotations from the
offender. Support information: **1800RESPECT 1800 737 732**, Lifeline **13 11 14**.

On the law: this reports a **plea**, not a sentence. As at the article's date Thompson had **not been
sentenced**, so nothing here tells you the outcome, the discount applied, or the remarks on
sentence — **check for the sentencing judgment** before using it for crime.4, because that is where
the legally interesting material is. The facts recited are the **agreed facts** presented to the
court and the police account as reported; they are reliable as far as that goes but they are
journalism, not a judgment. The claim that he contacted her "despite an AVO" and the four-month
sentence are as reported and not sourced to a document. The **16 women** figure covers the two years
to June 2022 and is now dated. Mackenzie Anderson is **named** and her family has spoken publicly;
treat her name with care rather than as a case label. The file is stored under `Family/` in the
library but the dot point it serves best is **crime.3.4**.
