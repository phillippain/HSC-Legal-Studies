Tenants' Union CEO Leo Patterson Ross says bond interest could fund stronger enforcement. Rental Commissioner Trina Jones responded. The Taskforce issued 470 penalty notices worth $560,000 in 2025–26, and complaints rose from 8,383 to 10,745.

## What bond interest may fund

| The law (s 186) | Issue |
|---|---|
| Tenancy advice, education and dispute services | Tenants' Union says funding is too thin |
| Administration of the Act | Taskforce enforcement relies on it |

A [second class summary of the same ABC report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1hXRZ22hfaiBmWGckSVAiinU6ei49esR4/view) adds that $31m+ of bond interest funds tenant advocacy and support (helping 69,000 people), and that the Tenants' Union's December 2025 submission modelled forgone returns of about $97m to $178.5m a year.
