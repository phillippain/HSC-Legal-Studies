Jack Westren, **25**, who lived with an acquired brain injury, was murdered at a train station. All six children were charged with murder; one admitted murder and five admitted **manslaughter**. A psychologist questioned the 16-year-old's claim he cannot remember the attack; sentence is due on **17 December 2026**.

## Murder v manslaughter pleas

| The 16-year-old: murder plea | Five co-offenders: manslaughter pleas |
|---|---|
| Murder and an unauthorised pistol (a gel blaster) | Murder, with back-up affray charges |
| Guilty to murder and pistol charge (Children's Court, 4 August 2026) | Guilty to manslaughter (21 July 2026) |
| Intent to kill or cause GBH, or reckless indifference (s 18) | Any other punishable homicide; no such intent |
| Stabbed Mr Westren with a hunting knife | Ambushed and kicked him while he was on the ground |
| Supreme Court; judgment reserved to 17 December | Due early December |

The 25-year standard non-parole period for murder does not apply to an offender under 18.
