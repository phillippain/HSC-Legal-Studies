Katherine Local Court handled an estimated **588** domestic and personal violence applications in 2025–26; police recorded
**79** DV-related assaults in Katherine in October 2025 alone. The NT's intimate partner homicide rate is about **7 times** the
national average, and **$2.55m** will expand the specialist DFV court model to two more NT locations from 2027–28.

## What Katherine has v what the sector asks for

| | Katherine now / proposed fence | Sector asks for |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | One entrance and screening point shared by victims and perpetrators | Separate victim-survivor entrance and exit |
| Waiting | No separate waiting room; waits can last hours | Private waiting or safe room, as in Darwin |
| Private meetings | One room, often occupied; advice given near a busy intersection | Twice the space for legal services |
| Grounds | Open garden; fence would funnel everyone through one gate | Alternative entrances before any fence |
| Specialist list | No specialist DFV approach (2023) | Alice Springs-style specialist court |

NSW requires a safe room, safe waiting area or alternative arrangement at all operational court locations. The NT's 2023
amendments added coercive control, economic and technology abuse, and a five-year default DVO.
