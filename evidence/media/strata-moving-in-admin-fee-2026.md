A new resident in Prospect, Western Sydney, was invoiced $1,000 after a strata manager reviewed security footage of an unapproved move-in; the fee was later reportedly waived. Fair Trading says owners corporations generally cannot fine owners directly. Strata mediation applications rose 30% to 3,591 in 2025–26.

## Lawful by-law enforcement v what happened

| What the law requires | What reportedly happened |
|---|---|
| Owners corporation resolution before acting | Strata manager invoiced after viewing footage |
| Written s 146 notice to comply first | $1,000 invoice within days |
| Only NCAT imposes a penalty (s 147) | Manager charged it directly as an 'admin fee' |
| Up to $1,100; $2,200 for a repeat breach | Flat $1,000 with no hearing |
