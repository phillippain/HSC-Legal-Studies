Linton Ryan, an Aboriginal man aged 31 or 32, was arrested in Kalgoorlie on 27 March 2025 and died in prison custody on 2 April 2025. The inquest opened in Perth on 23 November 2026. The interviewing constable had two weeks' service; it was her first interview, and she was later mocked by colleagues.

## What should happen v what the inquest heard
