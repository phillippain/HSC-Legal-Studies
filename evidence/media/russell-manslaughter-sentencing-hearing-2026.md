**Samuel Liszczak** was shot dead on 9 November 2023 between Taree and Port Macquarie. **Tyrone Russell**
was tried for murder and ran **self-defence**; after the jury could not agree unanimously, Weinstein J
allowed **majority verdicts** and on 20 March 2026 Russell was found not guilty of murder but guilty
of **manslaughter**, plus three counts of discharging a firearm with intent. Co-accused **Cory Glass**
was convicted as an **accessory after the fact** to manslaughter and of firearms offences. Sentencing
submissions began on 23 October 2026; the hearing resumes **17 December 2026**. **No sentence has
been passed.**

## Murder charged, manslaughter found

| | Murder | Manslaughter |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | s 18(1)(a): act causing death with intent to kill or cause GBH, or reckless indifference | s 18(1)(b): every other punishable homicide — e.g. excessive self-defence (s 421) |
| Maximum | Life (s 19A) | **25 years** (s 24) |
| Standard non-parole period | 20 years | **None** |

Juries give no reasons, so **why** the jury chose manslaughter is not known.

## The sentencing hearing

- Four relatives read **victim impact statements** (ss 28, 30D–30E); a cousin asked why Russell did not
  call Triple Zero. Harm to the community is a purpose of sentencing (s 3A(g)), but a victim's wish for
  vengeance or forgiveness should not change the sentence (*R v Palu*).
- During submissions the judge spoke of split-second decision-making and unsophisticated offending.
- Possible s 21A factors (Russell was reportedly on Victorian parole) are for the judge.

self-defence was the jury's basis. Check the remarks on sentence after 17 December 2026.
