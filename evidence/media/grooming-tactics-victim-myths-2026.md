Survivors took an average **23.9 years** to disclose child sexual abuse (Royal Commission). The ACCCE received **82,764** online child exploitation reports in 2024–25 (up 41%; 100,194 in 2025–26), and 17.7% of 16–24-year-olds were sexually solicited online by an adult as children.

## The myth of staged grooming v the research

| Myth | Research finding |
|---|---|
| Friendship, then trust, then secrecy, in order | 14 moves overlapped and repeated; no typical structure |
| A slow build-up the child could notice | Strategies used rapidly, out of order |
| The victim could spot the steps and leave | Rapport-building ran throughout, dismantling red flags |
| A child who kept replying chose to engage | Questions cast the target as in control, building deniability |

Based on seven offender chat transcripts; ACCCE reports cover all online exploitation, not grooming alone.
