NSW Police's **Rural Crime Prevention Team** (about 60 officers) investigates stock theft, theft of
farm machinery and firearms, and illegal hunting.

- In the **first six months of 2025**, **484 cattle** (worth $761,013) and **4,093 sheep** (worth
  $541,948) were stolen in NSW: about **$1.3 million**.
- Thieves use **drones** to watch properties before stealing up to 1,000 head at a time.
- Police use **DNA** from ear tissue and hair to match stolen animals, trialled **satellite tracking**
  (a stolen sheep was recovered within 25 minutes), and are exploring cattle **facial recognition**.
- Ear tags under the National Livestock Identification System are easily removed.
- Rural crime is **under-reported**: farmers lack faith in police and courts. A 2021 University of New
  England survey found farmers who had dealt with the team were more satisfied with police.
- Officers say rural crime peaks when **stock or fuel prices** are high.
