**Sonya Dutton** was found dead on 29 March 2022. The inquest opened in February 2025; on 10 November 2026 the coroner found no
evidence to suggest or exclude homicide, and made **five** recommendations (two described in coverage).

## First v second police investigation

| | First (2022, Barrier PD) | Second (2023–24, State Crime Command) |
|---|---|---|
| Lead | A detective with about one year's experience, appointed by email | Det Chief Insp Paul Smith with homicide detectives |
| Statements | No formal statements from key witnesses | **44** statements |
| Community | Officer had never worked in Wilcannia | Six visits, about three weeks, with Aboriginal liaison officers |
| CCTV | USB left with a person of interest; footage incomplete | Missing footage could not be recovered so late |
| Conclusion | Suicide view emailed before leave, despite doubts | Cannot suggest or exclude homicide |

The State Coroner ordered the reinvestigation (s 51(2)) in October 2023, about 18 months after the death.
