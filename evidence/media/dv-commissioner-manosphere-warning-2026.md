Commissioner **Micaela Cronin** says online misogyny ("the manosphere") is shaping young men's attitudes faster than
law and policy can respond. In one survey **24.7%** of boys aged 13–17 agreed feminism may be violently resisted; **70%**
of 2,600+ teachers saw manosphere ideas affect students in the past year; reported sexual violence rose **64%** over 15
years. Of 21 Rapid Review recommendations (August 2024), responses have been "progressive".

## Legal v non-legal responses

| | Legal | Non-legal |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | Mostly after harm; coercive control (s 54D) from 1 July 2024 | Prevention and early intervention target attitudes first |
| Online | Digital duty of care Bill (passed the House 28 October) | Respectful relationships education; 95% of teachers say some students need it |
| Oversight | Courts, police | The Commission monitors the National Plan; Second Action Plan from 2027 |
