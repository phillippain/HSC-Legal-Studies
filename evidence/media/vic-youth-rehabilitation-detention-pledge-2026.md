Premier Ben Carroll's pledge: **$204m** for secure, court-ordered "rehabilitation detention" of up to **12 months** for
theft, vandalism, property damage and minor assault, with mandatory drug, alcohol, life-skills, school or trades programs —
about **500** young people over two years, likely at Malmsbury. No costing, age range or legislation has been published.

## Current custody v the pledge

| | Youth justice custodial order (now) | Rehabilitation detention (proposed) |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Found guilty of an imprisonable offence | Low- and mid-level offenders |
| Threshold | Serious enough and no other sentence appropriate (s 325) | Not explained |
| Length | 15+: up to 3 years (one offence), 4 years (several) | Up to 12 months, held around the clock |
| Safeguards | Pre-sentence reports, written reasons, Aboriginal principles (s 210) | None announced |

## How far Victoria has moved

- **2024**: the *Youth Justice Act* raised the minimum age to **12**.
- **26 March 2025**: community safety became the paramount bail consideration; remand of a child is no longer "a last resort".
  Youth remand was reported up **46%** year-on-year (October 2025).
- **27 February 2026**: children 14+ charged with eight violent offences are tried in the County Court ("adult time").
- AAP reports Aboriginal Legal Service criticism; the ABC report includes no critics.
