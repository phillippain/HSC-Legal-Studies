## What changed (2025–26)

| | Before (to mid-2025) | After |
|---|---|---|
| Child Care Subsidy | Limited power to act on safety | Can be **suspended or cancelled** for safety or quality failures (2025 Cth Act) |
| Abuse reporting | 7 days | **24 hours** |
| National Law penalties | Existing maximums | **Tripled** from 1 January 2026; nine times for 25+ services |
| Misconduct | No specific offence | **Inappropriate conduct** offence, up to $172,000 |
| Workers | No national register | **National worker register**, mandatory child safety training |
| Transparency | Hard to find | StartingBlocks shows enforcement history |

**Enforcement so far:** **120** services put on notice to improve or lose subsidies (115 in July); **12**
have lost Commonwealth funding; about half under conditions met standards. But **1,300+** services fail the
National Quality Standard, large chains with poor records are not on the notice list, and **473** services
are outside the National Quality Framework (only 142 to be brought in).

**The Injinoo case (Cape York):** an educator was criminally sentenced, but **QCAT** overturned the
cancellation of the provider's approval in July 2026 because its systems had been fixed — criminal and
regulatory responses running separately.
