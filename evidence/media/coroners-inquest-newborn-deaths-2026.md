The inquest examines **four** newborn deaths (2022–2024). Baby DH lived about **8 hours**; the unregistered doula, paid **$2,000** and with no medical training, gave evidence as a witness and denies giving clinical advice. Paramedics spent **32 minutes** trying to revive the baby.

## An inquest is not a trial

| Coronial inquest | Criminal or civil courts |
|---|---|
| Who died, when, where, and how | Whether a known person committed an offence, beyond reasonable doubt |
| Findings must not suggest an offence; only the DPP decides on charges | A conviction or acquittal |
| The doula is a witness | Not charged with any offence |
| Findings and recommendations | Civil liability decided separately |
