Over three days police arrested and charged **681** people, laid **1,594** charges and executed **239** arrest warrants. **283** of **11,375** ADVO compliance checks found an alleged breach (about 2.5%). All Amarok rounds since 2023 have produced **9,478** arrests and over 20,000 charges.

## Compared with earlier rounds

| Amarok XII–XIV (Nov 2025 to Aug 2026) | Amarok 15 (Nov 2026) |
|---|---|
| Four, four and three days | Three days (12–14 November) |
| 752, 993 and 732 people charged | 681 — the lowest of the four rounds |
| 1,629, 2,063 and 1,589 charges | 1,594 charges |

## Use it for

- **fam.2.4** — enforcement of ADVOs and bail as a proactive response.
- **Effectiveness** — arrests are not convictions; remand is at record levels; critics say intervention comes too late.

## Caution

A police media release; round lengths differ, so totals are not like-for-like.
