HRW interviewed **42** people (June 2025 – June 2026) across nine SA prisons: **12 of 14** interviewees with psychosocial
disabilities had been held in solitary, **4** for 15 days or longer; a wheelchair user spent **8 days** without an
accessible bathroom. SA publishes no data on prisoners with disabilities; the Department refused HRW access to two
prisons and its reply is annexed.

## UN standards v South Australia

| | International standard | South Australia |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | Solitary = 22+ hours a day without meaningful contact (Rule 44) | "Separation"; s 36 sets no daily-hours test |
| Duration | Over 15 consecutive days is prolonged and prohibited (Rules 43–44) | 30-day directions, extendable repeatedly, no cap |
| Disability | Prohibited where it worsens a disability (Rule 45; CAT 2022) | No disability limit in the Act |
| Review | Independent review, last resort | Chief Executive orders; Minister told after 5 days |
| Oversight | Independent OPCAT mechanism | Official Visitors report to the Minister; DCS says they are not OPCAT |

Section 36 requires a written direction within 24 hours, but no interviewee recalled receiving one. Australia is bound by
the **Convention against Torture**; the **Mandela Rules** are non-binding minimum standards.
