## The seizure

In **June 2026** the AFP found **2.7 tonnes of cocaine** (about **$816 million**) in bunkers under
shipping containers at **Londonderry** in Western Sydney. Police allege it was part of a **3.1-tonne**
importation worth more than **$1 billion**, landed near Midge Point in Queensland; the vessel MV
Wealth was detained in the Solomon Islands. **Six people** were charged. The investigation began with a
truck fire at a boat ramp.

Importing a commercial quantity is punishable by **life imprisonment** (Criminal Code Part 9.1).

## The market

- **ACIC wastewater monitoring (2026)**: record cocaine and methamphetamine consumption.
- **UNODC World Drug Report 2026**: a record **331 million** people used drugs worldwide in 2024.

## Use it for

- **Transnational crime**: production in South America, shipping across the Pacific, help from the
  Solomon Islands, distribution in Sydney.
- **Effectiveness**: record seizures against record use. Supply-side enforcement alone has not reduced
  demand.
