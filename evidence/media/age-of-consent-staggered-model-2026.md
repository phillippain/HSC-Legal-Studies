The age of consent is **16** in most jurisdictions (17 in SA and Tasmania). Only **7.7%** of women sexually assaulted by a man contacted police (ABS Personal Safety Survey 2021–22).

## Current law v a staggered model

| Current law (NSW and most states) | Staggered model (UK proposal) |
|---|---|
| 16-year-old with an adult: lawful if consent is free and no special care | Offence unless the partner is 19 or younger |
| 17-year-old with an adult: lawful outside special care | Offence unless the partner is 20 or younger |
| Teachers, carers, coaches: s 73 special care offence | Positions of trust already criminalised, now incl. sport and religion |
| Similar-age defence (s 80AG): gap of two years or less, complainant 14+ | Three-year cap aims not to criminalise teen relationships |
