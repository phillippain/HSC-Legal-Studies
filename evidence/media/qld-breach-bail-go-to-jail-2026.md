Introduced on 27 August and passed in late October 2026, the Bill commences on **30 November 2026**. The government
concedes parts are incompatible with human rights and has made **override declarations** (expiring after five years).

## Before v after

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Offending on bail | Breach of conditions (s 29): max 40 penalty units or 2 years | **New s 29AA**: min **1 year**, max 3 years |
| Sentencing | Court's choice — 84.4% non-custodial (2005–16) | **Mandatory minimum**, served wholly in prison or detention |
| Bail test | Show cause (s 16(3); YJA s 48AF) | Show cause, **then** refuse unless a "high degree of confidence" of no reoffending |
| Youth diversion | Police **must** consider cautions (YJA s 11) | Police **may** consider |
| Human rights | s 29 already applied to children (2023) | New overrides for ss 16AA, 29AA, 48AG and watch-houses |
| Accountability | — | Courts must give reasons for granting bail; police must record them |

Historically **95.5%** of breach-of-bail offenders pleaded guilty. Compare NSW's s 22C "high degree of confidence"
youth bail test.
