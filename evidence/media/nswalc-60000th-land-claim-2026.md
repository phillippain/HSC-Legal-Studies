NSWALC used the milestone claim to put numbers on the *Aboriginal Land Rights Act 1983* (NSW) in practice.

## The figures (NSWALC, January 2026)

- **60,000** claims lodged since 1983; in 2025–26, 672 claims over about 13,000 parcels (the NSW Government cites 3,163 lodged in the last financial year — the two count differently).
- **More than 44,000** claims undetermined at the end of 2025, some lodged in 1984.
- Average time to determination **15–20 years**, according to several independent reviews.
- **274 hectares** returned in 2025–26.

## What NSWALC asked for

Properly resourced Aboriginal Land Agreement teams; complete, real-time land tenure data for Land Councils; statutory timeframes and accountability for determinations; a whole-of-government commitment to clearing the backlog — "corrective, not symbolic" reform.

## Use it for

- **chg.5.1** — the impact of land rights legislation measured against its own purpose: the right exists, the remedy arrives decades later.
- **chg.4.2 / chg.1.6** — limitations of a reform that outran the administration built to deliver it, and the ongoing need of the group it was meant to serve.

## Caution

An advocacy body's release reported by a specialist outlet; the counts differ from the government's. Use the Audit Office report for the independent figures and the mechanism.
