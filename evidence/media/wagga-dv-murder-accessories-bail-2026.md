**Danielle Madgwick**, 23, was found stabbed at a Kooringal home on 19 September 2026; her alleged partner, 31, was charged
with murder that night. On 7 November police executed a search warrant and charged three others — men aged 18 and 56 and a
woman aged 53 — as **accessories after the fact**; all were refused police bail and next appear on 23 December. No one has
been convicted.

## Principal v accessory

| | Murder | Accessory after the fact |
|---|---|---|
| Law | Crimes Act ss 18, 19A | s 349 |
| Conduct | Act or omission causing death | Knowingly assisting the principal after the crime |
| Fault | Intent to kill or cause GBH, or reckless indifference | Knows the offence was committed; aims to help avoid arrest or punishment |
| Maximum | Life | 25 years |
| Bail starting point | Show cause (punishable by life) | Not expressly listed; s 16B(k) may apply (unverified) |

## The 2024 DV bail reforms

After **Molly Ticehurst** was killed in April 2024, NSW made serious intimate partner DV offences show cause, required
electronic monitoring unless the interests of justice say otherwise, allowed grants of bail to be stayed while prosecutors
apply to the Supreme Court, and required bail decisions to consider red-flag behaviour and victims' views. BOCSAR found
53.5% of adults refused police bail in 2023 were later granted bail by courts.
