**32** producers emitted over half of 2024 fossil fuel and cement CO2. All **16** large Australian emitters studied had public net zero targets, but only **5** reviewed or audited their decarbonisation investments. ASIC's record greenwashing penalty is **$12.9m** (*ASIC v Vanguard*, 2024).

## Disclosure v action

| What the law requires | What it does not |
|---|---|
| Large companies disclose climate risks and targets (AASB S2) | Any obligation to cut emissions |
| Misleading claims can be greenwashing (ASIC, ACCC) | Independent review of transition plans |
| National targets (Climate Change Act s 10); Safeguard Mechanism for big facilities | Company-level targets enforceable by affected people |
