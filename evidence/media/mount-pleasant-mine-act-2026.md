The High Court (3–2) found the mine's 2022 consent invalid for not dealing with scope 3 emissions. The Act creates a separate statutory approval with the Planning Minister as consent authority, allowing 16.5 Mt a year to the end of 2032. The government cited 400 jobs at risk; that figure came from the operator and was disputed.

## 2022 consent v the Act

| 2022 IPC consent | Mount Pleasant Mine Act 2026 |
|---|---|
| Independent Planning Commission | Parliament; Planning Minister as consent authority |
| Up to 21 Mt a year to 2048 | Up to 16.5 Mt a year to end 2032 |
| No scope 3 conditions — held invalid | Export limits to Paris-aligned countries |
