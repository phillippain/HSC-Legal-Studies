The finalised Stage 1 rezoning covers 155 hectares near the new Orchard Hills Metro station, allowing up to 5,900 homes and 2,100 jobs. After 891 submissions across two exhibitions, the Department shrank the 2024 proposal (350 ha, 11,600 homes) and removed The Vines estate.

## 2024 proposal v finalised Stage 1

| 2024 proposal | Finalised (2026) |
|---|---|
| About 350 hectares | 155 hectares |
| About 11,600 homes | Up to 5,900 homes |
| Up to 4,000 jobs | 2,100 jobs |
| Affordable housing not fixed | 2% at rezoning, 3% a year later |
