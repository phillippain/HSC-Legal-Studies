An ABC data feature uses ACLED data and UN findings after the Seventh CCW Review Conference (16–21 November 2026). OHCHR verified at least 2,230 civilians killed in Sudan (January–October 2026), over 1,630 by drones; 64 Ukrainian civilians were killed by short-range drones in May 2026.

## What IHL requires v the gap

| IHL rule | Gap |
|---|---|
| Distinction (AP I art 48) | Autonomous systems may not recognise surrender |
| Proportionality and precautions (arts 51, 57) | Attribution and accountability are hard |
| Weapons review (art 36) | No dedicated treaty on autonomous weapons |
