The **Community Housing Industry Association NSW** (CEO Luke Achterstraat) presented research at NSW Parliament on 4
November 2026: a **7.5%** short-stay levy could raise about **$135m a year**, and its broader options (mostly land tax)
about **$26bn** over 10 years. NSW's $6.6bn Building Homes for NSW program promises 8,400 public homes.

## Victoria v NSW

| | Victoria (levy) | NSW (planning controls) |
|---|---|---|
| Law | *Short Stay Levy Act 2024*, from 1 January 2025 | SEPP (Housing) 2021, framework from 1 November 2021 |
| Mechanism | **7.5%** of booking fees for stays under 28 days, earmarked for housing | Mandatory register, code of conduct; 180-day cap on non-hosted stays in some areas |
| Who pays | Booking platform, or the owner for direct bookings | Host registers; $65 first-year fee |
| Housing revenue | Yes | None |

The Government, Opposition, Greens and industry responded; NSW announced a short-term rental review in December 2023.
