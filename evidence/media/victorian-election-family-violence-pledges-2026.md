On the day of the Walk Against Family Violence in Melbourne, advocates said the issue was absent from election pledges. Victoria Police recorded 102,836 family violence incidents in 2025–26 (down 3.4%), but serious family violence assaults rose 15.8%.

## Party pledges and responses

| Pledge | What advocates or evidence say |
|---|---|
| Labor: life maximum for rape; IVO breach maximum doubled to 10 years | Sexual assault rates do not respond to law and order reforms |
| Labor: $16m prevention over four years | Safe and Equal asks $554m a year for core services |
| One Nation: tough on crime | No specific policy |
