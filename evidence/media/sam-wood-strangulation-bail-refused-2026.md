Television personality **Sam Wood** was charged with **assault occasioning bodily harm** and **choking, suffocation or
strangulation in a domestic setting** over an alleged incident at Noosa on 3 October 2026. A magistrate refused bail
on 8 October; the **Supreme Court refused bail** on 30 October. By 31 October he had spent 28 nights in custody;
both sides agreed a likely head sentence of about two years with parole after 12 months. **He denies the
allegations and is presumed innocent.**

## Queensland v NSW

| | Queensland | NSW |
|---|---|---|
| Offence | s 315A: choking, suffocation or strangulation **in a domestic setting** | s 37(1A): intentional choking, suffocating or strangling without consent — any person |
| Introduced | 5 May 2016, after the Special Taskforce (*Not Now, Not Ever*) | December 2018 |
| Maximum | **7 years** | 5 years; 10 years if reckless as to unconsciousness (s 37(1)) |
| Bail | **Show cause** offence (s 16(3)(g), since 2017); court must weigh risk of further DV (s 16(2)(f)) | Check the Bail Act 2013 (NSW) |
| Next reform | Bill proposes a 14-year tier | Coercive control (s 54D) since 1 July 2024 |

A US study found prior non-fatal strangulation raises the odds of later partner homicide about **7.5 times**. Trial
may not occur until 2027–28; a new bail application needs a material change of circumstances.
