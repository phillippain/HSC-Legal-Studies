Fair Trading's 2026–27 priorities are **renting**, **property and strata**, and **consumer goods**. In
renting it will make sure the 2024–25 reforms are applied fairly; in property, **underquoting** and agent
conduct (new 2026 Act); in strata, **repairs and maintenance**.

## The reforms it is enforcing

| | Before | After (in force by 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Ending a periodic lease | No reason, 90 days' notice | **Valid reason with evidence** (from 19 May 2025) |
| End of fixed term | No-grounds, 30 days | Reason needed; 60 or 90 days |
| Rent increases | Not all lease types limited | Once a year for all (31 Oct 2024) |
| Pets | Consent, no deadline | Refuse on permitted grounds only; silence for 21 days = consent |
| Re-letting after eviction | Nothing to test | **Exclusion periods**; Taskforce data-matches rental ads |
| Renovation evictions | No reason | Since June 2025 a **written statement** is enough (tenant groups object) |

**First year:** the $8.4m Rental Taskforce handled **7,712** compliance matters and issued about **600 fines**
($421,650); eviction-ban fines totalled **$65,450** (about 30 breaches); one agency was fined $35,000 for
re-letting during an exclusion period.

- **shel.2.6 / effectiveness** — a regulator stating intent but no targets or budget; fines punish but do not
