The Queensland Government voted down the Opposition's Civil Liability (Holding Institutions Accountable for Child Abuse) Amendment Bill 2026 on 17 November. In **Bird v DP** (2024) the High Court held vicarious liability needs a true employee, excluding priests and volunteers. ACT and Victoria have passed 'akin to employment' laws; 59% of Royal Commission survivors were abused in religious institutions.

## Current Queensland law v the defeated bill

| Current law (after Bird) | Scanlon bill (defeated) |
|---|---|
| Vicarious liability needs a true employee | Persons 'akin to' an employee covered (s 33IB) |
| No liability for non-employees, whenever abuse occurred | Applies to past and future causes of action |
| Settlements made after Bird on weak terms stand | Court may set aside post-Bird settlements if just |
