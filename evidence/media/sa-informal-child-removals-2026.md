Across three complaints, SA's Department for Child Protection changed where children lived by placing
them with another parent or relatives under **safety plans**, despite their mothers' clear disagreement.
The Ombudsman found **administrative error**; the DCP says it has updated its manual of practice.

## Formal removal v informal placement

| | Formal removal (2017 Act) | Safety-plan placement |
|---|---|---|
| Legal basis | s 41: reasonable belief of serious harm, no practicable alternative | Agreement with the family; no statutory power |
| Time limit | Chief Executive custody ends by the **fifth business day** (s 43) | None |
| Court | Youth Court care and protection application; Department must prove need | **No court review** |
| Parent | Can contest; appeal to the Supreme Court | Policy requires genuine agreement — disregarded here |
| Child | Lawyer unless the child decides otherwise (s 64) | No independent representation described |

Compare **NSW**: a Children's Court care application within 3 working days of removal (s 45(1A)). SA's new
2025 Act (targeted for July 2027) adds active-efforts duties for Aboriginal children. SA had 4,482 children
in care at 30 June 2025 — informally placed children are not counted.
