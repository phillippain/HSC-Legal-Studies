The Treasurer rejected independent MP Allegra Spender's alternative CGT plan as unlikely to be fairer. New home loans fell **15.0%** in the September quarter (a record), investor loans **30.6%** and first home buyer loans **8.6%**; Grattan reports a Treasury estimate of **35,000** fewer homes built over a decade.

## The tax reforms

| Already law | Before the Senate |
|---|---|
| 50% CGT discount ends for gains from 1 July 2027 | Second tranche and carve-outs (Tax Reform Nos 3 and 4 Bills) |
| Negative gearing limited to new builds | Crossbench and Greens negotiation; the Spender alternative |

Fast-moving political reporting; the ABS does not attribute the lending falls to any cause, and interest rates are at an 18-year high.
