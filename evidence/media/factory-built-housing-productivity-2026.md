The Committee for Sydney (housing director Estelle Grech) calls for more modern methods of construction (MMC). Construction productivity fell about 21% from 2013 to 2025, and NSW has built 21% of its Accord target after 7 of 20 quarters. NSW now regulates MMC directly under the Building (Approvals and Practitioners) Act 2026.

## Traditional on-site v factory-built (MMC)

| Traditional on-site | Factory-built |
|---|---|
| About 95% of new homes | About 5% (45% in Scandinavia) |
| Three+ years for a NSW apartment | Up to 25–50% faster |
| Materials costs up over 60% since 2013 | Up to 20% lower costs claimed |
