The **Human Rights Bill 2025 (NSW)** was referred to a Legislative Assembly committee on 18 March 2026; 120+ civil
society organisations called for the inquiry. The HRLC gave evidence on 2 November 2026, calling NSW's current
protection a "patchwork".

## NSW now v under the Bill

| | NSW now | Under the Bill (first print) |
|---|---|---|
| List of rights | Scattered: common law, Constitution, statutes | **31 rights** (ss 14–44), including housing, health and education |
| Scrutiny of Bills | Legislation Review Committee, advice only | Statement of compatibility (s 56); joint human rights committee |
| Courts | Principle of legality — clear words needed to override | Read laws compatibly where reasonably possible (s 45) |
| Agencies | No general duty | Unlawful to act incompatibly or ignore relevant rights (s 49) |
| Remedies | Mainly discrimination complaints | Commission complaint, or Tribunal or Supreme Court action (s 50) |
| Validity | Parliament can limit rights with clear words | Incompatible laws **stay valid** (s 45(4)); no express damages |

The ACT (2004), Victoria (2006) and Queensland (2019) have Acts; the HRLC cites 101 examples of charters in action.
