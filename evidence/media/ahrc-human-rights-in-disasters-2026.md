Human Rights Commissioner Dr Lorraine Finlay released *Resilience Through Rights* and an explainer, *How to Protect Human Rights in Disasters*. A survey of 2,000 adults found 55% affected by a natural hazard disaster in 10 years, 67% reporting financial impacts and only 42% saying support was sufficient. First Nations respondents reported displacement at 87% compared with 60%.

## Rights-blind v rights-based response

| What people reported | Principle |
|---|---|
| Expected to fit rigid systems | People-centred: design around circumstances |
| First Nations, disabled and older people hit harder | Fair: prioritise those most at risk |
| Only 31% felt local government understood | Early, accessible, culturally safe support |

## Use it for

- **hr.2.4** — an independent statutory authority promoting rights.
- **Effectiveness** — recommendations without enforcement power.

## Caution

Survey data reflect self-reported experiences; the AHRC cannot compel adoption.
