The reforms respond to 'bait and bash' attacks. Publicly threatening or inciting violence (s 93Z) rises from 3 to 5 years, with a new aggravated offence of 7 years, and hate motive becomes easier to prove at sentencing. 197 anti-LGBTQIA+ hate incidents were recorded.

## Before v after

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Offence only once assault occurs | s 54BA luring complete at the lure (5 years) |
| s 93Z maximum 3 years | 5 years; aggravated 7 years |
