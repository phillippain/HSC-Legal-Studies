Wake stabbed his former wife 17 times on 25 December 2021 and pleaded guilty to murder about two years later. He was sentenced to **26 years** with a 17-year non-parole period. On 18 November 2026 the Court of Criminal Appeal heard his argument that the sentence is manifestly excessive; the decision was not reported.

## The appeal: defence v prosecution

| Defence | Prosecution (DPP) |
|---|---|
