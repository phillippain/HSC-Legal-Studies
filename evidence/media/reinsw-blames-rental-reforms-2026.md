REINSW's CEO says government is "at fault for the pain tenants are feeling". New housing loan commitments fell **27%** from their
November 2025 peak (RBA, all borrowers), though NSW had reported investor lending up **23.4%** from September 2024 to December
2025. Sydney's vacancy rate rose to **2.5%** (from 1.8%) and rents fell **0.4%** in the September quarter 2026.

## Tenant rights before and after the reforms

| | Before | After (from October 2024 / May 2025) |
|---|---|---|
| Ending a periodic lease | No reason; 90 days' notice | Valid reason plus evidence; usually 90 days |
| End of a fixed term | No reason; 30 days' notice | Reason needed; 60 or 90 days |
| Rent increases | — | Once every 12 months for all leases |
| Pets | Landlord consent required | Refusal only on permitted grounds |

The no-grounds eviction ban took effect on **19 May 2025**.
