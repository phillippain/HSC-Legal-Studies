**Cameron John Matthews**, 43, allegedly called police "skinheads" and gave Nazi salutes in Liverpool St, Hobart, about midnight
on 23 August 2026; next day, by video link, he swore at the magistrate and saluted **three** times. He was sentenced to **two
months** for the Nazi gesture offence (backdated to October), plus two Commonwealth counts of using a carriage service to threaten
to kill. The magistrate cited personal and general deterrence.

## Tasmania v the Commonwealth

| | Tasmania: Police Offences Act s 6D | Commonwealth: Criminal Code s 80.2H |
|---|---|---|
| In force | 30 November 2023 | Salute ban from 8 January 2024 |
| Conduct | Nazi gesture in or visible from a public place | Public Nazi salute or display of prohibited symbols |
| Maximum | 20 penalty units or 3 months (doubled for a repeat within six months) | 5 years after 2025 amendments |
| Minimum | None — full discretion | **12 months** mandatory |
| Defences | Genuine academic, artistic, religious, educational, legal or law enforcement purpose | Not checked |

NSW's s 93ZA Nazi symbol offence carries up to 12 months.
