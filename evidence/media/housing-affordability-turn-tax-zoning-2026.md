New home loan numbers fell **15%** in the September quarter 2026 (a record fall), investor loans **30.6%** against 5.3% for owner-occupiers, and the cash rate reached **4.85%**. The Accord is projected to fall about **220,000** homes short of 1.2 million.

## State density zoning now v the opposition pledge

| Now (state Labor governments) | Opposition pledge |
|---|---|
| NSW: state SEPP standards apply near 171 centres and stations | Councils may submit their own plan, certified by the Department |
| Up to 112,000 homes over five years expected | A council plan must meet or exceed its allocation |
| Non-discretionary standards limit council discretion | If no plan is approved, the state settings still apply |
| Opposition: only 4,900 homes approved by February 2026 | Critics: helps anti-development councils obstruct |
