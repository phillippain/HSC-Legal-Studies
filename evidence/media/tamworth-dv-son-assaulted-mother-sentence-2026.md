A man in his 20s admitted **seven** offences against his mother across two incidents (November 2025 and February 2026),
including intimidation, assault occasioning actual bodily harm, three common assaults and two counts of damaging property. The
magistrate called DV "a scourge in our society". He received a **one-year intensive correction order** with 50 hours' community
service, a supervised community corrections order and **$1,900** in fines. He now lives with his grandparents.

## Sentencing a DV offender in NSW

| Step | Rule |
|---|---|
| A DV offence | Personal violence against a relative (CDPVA s 11; parents and children are relatives) |
| Aggravating factors | E.g. offending on conditional liberty — here, months after an ADVO (s 21A(2)(j)) |
| Section 4A | Full-time detention or a supervised order, unless the court gives reasons for another option |
| Section 4B | An ICO only if the victim is adequately protected |
| Last resort | Jail is a last resort (s 5); an ICO is a prison sentence served in the community |

Maximums: intimidation and ABH 5 years each; common assault 2 years.
