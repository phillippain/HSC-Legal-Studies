Ministers Sophie Cotsis and Janelle Saffin announced the taskforce days after the Anti-slavery Commissioner's response to a parliamentary inquiry (31 recommendations, August 2026) was tabled. Woolworths said over $100,000 was returned to supply-chain workers in 2025–26.
