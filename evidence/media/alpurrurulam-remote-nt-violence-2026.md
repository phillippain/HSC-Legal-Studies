On **12 October 2026** a 16-year-old girl died at the clinic in **Alpurrurulam**, a dry community
near the Queensland border, after an alleged assault; police believe she and the accused were in a
domestic relationship. A 26-year-old man was charged with **murder** on 15 October, did not seek
bail, and is next listed in Alice Springs on 10 December. **He is presumed innocent.** The
community's police station closed in **2018**; officers came from Avon Downs, **170 km** away, with
Queensland police sworn as NT special constables.

On 21 October the ABC reported that **Marion Scrymgour** (Member for Lingiari and Special Envoy for
Remote Communities) had written to both governments seeking the station's reopening, and that
**AMSANT** wants dedicated DFV funding for Aboriginal health services, specialist workers and safe
accommodation. No government response was reported.

## The legal pathway, and where distance breaks it

1. **Report** — any adult must report a reasonable belief of serious domestic violence to police
   (*DFV Act* **s 124A**, 200 penalty units). Here the clinic reported the death.
2. **Attend** — no local police; officers, detectives and forensics travelled in.
3. **Protect** — police or the Local Court can make a DVO; breach is a crime. A DVO assumes police
   can serve it and somewhere safe to go.
4. **Charge and court** — murder charge, no bail application, matter moved to Alice Springs.
5. **Support** — AMSANT says the clinic is often the only after-hours contact; safe accommodation is
   shrinking.

## The numbers behind it

- First Nations women's family-violence hospitalisation rate **27×** the non-Indigenous rate
  (**41×** in very remote areas) — Commonwealth figures.
- **82 of 87** NT women killed by DFSV since 2000 were First Nations (cited at the 2024 inquest).
- Coroner Armitage, Nov 2024: **35 recommendations** after four Aboriginal women were killed by
  partners already reported to police; the NT rejected three, including a DFSV peak body; AMSANT's
  Feb 2026 report card found most only partly addressed and 12 omitted from the NT's 2025–28
  strategy (45 initiatives, no new funding).
- A new Alpurrurulam police complex was listed for 2026 in 2023 and reported funded in June 2025;
  a 2026 Estimates question asks whether it is proceeding.
- Commonwealth: *Our Ways – Strong Ways – Our Voices* (Feb 2026), **$218.3m** for up to 40
  community-controlled services. Closing the Gap Target 13: halve family violence by 2031; no data
  shown after the 2018–19 baseline.

## Caution

A **class-made summary** of a **short news report quoting advocates**, with no government or police
response; Scrymgour's letter is unpublished. **The murder charge is an allegation** — avoid any
comment on guilt and **do not identify the girl**. Statistics are secondary reports of the inquest
and a ministerial release; coronial response counts differ between sources (21/11/3 v 13/23/3); the
Wadeye claim is the envoy's opinion. Check the December court listing and any government reply
before using the station question as settled.
