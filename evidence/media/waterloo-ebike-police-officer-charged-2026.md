An e-bike rider died after a police pullover in Waterloo about 3am on 28 March 2025. About 20 months later a NSW senior constable was charged with **dangerous driving occasioning death** (s 52A, maximum 10 years); the first court date is 19 January 2027. The charge is unproven. The police union has reacted with a no-pursuit directive.

## Police power v the limits that hold police to account

| Power | Accountability limit |
|---|---|
| Stopping a rider on reasonable suspicion | Suspicion needs a factual basis (R v Rondo) |
| Pursuing a driver who refuses to stop | Must stop when danger to the public outweighs the need |
| Fleeing police is a crime (s 51B, up to 3 years) | Officers' own driving is judged under the same s 52A offence |
| NSW Police investigates its own critical incidents | LECC monitors in real time but does not investigate |
