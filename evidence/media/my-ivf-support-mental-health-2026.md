About **1 in 3** people in fertility treatment report anxiety or depression, but (per the article) fewer than
**25%** receive mental health support. **Dr Katrina Moss** (UQ), who stopped after 13 cycles, led a team with
Monash and Cardiff experts to build **My IVF Support**, with sections for patients, partners and supporters.

## Law and funding v the website

| | Law and funding | My IVF Support (non-legal) |
|---|---|---|
| Authority | State ART laws, RTAC accreditation, NHMRC guidelines | A university project; no legal force |
| Counselling | NSW: must be **available** to patients and spouses before treatment, **optional** to use (s 12) | Self-guided tools through every phase |
| Funding | Better Access: up to 10 sessions a year with a diagnosis and referral | Cost not stated |
| Accountability | Regulators; RTAC is not independent of providers | Rests on the project team |
| Reform | Draft national standards would require access to qualified counselling; transition by **December 2028** | Online now; not independently evaluated |

In 2024, **31.1%** of embryo transfers led to a live birth — about 1 in 5 per initiated cycle, so the "one in
three" fits only per transfer.
