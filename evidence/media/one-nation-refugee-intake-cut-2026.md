Australia resettled 19,000 refugees and resettles about 700 per million people, against an OECD average of 68. UNHCR projects 2.5 million refugees will need resettlement in 2026.

## Convention duties v resettlement

| Legal duty | Policy choice |
|---|---|
| Non-refoulement for those who arrive | Offshore resettlement numbers |
| Fixed by treaty | Set by government (20,000 or 2,000) |
