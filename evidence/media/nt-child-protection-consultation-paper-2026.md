More than 800 NT children are in out-of-home care; fewer than 18% of Indigenous children are placed with Indigenous carers. The July 2026 changes made the Child Placement Principle apply only 'as far as practicable'. DCF says the paper's options are not final policy.

## Current law v options

| Current law | Option in paper |
|---|---|
| Courts can order contact and kinship assessments | Courts limited to directing services |
| Families can propose carers | Only DCF proposes carers |
| — | Adoption pathway; workers seek DVOs |
