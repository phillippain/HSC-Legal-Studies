**Operation Shelter** began in October 2023 to police tension after the 7 October attack on Israel, rotating
officers from local commands. FOI documents show its annual cost rose from **$4.1m** in its first year to **$41.8m**
in 2025/26, and **$53.7m** in the ten months after the 14 December 2025 Bondi attack. The **Armed Response Command**
(ARC), announced in February 2026, became operational on **1 November 2026** at an establishment cost of **$94.3m**.

## Operation Shelter v the ARC

| | Operation Shelter (2023–) | Armed Response Command (2026–) |
|---|---|---|
| Model | Surge operation, officers rotated from local commands | Permanent 24/7 command with an operations centre |
| Staffing | About 97% of cost is shifts, overtime and allowances | **250** officers and 28 civilian staff |
| Weapons | General duties officers with pistols (e.g. Chanukah 2025) | Australian Combat Assault Rifles; 24 vehicles with gun safes |
| Cost | $41.8m (2025/26) | $94.3m to set up; operating budget "absorbed" |
| Protests | Remit included large protests | No rules restricting where it operates; protests not ruled out |

## How armed force is checked

LEPRA **s 230**: force reasonably necessary → if death or serious injury, a **critical incident** (LECC Act s 110) →
police investigate themselves (s 113; often 2–3 years) → the **LECC** monitors but cannot investigate the officers
until police finish (s 44(9)) → final advice and recommendations. Amnesty seeks independent oversight of the ARC.
