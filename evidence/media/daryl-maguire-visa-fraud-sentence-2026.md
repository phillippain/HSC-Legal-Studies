Daryl Maguire, former Member for Wagga Wagga, recruited local businesses into a scheme with migration agent Maggie Wang to obtain visas fraudulently. Judge Bourke placed the offending at the lower end and imposed **three years** with a one-year non-parole period; he is eligible for parole in November 2027.

## Two offenders, two sentences

| Daryl Maguire | Maggie Wang |
|---|---|
| Recruited Wagga Wagga businesses; important, pivotal role | Migration agent; the Crown called her Maguire's associate |
| Three years, non-parole one year | Aggregate 2 years 10 months, served in the community |
