Domain forecasts peak-to-trough falls of **13%** for Sydney and **11%** for Melbourne houses; the cash rate is **4.85%**. Treasury estimates the tax reforms add **75,000** owner-occupiers over ten years and less than **$2** a week to rents; economists dispute the rent figure.

## Who gains

| Effect on buyers | Effect on renters |
|---|---|
| Negative gearing and CGT reform: less investor competition | Under $2 a week more rent (Treasury); economists expect more |
| 5% Deposit Scheme: uncapped places, $1.5m Sydney cap | No direct benefit; low-deposit buyers risk negative equity |
| NSW: no stamp duty to $800,000 | No direct benefit |
