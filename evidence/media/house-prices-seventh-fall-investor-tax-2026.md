National home values fell **1.2%** in October 2026, a **seventh** monthly fall, leaving them **6.7%** (about $63,400) below
the March peak; sales were **23%** lower over three months than a year earlier. The national median weekly rent is
**$713**, up 5.5% over the year. Higher interest rates (four RBA rises) and the tax changes both contributed; the
article does not separate them.

## What changes for investors (from 1 July 2027)

| | Now | After 1 July 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Negative gearing | Rental losses on any property deductible against wages | Only for **new builds**; established-home losses offset property income only |
| Grandfathering | — | Properties held before 7:30pm AEST, 12 May 2026, exempt |
| CGT | 50% discount after a year | **Inflation indexation** on gains accruing from 1 July 2027 |
| Minimum tax | None | 30% minimum on real gains (some pensioners exempt) |
| Unresolved | — | "New dwelling" definition and build-to-rent exemptions still in a draft tranche |

The Tax Reform No. 1 Bill passed on 25 June 2026 with Greens amendments.
