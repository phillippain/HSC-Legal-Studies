The API and Homer analysed **102,681** Victorian sales: **43%** sold within the first price guide buyers saw, and only **68 of
1,919** agents sold within their own guide in four of five campaigns. Victoria's taskforce issued **185** fines worth over $2m
(September 2022 to July 2025).

## Victoria v NSW

| | Victoria | NSW |
|---|---|---|
| Law | Estate Agents Act 1980 | Property and Stock Agents Act 2002 |
| Estimate | Reasonable; single figure or range up to 10% | s 72A: reasonable; range up to 10% |
| Price in ads | Required since 1 May 2017 | Required from **17 November 2026** |
| Comparable sales | Property Price Statement from 1 October 2026 | Statement of Information from 8 February 2027 |
| Reserve price | Published at least 7 days before auction | Not in the reforms |
| Maximum penalty | About $50,000; possible loss of commission | $110,000 or three times commission |
| Valuation | No independent valuation power found | Fair Trading can order an independent valuer check (since 29 June 2026) |

Neither state requires a pre-sale independent valuation; the API is lobbying for one.
