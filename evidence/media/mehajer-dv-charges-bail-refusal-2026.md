**Salim Mehajer**, on parole since **18 July 2025** after serving part of a 7 year 9 month aggregate sentence for domestic violence and fraud offences, was arrested by the NSW Police **Domestic Violence High Risk Offender team** at a Wentworth Point café on **4 November 2026**. At Auburn he was charged with DV-related common assault, larceny over $15,000 and DV-related stalk/intimidate with intent to cause fear of physical harm (s 13, maximum **5 years**). Police refused bail. On **5 November** Parramatta Local Court also refused bail, citing his long record (including DV and dishonesty), his parole and bail status, and a strong prosecution case. **Everything about the new matter is an allegation; he intends to contest the charges and is presumed innocent.**

## From arrest to bail decision

| Step | What happened |
|---|---|
| Arrest (4 Nov) | Specialist DV High Risk Offender team after investigating an alleged incident on 26 Oct |
| Charge and police bail | Three charges at Auburn; police refused bail |
| First court appearance (5 Nov) | Bail application at Parramatta Local Court; prosecution outlined the allegations (about $41,500 of jewellery taken; a push and later threats) |
| Which test? | Section 13 carries 5 years, a serious indictable offence; allegedly committed on parole, so a **show cause** offence under *Bail Act 2013* s 16B(1)(h) — the accused must show why detention is not justified (s 16A). *This is the summary's reading of the Act; reports do not say how the court applied it.* |
| Decision | Bail refused; remanded in custody |

The 2024 DV bail reforms (show cause and mandatory electronic monitoring) cover only **serious DV offences** (14 years or more), so they probably did not apply to these charges.

## System context

- The Ticehurst killing in April 2024 (accused was on bail for DV charges) led to the *Bail and Other Legislation Amendment (Domestic Violence) Act 2024*.
- BOCSAR (February 2026): record **6,081 adults on remand** (46% of prisoners); **1,943** held for DV offences, about one in three adults on remand.
- The NSW Bar Association warned expanded show cause would jail people later acquitted and could worsen Aboriginal over-representation.
