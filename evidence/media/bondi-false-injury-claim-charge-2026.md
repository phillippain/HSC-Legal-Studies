The **Bondi Beach terror attack** (14 December 2025) killed 15 people; 41 were taken to hospital. On **27
October 2026** detectives arrested a 46-year-old man at Parramatta and charged him with **publishing false or
misleading material to obtain an advantage** and **making a false representation** causing a police
investigation, alleging he falsely claimed to have been injured to obtain compensation. He was granted
conditional police bail and is due at Parramatta Local Court on **18 November 2026**; Home Affairs then
detained him as a non-citizen. **Nothing has been proved; he is presumed innocent.**

## What the prosecution must prove

| Element | Likely offence |
|---|---|
| A statement false or misleading in a material particular, made dishonestly, intending to obtain property or a financial advantage — no payment need be proved | s 192G (max **5 years**) |
| Knowingly telling police something false that called for investigation | s 547B (max **12 months**) |

Victims Services offers injured primary victims counselling, up to $5,000 immediate needs, up to $30,000
economic loss, a recognition payment and a **$35,000 Bondi recovery payment**; money wrongly paid can be
recovered by restitution.
