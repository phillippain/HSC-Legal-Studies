About **72** people are homeless each night in the Southern Highlands; priority social housing waits are about 5 years
and average waits over 10 (Argyle Housing). Southern Highlands charity **Lydia's Legacy** wants to place moveable tiny
homes (8 m by 2.5 m, up to **$150,000** each) on private rural land offered by a landowner.

## Granny flat v tiny home on wheels

| | Granny flat (secondary dwelling) | Tiny home on wheels (moveable dwelling) |
|---|---|---|
| Legal category | A dwelling under the planning instruments and LEP | A caravan, if registrable as a trailer |
| Approval | Development application; fast-track CDC unavailable in rural zones | No approval if the **cl 77** exemption applies; otherwise DA and s 68 |
| Who may live there | Can be rented once approved | Long term only by the **owner's household** or disaster victims — not a homeless tenant |

A 2026 Legislative Council inquiry reported in March; the government supported **3 of 6** relevant recommendations in
principle.
