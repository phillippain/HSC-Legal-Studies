The AFP-led **Fraud Fusion Taskforce** alleges **$235m** in actual and potential fraud across seven investigations into
the NDIS, Centrelink and other programs. Over three months police executed **26 search warrants** in four states and
seized 44 devices and 37 cloud accounts. **Five people are charged; four investigations have no arrests yet.**

| Operation | Allegation | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Hawk (NDIA) | Over $700,000 taken through false invoices | Charged by Victoria Police; Horsham court 3 December |
| Inca-Varrakai (NDIA) | An NDIA employee gave 78 participants' records to linked providers | **s 142.2** abuse of public office (max 5 years); Adelaide 9 December |
| Cedar (Services Australia) | A fake myGov site compromised about 2,000 Centrelink records; about $2.5m lost | Three charged under **s 400.9(1A)** proceeds of crime (max 2 years) |

## Claim check and prevention (6 November summary)

- **Prevention v punishment**: the charges are the *punishment* side. The *Securing the NDIS for Future Generations Act
  2026* (20 August 2026) adds a Schedule of fraud measures, and a separate integrity Bill would let the NDIA ask for
  evidence before paying claims; breaching an NDIS banning order becomes an offence (max five years).
- **Process**: the Taskforce (co-led by the NDIA and Services Australia since November 2022, $126.3m over four years)
  used 26 warrants, 108 police and 156 agency staff; the CDPP prosecutes in state courts. The three Operation Cedar
  accused (two women, 23 and 22, and a man, 26) were reported by the ABC as bailed on conditions — surrender passports
  and report to police three days a week — but **the AFP release does not mention bail**.
- **Penalties**: s 400.9(1A) carries two years and/or a **$43,680** fine, modest beside a $2.5m loss; s 142.2 up to five
  years. The NDIA-employee matter involves a 42-year-old Adelaide woman; linked providers allegedly earned over $1.6m.
- **Not charged**: four investigations, including a $29m DVA billing matter (a 34-year-old man; warrants 14 October)
  and the Liverpool and Wakeley syndicate (claims of about $83m; $6.7m suspected laundering), have no arrests.
- **Nuance**: "rorted" overstates the $235m — nothing is proven. The government's "over $200m" savings estimate (2024)
  is not audited; the ANAO is auditing the Taskforce. A provider received a three-year sentence in Adelaide in 2025.
