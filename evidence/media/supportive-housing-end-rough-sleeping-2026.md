The 2026 NSW Street Count found **2,308** people sleeping rough, up 5%. Per Capita estimates **6,437** supportive homes are needed by
2033 (half from existing social housing, half new); yearly government savings of **$227m** would exceed the **$187m** net running
cost, returning **$1.39** per dollar over 60 years.

## Current approach v the proposal

| | Now | Per Capita proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Right to housing | Housing Act s 5 sets objects only | No new legal right; a funding commitment |
| Homes | Building Homes for NSW: $6.6bn, 8,400 social homes | 6,437 dedicated supportive homes |
| Support | $130m for 200+ crisis beds by 2029 | Ongoing case management, mental health, drug and alcohol, after-hours support |
| Public space | Non-binding Protocol; police move-on powers (LEPRA s 197) | Removes the need to sleep rough |
| Discrimination | No homelessness or accommodation-status ground in the ADA | Not addressed; NSWLRC review under way |

People would be identified through By-Name Lists and a planned NSW Street Sleeping Registry.

## Caution

**Advocacy research** commissioned by those who would deliver the homes, reported uncritically. The 6,437 figure covers persistent
homelessness, not only rough sleeping.
