Developer Landmark changed law firms and alleges only about $5m of $24m+ in deposits reached its new firm. Buyers in Dee Why (about 140) and Blacktown (145) were told on settlement day their deposits had not arrived. Justice McGrath froze the firm's and solicitor's accounts.

## Protection v the gap

| Protection | Gap |
|---|---|
| Deposits held in trust | Money allegedly diverted |
| Fidelity Fund compensation | $1m cap per practice unless lifted |
| Disclosure and cooling-off | Do not protect against trust fraud |
