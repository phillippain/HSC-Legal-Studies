New Zealand voted on **Saturday 7 November 2026**; the ABC analysis (published the day before) examines party policies on Te Tiriti. The National–ACT–NZ First coalition has already wound back Treaty-based provisions by **ordinary Acts passed on simple majorities**, because New Zealand has no entrenched constitution for these matters. Party pledges are campaign promises only.

## Before the coalition v after

| | Before (to 2023) | After (2024–2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Maori health | Te Aka Whai Ora (Maori Health Authority) operating | Disestablished under urgency, 68–54, 28 Feb 2024 |
| Children in care | s 7AA bound Oranga Tamariki to Treaty principles | s 7AA repealed 68–54, 3 Apr 2025 |
| Treaty principles | Developed by courts and the Tribunal | ACT's Treaty Principles Bill defeated **112–11**, Apr 2025 (300,000+ submissions, 90% opposed; hikoi of about 42,000) |
| Waitangi Tribunal | Hears claims; most recommendations non-binding | ACT (10 Sep) and NZ First (26 Sep) pledge to abolish it; a Crown review is unreleased |
| Maori seats | Seven electorates, created 1867 | NZ First wants a binding referendum; ACT wants abolition by statute |

## How a Waitangi Tribunal claim works

A permanent commission of inquiry (Treaty of Waitangi Act 1975), not a court: (1) a Maori person or group lodges a claim of a Crown breach; (2) claims are registered and grouped (new historical claims closed in 2008); (3) a panel hears claimants and the Crown; (4) it reports whether each claim is well-founded and recommends a remedy; (5) the Crown decides whether to act. Most recommendations are **non-binding**; binding powers over Crown forest licence and former state-owned enterprise land have never been fully used.

## Key facts

- Maori ethnic group: 887,493 people (**17.8%**, 2023 Census); the "nearly one million" figure is Maori descent (978,246).
- A Human Rights Commission survey (Horizon, Dec 2025): **70%** of New Zealanders want Te Tiriti protected in law and the constitution.
- In December 2025 the UN CERD urged NZ to restore s 7AA and revitalise a Maori health authority, and warned against reinterpreting the Treaty without Maori free, prior and informed consent.
- Comparison: Australia's Voice referendum failed in October 2023 (60.06% No); Victoria passed its statewide Treaty Bill in October 2025.

## Competing perspectives

- **ACT and NZ First**: services by need not race; the Tribunal's urgent inquiries resemble alternative policy processes; seats were meant to be temporary.
- **Te Pati Maori, Labour, rights bodies**: keep the Tribunal (Te Pati would make recommendations binding); the Tribunal found the Treaty Principles Bill policy breached Treaty principles; seats reflect Treaty partnership.
