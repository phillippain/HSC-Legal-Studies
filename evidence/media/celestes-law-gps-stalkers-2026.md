The VLRC's 2022 stalking report made **45** recommendations, none on electronic monitoring. For **45%** of women, their most recent male stalker was an intimate partner. In Queensland's 2019 police GPS trial, only **49%** of expected alerts were activated.

## The pledge v the Queensland model

| Victorian Coalition pledge | Queensland DVO pilot (from 1 October 2025) |
|---|---|
| High-risk stalkers (also described as anyone with an FVIO or PSIO) | High-risk adult DVO respondents with DV or violent offence charges |
| Court order; no legislation released | Magistrate imposes it if necessary or desirable, with reasons |
| Victoria-wide trial implied | Only Townsville and Caboolture courts |
| Two-year trial, starting up to two years after the election | Two-year pilot; provisions expire (s 66H) |

## Use it for

- **fam.2.4** — making intervention orders enforceable; technology as a legal response.
- **Law reform** — reform driven by a victim's family and an election.

## Caution

An election promise only; the Liberals' claim that only 5 of 45 VLRC recommendations are implemented could not be checked.
