At a firearms forum in Windsor, NSW One Nation leader Mike Newman ruled out a coalition with the Coalition and pledged to repeal reforms passed in December 2025: caps of 4 firearms (10 for sport, pest control and primary production) and licences cut from five years to two. About 274,000 registered firearms are affected by the buyback.

## Before, after and the pledge

| Reform (2025–26) | One Nation pledge |
|---|---|
| 4 / 10 firearm caps | Abolish both caps |
| Licence term cut to 2 years | Restore 5 years |
| Buyback; dealer grants up to $25,000 | Repeal |
