Attorney-General Marie-Clare Boothby's bill was introduced on 25 November 2026, with debate expected in February. Thirty legal, Aboriginal and family groups raised concerns. Naming a defendant before committal currently carries up to six months' jail.

## Current NT law v the bill

| Now | Bill |
|---|---|
| Accused anonymous until committal | Accused may be named |
| Good character can mitigate | Removed for sexual offences, as in NSW |

## Use it for

- **crime.4.3** — limits on mitigating factors.
- **Balance** — open justice, presumption of innocence, victim safety.

## Caution

A bill only; the summary reports concerns from stakeholder groups.
