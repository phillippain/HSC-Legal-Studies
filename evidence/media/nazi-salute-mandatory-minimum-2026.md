**Zachery James Hook** pleaded guilty (29 June 2026) to offensive behaviour on Commonwealth premises for neo-Nazi
stickers around the ANU's Tjabal Centre (August–September 2025), and to two public **Nazi salutes** at the O'Connor and
Chisholm shops (October and December 2025). On 30 October 2026 he was fined **$5,000** for the stickers and
**committed to the ACT Supreme Court** for sentence on the salutes, because the magistrate could not impose the
mandatory minimum. First Supreme Court appearance: 18 November 2026.

## Two offences, two outcomes

| | Stickers at the ANU | Nazi salutes in public |
|---|---|---|
| Law | Public Order Act s 12(2)(b) | Criminal Code **s 80.2H** |
| Penalty | Fine only (max reportedly $6,600) | **Mandatory 12 months' prison** (s 16AAA), max **5 years** |
| Discretion | Plea, clean record and apology reduced the fine | "No discretion" below 12 months |
| Outcome | $5,000 fine | Sentence pending; on bail |

Before the *Hate Crimes Act* (from 8 February 2025) the salute offence carried a maximum of one year and no minimum.

## Use it for

- **hr.2.9 / hr.3.1** — statute against racial hatred and Nazi symbols.
- **Mandatory sentencing** — community condemnation against judicial independence and proportionality.
