**156** Bougainville residents, represented by the HRLC, complained to the AusNCP in 2020. Over **1 billion tonnes** of tailings were released into the Jaba and Kawerong rivers, and a Phase 1 impact assessment made **30+** recommendations.

## Two routes to remedy

| AusNCP complaint (non-judicial) | PNG class action (court) |
|---|---|
| 156 downstream residents, represented by the HRLC | More than 5,000 claimants seeking billions |
| Good offices and conciliation under the OECD Guidelines | PNG National Court, commenced May 2024 |
| Not binding; no finding on the merits | Binding, but dismissed as an abuse of process |
| Result: a voluntary US$250m fund | No remedy |

## Caution

A voluntary commitment: no timetable, governance model or program split has been published.
