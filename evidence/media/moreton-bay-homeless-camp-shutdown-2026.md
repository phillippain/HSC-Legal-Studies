After the *Bobeldyk* ruling found earlier evictions breached the *Human Rights Act*, the council opened **Wyllie Park**, Petrie, as a free tent site for a **12-month** trial (August 2026). It was ordered closed about three months in. Moreton Bay had **84** crisis beds (July 2025); 7,636 Australians lived in improvised dwellings, tents or slept out (2021 Census).

## How a state planning law closed the camp

| Step | What happened |
|---|---|
| Priority development area | Moreton Bay Central PDA (about 460 ha, including Petrie) declared 2016; scheme from 13 October 2025 |
| Show cause notice | EDQ said the site was illegal inside the PDA; unlawful use is an offence (s 76) |
| Conditional no-action letter | No legal action if the council registers no new campers and removes campers as registrations expire |
| Park vacated | Registrations reportedly last 21 days |
