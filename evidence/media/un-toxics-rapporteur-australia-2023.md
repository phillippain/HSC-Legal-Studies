During a 12-day official visit the Rapporteur criticised **$1.5bn** in government support for the Middle Arm hub near Darwin. The UN General Assembly recognised a right to a healthy environment **161–0**, with Australia voting yes; in 2026 Australia accepted 128 of 332 UPR recommendations.

## Recommendations v response

| Rapporteur (2023–24) | Australia's response |
|---|---|
| Moratorium on new fossil fuel developments; a just transition | Silent on a moratorium |
| Middle Arm sited near homes; scope 3 emissions not counted | To be assessed under NT and EPBC laws |
| Air standards less protective than WHO guidance | Victoria cites stricter PM2.5 standards |
| A healthy environment right in the Constitution and a federal Human Rights Act | No position |
