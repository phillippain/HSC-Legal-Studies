On 18 November 2026 Foreign Minister Penny Wong announced sanctions on three individuals and four entities. October 2026 recorded 397 settler attacks, the most since 2006. Breaching sanctions carries fines up to $910,000 for individuals, plus jail.

## Targeted sanctions v a trade ban

| Targeted sanctions (Australia) | Settlement trade ban (UK, France, Canada) |
|---|---|
| Named people and entities | All goods from settlements |
| Asset freezes and travel bans | Import prohibitions |
