## Claims v official data

| Coalition claim | Official or primary data |
|---|---|
| Nearly 700,000 in NSW at risk of homelessness | Impact Economics model: **696,442** (2022) — a model, not a headcount |
| Almost two in three seeking emergency housing don't get it | AIHW 2024–25: **35%** of clients needing it were provided it |
| Rough sleeping up 75% in five years | Street Count: **1,319** (2020) → **2,308** (2026) |
| Priority waitlist doubled | **5,800** (June 2021) → **12,478** (March 2026) |
| Rents up 48% | Median $460 → $680, 2020–25 (DCJ, via Homelessness NSW) |
| $1bn over four years avoids up to $2.1bn in costs | **No costing published** |

The 2021 Census counted 35,011 people homeless in NSW. Several figures come from Homelessness NSW, itself a signatory.
