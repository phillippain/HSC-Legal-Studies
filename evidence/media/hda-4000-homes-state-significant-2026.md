The **Housing Delivery Authority** (announced November 2024; written into the Act in 2025) is a panel of three
senior officials that recommends large housing projects for **State Significant Development** status, taking
assessment away from councils. Order No 12 (27 October 2026) declared **11** more projects (10 in Sydney),
with **4,000+** potential homes.

## Council pathway v HDA pathway

| | Council DA / ordinary SSD | HDA-declared SSD |
|---|---|---|
| Decision-maker | Council or local planning panel | Minister or delegate; the HDA only recommends |
| Council role | Assesses and determines | Notified; may comment |
| Rezoning | Usual amendment process | Concurrent (about 80% of projects) |
| Independent Planning Commission | Referral if council objects or 50+ objections | Not the consent authority (from August 2026) |
| Exhibition | 28 days | **14 days** |
| Affordable housing | e.g. City of Sydney permanent scheme | Required only where uplift is significant |

**Pipeline:** 509 proposals; SSD applications lodged for 95 (about 38,000 homes); **26 approved** (4,722+
homes); construction started at 11 sites; **32 declarations revoked**. NSW completions to March 2026:
**44,700** against **88,386** needed a year.
