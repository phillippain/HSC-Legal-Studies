'Leanne' says she spent about **$500,000** in legal fees. **83%** of 2023–24 final parenting applications alleged family violence; **61%** of parties screened by Lighthouse were initially classed as high risk.

## The reforms v one case

| What the law promises | What Leanne reports |
|---|---|
| Safety of the child and each carer is the first s 60CC factor | Father convicted of assaults given unsupervised, increasing time |
| Courts must consider family violence history and orders | AVO expired; judge found he had done nothing to address his violence |
| Equal shared parental responsibility presumption repealed (6 May 2024) | Sole responsibility, but she must consult him and facilitate handovers |
| Family violence exempts parties from mediation | Sent back to mediation |

The orders were later overturned on appeal.
