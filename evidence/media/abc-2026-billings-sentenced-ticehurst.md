Molly Ticehurst, 28, a childcare worker and mother, was murdered at her Forbes home late on
**21 April 2024** by her former partner **Daniel Billings**, who was **on bail** for sexual assault,
stalking and intimidation charges against her and subject to an **AVO**.

- **Plea:** guilty (November 2025).
- **Sentence:** Adams J, Supreme Court at Orange, **11 September 2026**: **28 years, non-parole 20**,
  reduced from 35 by a 20% discount for the plea; backdated to 20 April 2024; parole eligibility 2044.
- **Findings:** planned (day off work, recordings, drove from Parkes with a hunting knife); "high end"
  of seriousness; a "very serious public justice offence" because a witness was killed before her
  allegations could be tested. Depressive illness a contributing factor, not an abnormal state of mind.
- **Appeal:** the DPP decided not to appeal. Billings lodged a **notice of intention to appeal** on
  **9 October 2026**. Her father, Tony Ticehurst, found out from the media: "it just drags it on for
  another 12 months".
- The case triggered the **Bail and Other Legislation Amendment (Domestic Violence) Act 2024**.
