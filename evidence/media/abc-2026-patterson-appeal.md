The Victorian Court of Appeal (Taylor, McLeish and Kidd JJ) heard both appeals in the week of 19 August 2026.

- **Patterson** sought a retrial on seven grounds, including that jurors were accommodated with police and prosecution staff during the closing stages, complaints about evidence, the fairness of her cross-examination, and the use of her Facebook messages.
- **The DPP** argued that allowing parole (a 33-year non-parole period) was an error and the crimes were in the 'worst category', seeking life without parole.
- Judgment was **reserved**.
