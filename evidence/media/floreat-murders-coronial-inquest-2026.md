Jennifer Petelczyc and her daughter were killed in Floreat. It took **11 minutes** from the daughter's Triple Zero call to the first police arrival; **8** officers were found to have failed in their duties; WA Police says **14 of 18** review recommendations are complete.

## What should have happened v what happened

| What the law or policy required | What happened |
|---|---|
| A correct risk assessment gave grounds for a 72-hour police order | Wife and daughter told grounds were not met |
| Police must apply, make an order or record reasons (s 62C) | Calls warning of instability and 13 guns led to no order |
| A warning about guns should prompt search and seizure | Safe not inspected; over 1,000 illegal rounds not found |
| Sentimental reasons are no longer grounds for a collector's licence | Licence reissued unlawfully six times from 2005 |
| Disconnected Triple Zero calls should be returned | No callback; job graded priority two |
