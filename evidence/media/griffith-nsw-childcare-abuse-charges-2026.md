Former childcare worker **Ashley Griffith** pleaded guilty in Queensland to **307 offences** (including 28 rapes)
against about 65 children aged one to nine, committed 2003–2022, and was sentenced on 29 November 2024 to **life**
with a **27-year** non-parole period (parole eligibility 2049); the Court of Appeal upheld it in June 2026. After an
AFP investigation that traced background objects in abuse images to a Brisbane centre, NSW Police charged him in
June 2023 with **202 offences** against 23 children aged one to five at a Sydney centre (July 2014 – December 2017).
He was transferred to NSW on 28 October 2026; bail was formally refused and the matter adjourned to January 2027.
**The NSW charges are untested allegations.**

## Queensland (proven) v NSW (alleged)

| | Queensland | NSW |
|---|---|---|
| Status | Guilty pleas; convicted and sentenced | Charged only; **presumed innocent** |
| Offences | 307 | 202 alleged, including 110 under-10 intercourse counts (s 66A) |
| Stage | Sentence upheld on appeal | Bail court; adjourned to January |
| Outcome | Life, 27-year non-parole period | Bail refused; no application made |

**Pathway:** Argos (Qld) finds images online (2014) → AFP traces the centre (2022) → NSW charges and warrant →
transfer approved by Brisbane Magistrates Court on application by both Attorneys-General → NSW bail court.
