A 21-page digest of housing news the Tenants' Union circulates to advice services and members,
each item tagged to a topic. Read it as a **sample of what the private rental market looked like in
the first months after no-grounds evictions ended** — and as a ready list of quotable, dated
sources.

## Use it for

- **shel.2.9 / shel.2.10** — how an NGO curates the media: the digest *is* the NGO–media relationship
  the syllabus asks about.
- **shel.2.2 (leasing: rights and obligations)** — three concrete instances of practice diverging from
  the Act: entry fees, repairs, misleading listings.
- **Evaluation** — enforceability after reform: the stories show why the Rental Taskforce exists and
  what it has to police.

## Caution

**Second-hand**: each item is a one-paragraph précis with a link; go to the original article before
quoting. The selection is the Tenants' Union's. Dates are 2025 — check whether any of the matters
(the Mosman challenge especially) have since been decided.
