Nicholas Westcott (SOAS, a former UK and EU diplomat) compares a possible **Trump–Xi–Putin meeting** (discussed by a Kremlin aide for APEC in Shenzhen, 18–19 November 2026; unconfirmed by the White House) with the **Yalta** deal of February 1945, and argues great powers cannot carve up the world as the "big three" did. The summary tests his claims against UN, ICJ and US sources.

## Yalta-era bargaining v the Charter

| | Yalta, Feb 1945 | UN Charter and later rules |
|---|---|---|
| Who decides | Big Three settle other nations' futures | Art 2(1): sovereign equality of all members |
| Territory and force | USSR promised a Manchurian sphere | Art 2(4): no threat or use of force against territorial integrity |
| Internal affairs | "Friendly" governments in Eastern Europe | Art 2(7): no UN intervention in domestic matters, except Chapter VII |
| Great-power privilege | A veto for each permanent member agreed | Art 27(3): the P5 can block enforcement against themselves |
| Protecting people | Pledge of free elections | R2P (2005): a political commitment, not a treaty |

## Why enforcement stalls when a permanent member is involved

1. Russia began military operations in Ukraine on **24 February 2022**.
2. On **25 February 2022** Russia vetoed a Security Council draft condemning its aggression, despite **11 votes in favour (11–1–3)**.
3. The General Assembly passed ES-11/1 (**141 to 5**) demanding withdrawal, but it is **not binding**; since April 2022 every veto triggers an Assembly debate within 10 working days.
4. On 16 March 2022 the ICJ ordered Russia to suspend operations (13–2); the same day Russia was expelled from the Council of Europe, and it left the ECHR in September 2022.
5. The ICC issued warrants for Putin and Lvova-Belova (17 March 2023); Russia, never a party to the Rome Statute, calls them meaningless, and Mongolia failed to arrest Putin in September 2024.

**Venezuela**: US forces seized Maduro on 3 January 2026 and Delcy Rodríguez was installed as interim president; the UN Secretary-General called it a "dangerous precedent". UN fact-finders warned that crimes against humanity in Venezuela must not be overshadowed.

## Claim check

- APEC meeting and Yalta details: confirmed with nuance. The 2025 US strategy asserting hemisphere pre-eminence ("Trump Corollary"): confirmed; that it implied Russian and Chinese spheres is the author's reading.
- UN grew from 43 members in 1946: **disputed** (the UN lists 51 original members, 55 by end 1946; 193 today is correct).
- World trade up about 4,500% since 1950: **unverified**.
