The author interviewed **26** people for Victorian prison research; a 2017 Ombudsman inspection found **8** pregnant women at the Dame Phyllis Frost Centre. **No** Australian jurisdiction has a legal ban.

## Standards v practice

| What the standards say | What happens in Victoria |
|---|---|
| Bangkok r 24, Mandela r 48(2): never restrain in labour or birth | No legal ban; women described ankle-chained to hospital beds |
| Guiding Principles: no restraints during and after childbirth | A non-binding statement of national intent |
| Pregnancy not mentioned in the Guiding Principles | 2017: pregnant women routinely handcuffed at outside medical visits |
| Restraint only as a last resort | The General Manager may authorise it |
| Charter ss 10, 22: humane treatment | Rights apply, but the practice continues |
