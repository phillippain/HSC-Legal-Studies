Calvary Health Care confirmed on **5 November 2026** that it will apply the Catholic Health Australia *Code of Ethical Standards* at **Newcastle Private Hospital** once it takes over from receivership-stricken Healthscope. Calvary is buying a group of Healthscope hospitals (14 in the September 2026 deal, with 16 the SMH figure); completion was targeted for **30 November**. **The licence transfer and ACCC review are still pending.**

## Before and after at Newcastle Private

| Service | Under Healthscope | Under Calvary (as reported) |
|---|---|---|
| IVF procedures | Egg collection and embryo transfer in hospital theatres | Banned; the Hunter IVF laboratory on site is unaffected |
| Terminations | Offered, usually for fetal abnormality | Elective terminations end; the Code bars providing or referring |
| Contraceptive procedures | Vasectomy and tubal ligation | Some cut |
| Assisted dying | Lawful in NSW for eligible patients | Not provided; the hospital must publish this and help transfer patients |
| Gender surgery | Available | Ends |
| Maternity | About 1,600 births a year | Continues; emergency pregnancy care still provided |

Doctors were reportedly told that from **1 December** any banned procedure performed at the hospital will lead to disciplinary action. More than **1,000 IVF patients a year** are reported to be treated there. Only the SMH reports the 1 December date and the disciplinary warning (from a staff meeting).

## What the law does and does not do

- **Licence**: NSW controls the licence under the *Private Health Facilities Act 2007* (a "fit and proper" test), **not the mix of services** a private hospital offers. The Health Minister conceded this.
- **Voluntary Assisted Dying Act 2022 (NSW) Pt 5**: a hospital may opt out, but must publish that fact, must not hinder access to information and must take reasonable steps to transfer patients.
- **Abortion Law Reform Act 2019 (NSW) s 9**: an objecting *practitioner* must disclose the objection and give information or transfer care. It binds practitioners, not hospitals.
- **Assisted Reproductive Technology Act 2007 (NSW)**: regulates providers; it creates no duty on any hospital to offer IVF.
- **Sex Discrimination Act 1984 (Cth) s 37** exempts practices of religious bodies that conform to their doctrines; whether it shields a hospital's refusal of services has not been tested here.
- **ACT precedent**: in 2023 the ACT compulsorily acquired Calvary's Bruce public hospital, partly over limits on services such as abortion; the Full Court upheld the law (*Calvary Health Care ACT Ltd v ACT* [2023] ACTSCFC 1).
