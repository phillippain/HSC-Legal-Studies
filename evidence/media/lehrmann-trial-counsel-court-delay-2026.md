Former NSW Supreme Court judge Stephen Rothman will represent Bruce Lehrmann, charged in January 2023 with two counts of rape. The trial set for 2 November 2026 was lost when no barrister was engaged; it is now listed for **26 July 2027**. He has indicated he will plead not guilty; nothing is proven.

## How the charge reached trial

| Stage | What happened |
|---|---|
| Charge (January 2023) | Two counts of rape (s 349), maximum life |
| Committal (July 2024) | No-case submission rejected; committed for trial |
| Listing | Trial set for November 2026, lost with no barrister; relisted July 2027 |
| Backlog | 24.7% of Queensland District Court active criminal cases over 12 months old (32.5% in Toowoomba), 2024–25 |
