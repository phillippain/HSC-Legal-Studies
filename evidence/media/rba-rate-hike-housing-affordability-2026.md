On **3 November 2026** the RBA lifted the cash rate 0.25 points to **4.85%** by a 6–3 vote — about **$456 a month**
more on a $600,000 loan than in January (Canstar). AFCA home loan complaints rose **21%** to 7,014 in 2025–26; Westpac
was penalised **$26m** for ignoring hardship notices.

## Owners v renters

| | Owner-occupier with a mortgage | NSW renter |
|---|---|---|
| How costs rise | Variable repayments rise as lenders pass hikes on | Only through the s 41 rent increase process |
| Limits | Serviceability buffer at approval; s 131 unsuitability test | Once per 12 months, 60 days' notice — **no cap on amount** |
| Hardship | **s 72 hardship notice**; written answer within 21 days | No hardship variation right; s 43 reduction only in limited cases |
| Disputes | **AFCA** (free); lender generally pauses enforcement | NCAT, judged against market rent |
| Losing the home | Default notice, 30 days to remedy | Reasonable ground needed since May 2025 |
