An NT inquest is examining the death of a woman referred to as Ms Mirniyowan. Evidence included 12 or more serious violent incidents by her partner since 2018, and a CRAT risk score of 51. It took six to eleven weeks from a Family Safety Framework discussion to first specialist contact.

## What the framework required v what happened (evidence)

| Requirement | Inquest evidence |
|---|---|
| Every adult must report DV harm to police | 2020 clinic visit with injuries: no report recorded |
| High-risk cases referred promptly | Weeks before first specialist contact |
