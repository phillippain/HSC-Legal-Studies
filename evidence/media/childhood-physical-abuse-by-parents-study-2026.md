The **Australian Child Maltreatment Study** (8,503 people surveyed in 2021) found **32%** of Australians experienced
physical abuse before 18. A new analysis of the 2,623 survivors found **84.8%** were abused by **family** (parents,
step-parents, adult relatives) and **4.3%** by institutional adults (teachers, coaches, religious adults). Girls
were more likely than boys to be abused at home; institutional abuse was linked to boys' historical exposure to
punitive institutions. Most familial abuse began before age 10. (The headline's "one in four" refers to
16–24-year-olds.)

## The NSW pathway — and where it strains

Mandatory report (s 27) → Helpline screening against s 23 grounds → investigation (s 30) → Children's Court care
orders (ss 61, 71). In **2024–25, 65%** of screened-in risk-of-significant-harm reports were **closed unallocated**,
and only about a quarter of reported children were seen (2022–23). Meanwhile **s 61AA** still lets parents use
reasonable force to punish a child (not to the head or neck); the UN CRC Committee has urged a ban.
