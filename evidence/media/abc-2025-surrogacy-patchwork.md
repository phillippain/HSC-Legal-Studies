In January 2025 **Justice Catherine Carew** of the Federal Circuit and Family Court **refused a
parenting order** to a Brisbane couple who had used a commercial surrogacy service to have a baby
boy born overseas, and **referred the documents in their case to the Queensland DPP** to decide
whether charges should be laid. The depositions showed they had paid a company about **$140,000**.
Queensland Police confirmed it was making enquiries.

Experts estimate **hundreds** of Australian children are living with uncertain parentage.

## The law the couple ran into

**Commercial surrogacy** — where a surrogate is "paid more than their reasonable expenses" — is
**illegal in every Australian state and territory** for domestic arrangements. **Queensland, NSW and
the ACT** go further and make it a crime for their residents to pay a surrogate **abroad**. In
Queensland the offence carries up to **three years' jail**.

**Altruistic surrogacy**, where expenses may be covered but the pregnancy cannot be used for profit,
is lawful. The stated reason for the ban is to "protect the rights of each of the people involved" —
the concerns being **exploitation of women** and the **legal rights and citizenship of the
children**.

## Use it for

- **fam.3.3 (surrogacy and birth technologies)** — the single best illustration that the prohibition
  has teeth and that the teeth bite the child. The couple sought an order about their son and left
  court facing a police investigation.
- **The conflict at the heart of the dot point** — the court's two jobs pull opposite ways: the
  child's best interests say make the order; the criminality of the arrangement says do not reward
  it. Pair with ***Bernieres***, where the Full Court said the gap is Parliament's to close, and
  with ***Farnell & Chanbua*** (Baby Gammy).
- **fam.1.2 / inv.5.1 / inv.5.2 (state and federal jurisdiction)** — the extraterritorial offence in three
  states and not the others is the cleanest "patchwork" fact available: the same conduct by a
  Victorian and a Queenslander is a crime for only one of them.
- **chg.1 / chg.2 (conditions and agencies of reform)** — a single case generating a prosecution
  referral, national media coverage and a push for reform, with the ABC reporting it as the trigger.
- **Evaluating effectiveness (exp.6)** — measure the ban against what it was for. It has not
  stopped Australians going overseas; it has produced children whose parentage is uncertain.

## Caution

A **news report, not the judgment**: the case is not named or cited here, the reasons are summarised
in a sentence, and the figure of "hundreds" of affected children is attributed to unnamed experts.
Find the decision before relying on the reasoning. The **referral to the DPP is not a charge** —
charges had not been laid and may never be, so do not write that the couple was prosecuted or
convicted. The article is from **May 2025**; check whether anything came of the Queensland
enquiries, and whether the law reform push it describes has gone anywhere, before using it as
current.
