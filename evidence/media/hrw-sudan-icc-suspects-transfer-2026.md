Human Rights Watch called on Sudan to surrender three Darfur suspects to the International Criminal Court. Former minister **Ahmed Haroun** (2007 warrant, 42 counts) was reportedly detained on 8 November 2026 but not transferred. Sudan is not a Rome Statute party, but Security Council Resolution 1593 binds it to cooperate. Ali Kushayb, by contrast, was convicted on 27 counts and sentenced to 20 years.

## What international law requires v what Sudan has done

| Requirement | Sudan's record |
|---|---|
| Res 1593: cooperate fully with the Court | Hosted the Prosecutor's team; cooperation called positive |
| Art 89: arrest and surrender on request | Haroun reportedly detained; not transferred |
| Execute outstanding warrants | Four Darfur warrants still unexecuted after 19 years |
| Non-cooperation findings | Six referrals to the Security Council; no measures taken |

## Use it for

- **crime.6.2** — the ICC's reliance on state cooperation.
- **Effectiveness** — Kushayb's conviction against long-unexecuted warrants.

## Caution

An NGO release with an advocacy aim; Haroun's detention and whereabouts are unconfirmed.
