PRD Research's report lists affordable, liveable suburbs; Regents Park ($1.4m median) tops Sydney's list. 60,043 5% Deposit Scheme guarantees were issued in 2025–26, while only 308,000 of 1.2 million Accord homes were built in 7 of 20 quarters.

## Demand-side v supply-side responses

| Demand-side | Supply-side |
|---|---|
| 5% Deposit Scheme, Help to Buy, stamp duty relief | Planning reform, Housing Accord |
| Can push up prices in cheap suburbs | Behind target |
