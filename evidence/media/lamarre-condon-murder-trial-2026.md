Former NSW police officer **Beau Lamarre-Condon** has pleaded not guilty to the **murders** of Jesse Baird and Luke
Davies, allegedly shot with his police pistol on 19 February 2024; he surrendered at Bondi four days later and has
been in custody since. The jury trial is expected to run about three months. On 3 November a police officer's
statement of what the accused told police was read to the jury. **He is presumed innocent; nothing has been
decided.**

## Crown v defence (as opened)

| | Crown | Defence |
|---|---|---|
| Why he entered | Planned to kill Mr Baird after an alleged obsession | Went to collect belongings |
| The pistol | Taken from a Balmain station locker on 16 February | Carried because protocol barred leaving it in a car |
| Shootings | Three shots; Mr Davies killed to leave no witness | Two struggles over the gun, Mr Baird first with a knife |
| Afterwards | Deliberate cover-up | Panic after the deaths |
| Result sought | Murder, self-defence excluded beyond reasonable doubt | Not guilty — self-defence |

## The law

Self-defence (s 418) is a complete defence if the accused believed the conduct necessary and it was a reasonable
response to the circumstances as perceived. Once raised, the **Crown must disprove it** beyond reasonable doubt
(s 419). If the belief was genuine but the response unreasonable, **excessive self-defence** (s 421) reduces murder to
manslaughter. Agreed facts include the disguise and moving the bodies.
