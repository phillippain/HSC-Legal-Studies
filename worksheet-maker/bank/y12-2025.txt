=== terms y12-terms-cj
c: cj.3 cj.4 cj.5 cj.6
- Remand => Being held in custody while waiting for a trial or sentence.
- Judge-alone trial => A trial for an indictable offence in which a judge, without a jury, decides the verdict.
- Diversionary program => A program that directs an offender away from the usual court or prison path and into treatment, education or support.
- Security classification => The level of security, such as maximum, medium or minimum, at which a prisoner is held.
- Extended supervision order => A Supreme Court order that keeps a high-risk offender under strict supervision in the community after their sentence ends.
- Continuing detention order => A Supreme Court order that keeps a high-risk offender in custody after their sentence ends.
- Non-parole period => The minimum time an offender must spend in custody before being eligible for release on parole.

=== terms y12-terms-ir
c: ir.1 ir.2
- International relations => The ways nation-states and international organisations deal with each other, including through diplomacy, trade, law and conflict.
- Diplomacy => The management of relations between states by negotiation.
- Multilateral treaty => A treaty between three or more states.
- Bilateral treaty => A treaty between two states.
- Globalisation => The growing connection of countries through trade, technology, travel and communication.
- United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples => The 2007 declaration that sets out the individual and collective rights of Indigenous peoples. It is not a binding treaty.

=== terms y12-terms-fl
c: fl.2 fl.3
- Forced marriage => A marriage entered into without the free and full consent of one or both people, because of coercion, threat or deception, or where a person is under 16.
- Foster care => Care of a child in the home of an authorised carer when the child cannot live safely with their parents.
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Child Placement Principle => The principle that an Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander child who cannot live at home should be placed, in order of priority, with family, community or other Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander carers.

=== terms y12-terms-hl
c: hl.1 hl.4 hl.5
- Real property => Land and anything fixed to it, such as a house.
- Community housing => Social housing managed by not-for-profit organisations.
- Public housing => Social housing owned and managed by the state government.
- Specialist homelessness service => A funded service that gives accommodation and support to people who are homeless or at risk of homelessness.
