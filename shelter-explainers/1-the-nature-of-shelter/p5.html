Bringing it together

A scenario, a model paragraph, your evidence and six questions.

Worked example: Amira

Amira, 19, paid weekly for a room in a share house where the owner also lived. The owner sold the house and gave her five days to leave. She is now staying on a friend's couch.

What type of shelter did she have?A room as a lodger. She had no lease and no exclusive possession.
Which law applied?Not the Residential Tenancies Act 2010 (NSW). Only her agreement with the owner.
Is she homeless now?Yes, under the ABS definition: no secure tenure and no control over the space.
Does she have a right to shelter she can enforce?No. ICESCR art 11 is not part of Australian law.
Who has an obligation to help?NSW (Homes NSW, homelessness services); the Commonwealth through income support.

🔑 Your turn: Dev and Priya

Dev and Priya rent a two-bedroom flat with their three children. Their lease has ended and they are on a periodic agreement. Rent takes 55% of their income. The landlord wants them out so he can raise the rent for new tenants.

What type of shelter do they have?A private rental; they are tenants under a periodic agreement.
Which law applies?The Residential Tenancies Act 2010 (NSW), as amended in 2024.
Which elements of adequate housing are at risk?Affordability (55% of income), security of tenure, and habitability (five people in two bedrooms).
Can the landlord end the tenancy for that reason?No. Since 19 May 2025 he needs a ground set out in the Act; wanting a higher rent is not one.
Which government is responsible for each problem?NSW for tenancy law; the Commonwealth for Rent Assistance and tax settings that affect rents.

Model paragraph (TEEACL)

Explain why the right to shelter is not provided for in Australian law.

T
Topic
Although Australia has accepted the right to adequate housing in international law, the right is not provided for in Australian law because Parliament has never enacted it.
E
Explain
Australia ratified the ICESCR in 1975, and art 11(1) recognises everyone's right to an adequate standard of living, including housing. However, Australia is a dualist system, so a treaty has no effect in domestic courts until it is put into an Act.
E
Evidence
No Commonwealth or NSW Act does this, there is no right to housing in the Constitution, and the Housing Act 2001 (NSW) states only objects for public housing.
A
Analysis
As a result the right is not justiciable: 67,721 households were waiting for social housing in NSW in October 2025 and none could ask a court for a home. Grootboom shows this is a choice, because South Africa's Constitution lets a court test its government's housing programme.
C
Counter
It can be argued, as in Tanudjaja, that housing policy involves budgets that elected governments should control.
L
Link
Even so, without an enforceable right the people with the greatest need have the least legal protection, which limits how far the law can achieve justice in the provision of shelter.
